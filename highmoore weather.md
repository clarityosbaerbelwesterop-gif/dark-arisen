## 1. THE REGION'S IDENTITY

### 1.1 The Wind Is Permanent

**Cross-reference:** physics/wind_systems.md, highmoore_region.md Section 7.1.

**Highmoore is the game's wind region.** Cross-reference physics/wind_systems.md — the archipelago has wind states; Highmoore has wind as a constant with variable strength, and it is audible everywhere, always.

**Design law:** cross-reference light_elf_boss.md Section 3.4 — **there is exactly one place in Phase 8 where the wind stops**, and it is eleven minutes long, and it is the most frightening audio state in Dark Arisen because of this document.

### 1.2 The Contrast

Cross-reference physics/lava_heat_physics.md, sand_desert_physics.md, snow_ice_physics.md.

The archipelago's weather is **hostile** — heat that kills, sand that blinds, cold that takes fingers.

**Highmoore's weather is not hostile. It is relentless.** Nothing here is trying to kill Jake. It is simply always grey, always moving, and always slightly worse than comfortable.

## 2. THE FOUR STATES

**Cross-reference:** physics/weather_physics.md.

### 2.1 OVERCAST — the default

Cross-reference highmoore_region.md Section 3.2. Flat grey light, moderate wind, no rain. **Roughly forty-five percent of all Highmoore hours.**

### 2.2 BROKEN — the signature

**Cross-reference:** physics/sun_shadow_systems.md, highmoore_region.md Section 3.2.

Cloud with gaps. Cross-reference physics/sun_shadow_systems.md Section 3 — **shafts and cloud shadows crossing open moorland at wind speed**, which is the region's defining visual and cross-reference light_elves.md Section 3.2: **it is also the threat vector, and the player learns this in Movement Three.**

**Design law:** Broken must be common enough to be reliable and rare enough to be an event. Cross-reference highmoore_region.md Section 3.2 — **roughly twenty percent.**

### 2.3 RAIN — the workhorse

Cross-reference highmoore_region.md Section 7.3. Grey, persistent, cold, from the west.

**Roughly thirty percent**, and cross-reference Section 5: **it is readable.**

### 2.4 CLEAR — the rarity

Under five percent. Cross-reference physics/sun_shadow_systems.md — and on a clear day the basin is visible end to end from any rise, and cross-reference highmoore_region.md Section 4.3: **the whole political skyline is legible at once.**

**Design note:** cross-reference npcs/regional_populations.md — Highmoore's ambient dialogue **remarks on clear days**, every time, in every register, and the player will hear this several dozen times before noticing it is a pattern.

## 4. THE BURNING SEASON

**Cross-reference:** house_system.md Section 4.5.

### 4.1 The Meteorology

**After harvest, before the wet.** Cross-reference Section 3.1 — a window of roughly six weeks in which the ground is hard enough to move men across and the crops are in and burnable.

**Design note:** cross-reference house_system.md Principle 1.3 — **the convention is not a moral agreement. It is what the weather permits**, and Highmoore has built an institution on a meteorological fact and forgotten that it did.

### 4.2 The Wind and the Fire

**Cross-reference:** physics/fire_physics.md, physics/wind_systems.md.

**A wind region burns fast.** Cross-reference physics/fire_physics.md Section 4.1 — wind-driven spread, and cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md Section 2: this is why every farm in the Folds is stone with a slate or turf roof and the thatch is confined to the Waterlands.

### 4.3 The End of It

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_the_ball.md Section 2.4, house_arion.md Section 4.3.

**The ball happens the week the season ends.** Cross-reference princess_quest_the_ball.md Section 2.4 — the levy coming home, six hundred people on a road, and cross-reference Section 5: **and the first of the autumn rain about four days later.**

## 5. THE READABLE RAIN

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_the_lake.md Section 4.2, highmoore_region.md Section 7.3.

### 5.1 The Mechanic

**Cross-reference:** physics/weather_physics.md, physics/wind_systems.md.

Highmoore rain comes from the west across open ground and **it is visible for up to half an hour before it arrives.**

A grey wall on the horizon, moving at wind speed, and cross-reference physics/water_physics.md — **a player can watch it cross a lake toward them from four hundred metres.**

### 5.2 Why It Matters

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_the_lake.md Sections 4.2 and 7.1.

**The lake sequence's entire clock is this system.**

The rain is visible from minute one of the nine-minute ride and arrives at minute twenty-two of the lake. Cross-reference princess_quest_the_lake.md Section 4.2 — **the player watches it come for half an hour and it is the only timer in the sequence and nothing indicates that it is one.**

### 5.3 Elsewhere

Cross-reference mounted_travel.md — a rider can outrun it, shelter from it, or ride into it. Cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md Section 8.2 — **and inns matter in this region for a reason.**

## 6. WINTER

**Cross-reference:** physics/snow_ice_physics.md.

### 6.1 It Is Real

Snow on the High Moor and the fells, ice in the Waterlands, and cross-reference physics/snow_ice_physics.md — the Fjordlund systems apply at reduced severity.

### 6.2 What Changes

- **The High Moor becomes dangerous.** Cross-reference physics/movement_physics.md, Section 7 — **fog and snow together are the region's only genuinely lethal weather.**
- **The Waterlands freeze**, and cross-reference the_lesser_houses.md Section 2.2: **Mere's causeway is passable at all hours and Mere is briefly vulnerable**, and everyone knows, and cross-reference house_system.md Section 4.5: **nobody raids in winter, because the convention holds.**
- **Roads close.** Cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md Section 8.
- **The halls fill.** Cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md Section 4.3 — Highmoore's indoor season, and cross-reference house_system.md Section 8.2: this is when the stories get told and the feuds get talked about.

### 6.3 The Ambient

*"...four month of this. Four. And then it's spring and the bog and then it's the season again."*

*"...I'll say this for winter. Nobody's burning anything."*

## 7. FOG

**Cross-reference:** physics/weather_physics.md, highmoore_region.md Section 7.2.

### 7.1 The High Moor

**The region's one lethal weather state.**

Cross-reference physics/sun_shadow_systems.md, physics/sound_physics.md — visibility under twenty metres, sound distorted, and cross-reference highmoore_region.md Section 2.3: **the High Moor has no landmarks and every direction looks identical.**

### 7.2 The Practice

**Cross-reference:** house_system.md Principle 1.3, light_elves.md Section 4.

Highmoore has a rule about fog too. **Stop moving. Sit down. Wait.**

**Design note:** cross-reference light_elves.md Section 4.2 — every yeoman in the basin will tell Jake this, and describe it as obvious rather than as a practice, and cross-reference Section 7.3: **it is the one rule they do not deny believing in.**

### 7.3 Why That Is Interesting

Cross-reference light_elves.md Section 4.2 — Highmoore denies believing in the light practices while observing them absolutely.

**Nobody denies the fog rule.** The design does not comment, and cross-reference style_bible.md Pillar 2 — **the difference between the two is the whole of the region's relationship with what it knows.**

## 10. MVP CORE vs FULL VISION

### 10.1 MVP CORE

- Permanent wind with variable strength
- All four weather states at their target frequencies
- The readable rain with its half-hour approach
- Fog on the High Moor as the one lethal state, and the rule
- The burning season as a weather window

### 10.2 FULL VISION

- Full seasonal cycle including winter and the frozen causeway
- Byre-pit calendars readable off farm yards
- Ambient remarks on clear days
- The fog rule's undenied status
- Both questline weather events fully systemic rather than scripted

## 11. STYLE BIBLE COMPLIANCE

- ✅ **Pillar 1 (Staggered Register):** every register in Highmoore remarks on a clear day and none of them notice they all do it. *"I'll say this for winter. Nobody's burning anything."*
- ✅ **Pillar 2 (Lore Through Objects):** a byre-pit dug or filled. Stone roofs in the Folds and thatch only in the Waterlands. A calendar readable off a farmyard.
- ✅ **Pillar 3 (Layered Death):** fog is the only weather that kills, and the rule for it is the only practice nobody denies believing in.
- ✅ **Pillar 4 (Layered Humor):** four months of winter followed by the bog followed by the season, delivered flatly by people who have described their entire year and find it unremarkable.
- ✅ **Pillar 5 (Theme — Revenge):** a six-generation war with a season, and the season is a meteorological fact about when the ground is hard enough to move men across, and nobody in the basin remembers that this is why.

## 12. CONCLUSION

The Wind Region. Not hostile — relentless. Nothing here is trying to kill anybody. It is grey forty-five percent of the time, raining thirty, broken twenty, and clear under five, and every register in the basin remarks on a clear day and none of them have noticed they all do.

Rain from the west, visible for half an hour before it lands, crossing four hundred metres of open water toward two people who are about to be bad at dancing. Broken cloud with shafts and shadows running across open moor at wind speed, which is the most beautiful thing in the region and, from Movement Three onward, the thing the player watches with dread.

A raiding convention that is not a moral agreement but a six-week window when the ground is hard and the crops are in, built into an institution by people who have forgotten it was weather. Four months of winter when nobody burns anything and the halls fill and the stories get told.

And fog on the High Moor, where there are no landmarks and every direction is identical, about which Highmoore has a rule — stop moving, sit down, wait — and it is the only rule in the basin that nobody claims not to believe in.
