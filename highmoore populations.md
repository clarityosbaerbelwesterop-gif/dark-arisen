## 1. THE FIFTH REGISTER

**Cross-reference:** style_bible.md Pillar 1, npcs/regional_populations.md, highmoore_region.md Section 8.2.

### 1.1 What Dark Arisen Already Has

Four cultural registers across the archipelago: Imperial Spanish-coded, Albion English-mercantile, indigenous Rexan, Fjordlund Norse, plus Ashenmoor volcanic.

### 1.2 What Highmoore Adds

**The yeoman Middle.**

Direct, unimpressed, undeferential, dry, and **not poor.** Cross-reference house_system.md Section 2.1 — Highmoore's free farmers are armed, organised, and answer to a House without belonging to one.

**Design law:** cross-reference npcs/regional_populations.md — **no population in the archipelago speaks like this.** The Quiet Coast is the closest and it is not close.

### 1.3 The Test

**A Highmoore yeoman is not impressed by Jake and never becomes impressed by Jake.**

Cross-reference Section 5 — not after Belos, not after the reconstruction, not ever. **They will respect what he does and they will not defer.**

## 2. THE FOUR CLASSES

### 2.1 THE HOUSES — Anglo-noble High

**Cross-reference:** house_system.md, the_lesser_houses.md, house_arion.md Section 3.

Lords, heirs, household knights, and their families. Cross-reference the_lesser_houses.md — **sixteen lords in sixteen voices.**

**Design note:** cross-reference house_belos.md Section 3.4 — **Aldric Belos is the only High-status figure who talks like a yeoman, and knows it, and it makes Arion's charm read differently in hindsight.**

### 2.2 THE YEOMANRY — the Middle

Free farmers, mostly the Folds. Cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md Section 2 — **fortified farms, armed households, and a drill.**

They are called to the levy seasonally and go, and come home, and cross-reference highmoore_weather.md Section 3.2: **their year is a calendar and they can recite it.**

**Sample:**

*"Ride on or ride in. Either's fine. Just pick one from here so I know."*

*"I'm sworn to Pennard for the levy and that's the whole of it. He doesn't own the land and he doesn't own me and if he forgets I'll remind him."*

*"You've a good horse and a bad seat. Don't take that hard. Everyone here's had eleven years on one."*

### 2.3 THE VILLAGES — the Low

Cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md Section 3. Labourers, craftsmen, the church, and cross-reference Section 4.3: **Highmoore's Low is exhausted rather than frightened.**

*"...nobody remembers what started it. Nobody's asked in my lifetime. It's just what we've got."*

*"...it's like the weather, love. You don't ask the weather why."*

### 2.4 THE ROADS — transient

Carters, drovers, pedlars, and cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md Section 8.3: **people who move between Houses and are trusted by none of them and know everything.**

**Cross-reference princess_quest_the_ball.md Section 2.2, princess_quest_arion_falls.md Section 2.4, princess_quest_the_return.md Section 2.3** — **the drover, three times, and the third time he says good evening.**

## 3. WHOSE MAN ARE YOU

**Cross-reference:** house_system.md Section 6.1, Principle 1.4.

### 3.1 The Question

**Every interaction in Highmoore opens with it**, and cross-reference house_system.md Section 6.1: there is no *nowhere* in Highmoore's understanding of the world.

### 3.2 The Three Answers

- **The truth** — nobody believes it. Cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md — *"there isn't another side of the mountain."*
- **A lie** — cross-reference princess_quest_the_ball.md Section 5.1: **the ambassador, invented at a gate and kept for the rest of the questline.**
- **Nothing** — read as being somebody's man and hiding it, which is worse.

### 3.3 What It Costs

Cross-reference the_wizard.md Section 2.2 — being unlocatable makes Jake useless to everyone, dangerous to everyone, **and the only man in the basin who can cross every boundary.**

## 4. THE AMBIENT SYSTEM

**Cross-reference:** npcs/regional_populations.md Section 10.

### 4.1 The Four Layers

Cross-reference npcs/regional_populations.md Section 10.2 — the same architecture:

**LAYER ONE — BASE CULTURAL.** The war, the season, the weather, the ford, Ashby's fields, the price of wool.

**LAYER TWO — CURRENT EVENTS.** Cross-reference house_system.md Section 7 — which Houses hold what, whose roads are safe, who lost a holding.

**LAYER THREE — JAKE-REACTIVE.** Cross-reference Section 5.

**LAYER FOUR — WEATHER AND TIME.** Cross-reference highmoore_weather.md Section 2.4 — **and everyone remarks on a clear day.**

### 4.2 The Ford

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_settlements.md Section 7.2.

**Design law:** *"the ford"* is said constantly, across the whole basin, by everyone, in every register, and cross-reference house_belos.md Section 3.2 — **Aldric buried two sons there and so did about four hundred other families.**

### 4.3 The Denial

**Cross-reference:** light_elves.md Section 4.2.

Every practice about the light, described as grandmother's nonsense, observed absolutely, by everyone, forever.

*"I don't hold with it. Course I don't. And I'll not be up on the tops after harvest, so let's not have the conversation."*

## 5. STANDING WITHOUT A METER

**Cross-reference:** house_system.md Section 6.4, colonial_war/war_state_system.md Section 8.1.

### 5.1 The Rule

**No reputation meter, anywhere, ever.** Cross-reference highmoore_region.md Section 6.2 — the same discipline as the whole region.

### 5.2 What the Player Reads Instead

- Whether a gate opens
- Whether a lord uses his name
- **Whether the yeomanry stop calling him *you***
- Whether a road warden waves him through
- Whether the drover says good evening

### 5.3 After the Ball

Cross-reference princess_quest_the_ball.md Section 8.4 — Arion's gates close and cross-reference house_arion.md Section 7.3: **the guards are apologetic about it.**

### 5.4 After Belos

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_endgame_state.md Section 2.

The ambient shifts, and cross-reference highmoore_endgame_state.md Section 2.4 — **on the stair path it does not shift at all**, and the gossip web that discusses everything has nothing to say, and the silence is total and unexplained.

### 5.5 After the Reconstruction

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_reconstruction.md Section 7.2, colonial_war/castle_ownership.md Section 10.

**The Governor Problem arrives faster here.**

*"...road's sworn now. Not by a House. By that one."*

*"...him. The one over the mountain. No — I'll not call him a lord. I'll not."*

**Design note:** cross-reference colonial_war/castle_ownership.md Section 10.2 — the word changes in mouths, and in Highmoore it changes in a basin where he killed three hundred people, and **the yeomanry are the ones who refuse the word.**

## 7. HOW THE POPULATION CHANGES

**Cross-reference:** npcs/regional_populations.md Section 11, npcs/building_evolution.md.

### 7.1 Belos

Cross-reference princess_quest_belos.md, highmoore_endgame_state.md Section 2.2.

Three hundred dead in a basin of perhaps forty thousand. Cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md Section 6 — **the graveyards absorb it, because Highmoore's graveyards are already too large.**

**Design law:** cross-reference npcs/regional_populations.md — **there is no ambient dialogue anywhere in Highmoore in which anyone blames Jake for it out loud.** They discuss it as an event. Cross-reference highmoore_endgame_state.md Section 2.2: *"they say there's still folk under it and they say it like it's weather."*

### 7.2 The Belos Villages

Cross-reference house_belos.md — the House is gone and the villages are not. Cross-reference the_lesser_houses.md Section 6.3 — **Cadwall takes them**, and cross-reference highmoore_reconstruction.md Section 7.1: legally, slowly, with masons.

*"...blue and black now. Grey and green before. My father was under three different colours and he never left this field."*

Cross-reference highmoore_reconstruction.md. Building states, population growth, and cross-reference npcs/children_and_families.md Section 4: **children on greens that did not have them.**

## 11. STYLE BIBLE COMPLIANCE

- ✅ **Pillar 1 (Staggered Register):** **this document is Pillar 1.** A fifth register, built on a class that exists nowhere else in Dark Arisen — armed, organised, unimpressed, and permanently undeferential.
- ✅ **Pillar 2 (Lore Through Objects):** a father under three different colours who never left the field. Byre-pits. Graveyards that were already too large.
- ✅ **Pillar 3 (Layered Death):** *"they say it like it's weather."* Four hundred families and the same ford. And on one path, a basin that has nothing at all to say about Jake, forever.
- ✅ **Pillar 4 (Layered Humor):** **Highmoore's charm lives here.** Tam. The drover. Odo. A road warden's opinion of Ridd. A yeoman telling Jake he has a good horse and a bad seat and not to take it hard.
- ✅ **Pillar 5 (Theme — Revenge):** a population that has absorbed six generations of a war nobody can explain, that discusses three hundred dead the way it discusses weather, that never once blames Jake out loud — and whose yeomanry, at the end of all of it, will still not call him a lord.

## 13. CONCLUSION

The Fifth Register. Armed free farmers in stone houses with a drill and a calendar they can recite, who are sworn to a House for the levy and that is the whole of it, and who will remind him if he forgets. Villagers who compare a six-generation war to the weather and do not ask the weather why. Carters and drovers who move between Houses and are trusted by none of them and know everything, one of whom takes eleven minutes to move a hundred sheep and, the third time, says good evening.

A basin that opens every conversation with *whose man are you* and has no word for *nobody*. A population that discusses three hundred dead under a collapsed range the way it discusses rain, that never once blames him out loud, and whose yeomanry — after the roads are sworn and the bridge is up and the forges are lit — will still not call him a lord.

*I'll not. No. I'll not.*
