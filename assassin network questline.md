# THE ASSASSIN NETWORK QUESTLINE — GOING ON OFFENCE

**Status:** Design Draft v1.0
**Phase:** Phase 7 — Colonial War, Castles & Dungeons (Boss Tier Document 3 — FINAL Boss Tier — Document 13 of 16)
**Tier:** Boss Tier
**Related:** colonial_war/assassin_network.md, colonial_war/retaliation_system.md, colonial_war/colonial_war_bosses.md, colonial_war/krone_colonies.md, colonial_war/imperial_colonies.md, npcs/black_market_fences.md, npcs/imperial_contacts.md, npcs/shippable_specialists.md, npcs/named_crew_deep_dives.md, npcs/the_named_dead.md, mechanics/stealth.md, style_bible.md
**Purpose:** The offensive questline against both hunter organisations — tracing contracts, infiltrating cells, the two reveals, the destroy-or-turn decision, and the long consequences of each.
**Inspirations:** Assassin's Creed II (the target list), The Wire (following the money to the institution), Tinker Tailor Soldier Spy (the mole hunt), Hitman: World of Assassination (infiltration variety), Godfather Part II (the conspiracy that goes further up than expected)

---

## 1. QUESTLINE PHILOSOPHY — THE FIVE PRINCIPLES

### 1.1 Principle 1: Enduring Is Not the Only Option

Cross-reference assassin_network.md Principle 1.5. Retaliation's hunter layer is designed to feel inexorable. **This questline is the proof that it isn't.** The player who decides to stop being hunted has a way to do it, and it is a campaign.

### 1.2 Principle 2: Follow the Paper, Not the Blood

Both organisations run on documents. Cross-reference style_bible.md Pillar 2, and the Phase 7 pattern established across every colony and castle: **the crimes are the paperwork.** Contracts, writs, ledgers, dispatch copies. The questline is an investigation, not a killing spree.

### 1.3 Principle 3: Two Organisations, Two Endings

The Cofradía and the Regulators cannot be dealt with the same way, because they are not the same kind of thing. **One is a faith. One is a department.** You destroy a faith or you don't. You restructure a department.

### 1.4 Principle 4: The Reveal Must Cost Something

Both branches end in a revelation that reframes the hunt. Neither reveal is a twist for its own sake; both make Jake's situation worse in a way he has to live with.

### 1.5 Principle 5: Turning Is Harder and Worth More

Destroying either network is achievable, satisfying, and permanent. **Turning one is significantly harder, morally muddier, and produces the strongest strategic asset in Phase 7.** Cross-reference indigenous_liberation_alliance.md Section 5.1 — the same design law: the best outcomes are not the loudest.

## 2. ENTRY CONDITIONS

### 2.1 The Trigger

The questline becomes available when **all** of:
- Retaliation Heat with either faction has reached Stage 3 at least once
- Jake has survived (killed, spared, or bought off) **three or more named hunters**
- Momentum Phase Beta or later (cross-reference war_state_system.md Section 5)

### 2.2 The Instigating Scene

Cross-reference npcs/named_crew_deep_dives.md.

The questline does not begin with a quest-giver. It begins with **the crew raising it.**

After the third hunter, in the great cabin, without ceremony:

**BIG TOM:** *"That's three. Three in — what, two months? And I'll say the thing nobody's saying: one of these is going to get lucky. Not because he's good. Because there's more of them than there is of us."*

**MIRA:** *"So we stop waiting."*

**INES:** *"They keep records. Everything else in this world keeps records. So do they."*

**FATHER SALVIO:** *"...and if we find the records, Captain, we will have a decision to make about what we do with the men whose names are in them. I would like that decision made in advance, and made once."*

**Design note:** the questline is framed from the outset as a decision about mercy under pressure, not a revenge arc. Cross-reference style_bible.md Pillar 5.

## 3. THE INVESTIGATION LAYER — SHARED

### 3.1 Evidence Types

Every hunter encounter can yield evidence, and the player must have been **collecting it without knowing it mattered:**

- **Cofradía:** the black candles (each is stamped with a chapter-house mark), writs of release carried on the person, rosaries with a house's cipher
- **Regulators:** the Notices of Engagement (each bears a file reference and an issuing office), expense receipts, contract stubs

**Design note:** cross-reference assassin_network.md Section 2.3 and 3.3 — **the collectibles established as flavour become the questline's evidence base.** A player who kept them is rewarded. A player who did not can reacquire them the hard way (Section 3.3).

### 3.2 The Brokers

Cross-reference npcs/black_market_fences.md, npcs/imperial_contacts.md, npcs/shippable_specialists.md.

Four brokers move the investigation, and each will only go so far:

**THE WHISPER LADY** — sells the structure but not the names. *"I deal in knowing. I do not deal in the Cofradía. Ask why and I will tell you the price of the answer is higher than you want to pay."*

**FRAU HELGA BRENNAN** — Imperial spymaster. Will trade Cofradía intelligence **for Regulator intelligence** and vice versa, which forces the player to work both branches at least partially in parallel.

**MATEUSZ VOLTARI** — cross-reference npcs/shippable_specialists.md. If recruited, the underground hears about contracts before execution; if not, he can still be hired. Mateusz is the only broker who will physically go with Jake.

**CASSANDRA "CASS" WELLS** — if recruited, she is the questline's analyst and reduces its length substantially. Cross-reference npcs/shippable_specialists.md Section 7.

### 3.3 The Alternative Path

A player with no collected evidence can start from a captured hunter instead. Cross-reference assassin_network.md Section 8.2 — **a spared hunter is a source.** This is the questline's quiet argument for mercy, and it is not stated anywhere in dialogue.

## 4. BRANCH ONE — THE COFRADÍA

### 4.1 Stage 1: The Chapter-House Marks

Three candles from three different hunters carry three different marks. The marks are architectural — each identifies a chapter-house, and Cross-reference npcs/imperial_contacts.md: **Father Konstantin Vossberg can read them**, and is appalled to be asked.

**VOSSBERG:** *"...I know this mark. I know the building. Captain, I have taken Communion in that building."*

**Cross-reference:** npcs/named_crew_deep_dives.md — Father Salvio's involvement from here on is constant and complicated.

### 4.2 Stage 2: The Three Chapter-Houses

Three infiltrations, three different locations, three different textures. Cross-reference mechanics/stealth.md.

**THE HOUSE AT SAN BENITO (Puerto de la Corona)** — attached to a working parish. Cross-reference castle_catalog.md Section 3.4 — the cathedral wing's Inquisition office is upstairs. The infiltration happens **during a Mass**, and the congregation is entirely innocent.

**THE HOUSE AT VALDEÑA-EN-MAR (a small coastal town)** — a seminary. **Cross-reference npcs/children_and_families.md protection rules absolutely:** there are boys here, students, aged twelve to sixteen, and the design's rules apply without exception. They are never in danger, never targets, and never used. Their presence is the point: **the Cofradía recruits.**

**THE HOUSE AT PALE ISLE** — the administrative chapter, and the writs are signed here. Cross-reference npcs/imperial_contacts.md Section 5: **Inquisitor Theodora Blanc's signature is on all of them.**

### 4.3 Stage 3: The Writ Ledger

The Pale Isle house holds the order's release ledger — every writ, every brother, every target, going back forty years.

**What it shows:**
- The Cofradía has killed for the Empire, for the Church, and on nine occasions for private parties who paid the order directly
- Blanc's signature is on Jake's writ
- **And on a writ from eleven years ago, for a target in Ashenmoor, connected to a shrine-clearance**

**Cross-reference:** imperial_colonies.md Section 5.9, npcs/the_named_dead.md Section 3.1 — **the Cofradía was involved at San Rafael.** Not in Jaya's death directly, but in what came after: the brothers who were sent to ensure the shrine did not become a site of resistance.

**Design note:** this is a thread, not a resolution. Mbah Seruni does not learn it unless Jake tells her, and cross-reference the_named_dead.md Section 2.3 — the design offers no closure mechanic.

### 4.4 Stage 4: The Reveal — The Primera

**The order's master.**

**La Sombra Primera is Padre Anselmo Vidal.**

Cross-reference assassin_network.md Section 5.4. The Confessor — the elite hunter who may have already sat with Jake, heard him, absolved him, and tried to kill him — is the man who runs the Cofradía. He came himself, at Stage 4, because he has come himself for every significant target for twenty-two years.

**He does not hide it and never did.** He simply was not asked.

**VIDAL (on the reveal):**
*"You seem surprised. Hermano, I told you I had killed nineteen men and heard nineteen confessions. Did you imagine someone assigned me that work?"*

*"I do not send brothers to do what I will not do. That is the whole of my authority. It is also, I am aware, the whole of my sin."*

### 4.5 Stage 5: The Cofradía Decision

**DESTROY:**
Take the writ ledger and publish it. Cross-reference npcs/imperial_contacts.md — hand it to Kessler, to Vossberg, to the Reform faction. **The Cofradía is dissolved by the Church itself**, which cannot survive nine private contracts becoming public.

Vidal does not resist the dissolution. He resists Jake, once, finally, and cross-reference colonial_war_bosses.md — his death is Style D and he faces it with complete equanimity.

**Consequences:** all Cofradía hunters cease permanently. Blanc's position collapses (cross-reference npcs/imperial_contacts.md Section 5 — a major Imperial antagonist neutralised). And Vossberg, who took Communion in that building, has to live in a Church that had this in it.

**TURN — and it is barely possible:**
Vidal cannot be bought, frightened, or reasoned with. **He can be confessed to.**

Cross-reference assassin_network.md Section 5.4: if Jake took the confession when it was offered — genuinely, with the real dialogue options, about Ethan and the colonies and what he has become — **Vidal remembers it.** And a man who has heard nineteen confessions and found them the harder work is, it turns out, reachable through exactly one door.

The turn is not an alliance. Vidal does not work for Jake. **He withdraws the order from the hunt, on the grounds that he no longer believes the writ was righteous**, and he tells Blanc so to her face.

**Consequences:** Cofradía hunters cease. Vidal survives, and appears twice more in the endgame — not as an ally, as a conscience. Cross-reference style_bible.md Pillar 5: **he is the only character in the game who asks Jake about his revenge and has standing to.**

## 5. BRANCH TWO — THE REGULATORS

### 5.1 Stage 1: The File References

Every Notice of Engagement bears a file reference. Cross-reference assassin_network.md Section 3.3.

The references decode — with a Company clerk's help, or Cass Wells's, or Mateusz's — into an issuing office, a contract number, and **an engaging party.**

**The first surprise:** not all of them say the Honourable Archipelago Company.

### 5.2 Stage 2: The Coventry House Station

Cross-reference castle_catalog.md Section 8.4, colonial_war_bosses.md Section 13.2.

The Office of Contract Enforcement, Irregular Division, occupies the second floor of Coventry House. Cross-reference castle_sieges.md Section 4 — the night infiltration through the counting halls leads here.

**What is in it:**
- The engagement register (every contract, every Regulator, every fee)
- The settlement receipts
- Kettle's own working files, which are meticulous and include his private assessments of every Regulator under him
- **The private-party contracts**

### 5.3 Stage 3: The Reveal — The Engaging Parties

**Nine of the contracts on Jake are not from the Company.**

They are private engagements, paid by:
- Two rival pirate captains (cross-reference npcs/recurring_quest_givers.md — one of them may be Captain Catalina "Red" Vasquez, depending on the state of that relationship, and if so the questline forces a reckoning)
- A merchant house Jake ruined by raiding
- **Gobernador Francisco de Silva** — cross-reference imperial_colonies.md Section 6.3. If he is alive and Jake spared him, he has been hedging. Cross-reference colonial_war_bosses.md Section 7.4: *"He is also, obviously, selling Jake to someone else. The game does not resolve which."* **This is the resolution.**
- And one engagement, older than the rest, with no name attached — only a Company reference and a note in Kettle's hand: *"Do not action. Keep on file. — B.K."*

**The unactioned contract:** Full Vision. Cross-reference bosses/crimson_armada.md — the reference traces to a party the Crimson Armada uses for legitimate business, and Kettle has been sitting on it for two years because he did not like the smell of it. **He is the only man in either empire who noticed something.**

### 5.4 Stage 4: Kettle

Cross-reference assassin_network.md Section 6.4, colonial_war_bosses.md Section 13.2.

Kettle knows Jake is coming before Jake arrives, because Kettle keeps good books and Jake has been asking questions in places that generate paper.

He does not ambush. He waits in his own office with the register on the desk.

**KETTLE:**
*"Sit down. No — genuinely, sit down, this'll go quicker. I've had four Regulators killed and I've written four letters to four families and I've costed the whole business at just under nine hundred pounds including the letters. That's what you are to me. Nine hundred pounds and four letters."*

*"Now. You've come for the register. Take it. I'll not die for a filing cabinet. But before you do — there's a contract in there I've never actioned, and I'd like to talk to you about why."*

### 5.5 Stage 5: The Regulator Decision

**DESTROY:**
Take the register, burn the station, kill or rout Kettle.

**Consequences:** cross-reference castle_catalog.md Section 8.4 — the Albion retaliation network stalls archipelago-wide for the remainder of the game. Regulator hunters cease. **And the settlement receipts burn with it, which means every debtor who had been paying off an engagement now has no proof they paid.** Cross-reference krone_colonies.md Section 3.4 — the same cruelty as withdrawal, in miniature.

**TURN — and Kettle offers it himself:**
Kettle is a department head, not a believer. He has an instruction and a fee, and cross-reference retaliation_system.md Section 7.1: **Albion respects payment.**

Jake can settle. Not one contract — **all of them, plus the department's operating costs, plus a retainer.** The figure is enormous, roughly equivalent to a state treasure.

**What Jake gets:**
- All Regulator engagements against him closed, permanently, on the books
- **The Irregular Division's intelligence apparatus, pointed elsewhere.** Cross-reference retaliation_system.md Section 6.1: Kettle's people become Jake's warning network. They are extremely good at it.
- The unactioned contract, and Kettle's two years of quiet suspicion about it
- Kettle himself as a recurring contact — cross-reference npcs/shippable_specialists.md format, though he does not join the crew. He has a desk. He likes his desk.

**KETTLE (on being paid):**
*"Right. That's cleared, then. I'll have the closure notices out by the end of the week — you'll want to keep those, some of my lads don't read their post."*

*"And Captain. The unactioned one. I'll send you what I have. Not as part of the arrangement. Just because I've been carrying it two years and I'd like it to be somebody else's problem."*

## 6. THE DOUBLE OUTCOME

### 6.1 Both Branches Completed

A player who resolves both organisations — by any combination of destroy and turn — reaches the questline's terminal state:

**Retaliation's Stage 2 layer is permanently disabled.** Cross-reference retaliation_system.md — the empires can still counter-attack, take hostages, and burn settlements, but **nobody is hunting Jake personally any more.**

### 6.2 What That Feels Like

Deliberately anticlimactic. No fanfare. Just: the candles stop arriving. The notices stop arriving. And a player who spent thirty hours flinching at every tavern door notices the absence about two chapters later.

Cross-reference npcs/named_crew_deep_dives.md — **Big Tom is the one who says it:**

*"...huh. Y'know I haven't looked at the door in a while."*

### 6.3 The Cost Ledger

| Branch | Destroy | Turn |
|---|---|---|
| **Cofradía** | Blanc's collapse; Vossberg's crisis of institution; Vidal dead | Vidal alive as Jake's conscience; Blanc humiliated but standing |
| **Regulators** | Network stalled; debtors' receipts burned | Enormous cost; Kettle's apparatus as Jake's warning network; the unactioned contract |

## 7. THE THREADS THIS OPENS

### 7.1 The Blanc Thread

Cross-reference npcs/imperial_contacts.md Section 5. Whichever way the Cofradía branch resolves, **Inquisitor Theodora Blanc's signature is now documented on forty years of writs.** She becomes reachable — politically, via Kessler and the Reform faction, or otherwise.

### 7.2 The De Silva Thread

Cross-reference Section 5.3. If de Silva was spared and has been paying for private contracts on Jake, the questline hands the player that information and no instruction about it.

### 7.3 The San Rafael Thread

Cross-reference Section 4.3, imperial_colonies.md Section 5.9. The Cofradía's involvement in the shrine's aftermath is one more piece of a wound that already has an address and a name. Cross-reference npcs/the_named_dead.md Section 2.3: **the game does not require Jake to tell Mbah Seruni anything.**

### 7.4 The Unactioned Contract

Cross-reference Section 5.3, bosses/crimson_armada.md. Full Vision: Kettle's two-year suspicion is a genuine lead into the main plot, and the questline that seemed like a side campaign against hired killers ends by handing Jake a thread that goes somewhere much worse.

## 10. STYLE BIBLE COMPLIANCE

- ✅ **Pillar 1 (Staggered Register):** Vidal's devout High-Middle against Kettle's clerical Middle, and the questline's structure gives each a long scene to be entirely themselves in.
- ✅ **Pillar 2 (Lore Through Objects):** The candles and the notices, established as flavour in the previous document, become the evidence base. The writ ledger. The engagement register. The settlement receipts that burn.
- ✅ **Pillar 3 (Layered Death):** Vidal's equanimity. The four letters Kettle wrote and costed. Style D, and the questline's body count is low by design.
- ✅ **Pillar 4 (Layered Humor):** Kettle costing a vendetta at just under nine hundred pounds including the letters. *"Some of my lads don't read their post."* Big Tom noticing two chapters late that he has stopped watching the door.
- ✅ **Pillar 5 (Theme — Revenge):** Father Salvio asks in the first scene that the decision about mercy be made in advance and made once, and the questline is thirty hours of testing whether Jake meant it. And at the end, if Vidal lives, Jake has acquired the only person in the archipelago with standing to ask him what all of this was for.

## 12. CONCLUSION

Going on Offence. It starts in the great cabin after the third hunter, with Big Tom saying the thing nobody is saying and Father Salvio asking that the decision about mercy be made in advance and made once. It runs through candle-marks read by a priest who has taken Communion in the building, through a Mass and a seminary and an administrative chapter where an Inquisitor's signature sits on forty years of writs. It runs through a night in the counting halls to a second-floor office where a department head keeps very good books.

And it ends twice: with a man who has heard nineteen confessions discovering he does not believe the twentieth writ was righteous, and with a man who costed a blood feud at just under nine hundred pounds handing over a contract he has been sitting on for two years because he did not like the smell of it.

Two chapters later, Big Tom notices he has stopped watching the door.

**Phase 7 Subsystem 13 of 16 — complete.**
