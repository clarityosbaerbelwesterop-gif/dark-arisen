## 5. THE QUALITY LAW

**Cross-reference:** quest_design_philosophy.md Law Four.

**There is no writing-quality tier.**

The Princess questline is optional. Cross-reference princess_quest_overview.md — **it is also the most emotionally significant content in Dark Arisen**, and cross-reference main_story_structure.md Section 1.1: **the spine never mentions it.**

**Acceptance test:** every Thread must contain at least one moment that would survive being the main story's ending.

## 6. STYLE BIBLE COMPLIANCE

- ✅ **Pillar 1:** seventeen threads across five cultural registers, and Highmoore's twenty-four Turn quests are mostly people arguing about fields.
- ✅ **Pillar 2:** a chart in a sixty-year sailor's quarters marking one place he never went.
- ✅ **Pillar 3:** the Named Dead is a Thread with no ending, and Thread #17 resolves in Chapter 9 whether or not it was pursued.
- ✅ **Pillar 4:** a hunting trespass, resolved by lawyers.
- ✅ **Pillar 5:** the longest questline in the game is a reconstruction that balances nothing, and the most important one is optional and unmarked and never mentioned by the plot.

---

## 7. CONCLUSION

The Seventeen Threads. Five crew questlines, of which the strongest is a woman looking for her daughter and is never once presented as a reward for owning a settlement. Seven in the archipelago, including four sets of labour records from four unrelated places that together let freed families find each other across a century. Three in Highmoore, of which the longest is twelve hours of rebuilding a basin with no completion bonus and nobody thanking him.

One that is not a questline at all but an accumulation of names and has no ending. One that touches the spine and resolves in Chapter 9 whether the player pursued it or not.

A hundred and thirty-two shorter ones, and not one of them is a fetch or a kill-ten, and twenty-four of them are in Highmoore and are mostly people arguing about fields.

And an acceptance test that every one of the seventeen must contain a moment that would survive being the ending of the main story.
