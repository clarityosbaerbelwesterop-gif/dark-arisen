# M7 authoritative Tier-1 boss register

**Authority:** `colonial war bosses.md` — *The Nine Who Hold*  
**Purpose:** resolve the historical boss-count ambiguity before M7 content scale-out.

The authoritative Tier-1 commander set contains exactly nine entries:

1. `boss.herrera` — General-Gobernador Alonso de Herrera — Puerto de la Corona / La Ciudadela de la Corona.
2. `boss.reyes` — General Baltasar Reyes — Nueva Esperanza / Fuerte Esperanza.
3. `boss.cruz` — Comandante Isabel Cruz — Fuerte San Rafael.
4. `boss.de_silva` — Gobernador Francisco de Silva — Bahía Dorada / Castillo Dorado.
5. `boss.vega` — General Rodrigo Vega — Puesto del Norte.
6. `boss.blackwood` — Lord-Director Edmund Blackwood — New Coventry / Coventry House.
7. `boss.sterling` — Admiral Katherine Sterling — Port Sterling / The Sterling Bastion.
8. `boss.ashcroft` — General Percival Ashcroft — Blackwood Landing / Ashcroft Hall.
9. `boss.thorne` — Colonel Marcus Thorne — Fort Resolute.

## Rules

- These nine and only these nine satisfy the M7 `Tier1Boss` manifest count.
- Tier-2 castle commanders, named assassins, dungeon bosses, secret bosses, creature bosses and legacy Phase-1/2 boss files remain valid in their own categories but do not increase this count.
- Every Tier-1 entry must preserve its authored killed/spared/avoided resolution contract, including Vega's refusal-to-be-spared behavior where the apparent spare path becomes leaving rather than a clean spared resolution.
- This register does not replace the source boss documents. It is the engineering counting authority used by the M7 content manifest and validator.
