# Core crew audition pronunciation guide

This guide is deliberately narrow: it covers only words used in the first locked AI-voice audition lines. It is not the final multilingual pronunciation bible.

- **Captain** — ordinary English pronunciation; role address, not shouted unless the voice direction says so.
- **Mi capitán** — Spanish reading, approximately “mee kah-pee-TAHN”; stress the final syllable of *capitán*.
- **Esmeralda** — approximately “es-meh-RAHL-dah”.
- **harbor** — neutral English; do not force a modern regional accent inconsistent with the role bible.
- **antidote** — clear, clinical delivery for Ines; do not dramatize.

## Direction rule

The written character voice bibles remain authoritative for accent, rhythm and register. This document supplies pronunciation only. AI auditions must not exaggerate Fjordlund, Rexan, Pale Isle or Caribbean-coded accents into caricature.
