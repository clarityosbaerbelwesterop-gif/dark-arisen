# Kimi K3 job — finite mission authorship gap review

This is a **canon-gap analysis and production-plan proposal**, not a mission generator.

Dark Arisen requires finite authored mission pools. The current source reconciliation records:

- 17/17 Narrative Threads source-grounded;
- 3/132 Turn identities individually grounded;
- 1/147 Standing variants individually grounded.

Your task is to determine what the existing repository already specifies strongly enough to promote into additional stable mission identities **without inventing people, places, motives, outcomes, rewards or region allocations**.

## Required output

1. Cite every source-backed mission candidate with exact source document and section/heading where possible.
2. Separate candidates into:
   - `READY_FOR_NATIVE_ID` — all identity fields genuinely sourced;
   - `PARTIAL` — some fields sourced, exact gaps listed;
   - `DESIGN-GAP` — quota exists but canon does not supply a mission identity.
3. Reconcile the 132 Turn regional quota and the 147 Standing total against what can actually be supported.
4. Identify reusable mission mechanics/components that can support authored variants without radiant/procedural content.
5. Identify mutual exclusions, expiration/world-time consequences, NPC recurrence and journal requirements.
6. Produce a dependency-ordered human authoring backlog for the unresolved identities.

## Hard prohibitions

- Do not fabricate the missing 129 Turns or 146 Standing variants.
- Do not invent a random mission table, radiant generator, template filler, anonymous quest-giver or generic reward.
- Do not turn a quota slot into an authored mission merely by assigning an ID.
- Do not add quest markers, minimap tracking or UI accept buttons.
- Do not change locked economy, Standing, BODY/CRAFT/STANDING, save, time or social laws.

A high-quality result may conclude that many quota slots remain unresolved. Accuracy is more important than apparent completion.
