## Important visual boundaries

### Impossible does not mean unconstrained

Region-06 flora may break ordinary geometry, but the source still controls what remains unresolved. The Infinite Branch can guide impossible-perspective art/reference work without selecting one final silhouette or exact dimensions. Memory Oak player-history imagery belongs to runtime/state presentation rather than one baked universal texture.

### Ethan's Grove

The grove has concrete legacy plant composition, but its discovery is framed as a Chapter-9 Ethan story event. `Docs/DesignAuthority.md` requires Ethan/final-act material to be reconciled against Phase 11 instead of silently restored. The grove therefore remains blocked for final current-story geometry/placement until that story decision is resolved.

### Master-count discrepancy

`docs/design/fauna/legendary_creatures.md` summarizes three legendary trees in its introduction, while later records/cross-references expose five tree/grove identities: World Root, Ethan's Grove, Memory Oak, Whispering Grove and Infinite Branch. This matrix follows individually grounded records and preserves the summary mismatch as a design gap.
