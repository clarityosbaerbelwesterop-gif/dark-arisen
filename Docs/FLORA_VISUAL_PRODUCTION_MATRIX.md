## Important visual boundaries

### Impossible does not mean unconstrained

Region-06 flora is allowed to break ordinary geometry, but the source still controls what remains unresolved. The Infinite Branch may receive impossible-perspective reference work without selecting one final silhouette or exact dimensions. Memory Oak bark may be visually intricate, but player-history scenes belong to runtime rather than a baked canonical texture.

### Ethan's Grove

The grove has a concrete legacy plant composition, but its discovery is framed as a Chapter-9 Ethan story event. Current `Docs/DesignAuthority.md` requires Ethan/final-act material to be reconciled against Phase 11 rather than silently restored. The grove therefore remains `RequirementOnly` and provider-blocked until that story decision is current authority.

### Master-count discrepancy

`docs/design/fauna/legendary_creatures.md` summarizes three legendary trees in its introduction, while its own later records/cross-reference expose five tree/grove identities: World Root, Ethan's Grove, Memory Oak, Whispering Grove and Infinite Branch. This matrix follows the individually grounded records and preserves the summary mismatch as a design gap rather than silently changing canon.

## Evidence boundary

Provider-ready means only that a bounded concept/reference request can be constructed. No Higgsfield generation, 3D vegetation asset, wind simulation, foliage instance, Unreal import, material, collision, LOD/Nanite setup, runtime ecology or Alpha acceptance is claimed here.
