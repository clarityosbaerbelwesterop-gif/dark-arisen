**Status:** Design Draft v1.0
**Phase:** Phase 8 — Highmoore (Movement Three — Document 15 of ~30)

## 1. THE SEQUENCE'S BRIEF

### 1.1 What It Is

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_overview.md Section 1.2.

Twenty-five minutes at a lake in which nothing whatsoever happens, followed by four seconds, followed by half an hour in which the game asks the player for nothing at all.

### 1.2 The Rule Above All Others

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_overview.md Section 7.1.

**The game does not help.** No slow motion. No score. No camera move. No telegraph of any kind. Cross-reference physics/sound_physics.md — the arrow sounds like almost nothing and the player will not understand for about one second.

## 2. MOVEMENT FIVE — THE LETTER

### 2.1 The Delivery

**Cross-reference:** wizard_questline.md Section 8, princess_quest_overview.md Section 6.2.

After the Light Elves. Four to eight hours of play since Arion. The Wizard produces it and **he has been carrying it for some time**, and cross-reference wizard_questline.md: he says why, and his reason is not good, and he knows it, and he says so.

**THE WIZARD:** *"I have had this eleven days."*

*(a pause)*

*"You would have gone. That is the whole of my reasoning and I am aware of what it is worth."*

### 2.2 The False Letter

**Cross-reference:** the_princess.md Section 9.1.

In her hand. Signed **Elowen**, in full.

---

*Captain —*

*I am with child and it is yours.*

*I am not asking you for anything and I would rather you did not come. My father knows. He is being better about it than I expected, which frightens me more than shouting would have.*

*If you do come, do not come to the gate. There is a track past the eastern paddock.*

*I am sorry to have written this at all.*

*Elowen Arion*

---

**Design note:** cross-reference the_princess.md Section 9.4 — she signs the false letter in full because her father was watching her write it. **The player has no reason to notice this until they see the other one signed E.**

### 2.3 The Ride Back

**Cross-reference:** mounted_travel.md Section 6.2 — no fast travel.

Long. Cross-reference physics/weather_physics.md — Highmoore weather, and the player wants to be there, and cannot be there faster.

## 3. ARION, THE SECOND TIME

### 3.1 The Track Past the Eastern Paddock

Cross-reference house_arion.md Section 7.3 — the gates are closed to Jake. The track is not.

### 3.2 The Argument

**Cross-reference:** the user's original outline — *"sie streitet bis Jake ihr ein Kuss gibt."*

She is furious. Not at the pregnancy. **At his being there.**

**ELOWEN:** *"I wrote *do not come.* I wrote it in the first three lines. Did you read past them?"*

**ELOWEN:** *"You have no idea what this house is. You had one night in it and a good breakfast and you think you know what you have ridden into."*

**ELOWEN:** *"Go back. Please. Go back over your mountain that doesn't exist."*

**Design note:** cross-reference the_princess.md Section 7.4 — **every word of this is true and it is also her trying to save his life**, and the player will read it as cold feet, and that is intended.

### 3.3 The Kiss

**Player-initiated.** A single prompt, once, and cross-reference princess_quest_overview.md Section 3.2 — **it can be declined**, and if it is, she says *"good"* and turns away and the sequence ends and the questline ends there.

If taken: she stops. She stands there. And then —

**ELOWEN:** *"...right."*

*(a long pause)*

**ELOWEN:** *"Can you ride?"*

**JAKE:** *"...a bit."*

**ELOWEN:** *"That's a no. Come on."*

## 4. THE RIDE

### 4.1 The Mechanics

**Cross-reference:** mounted_travel.md Section 8.1.

**Nine minutes. Gait capped at a trot. Cannot be skipped, sped up, or fast-travelled.**

Two horses. She is on an Arion grey and cross-reference house_arion.md Section 5 — she rides beautifully. Jake is on the grey her father gave him and cross-reference princess_quest_the_ball.md Section 9.1 — **he is riding a gift from the man who is arranging this.**

### 4.2 The Rain

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_region.md Section 7.3, physics/weather_physics.md.

**It is visible on the western horizon from minute one and it arrives at minute twenty-two.**

The player watches it come for the entire ride and the entire time at the lake. Cross-reference princess_quest_overview.md Section 5.2 — **the weather is the scene's clock and the player can read it.**

### 4.3 What She Says

**Cross-reference:** the_princess.md Sections 5.5 and 8.5. Three of the things she says are goodbyes.

**ELOWEN:** *"You ride like a man who has spent his life on something that moves under him for other reasons."*
*"That's not an insult. It's nearly a compliment. Stop looking like that."*

**ELOWEN:** *"I want you to describe the sea to me. Not — no, properly. Take your time, we have ages."*
*(and cross-reference princess_quest_the_ball.md Section 6.3 — **she has never seen it**, and Jake describes it for two full minutes, and it is the longest uninterrupted speech he gives in the entire game)*

**ELOWEN:** *"I'm glad it was you. I want to say that now, in case I forget, because I forget things when I'm happy."*

**ELOWEN:** *"Do you know what the worst of it is? Everyone is kind. Every single person in that house is kind to me every day."*

**ELOWEN:** *"Don't answer that. I don't want you to answer it. I want to get to the water."*

### 4.4 The Boundary Stone

**Cross-reference:** house_system.md Section 3.3, house_belos.md Section 7.3, the_princess.md Section 7.3.

At minute seven they pass it. Three sigils cut over each other. She does not look at it. **Jake, if the player is looking, can read it.**

**Design law:** no dialogue, no camera emphasis, no prompt. It is a stone by a track and cross-reference the_princess.md Section 9.3 — *"eleven yards on their side."*

## 5. THE LAKE

### 5.1 The Place

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_region.md Section 2.3.

The Waterlands. A tarn under a low fell, grey water, reeds, and a beach of small stones. Cross-reference physics/water_physics.md, physics/wind_systems.md — the wind is on the water the whole time.

**It is the most beautiful location in Highmoore and it is not spectacular.** It is just very quiet and very good.

### 5.2 The Swim

**Cross-reference:** the user's original outline.

She swims. Jake does not. Cross-reference physics/water_physics.md — cold water, and she does not care, and cross-reference the_princess.md Section 2.3: **she is good at this too.**

**ELOWEN:** *"You're not coming in."*
**ELOWEN:** *"You are a sailor."*
**ELOWEN:** *"You are a *sailor* and you will not get in the water."*

### 5.3 The Weapons

**Cross-reference:** the user's original outline — *"während Jake seine Waffen reinigt."*

Jake cleans his weapons on the stones. Cross-reference mechanics/inventory_crafting.md — **it is a real animation and it takes real time**, and the player can do it or not, and cross-reference crystal_katana.md Section 5.1:

**If the Crystal Katana is in the inventory, it is the one thing on the cloth that does not need cleaning.** He picks it up, turns it over, and puts it down again. **The game does not comment.**

### 5.4 The Time

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_overview.md Section 5.1.

**No timer, no objective, no prompt.** The player can stay at the lake as long as they want before triggering the next beat, and cross-reference Section 4.2: **the rain arrives when it arrives, and it is coming, and it is visible.**

## 6. THE TRUTH OPTION

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_overview.md Section 4.4.

### 6.1 The Window

On the stones, after the swim, before the rain. **One prompt, once.**

### 6.2 If Taken

Jake tells her everything. The ship, the crew, the archipelago, the war, the brother.

**Cross-reference princess_quest_the_ball.md Section 7.5** — if the player already named Ethan on the terrace, she remembers, and says so, and it lands differently.

**ELOWEN:** *"...you were serious. About the mountain."*

*(a long time)*

**ELOWEN:** *"Then it's real. There's a whole — there's an *outside.*"*

*(and cross-reference the_princess.md Section 2.4 — she has never once been asked what she would do instead, including by herself)*

**ELOWEN:** *"Say the sea part again."*

### 6.3 What It Changes

**Cross-reference:** Section 8.4.

**Nothing about the outcome.** She dies twenty minutes later either way.

**Her last words are different.** Cross-reference Section 8.4 — and a player who told the truth gets a sentence that is significantly worse to carry.

### 6.4 If Not Taken

She dies having danced with an ambassador.

**Design law:** cross-reference princess_quest_overview.md Section 10.1 — the game does not indicate that this window existed. A second playthrough discovers it.

## 7. THE RAIN

### 7.1 The Arrival

**Cross-reference:** Section 4.2, physics/weather_physics.md.

It has been visible for half an hour. It crosses the last of the moor and comes onto the water, and cross-reference physics/water_physics.md — **the player watches it hit the tarn from four hundred metres out and move toward them.**

### 7.2 The Dance

**Cross-reference:** the user's original outline — *"als es anfängt zu regnen tanzen sie ein bisschen."*

**ELOWEN:** *"Oh — *no.*"*
**ELOWEN:** *"...well. We're not getting less wet standing still."*

**Design:** cross-reference princess_quest_the_ball.md Section 6.1 — the same staging as the hall. **No minigame. One long shot. The camera does very little.**

And cross-reference physics/cloth_hair_physics.md, physics/water_physics.md — soaked cloth, soaked hair, and the wind on the tarn. **This is the single best-looking shot in Dark Arisen and it is two people being bad at dancing in the rain.**

### 7.3 The Music

**There is a score here.** Cross-reference princess_quest_overview.md Section 7.1 — it is the only music in the entire sequence, it is quiet, it is warm, and it does not resolve.

**And it does not stop when the arrow lands.** Cross-reference Section 8.2. It plays out its full length over the next ninety seconds.

## 8. THE ARROW

### 8.1 The Moment

Mid-sentence. Mid-step.

**ELOWEN:** *"—and I want to see it in winter, because you said the light goes—"*

### 8.2 The Design Law

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_overview.md Section 7.1.

- **No slow motion.**
- **No music cue.** Cross-reference Section 7.3 — the rain-dance score continues, unchanged, for ninety more seconds.
- **No camera move.** The camera does not turn toward the treeline. It stays where it was.
- **No audio telegraph.** Cross-reference physics/sound_physics.md, physics/wind_systems.md, physics/combat_physics.md — **a two-hundred-metre shot in rain and wind is almost silent**, and what the player hears is a small impact and the sentence stopping.

### 8.3 The Thirty Seconds

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_overview.md Section 7.2. **This is the sequence.**

Jake does not understand. Cross-reference physics/combat_physics.md — there is no health bar, no damage indicator, no red vignette, because it did not hit him.

**He catches her.** Cross-reference the game's animation standards — badly, and they both go down onto the stones.

**Player control is retained the entire time.** There is no cutscene. **There is also nothing to do.** No prompt. No item. No revive. No dialogue wheel.

**She says nothing.** Cross-reference physics/combat_physics.md and the wound's location — she is not able to.

**And the music finishes.**

### 8.4 Her Last Words — Which Were Earlier

**Design law:** cross-reference Section 8.3 — **she does not get a death speech.** She said everything she was going to say on the ride and at the stones.

**If Jake told the truth (Section 6.2):** the last thing she said to him was *"say the sea part again."*

**If he did not:** the last thing she said to him was *"—and I want to see it in winter, because you said the light goes—"*

**Cross-reference princess_quest_overview.md Section 4.4:** the truth is worse.

## 9. THE ARCHER

### 9.1 Who He Is

**Cross-reference:** house_belos.md Sections 4.2 and 7.3, the_princess.md Section 7.5.

A Belos yeoman. **Nineteen.** Cross-reference house_belos.md Section 4.2 — he learned in the butts as a child, in the evening, for fun, like everybody in that House.

He shot a trespasser at range on his own land in bad light and rain. **Cross-reference house_system.md Section 4.3 — that is what his oath requires and he did it correctly.**

### 9.2 What Her Father Did

**Cross-reference:** the_princess.md Section 7.5, princess_quest_the_truth.md Section 3.

Cassian Arion did not corrupt anyone. He did not bribe an archer or plant a man in Belos colours.

**He knew the stretch was patrolled and he knew what Belos patrols do, and he made certain his daughter and a foreign witness would be standing on it at the right hour.**

**The murder weapon is Highmoore's own customary law.**

### 9.3 The Encounter

He comes down to see what he hit. It takes him about four minutes and cross-reference Section 8.3 — Jake has been on the stones for those four minutes.

The boy comes out of the reeds. He looks at her. He looks at the white and gold.

**THE ARCHER:** *"...that's Arion. That's — she's *Arion.*"*

**THE ARCHER:** *"She's on our— she was on our—"*

**THE ARCHER:** *"I didn't— I couldn't see the—"*

### 9.4 The Kill

**Cross-reference:** the user's original outline — *"Jake tötet den Bogenschützen sofort."*

**Gameplay. Not a cutscene.** Cross-reference mechanics/combat_feel.md — the boy has a bow and no time and he does not raise it.

It takes two seconds. Cross-reference crystal_katana.md — **it is the first thing the Crystal Katana does in this questline.**

**Design law:** cross-reference style_bible.md Pillar 3 — no combat music, no finisher, no camera flourish. And cross-reference princess_quest_belos.md Section 1: **the first death of the rampage is a nineteen-year-old who followed the rules**, and the game does not say so.

## 10. THE STANDING

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_overview.md Section 7.3.

### 10.1 The Design

Jake is at a lake in the rain with two bodies and a horse.

**There is no prompt. No objective. No timer. No marker. No music.**

Cross-reference physics/weather_physics.md — the rain continues. Cross-reference physics/sound_physics.md — water on water, wind in reeds, and one horse shifting.

### 10.2 What the Player Can Do

- Stand
- Walk around
- Sit
- **Nothing at all**

### 10.3 What Ends It

**Mounting the horse.** That is the only trigger, and cross-reference mounted_travel.md Section 8.2 — the moment the player does, the stamina system suspends and does not come back.

### 10.4 The Log

**Cross-reference:** the RDR2 discipline.

**The game records how long the player stood there and never mentions it, ever, to anyone.**

### 10.5 What He Does Not Do

**He does not take her.** Cross-reference the_princess.md Section 10.2 — she is collected by her father's people the next morning and buried correctly in the Arion churchyard, exactly as she predicted in the letter Jake has not read yet.

**He leaves her at the lake to go and kill her father.**

**Design law:** the game does not comment, does not offer an alternative, and does not give the player the option to carry her. Cross-reference style_bible.md Pillar 5 — **it is part of what he has to live with, and it is a consequence of choosing to get on the horse.**

## 13. STYLE BIBLE COMPLIANCE

- ✅ **Pillar 1 (Staggered Register):** she is entirely plain for the whole sequence — the High is gone and never returns. And a nineteen-year-old archer's register breaks down mid-sentence three times in four lines.
- ✅ **Pillar 2 (Lore Through Objects):** a letter signed in full. A boundary stone with three cuts, unmentioned. A sword on a cleaning cloth that does not need cleaning.
- ✅ **Pillar 3 (Layered Death):** no slow motion, no cue, no camera move, no death speech, no ghost. **The music that was already playing simply finishes.** And the first kill of the rampage is a boy who did his job correctly.
- ✅ **Pillar 4 (Layered Humor):** *"You are a sailor and you will not get in the water."* The dancing. The last genuinely funny lines in Phase 8 are eight minutes before the arrow, and that is deliberate.
- ✅ **Pillar 5 (Theme — Revenge):** she said *don't come*, twice, in writing and to his face, and it read as cold feet. The murder weapon is a customary law her father did not have to corrupt. And the sequence ends with a man choosing, with no prompt and no timer, to get on a horse and leave her on the stones.

## 15. CONCLUSION

The Lake. A letter eleven days late from a man who says his reasoning is worth nothing and knows it. An argument that is entirely true and reads as cold feet. A kiss the player has to choose. Nine minutes at a capped trot with the rain visible on the western horizon the whole way, and a two-minute description of the sea for someone who has never seen it, and *I'm glad it was you, I want to say that now in case I forget.*

A grey tarn under a low fell. A woman who swims in cold water and cannot believe a sailor won't get in. A sword on a cleaning cloth that does not need cleaning. Rain crossing four hundred metres of open water toward two people who are about to be bad at dancing again.

And then a sentence that stops.

No slow motion. No cue. The music that was already playing finishes over the next ninety seconds. Thirty seconds of full player control with absolutely nothing to do. A nineteen-year-old coming out of the reeds four minutes later saying *she was on our—* and not finishing that sentence either.

And then a man standing at a lake in the rain for as long as he wants, with no prompt, until he decides to get on the horse.

**Phase 8 Document 15 of ~30.**

**Say the sea part again.** 🌧️🏹
