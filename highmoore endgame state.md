## 1. THE DESIGN LAW

### 1.1 The Endgame Does Not Mention Highmoore

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_overview.md Pillar 3, bosses/crimson_armada.md.

Phase 8 is optional. Cross-reference colonial_war/war_state_system.md Section 9.2 — the colonial war feeds the main plot's climax directly.

**Highmoore does not.**

**Design law:** no Crimson Armada content references Highmoore. No main-story NPC mentions it. No final-act dialogue acknowledges it. **The game's ending is identical whether Jake went through the mountain or never found the inlet.**

### 1.2 Except in One Person

**Cross-reference:** characters/jake_harlow.md, Section 7.

Cross-reference crystal_katana.md Section 10.2 — **he carries the sword into the final act and the final act does not mention it.**

### 1.3 Why

Cross-reference style_bible.md Pillar 5. The archipelago's war is a war Jake is fighting. **Highmoore is a thing Jake did.** The two are not the same kind of object and the game does not let the second become plot.

## 2. THE FOUR FINAL STATES

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_the_return.md Section 6, highmoore_reconstruction.md Section 6.

### 2.1 STATE ONE — THE REBUILT BASIN

*(Standard path, reconstruction pursued.)*

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_reconstruction.md.

Belos cleared and partly rebuilt. Roads sworn. Possibly a bridge at the ford. Cadwall grown. Ashby with grain. **Arion still without a lord** and Maerwyn still in it.

**The ambient register:** cross-reference npcs/regional_populations.md — Highmoore's exhausted Low has changed slightly, in one direction only.

*"...road's sworn now. Not by a House. By that one. Don't ask me how that works, I only know I got to market and back."*

*"...my boy's on the bridge crew. Bridge. At the ford. I've had to explain to my father three times and he still doesn't have it."*

### 2.2 STATE TWO — THE BASIN LEFT

*(Standard path, reconstruction ignored.)*

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_reconstruction.md Section 1.2.

Belos is rubble. Cadwall has taken what was available. **Ridd is doing well.** The Burnt March has grown.

*"...nobody's cleared the west range. Four year now. They say there's still folk under it and they say it like it's weather."*

### 2.3 STATE THREE — THE TWO HOUSES

*(Turned west at Arion.)*

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_reconstruction.md Section 6.2, princess_quest_arion_falls.md Section 8.4.

Belos stands. Aldric Belos is alive. **Three hundred people are alive.** Arion is decapitated and Mere has moved to hold it.

Cross-reference highmoore_reconstruction.md Section 6.5 — Aldric knows everything now, and has for years, and cross-reference house_belos.md Section 3.5: **he still does not know if the sum was wrong.**

*"...quiet, this last while. Quietest anyone remembers. Nobody's saying why and I'll not be the one to."*

### 2.4 STATE FOUR — THE STAIR

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_belos.md Section 9.5, highmoore_reconstruction.md Section 6.3.

**No reconstruction content exists.**

Cross-reference the_wizard.md Section 7.4 — Ellis has not spoken since. Cross-reference princess_quest_the_return.md Section 6.3 — there was no last cue.

**And the ambient register in Highmoore does not mention Jake at all.** Cross-reference npcs/regional_populations.md Section 10 — **the gossip web, which discusses everything, has nothing to say**, and the design's instruction is that the silence is total and never explained.

## 3. WHAT CROSSES THE MOUNTAIN

### 3.1 Almost Nothing

**Cross-reference:** crystal_caves.md, highmoore_overview.md Section 1.1.

**The archipelago does not know Highmoore exists.**

Cross-reference crystal_caves.md Section 2.2 — the Empire surveyed the coast and marked the Sundered Spine as impassable. It is. Cross-reference house_system.md Section 6.1 — **and Highmoore does not know there is another side either.**

### 3.2 The Crew

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_the_return.md Section 5.3.

**They know exactly what Jake told them in the great cabin, once, and nothing else, ever.**

### 3.3 The Gossip Web

**Cross-reference:** npcs/regional_populations.md Section 10.3.

**It does not reach through a mountain.**

**Design law:** no archipelago NPC ever mentions Highmoore, the Houses, the Princess, or anything Jake did there. Not merchants, not brokers, not the Whisper Lady, not Cass Wells.

Cross-reference npcs/black_market_fences.md — **the Whisper Lady sells knowing, and she does not have this.**

### 3.4 The Sword

**Cross-reference:** crystal_katana.md Section 7.

**The one thing that crosses.**

Cross-reference crystal_katana.md Section 6.2 — it glows, it cannot be hidden, and cross-reference npcs/blacksmiths_and_armorers.md: **no smith in the archipelago recognises the type, and several ask to look at it.**

Cross-reference crystal_katana.md Section 7.3 — **Esteban's anecdote is the only lore anyone in the archipelago has**, and it is one old sailor's half-memory of a man who was not right afterward, and *it wasn't the sword's doing, mind.*

## 4. THE FOUR SAGES

**Cross-reference:** colonial_war_overview.md Section 10.3, and the four sage documents.

### 4.1 The Design Question

The wisdom keepers track what Jake does in the colonial war. Cross-reference colonial_war/imperial_colonies.md Section 9.

**Do they know about Highmoore?**

### 4.2 The Answer

**No — unless Jake tells them.**

**Design law:** cross-reference Section 3.3. **There is no route by which the information reaches them independently.** No rumour, no broker, no gossip web.

### 4.3 If Jake Tells Them

**Cross-reference:** npcs/cannabis_keeper_mama_jacinta.md, npcs/desert_sage_mbah_seruni.md, npcs/healer_mormor_astrid.md, npcs/cigar_collector_don_alejandro.md.

**Full Vision:** one dialogue option each, available once, late.

**MBAH SERUNI** — cross-reference npcs/desert_sage_mbah_seruni.md, and she has spent sixty-eight years with an address and a name and has not gone.

*"You went. I did not."*

*(a long time)*

*"I am not going to tell you which of us was right. I have not decided."*

**MORMOR ASTRID** — cross-reference npcs/healer_mormor_astrid.md.

*"How long did you stand at the water before you got on the horse?"*

*(and cross-reference princess_quest_the_lake.md Section 10.4 — **the game knows the number and she does not ask for it and Jake does not say it**)*

**MAMA JACINTA** — cross-reference npcs/cannabis_keeper_mama_jacinta.md, fauna/cannabis_system.md Section 7.

She offers a third ceremony and cross-reference fauna/cannabis_system.md Section 6 — **and the design's instruction is that the Dream Fight's arena does not contain Elowen.** Cross-reference the_princess.md Section 10.1: she does not appear, in any form, ever, including here.

**DON ALEJANDRO** — cross-reference npcs/cigar_collector_don_alejandro.md, and he buried a son.

*"Was she buried properly?"*

**JAKE:** *"...yes."*

**DON ALEJANDRO:** *"Then that is one thing. It is a small thing and I will not pretend otherwise, and I have found that small things carry surprisingly well."*

*(and cross-reference npcs/cigar_collector_don_alejandro.md — **he pours two glasses and does not ask anything else**)*

## 6. THE HORSE

**Cross-reference:** mounted_travel.md Section 9.3, princess_quest_the_return.md Section 2.1.

### 6.1 It Stays in Highmoore

There is no way to bring a horse through the Crystal Caves' counterweight lift, and the design does not add one.

### 6.2 Where

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_settlements.md Section 8.1.

Stabled at the staging post at the bottom of the switchback, where Jake found it. Cross-reference mounted_travel.md Section 2.1 — **where somebody had been feeding a horse for sixty years and the game never said who.**

### 6.3 It Is Still There

Every return. Cross-reference mounted_travel.md Section 9.3 — **and it is never the same animal again**, and it is fine, and it is old, and it does more than a walk now but not much.

## 9. MVP CORE vs FULL VISION

### 9.1 MVP CORE

- All four final states with their ambient registers
- The gossip web's total failure to cross the mountain
- The sword as the only thing that crosses
- The crew's knowledge limited to one conversation
- No endgame reference to Highmoore anywhere

### 9.2 FULL VISION

- The four sages' single dialogue options
- Salvio's *"I have been calling him something worse for two years"*
- Big Tom and the half-tone
- The horse at the staging post, old and fine
- The theme audible only in a restored hall

## 10. STYLE BIBLE COMPLIANCE

- ✅ **Pillar 1 (Staggered Register):** a Highmoore farmer explaining a bridge to his father three times. Mbah Seruni's *"I am not going to tell you which of us was right."* Don Alejandro's small things carrying surprisingly well.
- ✅ **Pillar 2 (Lore Through Objects):** a sword no smith in two worlds recognises. An old horse at a staging post. A dance tune that exists in exactly one building.
- ✅ **Pillar 3 (Layered Death):** *"they say there's still folk under it and they say it like it's weather."* And on one path, a gossip web that discusses everything and has nothing at all to say.
- ✅ **Pillar 4 (Layered Humor):** Ashby's grain. The bridge nobody's father can understand. Father Salvio wanting to stop calling a man something worse.
- ✅ **Pillar 5 (Theme — Revenge):** the endgame is identical whether Jake went through the mountain or never found the inlet. The archipelago never learns. The gossip web cannot cross stone. **Nothing he did there is ever plot, and he carries the sword into the last act, and nobody mentions it.**

---

## 11. CONCLUSION

What the World Learns. Nothing.

The Empire surveyed that coast and marked the range impassable and it is. Highmoore does not know there is another side. The gossip web that carries a captain's mercy across six weeks and four regions and distorts it into something he barely recognises **cannot cross stone**, and the Whisper Lady sells knowing and does not have this.

The crew know exactly what Jake told them in the great cabin once. The four sages know nothing unless he says it, and if he does, an old woman in a volcanic cave who has had an address and a name for sixty-eight years says *you went, I did not — and I am not going to tell you which of us was right, I have not decided.*

A horse stays at a staging post at the bottom of a switchback where somebody was feeding it for sixty years and nobody ever said who, and it is old now and it is fine.

A dance tune exists in exactly one building in the world, played by people who have always played it.

And a man walks into the last act of Dark Arisen carrying a sword that does not tire, which no smith in either world can name, and the last act does not mention it once.

**The ending is identical. That is the point.** 🏔️
