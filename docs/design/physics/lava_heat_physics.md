## 1. LAVA & HEAT PHILOSOPHY — THE FIVE PRINCIPLES

### 1.1 Principle 1: Heat Is Death Made Slow

In Fjordlund, cold kills quickly through obvious threats. In Ashenmoor, heat kills slowly through accumulated stress. Dehydration. Heatstroke. Burning sun. Each individual moment survivable, but cumulative pressure deadly.

**Design intent:** Survival through resource management and cultural wisdom.

### 1.2 Principle 2: Lava Is Living Fluid

Lava is not a hot floor texture. It's a living, flowing substance with its own physics. It moves. It cools. It burns. It creates. Treating it as decoration disrespects its reality.

**Design intent:** Lava as character, not obstacle.

### 1.3 Principle 3: The Volcano Has Moods

Ashenmoor's volcanoes are not always erupting. They sleep. They mutter. They warn. They roar. Mbah Seruni reads them like Mira reads waves. Players who learn the volcanic moods survive.

**Design intent:** Cultural environmental literacy as gameplay skill.

### 1.4 Principle 4: Heat Has Cultural Adaptation

Nomads don't fight heat — they negotiate with it. Travel at night. Rest at noon. Specific clothing. Specific water rituals. Cultural wisdom solves what individual strength cannot.

**Design intent:** Cultural respect through environmental adaptation.

### 1.5 Principle 5: The Ashen Wyrm's Memory Lives Here

This is the dragon's land. Even after the boss is defeated (or befriended), Ashen Wyrm's presence remains in the geography itself. The volcanic landscape is its body and its grave.

**Design intent:** Story integration through environmental atmosphere.

## 2. LAVA PHYSICS

### 2.1 Lava States

**Three Primary States:**

**Molten (Liquid Lava):**
- Bright orange-yellow
- Fluid motion
- ~1100-1200°C
- Instant death contact

**Cooling (Crusted):**
- Dark crust on top
- Hot interior
- Black-red surface
- Still deadly
- Walking impossible

**Cooled (Solid):**
- Black volcanic rock
- Walkable but warm
- New terrain
- Eventually safe

### 2.2 Lava Flow Mechanics

**Realistic Flow:**

**Velocity:**
- Fast lava: 5-10 m/s (river-like)
- Moderate: 1-3 m/s (walking pace)
- Slow: 0.1-0.5 m/s (creeping)
- Variable based on incline

**Direction:**
- Follows gravity
- Lower paths
- Pools in low spots
- Realistic physics

**Volume:**
- Affects spread
- Larger flows wider
- Specific eruption-dependent
- Realistic
