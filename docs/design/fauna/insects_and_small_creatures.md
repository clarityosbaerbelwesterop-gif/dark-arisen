## 1. INTRODUCTION

Small creatures fill the spaces between larger animals. They pollinate flowers, process waste, provide food for larger species, produce materials humans value, and sometimes bite back. This document defines 45 small creature types.

**Content Summary:**
- **Useful Insects:** 10 species (bees, silkworms, beneficial)
- **Dangerous Insects:** 8 species (venomous, disease-carrying)
- **Snakes:** 8 species (venomous and non-venomous)
- **Lizards:** 6 species (tropical and desert)
- **Amphibians:** 6 species (frogs, salamanders)
- **Arachnids:** 5 species (spiders, scorpions)
- **Other:** 2 special creatures

**Plus 3 Legendary Small Creatures**

## 2. USEFUL INSECTS — THE ECOSYSTEM WORKERS

### 2.1 Bees

**Honey Bee (Apis mellifera):**
- **Size:** 1-2 cm
- **Habitat:** Everywhere except Ashenmoor extreme
- **Behavior:** Colony (10,000-50,000), queen-driven
- **Products:**
  - **Honey** (primary economic product)
  - **Beeswax** (candles, preservation, crafting)
  - **Royal Jelly** (rare, valuable, alchemy)
  - **Propolis** (resin, medicinal)
  - **Bee Pollen** (superfood, alchemy)
- **Hive Locations:**
  - Hollow trees (wild)
  - Managed apiaries (cultivated)
  - Rock crevices
  - Abandoned structures
- **Harvesting:**
  - **Wild Hives:** Dangerous (swarm attack)
  - **Smoked Harvesting:** Traditional (bees docile)
  - **Managed Hives:** Regular harvests
- **Combat Capability:** Swarm attack if hive threatened — 30+ bee stings = serious damage
- **Cultural Significance:**
  - Fjordlund: Mead production critical
  - Rexa: Tropical honey varieties valued
  - Imperial: Regulated apiaries, aristocratic beekeeping
  - Universal: Sweetness scarcity = honey premium

**Bumblebee (Bombus ssp.):**
- **Size:** 2-3 cm
- **Habitat:** Temperate meadows
- **Behavior:** Small colonies (50-500), less aggressive
- **Products:** Small honey amounts, pollination service
- **Notes:** Important pollinator, peaceful

**Giant Jungle Bee (Apis rexana):**
- **Size:** 3-4 cm (large)
- **Habitat:** Rexa deep jungle
- **Behavior:** Massive aggressive colonies
- **Products:**
  - **Jungle Honey** (intense flavor, rare)
  - **Giant Bee Venom** (alchemy — poison base)
- **Hazard:** Extremely aggressive, can kill unprotected humans
- **Cultural Significance:** Indigenous Rexan honey sacred

### 2.2 Silkworms

**Common Silkworm (Bombyx mori):**
- **Size:** Larva 4-6 cm
- **Habitat:** Mulberry plantations (cultivated)
- **Behavior:** Eats mulberry leaves, spins cocoon
- **Products:**
  - **Raw Silk** (luxury textile)
  - **Silk Thread** (fine sewing)
  - **Silk Fabric** (luxury goods)
- **Locations:**
  - Imperial silk farms (major production)
  - Small cottage industries
  - Rexan experimental cultivation
- **Cultural Significance:** Silk = luxury commodity, trade wealth

**Wild Silk Moth (Antheraea pernyi):**
- **Size:** Adult 10-15 cm wingspan
- **Habitat:** Temperate forests
- **Behavior:** Wild, harder to cultivate
- **Products:** Wild silk (rougher, distinctive character)
- **Notes:** Rare, valuable

### 2.3 Beneficial Insects

**Ladybug (Coccinellidae):**
- **Size:** 5-10 mm
- **Habitat:** Gardens, fields
- **Behavior:** Aphid predators (beneficial)
- **Cultural Significance:** Luck symbol, farmer's friend

**Mantis (Mantodea):**
- **Size:** 7-15 cm
- **Habitat:** Various (regional variants)
- **Behavior:** Predators, striking hunters
- **Uses:** Alchemy (specific species), specimens

**Butterfly (Various):**
- **Size:** 3-15 cm wingspan
- **Habitat:** Flowers, gardens, meadows
- **Behavior:** Pollinators, daytime
- **Uses:** Collectible, alchemy (wing scales), atmosphere
- **Species Highlighted:**
  - **Monarch (Danaus plexippus):** Migratory
  - **Morpho (Rexan blue):** Iridescent, tropical
  - **Imperial Swallowtail:** Aristocratic gardens

**Firefly (Lampyridae):**
- **Size:** 1-2 cm
- **Habitat:** Moist areas, summer evenings
- **Behavior:** Bioluminescent (mating displays)
- **Uses:** Atmosphere, alchemy (rare luciferin)
- **Magical:** Jar of fireflies = rustic lantern

**Dung Beetle (Scarabaeidae):**
- **Size:** 2-5 cm
- **Habitat:** Where large animals
- **Behavior:** Recycling waste
- **Cultural Significance:** Ancient Egyptian parallel lore
- **Uses:** Fertilizer indirect

## 3. DANGEROUS INSECTS

### 3.1 Biting/Stinging

**Tropical Wasp (Vespa rexana):**
- **Size:** 3-4 cm (large)
- **Habitat:** Rexa, Moran
- **Behavior:** Aggressive territorial defense
- **Hazard:** Multiple stings possible, painful
- **Combat:** Quick but weak individually, swarms dangerous
- **Cultural:** Known biohazard in jungles

**Horse Fly (Tabanus):**
- **Size:** 1-2 cm
- **Habitat:** Near water, horses, cattle
- **Behavior:** Biting for blood
- **Hazard:** Painful bites, disease vectors
- **Uses:** Nuisance, no positive

**Mosquito (Culicidae):**
- **Size:** 0.5-1 cm
- **Habitat:** Swamps, tropical, wet areas
- **Behavior:** Blood feeding (females)
- **Hazard:** Disease vectors (malaria, yellow fever)
- **Gameplay Effect:**
  - Health decay in infested areas
  - Specific quests about insects
  - Protection clothing/repellent

**Tsetse Fly (Glossina):**
- **Size:** 0.5-1 cm
- **Habitat:** Rexa specific
- **Behavior:** Blood feeding
- **Hazard:** Sleeping sickness carrier
- **Narrative:** Specific disease storyline

### 3.2 Disease Vectors

**Tropical Flea:**
- **Size:** 1-2 mm
- **Habitat:** Jungle floors, animals
- **Hazard:** Plague carriers, typhus
- **Gameplay:** Disease outbreaks in unclean conditions

**Tick (Ixodidae):**
- **Size:** 2-10 mm (depending on engorgement)
- **Habitat:** Temperate forests
- **Behavior:** Attaches to hosts, feeds on blood
- **Hazard:** Lyme-like disease, tick fever
- **Gameplay:** Check for ticks after forest travel

## 4. SNAKES

### 4.1 Venomous Snakes

**Fer-de-Lance / Bushmaster (Bothrops asper):**
- **Size:** 120-180 cm
- **Habitat:** Rexa/Moran jungles
- **Behavior:** Ambush predator, well-camouflaged
- **Venom:** Hemotoxic (tissue damage, hemorrhaging)
- **Hazard:** EXTREMELY DANGEROUS
- **Antidote:** Rexan-specific antivenom (Ines can craft)
- **Cultural:** "La muerta silenciosa" — the silent death
- **Notes:** Major jungle danger

**Coral Snake (Micrurus fulvius):**
- **Size:** 60-100 cm
- **Habitat:** Rexa, Moran
- **Behavior:** Shy but deadly
- **Venom:** Neurotoxic (paralysis)
- **Hazard:** Lethal if untreated
- **Identification:** "Red on yellow, kill a fellow; red on black, friend of Jack"
- **Cultural:** Brilliantly colored warning

**Rattlesnake (Crotalus):**
- **Size:** 80-150 cm (species dependent)
- **Habitat:** Ashenmoor, Quiet Coast
- **Behavior:** Warning rattle, defensive
- **Venom:** Hemotoxic
- **Hazard:** Dangerous but warns
- **Cultural:** Western desert icon
- **Notes:** Respects warning = no bite

**Imperial Viper (Vipera berus imperialis):**
- **Size:** 60-80 cm
- **Habitat:** Pale Isle, Quiet Coast
- **Behavior:** Less aggressive than tropical
- **Venom:** Hemotoxic (less potent)
- **Hazard:** Painful, rarely lethal
- **Antidote:** Common in Imperial apothecaries

**Anaconda (Eunectes murinus) [Large]:**
- **Size:** 400-700 cm, 50-100 kg
- **Habitat:** Rexa swamps, rivers
- **Behavior:** Ambush, constrictor
- **Hazard:** EXTREME for unwary
- **Not Venomous:** But can kill via constriction
- **Uses (If Killed):** 
  - Massive hide (premium leather)
  - Meat (exotic)
  - Materials (bones, organs)
- **Cultural:** Jungle apex mystique

**Boa Constrictor (Boa constrictor):**
- **Size:** 200-400 cm, 10-30 kg
- **Habitat:** Rexa, Moran
- **Behavior:** Ambush, constrictor
- **Hazard:** Moderate danger
- **Uses:** Premium hide, alchemy components

### 4.2 Non-Venomous Snakes

**Garter Snake (Thamnophis):**
- **Size:** 50-90 cm
- **Habitat:** Gardens, temperate
- **Behavior:** Common, harmless
- **Uses:** Minor specimen value

### 4.3 Snake Venom Uses

**Ines's Alchemy:**
- **Poison Extraction:** Specific antidotes require venom
- **Weapon Coating:** Poison arrows from specific venoms
- **Medicinal:** Small doses of certain venoms curative
- **Experimentation:** Ines studies with care

**Snake Hunting Quests:**
- Specific NPCs pay for specific venoms
- Dangerous work
- Rewarding financially

**Antivenom System:**
- Must be crafted in advance
- Specific to specific snakes
- Ines's specialty
- Lifesaving supply

## 9. LEGENDARY SMALL CREATURES

### 9.1 The Blood Moth

**The Blood Moth (Actias sanguinea):**
- **Classification:** Legendary moth
- **Size:** 30 cm wingspan (massive)
- **Appearance:** Deep blood-red wings, white body markings that shift when viewed
- **Habitat:** Rexa deep jungle, specific grove
- **Behavior:**
  - Appears only on nights with specific moon phases
  - Attracted to specific rare flowers
  - Singular individual (never documented in pairs)
  - Supernatural calm around it
- **Signs of Presence:**
  - Specific flower blooms
  - Unusual silence in jungle
  - Indigenous songs reference
  - Older Rexans claim to have seen once
- **Capturing:**
  - Cannot kill (dissolves on death)
  - Must be captured alive
  - Specialized net + specific bait
  - One attempt per moon cycle
- **Uses:**
  - **Blood Moth Wing:** Unique alchemy component (legendary tier)
  - **Sight:** Observing grants "Witness" buff (+10% perception permanent)
- **Cultural Significance:** Rexan mythology, messengers of the dead

### 9.2 The Ancient Tortoise

**The Ancient Tortoise (Testudo antiquitas):**
- **Classification:** Legendary reptile
- **Size:** 180 cm shell length, 300+ kg
- **Age:** Estimated 200+ years (impossible)
- **Habitat:** Remote Rexan islands
- **Appearance:** Massive tortoise, shell etched with natural patterns that resemble maps
- **Behavior:**
  - Extraordinarily slow
  - Unafraid of anything
  - Has survived multiple generations of humans
  - Locals speak of it from grandparents' grandparents
- **The Encounter:**
  - Peaceful observation
  - Cannot be killed (shell impossibly hard)
  - If Jake sits with it, deep peace
  - Shell patterns offer clues to hidden locations
- **Rewards:**
  - **Tortoise's Patience:** Permanent +20% stamina
  - **Map Shell Patterns:** Reveals hidden treasure locations
  - No violence possible (mechanical impossibility)
- **Narrative Weight:**
  - Symbol of time and endurance
  - The opposite of Jake's urgency
  - Philosophical encounter

### 9.3 The Phoenix Spider

**The Phoenix Spider (Theraphosa renata):**
- **Classification:** Legendary arachnid
- **Size:** 35 cm legspan
- **Habitat:** Volcanic caves of Ashenmoor
- **Appearance:** Fire-red and gold coloration, seems to flicker with inner light
- **Behavior:**
  - Territorial but not aggressive
  - Seems to embody flame
  - Shed skin is valuable
  - Connected to volcanic activity
- **The Encounter:**
  - Must enter specific volcanic cave
  - Heat damage in area
  - Observation or confrontation
- **Options:**
  - **Kill (Difficult):** Extremely valuable materials
  - **Observe:** Silent knowledge
  - **Feed (Specific Prey):** Friendship possible (unique)
- **Rewards (Varying by Approach):**
  - **Phoenix Silk:** Legendary material (fire-resistant)
  - **Phoenix Skin:** Alchemy legendary
  - **Phoenix's Blessing (If Befriended):** +50% fire resistance permanent
- **Cultural Significance:** Desert nomads speak of it, Mbah Seruni may reference

## 11. DANGEROUS ENCOUNTERS

### 11.1 Swarm Mechanics

**Bee Swarms:**
- **Triggered:** Harvesting wild hive without smoke
- **Damage:** 5 HP per sting, multiple stings
- **Escape:** Water, running distance
- **Counter:** Smoke, specific clothing

**Mosquito Clouds:**
- **Triggered:** Entering swamp at dusk
- **Damage:** HP drain while exposed
- **Protection:** Clothing, repellent (herbal)
- **Disease:** Random chance of illness

**Wasp Attacks:**
- **Triggered:** Near nest
- **Damage:** Significant per sting
- **Counter:** Fire, smoke

### 11.2 Envenomation

**Snake Bite:**
- **Immediate:** HP damage
- **Ongoing:** Poison tick (depends on venom)
- **Treatment:** Antivenom (if crafted)
- **Consequences:** If untreated, severe or death

**Spider Bite:**
- **Immediate:** Minor HP
- **Ongoing:** Paralysis (some), necrosis (others)
- **Treatment:** Specific antidotes

**Scorpion Sting:**
- **Deathstalker:** Major threat
- **Imperial:** Moderate
- **Treatment:** Required quickly for dangerous species

### 11.3 Disease

**Malaria (Tropical Mosquitoes):**
- **Symptoms:** Recurring fever, weakness
- **Effect:** Reduced stats until cured
- **Cure:** Ines's specific tonic, quinine
- **Prevention:** Nets, repellent

**Tick Fever:**
- **Symptoms:** Fatigue, joint pain
- **Effect:** Slow recovery
- **Cure:** Herbal treatment

**Swamp Fever:**
- **Symptoms:** Persistent weakness
- **Location:** Wetlands
- **Cure:** Specific herbs

## 13. ECOSYSTEM SERVICES

### 13.1 Pollination

**Critical Role:**
- Bees pollinate crops
- Butterflies pollinate flowers
- Absence = agricultural collapse

**Gameplay:**
- Over-harvesting bees affects crops
- Some quests about protecting pollinators
- Cultural awareness

### 13.2 Pest Control

**Natural Pest Control:**
- Ladybugs eat aphids
- Mantis eat mosquitoes
- Spiders eat flies
- Snakes eat rats

**Player Impact:**
- Killing spiders = more flies
- Removing snakes = rat infestations
- Interconnected systems

### 13.3 Decomposition

**Essential Role:**
- Dung beetles recycle
- Carrion insects process death
- Maintains ecosystem

**Not Hunted:**
- Usually ignored
- Ecosystem health indicator
- Smell of decomposition realistic
