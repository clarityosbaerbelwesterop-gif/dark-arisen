## 1. TOBACCO PHILOSOPHY — THE FIVE PRINCIPLES

### 1.1 Principle 1: Tobacco Is Contemplation

Where beer is community and cannabis is transcendence, tobacco is contemplation. A man smoking alone on deck at sunset. A gathering of sailors sharing pipes before battle. Jake lighting a cigar after a hard kill. Tobacco marks pauses in life — moments of reflection between actions.

**Design intent:** Tobacco mechanics reward stillness. Smoking is when Jake thinks.

### 1.2 Principle 2: Every Leaf Tells Origin

Where a tobacco grew shapes what it is. Rexan tobacco is bold and dark. Fjordlund tobacco is smoky from peat curing. Ashenmoor tobacco carries volcanic soil minerality. The leaf remembers the land.

**Design intent:** Regional specialties matter. Connoisseurs can taste origin.

### 1.3 Principle 3: Time Makes the Master

A fresh leaf is nothing. A cured leaf is useful. An aged leaf is legendary. Tobacco demands patience — curing takes weeks, aging takes months or years. Master cigars aren't made quickly.

**Design intent:** Time investment creates value. Jake learns patience through tobacco.

### 1.4 Principle 4: The Craft Is Sacred

Rolling a cigar is not manufacturing — it is craft. The leaves must be chosen. The proportions matter. The wrap must be perfect. Each cigar is signed. Master rollers are artists.

**Design intent:** Tobacco craft is genuine skill, respected throughout the game.

### 1.5 Principle 5: Sharing Is Meaning

Alone, a cigar is peace. Shared, a cigar is bond. Offering a cigar to another man is gesture with weight. Refusing can insult. Accepting creates connection.

**Design intent:** Social tobacco use carries mechanical and narrative weight.

## 3. GROWING TOBACCO

### 3.1 Climate Requirements

**Tobacco Needs:**
- Warm growing season (120+ days frost-free)
- Rich soil
- Moderate moisture
- Sunlight

**Regional Variations:**
- Rexa/Moran: Year-round warm
- Pale Isle: Summer only
- Fjordlund: Extreme southern edges only
- Ashenmoor: Volcanic slopes specifically
- Quiet Coast: Summer cultivation

### 3.2 Cultivation Process

**Stage 1 — Seedbed:** 6-8 weeks in protected beds
**Stage 2 — Transplanting:** Young plants to field
**Stage 3 — Growing:** 8-12 weeks (topping, suckering)
**Stage 4 — Priming:** Multi-pass harvest over weeks
**Stage 5 — Curing:** Multi-week transformation

### 3.3 Leaf Positions

**Ligero (Top):** Strongest, premium for cigars
**Viso (Upper Middle):** Complex, quality wrappers
**Seco (Lower Middle):** Mild, filler
**Volado (Bottom):** Mildest, pipe tobacco

Master cigars combine leaves from multiple positions.

### 3.4 Growing Jake's Own

**Options:**
- **Land Acquisition (Chapter 7+):** Own plantation
- **Ship Tobacco:** Very limited
- **Partnership:** Invest in existing plantations

## 4. CURING METHODS

### 4.1 Air Curing
- 4-8 weeks ventilated barns
- Mild flavors
- Use: Rexa Dark, Quiet Coast

### 4.2 Fire Curing
- Low-temperature smoke exposure
- 3-10 weeks
- Distinctive smoky flavor

### 4.3 Flue Curing
- Heat-controlled barns
- 5-8 days
- Mild flavor result
- Use: Imperial Virginian

### 4.4 Sun Curing
- Direct sun, 2-4 weeks
- Sweet, bright character
- Use: Moran Highland

### 4.5 Peat Curing (Fjordlund Unique)
- Peat fires, 6-12 weeks
- Deep smoky character
- Produces: Fjordlund Peat-Cured

### 4.6 Volcanic Ash Curing (Ashenmoor)
- Ash layers, 8-14 weeks
- Mineral complexity
- Produces: Ashenmoor Volcanic Ash

### 4.7 Sacred Curing (Monastery)
- Standard + blessings
- Father Salvio understands
- Produces: Pale Isle Sacramental

## 6. ROLLING CIGARS

### 6.1 Cigar Anatomy

- **Wrapper:** Highest quality outer leaf
- **Binder:** Holds filler, affects burn
- **Filler:** Blend determining main flavor
- **Cap:** Sealed tip

### 6.2 Rolling Process

1. Select leaves carefully
2. Precise measuring
3. Spread wrapper flat
4. Arrange filler
5. Place binder
6. Roll firm but not tight
7. Seal ends with natural gum

### 6.3 Jake's Skill Progression

- **Novice (Ch 1-3):** Basic cigars, poor quality
- **Apprentice (Ch 4-6):** Functional, standard
- **Skilled (Ch 7-9):** Quality, own preferences
- **Master (Post-Game):** Legendary rolling

### 6.4 Tools

- **Chaveta:** Curved cutting knife
- **Rolling Board:** Firm flat surface
- **Cedar Humidor:** Storage
- **Guillotine Cutter:** Cigar cuts

- Save quality cigars for major fights
- Boss encounters worth premium consumption
- Economic decisions (smoke vs sell)

*"For Jake, when he returns. I've been perfecting this for years. It's bittersweet — like him. Like us. The earth of our homeland, shaped by jungle rains, cured the old way grandfather taught us. He won't believe I did this. But I did. For the day he sails back."*

**The Emotional Weight:**
- Ethan was planning for Jake's return
- The tobacco has been aging since before Ethan's death
- Ethan's cigars are already rolled (perfectly aged)
- Don Alejandro's "impossible tenth" — it existed all along, cultivated by Jake's own brother

**Don Alejandro's Reaction:**
When Jake brings Ethan's Strain to Don Alejandro:

*"This... Captain. This is... your brother?"*
*"He rolled these himself. He knew you would come."*
*[Long silence]*
*"My son would have been a man like your brother. I think I understand now why we sought these cigars."*
