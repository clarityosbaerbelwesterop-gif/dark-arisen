## 1. INTRODUCTION

Trees shape every landscape in Dark Arisen. They provide shade, shelter, lumber, fruit, bark, and sap. They define regions — the mangroves of Rexa, the pines of Fjordlund, the Joshua trees of Ashenmoor. This document covers 40+ tree species, plus their fruits, lumber uses, and cultural roles.

**Content Summary:**
- **Tropical Trees (Rexa/Moran):** 12 species
- **Temperate Trees (Pale Isle, Quiet Coast):** 10 species
- **Arctic Trees (Fjordlund):** 6 species
- **Desert Trees (Ashenmoor):** 4 species

**Plus 3 Legendary Trees** — named trees with story significance

## 2. TROPICAL TREES (REXA/MORAN)

### 2.1 Palm Trees

**Coconut Palm (Cocos nucifera):**
- **Size:** 20-30 m
- **Habitat:** Rexa coasts, islands
- **Appearance:** Iconic curved trunk, fronds
- **Fruit:** Coconuts (year-round)
- **Harvesting:**
  - **Climb:** Requires skill, risk of falling
  - **Pole Tool:** Knock down from ground
  - **Wait:** Fallen coconuts ground harvest
- **Complete Resource Chain (EVERYTHING USED):**
  - **Coconut Water:** Drink directly from green coconuts (refreshing, hydrating)
  - **Coconut Meat:** White flesh inside
    - Fresh eating
    - **Dried for Chips** (preserved, travel food)
    - Grated for cooking
  - **Coconut Oil:** Pressed from meat
    - Cooking fuel
    - Skin protection
    - Preservation agent
    - Trade good
  - **Coconut Milk:** From pressed meat + water
    - Culinary use (curries, stews)
  - **Coconut Shell:** Emptied shells
    - **Containers** (cups, bowls, storage)
    - Fuel for fire
    - Crafting material
  - **Coir Fiber:** From husk
    - Rope making
    - Matting
    - Brush bristles
  - **Palm Fronds:** Leaves
    - Thatching (roof material)
    - Weaving (baskets, mats)
    - Temporary shelter
  - **Palm Wood:** When tree dies
    - Soft wood, limited uses
    - Carving
- **Cultural Significance:** 
  - Rexan "tree of life" — sustains entire villages
  - Pirate survival staple
  - Trade commodity
- **Regrowth:** Slow (5-7 years to mature)

**Date Palm (Phoenix dactylifera):**

- **Appearance:** Tall, feathery fronds

## 9. FRUIT HARVESTING

### 9.1 Harvesting Methods

**Hand Picking (Low Branches):**
- Simple, direct
- Fresh fruit
- Quality control
- Selective harvest

**Climbing (High Branches):**
- Risky (falls possible)
- More fruit accessible
- Skill-based
- Requires care

**Pole Tools:**
- Safer than climbing
- Less fruit damage
- Slower harvest
- Good for coconuts

**Ladder Harvest:**
- Orchard-style
- Largest quantities
- Commercial approach
- Port-based

**Shake Harvest:**
- Fruit falls to ground
- Some damage
- Fast for bulk
- Quality lower

### 9.2 Quality Tiers

**Peak Ripe:**
- Perfect moment
- Best flavor
- Premium price
- Immediate use ideal

**Standard Ripe:**
- Good quality
- Common sale
- Standard preservation

**Underripe:**
- Harvested early (transport)
- Ripens in storage
- Merchant choice often

**Overripe:**
- Immediate use or waste
- Best for cooking
- Poor for selling fresh

### 9.3 Seasonal Availability

**Tropical (Rexa/Moran):**
- Year-round mostly
- Slight dry/wet variations
- Continuous harvest
- Economic reliability

**Temperate:**
- Spring: First fruits (cherries, strawberries)
- Summer: Peak variety
- Autumn: Harvest season (apples, pears)
- Winter: Stored/preserved only

**Arctic (Fjordlund):**
- Brief summer window
- Hardy varieties
- Preservation critical
- Berries more than tree fruits

**Desert (Ashenmoor):**
- Seasonal blooms
- Post-rain abundance
- Storage critical
- Limited but important
