## 1. INTRODUCTION

This document defines the 42 land mammal species of Dark Arisen across six regions. Each species is treated with full respect — behavior, biology, cultural significance. The world is populated by creatures, not spawn points.

**Content Summary:**
- **Small Game:** 12 species (rabbits, squirrels, foxes, etc.)
- **Medium Game:** 14 species (deer, boars, goats, etc.)
- **Large Game:** 10 species (bears, moose, etc.)
- **Predators:** 8 species (wolves, cats, etc.)
- **Plus 6 legendary variants** (one per region, details at end)

**Regional Distribution:**
- Rexa/Moran (Tropical): 12 species
- Fjordlund (Arctic): 9 species
- Ashenmoor (Volcanic Desert): 7 species
- Pale Isle (Imperial): 8 species
- Quiet Coast (Temperate): 10 species
- Region 06 (Post-Game): 3 unique species
- Cross-Regional: 4 widespread species

## 3. REXA / MORAN (TROPICAL REGION)

### 3.1 Small Game

**Howler Monkey (Alouatta rexana):**
- **Classification:** Primate
- **Size:** 50-70 cm, 5-8 kg
- **Behavior:** Troops of 10-20, arboreal, loud morning calls, fruit eaters, territorial
- **Detection:** Excellent sight (60m), poor ground smell, sharp hearing (50m)
- **Tracking Difficulty:** Medium (follow calls, broken branches above)
- **Combat Capability:** Passive but troop mobbing if threatened
- **Weapons of Choice:** Bow (arrows), precision necessary
- **Materials Provided:**
  - 1 Monkey Pelt (small)
  - 1-2 Monkey Meat
  - 1 Skull (trophy/alchemy)
- **Uses:** Skulls valuable to specific collectors (controversial market), meat rare delicacy
- **Narrative Hooks:** Some Rexan groups consider monkeys sacred — hunting them harms reputation with specific villages
- **Cultural Significance:** Dual — food for some, taboo for others

**Coati (Nasua moranensis):**
- **Classification:** Small omnivore
- **Size:** 40-60 cm body, 3-5 kg
- **Behavior:** Small groups of 5-15, ground foragers, omnivorous, daytime active
- **Detection:** Good sight (40m), excellent smell (70m), average hearing
- **Tracking Difficulty:** Easy (group tracks very visible)
- **Combat Capability:** Passive (flees easily)
- **Weapons of Choice:** Bow (small arrows) or slingshot
- **Materials Provided:**
  - 1 Coati Pelt (small)
  - 1-2 Coati Meat
  - Claws (craft material)
- **Uses:** Pelt for small goods, meat for common cooking
- **Narrative Hooks:** Farmer protection quests (coatis raid fields)
- **Cultural Significance:** Village pests sometimes, food commonly

**Jungle Hare (Sylvilagus rexanus):**
- **Classification:** Lagomorph
- **Size:** 30-45 cm, 1-2.5 kg
- **Behavior:** Solitary, crepuscular, skittish, feeds on vegetation
- **Detection:** Average sight (30m), good smell (40m), excellent hearing (60m)
- **Tracking Difficulty:** Medium (fast response)
- **Combat Capability:** Passive (runs)
- **Weapons of Choice:** Small bow, snare traps ideal
- **Materials Provided:**
  - 1 Small Pelt
  - 1 Rabbit Meat
  - 1 Rabbit's Foot (luck charm)
- **Uses:** Basic meat and small leather
- **Narrative Hooks:** Common early-game hunt, teaches tracking
- **Cultural Significance:** Peasant staple food

**Tropical Rodent (Dasyprocta ssp.):**
- **Classification:** Large rodent (agouti-type)
- **Size:** 40-60 cm, 3-6 kg
- **Behavior:** Solitary or pairs, diurnal, burrow dwellers, nut eaters
- **Detection:** Good sight (45m), good smell (40m), good hearing (45m)
- **Tracking Difficulty:** Medium
- **Combat Capability:** Passive (runs to burrows)
- **Weapons of Choice:** Precise bow work, traps
- **Materials Provided:**
  - 1 Small Pelt
  - 1-2 Meat
  - Teeth (small crafting)
- **Uses:** Common hunt, easy meal
- **Narrative Hooks:** None special
- **Cultural Significance:** Village food

### 3.2 Medium Game

**Jungle Deer / Red Brocket (Mazama americana):**
- **Classification:** Small deer
- **Size:** 100-120 cm, 20-40 kg
- **Behavior:** Solitary or small family, crepuscular, dense forest
- **Detection:** Excellent sight (60m), excellent smell (80m), good hearing (50m)
- **Tracking Difficulty:** Hard (dense jungle hides them)
- **Combat Capability:** Passive (flees quickly)
- **Weapons of Choice:** Bow (patience required)
- **Materials Provided:**
  - 1 Medium Pelt
  - 3-5 Venison Cuts
  - Small Antlers
  - Hooves (craft)
  - Small Bones
- **Uses:** Standard venison meals, regional specialty
- **Narrative Hooks:** Traditional hunt in some villages
- **Cultural Significance:** Respectable hunt

**Wild Boar / Peccary (Tayassu pecari):**
- **Classification:** Pig-family
- **Size:** 80-110 cm, 40-65 kg
- **Behavior:** Herds of 50-300 (dangerous numbers), foragers, aggressive when threatened
- **Detection:** Poor sight (20m), exceptional smell (100m), good hearing (50m)
- **Tracking Difficulty:** Easy (large group tracks)
- **Combat Capability:** DANGEROUS in groups, tusks can kill
- **Weapons of Choice:** Heavy bow or firearm, distance crucial
- **Materials Provided:**
  - 1 Tough Pelt
  - 4-7 Pork Cuts
  - Tusks (craft, weapon component)
  - Larger Bones
  - Fat (cooking)
- **Uses:** Major food source, trading goods
- **Narrative Hooks:** Village raid quests — peccary herds damage crops
- **Cultural Significance:** Staple hunt, communal activity

**Tapir (Tapirus terrestris):**
- **Classification:** Large herbivore, ungulate
- **Size:** 180-220 cm, 150-300 kg
- **Behavior:** Solitary, nocturnal, water-loving, browsers
- **Detection:** Poor sight (15m), excellent smell (80m), good hearing (50m)
- **Tracking Difficulty:** Medium (heavy tracks by water)
- **Combat Capability:** Defensive but not aggressive
- **Weapons of Choice:** Heavy weapons, rifle if available
- **Materials Provided:**
  - 1 Large Pelt (very valuable)
  - 8-12 Tapir Meat
  - Large Bones
  - Major Fat
- **Uses:** Rare pelt for high-end goods, lots of meat
- **Narrative Hooks:** Conservation concerns in some villages (rare animal)
- **Cultural Significance:** Respected, some consider spiritual

### 3.3 Large Game / Predators

**Jaguar (Panthera onca):**
- **Classification:** Apex predator
- **Size:** 160-180 cm body, 60-100 kg
- **Behavior:** Solitary, territorial (20-200 km² territories), nocturnal-crepuscular, ambush hunter
- **Detection:** Exceptional sight (100m day/night), excellent smell (70m), excellent hearing (80m)
- **Tracking Difficulty:** Expert (jaguars hide tracks)
- **Combat Capability:** EXTREMELY DANGEROUS, apex predator
- **Weapons of Choice:** Heavy firearm, rifle, or death
- **Materials Provided:**
  - 1 Jaguar Pelt (legendary quality possible)
  - 4-6 Big Cat Meat
  - Teeth + Claws (legendary crafting)
  - Skull (trophy)
- **Uses:** Legendary armor, prestigious trophies, unique crafting
- **Narrative Hooks:** **The Patriarch** (legendary) lives here; several named jaguar legends
- **Cultural Significance:** Sacred to many Rexan peoples; hunting one brings reputation or condemnation depending on village

**Caiman (Caiman crocodilus):**
- **Classification:** Large reptile (technically not mammal but grouped for hunting)
- **Size:** 150-250 cm, 40-80 kg
- **Behavior:** Solitary, water-based, ambush predator, mostly calm
- **Detection:** Good sight (40m), exceptional smell (90m in water), good hearing
- **Tracking Difficulty:** Hard (underwater/hidden)
- **Combat Capability:** DANGEROUS in water
- **Weapons of Choice:** Rifle from shore
- **Materials Provided:**
  - 1 Caiman Hide (premium)
  - 3-5 Caiman Meat
  - Teeth (trade value)
- **Uses:** Luxury leather goods, exotic meat
- **Narrative Hooks:** Village safety quests, specific hunts
- **Cultural Significance:** Feared and respected

### 3.4 Rexa Legendary: The Patriarch

**The Patriarch (Panthera onca maxima):**
- **Classification:** Legendary Jaguar
- **Size:** 220 cm, 130 kg (40% larger than normal)
- **Age:** Estimated 20+ years
- **Appearance:** Massive male, extensive scarring across face and body, one ear half-missing, golden eyes
- **Territory:** Deep Rexan jungles, specific cave network
- **Behavior:** 
  - Solitary, has outlived multiple territory challengers
  - Hunts at night exclusively
  - Stalks Jake more than Jake stalks him (reverse hunting)
  - Appears when Jake has been hunting in his territory repeatedly
- **Signs of Presence:**
  - Claw marks on specific trees (unusually high)
  - Larger tracks than any other jaguar
  - Local NPCs speak of "the old ghost"
  - Other predators avoid his territory
- **The Hunt:**
  - Multiple encounters before true confrontation
  - Jaguar studies Jake
  - Must be perfect when Jake finally takes shot
  - Any wound leaves Jaguar to track Jake down later
- **Requirements:**
  - Chapter 6+ availability
  - Must have hunted 10+ regular jaguars
  - Specific weather (rainy night ideal)
  - Perfect weapon (heavy bow or rifle only)
- **Materials:**
  - **The Patriarch's Pelt:** Legendary armor material (+25% Jungle stealth, +15% damage)
  - **The Patriarch's Claws:** Weapon component (legendary tier)
  - **The Patriarch's Heart:** Unique cooking ingredient (legendary meal)
  - **The Patriarch's Skull:** Trophy (displays in cabin)
- **Narrative Weight:**
  - NPCs speak of him by name
  - Killing him changes regional ecosystem
  - Old hunters mourn his death (respected)
  - Some call it murder, others triumph

## 4. FJORDLUND (ARCTIC REGION)

### 4.1 Small Game

**Arctic Hare (Lepus arcticus):**
- **Classification:** Lagomorph
- **Size:** 50-70 cm, 3-5 kg
- **Behavior:** Solitary or small groups, diurnal in summer, burrow dwellers, winter-white camouflage
- **Detection:** Excellent sight (80m), average smell, good hearing (50m)
- **Tracking Difficulty:** Easy in snow, Hard when color matches
- **Combat Capability:** Passive (extremely fast)
- **Weapons of Choice:** Bow, snare traps ideal
- **Materials Provided:**
  - 1 White Pelt (winter) or Brown Pelt (summer)
  - 1-2 Hare Meat
  - Feet (warm goods)
- **Uses:** Winter pelts extremely valuable for cold clothing
- **Narrative Hooks:** Mormor Astrid appreciates winter pelts
- **Cultural Significance:** Traditional winter food

**Ptarmigan (Lagopus muta) [Bird but grouped]:**
- **Classification:** Grouse-family bird
- **Size:** 30-40 cm, 400-600 g
- **Behavior:** Small groups, ground-dwelling, winter-white plumage
- **Detection:** Average all senses
- **Tracking Difficulty:** Easy (ground tracks visible in snow)
- **Combat Capability:** Passive
- **Weapons of Choice:** Small bow or shotgun-style
- **Materials Provided:**
  - Feathers (white, premium)
  - 1 Bird Meat
  - Eggs (if nest found)
- **Uses:** Fletching, decoration, food
- **Cultural Significance:** Common winter fare

**Arctic Fox (Vulpes lagopus):**
- **Classification:** Small canid
- **Size:** 50-75 cm, 3-8 kg
- **Behavior:** Solitary or small family, opportunistic, winter-white coat
- **Detection:** Good sight (50m), excellent smell (80m), excellent hearing (70m)
- **Tracking Difficulty:** Medium (cautious)
- **Combat Capability:** Defensive (bites if cornered)
- **Weapons of Choice:** Bow (precise) or traps
- **Materials Provided:**
  - 1 Arctic Fox Pelt (PREMIUM — very valuable)
  - 1-2 Fox Meat
  - Teeth
  - Claws
- **Uses:** Luxury fur goods, fetches high prices
- **Narrative Hooks:** Conservation quests (over-trapping concerns)
- **Cultural Significance:** Valuable resource, treated respectfully in tradition

### 4.2 Medium Game

**Reindeer / Caribou (Rangifer tarandus):**
- **Classification:** Deer
- **Size:** 180-220 cm, 100-180 kg
- **Behavior:** Herds of 20-200, migratory, cold-adapted, antlered (both sexes)
- **Detection:** Good sight (60m), good smell (70m), good hearing (60m)
- **Tracking Difficulty:** Easy (large herds)
- **Combat Capability:** Flees, bulls protective during rut
- **Weapons of Choice:** Bow, rifle
- **Materials Provided:**
  - 1 Large Pelt (winter-premium)
  - 5-8 Reindeer Meat (premium cuts)
  - Antlers (crafting, trophy)
  - Bones
  - Hooves
- **Uses:** Primary Fjordlund food source, trade goods
- **Narrative Hooks:** **Ghost Reindeer** legendary; Fjordlund cultural center
- **Cultural Significance:** Sacred to many Fjordlunders, herds tracked spiritually

**Moose (Alces alces):**
- **Classification:** Largest deer
- **Size:** 250-300 cm, 400-700 kg
- **Behavior:** Solitary or small family, inland forests, browsers, aggressive during rut
- **Detection:** Poor sight (30m), excellent smell (90m), good hearing (60m)
- **Tracking Difficulty:** Easy (huge tracks)
- **Combat Capability:** DANGEROUS especially rutting males
- **Weapons of Choice:** Heavy rifle, large bow
- **Materials Provided:**
  - 1 Large Pelt (premium)
  - 12-18 Moose Meat
  - Massive Antlers (trophy)
  - Large Bones
  - Major Fat
  - Hooves
- **Uses:** Feeds crew for weeks, premium materials
- **Narrative Hooks:** Specific legendary named bulls
- **Cultural Significance:** Respected giant, hunt is ceremonial

**Mountain Goat (Capra hircus [wild]):**
- **Classification:** Caprine
- **Size:** 140-170 cm, 50-80 kg
- **Behavior:** Small groups, cliff-dwellers, agile, wary
- **Detection:** Excellent sight (70m), good smell (60m), good hearing (60m)
- **Tracking Difficulty:** Hard (cliff terrain)
- **Combat Capability:** Defensive (horns)
- **Weapons of Choice:** Precision rifle (distance shots)
- **Materials Provided:**
  - 1 Goat Pelt (cold-hardy wool)
  - 3-5 Goat Meat
  - Horns (crafting)
  - Hooves
- **Uses:** Excellent wool, specific cuts prized
- **Narrative Hooks:** Difficult hunt tests player skill
- **Cultural Significance:** Mountain people's staple

### 4.3 Predators

**Brown Bear / Grizzly (Ursus arctos):**
- **Classification:** Apex predator
- **Size:** 200-280 cm, 180-350 kg (males larger)
- **Behavior:** Solitary (except mothers with cubs), omnivorous, territorial, aggressive
- **Detection:** Poor sight (30m), exceptional smell (120m), good hearing (50m)
- **Tracking Difficulty:** Easy (huge tracks and signs)
- **Combat Capability:** EXTREMELY DANGEROUS
- **Weapons of Choice:** Heavy rifle mandatory
- **Materials Provided:**
  - 1 Large Bear Pelt (premium)
  - 10-15 Bear Meat (heavy)
  - Claws (4 — valuable)
  - Teeth
  - Large Bones
  - Major Fat
  - Heart (special cooking)
  - Gall Bladder (alchemy)
- **Uses:** Massive resource haul, prestigious hunt
- **Narrative Hooks:** Many legendary named bears
- **Cultural Significance:** Power animal, respect/fear

**Gray Wolf (Canis lupus):**
- **Classification:** Pack predator
- **Size:** 110-150 cm, 30-60 kg
- **Behavior:** Packs of 5-12, territorial, intelligent, coordinated hunters
- **Detection:** Good sight (70m), exceptional smell (120m), excellent hearing (90m)
- **Tracking Difficulty:** Medium individually, Easy as pack
- **Combat Capability:** DANGEROUS in packs
- **Weapons of Choice:** Rifle, multiple arrows ready
- **Materials Provided:**
  - 1 Wolf Pelt (premium in winter)
  - 2-3 Wolf Meat
  - Teeth (canines valuable)
  - Claws
- **Uses:** Fur goods, teeth for jewelry
- **Narrative Hooks:** Wolf encounters common, pack dynamics complex
- **Cultural Significance:** Spirit animal in Fjordlund lore

**Lynx (Lynx lynx):**
- **Classification:** Medium predator (cat)
- **Size:** 100-130 cm, 18-30 kg
- **Behavior:** Solitary, territorial, stealthy, ambush hunter
- **Detection:** Exceptional sight (90m), good smell, excellent hearing (80m)
- **Tracking Difficulty:** Hard (very stealthy)
- **Combat Capability:** Dangerous if cornered
- **Weapons of Choice:** Bow (silence matters), rifle
- **Materials Provided:**
  - 1 Lynx Pelt (premium)
  - 1-2 Lynx Meat
  - Claws
  - Teeth
  - Tufted Ears (decorative)
- **Uses:** Luxury fur, specialized crafting
- **Cultural Significance:** Elusive, rare, respected

### 4.4 Fjordlund Legendary: The Ghost Reindeer

**The Ghost Reindeer (Rangifer tarandus albinus maximus):**
- **Classification:** Legendary Caribou
- **Size:** 230 cm, 200 kg, 180 cm antler spread
- **Age:** Unknown — local legend suggests 50+ years (impossible)
- **Appearance:** Pure white throughout, including antlers. Eyes seem to glow in moonlight. Ethereal presence.
- **Territory:** Northern Fjordlund, specifically during Northern Lights periods
- **Behavior:**
  - Appears only during Aurora Borealis
  - Never fleeing, never attacking
  - Seems aware of Jake's presence
  - Moves with intention
  - Makes eye contact
- **Signs of Presence:**
  - Old hunters speak of seeing it in their youth
  - Mormor Astrid claims she saw it as a child (50 years ago)
  - Never caught on paper or drawn
  - Folk songs reference it
- **The Encounter:**
  - Must be Peace Route with Havfrue (respectful to nature)
  - Must be out at night during Northern Lights
  - Deep in specific fjord
  - Jake can:
    - **Shoot it:** Kills legendary, gains materials, but ecosystem curses descend
    - **Approach peacefully:** Ghost Reindeer accepts Jake's presence, grants blessing
    - **Retreat:** Animal walks away, never appears again this playthrough
- **Requirements:**
  - Peace Route active with Havfrue
  - Chapter 7+
  - Northern Lights night (specific in-game conditions)
  - Respectful approach required
- **Materials (If Killed):**
  - **Ghost Antlers:** Legendary alchemy component (massive potency boost)
  - **Ghost Pelt:** Unique armor (looks impossible)
  - **Ghost Heart:** Single-use legendary meal
- **Blessing (If Peaceful):**
  - **Ghost's Blessing:** Permanent +20% cold resistance
  - Mormor Astrid recognizes Jake saw it, deeper relationship
  - Specific dialogue throughout remainder of game
  - Northern Lights seem brighter to Jake afterward
- **Narrative Weight:**
  - Killing it: Village mourns, Mormor Astrid weeps, Fjordlund's magic diminishes
  - Sparing it: Jake carries its blessing, unique ending resonance
  - **This is a major character moment**

  - **Shadow-Wolf Claws:** Crafting material (legendary tier)
- **Narrative Weight:**
  - Mbah Seruni knows the legend
  - Killing it changes regional atmosphere
  - Volcanic activity seems to increase afterward (connection?)
  - Some nomads mourn, others celebrate

**Autumn:**
- Mating seasons (rut)

- Ideal hunt time

- Hardy animals visible

- Some hibernation
- Survival focus

**Afternoon (3-6 PM):** Activity returns
**Evening (6-9 PM):** Peak activity, water concentration
**Night (9 PM-5 AM):** Nocturnal only, predator hunts
