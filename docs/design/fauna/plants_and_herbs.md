# PLANTS & HERBS

**Status:** Design Draft v1.0  
**Phase:** Phase 4 — Fauna & Flora (Subsystem 10 of 16)  
**Tier:** Content Tier  
**Related:** fauna_overview.md, fauna/gathering_system.md, fauna/processing_and_cooking.md, characters/ines.md (forthcoming), style_bible.md  
**Purpose:** Comprehensive documentation of all medicinal herbs, alchemical ingredients, flavoring plants, and dangerous vegetation — the complete herbalism system of Dark Arisen.  
**Note:** Tobacco, Cannabis, and Hops have dedicated separate documents. Trees and Fruits have their own document. This document focuses on herbs, small plants, and non-woody vegetation.  
**Inspirations:** The Witcher 3 (primary — herbalism depth), Red Dead Redemption 2 (plant variety), Monster Hunter (regional specialties), real-world ethnobotany and herbalism traditions

---

## 1. INTRODUCTION

The herbalism of Dark Arisen is Ines's domain — and through her, Jake's education in the plants that heal, harm, and transform. This document defines 60+ plant species across all regions.

**Content Summary:**
- **Medicinal Herbs:** 18 species
- **Alchemical Ingredients:** 15 species
- **Flavoring/Cooking Herbs:** 12 species
- **Dangerous/Toxic Plants:** 10 species
- **Ritual/Ceremonial Plants:** 5 species

**Plus 4 Legendary Plants**

## 2. MEDICINAL HERBS

### 2.1 Healing Herbs

**Red Valerian (Centranthus ruber):**
- **Size:** 50-80 cm
- **Habitat:** Rocky slopes, temperate
- **Appearance:** Clusters of small red flowers
- **Peak Harvest:** Mid-summer
- **Uses:**
  - **Calming tonic** (sleep aid)
  - **Pain relief** (mild)
  - **Anxiety treatment**
- **Preparation:** Root dried, leaves fresh
- **Ines's Craft:** Sleep Tonic recipe base
- **Cultural:** Common remedy across regions

**Mountain Sage (Salvia officinalis alpinus):**
- **Size:** 30-60 cm
- **Habitat:** Mountain slopes, dry
- **Appearance:** Gray-green leaves, purple flowers
- **Peak Harvest:** Late spring
- **Uses:**
  - **Wound cleaning** (antiseptic)
  - **Cough relief**
  - **Memory/clarity**
- **Preparation:** Dried leaves for tea, fresh for poultices
- **Cultural:** Sacred in some regions (smudging)

**Blessed Thistle (Cnicus benedictus):**
- **Size:** 30-60 cm
- **Habitat:** Disturbed soil, temperate
- **Appearance:** Yellow flowers, spiny leaves
- **Peak Harvest:** Summer flowering
- **Uses:**
  - **Detoxifying** tonics
  - **Digestion aid**
  - **Poison cure base**
- **Preparation:** Extensive (specific process)
- **Ines's Craft:** Essential for antivenom

**Devil's Claw (Harpagophytum procumbens):**
- **Size:** Vine-like, ground cover
- **Habitat:** Ashenmoor (desert adapted)
- **Appearance:** Claw-shaped fruit, yellow flowers
- **Peak Harvest:** After rare rain
- **Uses:**
  - **Anti-inflammatory**
  - **Joint pain relief**
  - **Elder care**
- **Preparation:** Root extraction
- **Cultural:** Nomadic traditional medicine

**Yarrow (Achillea millefolium):**
- **Size:** 30-90 cm
- **Habitat:** Meadows, widespread
- **Appearance:** White flower clusters, feathery leaves
- **Peak Harvest:** Summer
- **Uses:**
  - **Wound stopping** (stops bleeding)
  - **Fever reduction**
  - **General healing**
- **Preparation:** Fresh for immediate use, dried for storage
- **Cultural:** "Soldier's herb" across cultures

**Marshmallow Root (Althaea officinalis):**
- **Size:** 90-120 cm
- **Habitat:** Wet areas, temperate
- **Appearance:** Tall, pale pink flowers
- **Peak Harvest:** Autumn (root)
- **Uses:**
  - **Sore throat relief**
  - **Digestive soothing**
  - **Skin treatments**
- **Preparation:** Root extraction, syrup
- **Cultural:** Ancient remedy

## 5. DANGEROUS AND TOXIC PLANTS

### 5.1 Deadly Plants

**Deadly Nightshade (Atropa belladonna):**
- **Toxicity:** Extreme
- **Symptoms:** Dilated pupils, hallucinations, death
- **Uses:**
  - **Poisons (careful preparation)**
  - **Eye drops (aristocratic — widens pupils "beautifully")**
  - **Alchemy**
- **Identification:** Purple-black berries (look appealing)
- **Cultural:** "Belladonna" = beautiful lady (historical cosmetic use)

**Water Hemlock (Cicuta):**
- **Toxicity:** Among deadliest in world
- **Symptoms:** Convulsions, death
- **Habitat:** Wet areas
- **Identification:** Looks like edible wild carrot
- **Warning:** Identification expertise critical

**Oleander (Nerium oleander):**
- **Toxicity:** Every part poisonous
- **Symptoms:** Heart failure
- **Habitat:** Mediterranean-style gardens
- **Appearance:** Beautiful pink/white flowers
- **Cultural:** Beautiful but lethal

**Monkshood/Aconite:**
- **Already covered in alchemy (legendary poison)**

**Castor Oil Plant (Ricinus communis):**
- **Toxicity:** Ricin (seeds)
- **Symptoms:** Severe poisoning
- **Habitat:** Tropical and warm temperate
- **Uses:** Oil from seeds (non-toxic after processing)

### 5.2 Skin-Dangerous Plants

**Manchineel (Hippomane mancinella):**
- **Size:** Tree
- **Habitat:** Rexan/Caribbean coasts
- **Toxicity:** Everything toxic, sap burns skin
- **Warning:** Standing under in rain = skin damage
- **Cultural:** "Tree of death" — Rexan warning
- **Fruit:** Looks like small apple but deadly
- **Uses:** Indigenous arrow poison

**Poison Ivy/Oak/Sumac:**
- **Toxicity:** Urushiol causes severe rash
- **Habitat:** Temperate forests
- **Warning:** Three leaves, let it be
- **Cultural:** Widespread warning

**Giant Hogweed (Heracleum mantegazzianum):**
- **Size:** 200-400 cm
- **Toxicity:** Sap + sunlight = severe burns
- **Habitat:** Temperate
- **Warning:** Specialized handling

### 5.3 Hallucinogenic Plants

**Jimsonweed (Datura stramonium):**
- **Toxicity:** High (hallucinations, death possible)
- **Cultural:** "Devil's trumpet"
- **Uses:** Dark alchemy only
- **Warning:** Inconsistent potency deadly

**Morning Glory (Ipomoea):**
- **Source:** Seeds
- **Effect:** Hallucinogenic
- **Uses:** Specific alchemy
- **Cultural:** Indigenous ritual use (carefully)

## 8. SEASONAL CYCLES

### 8.1 Spring

**Peak Harvests:**
- First greens emerging
- Mandrake roots accessible
- Blood Root in flower
- Many seedlings plantable

**Activities:**
- Planting season
- Wild forage begins
- Apprentice herbalists taught

### 8.2 Summer

**Peak Harvests:**
- Most herbs at peak
- Flowers at maximum potency
- Tropical year-round reliable
- Fjordlund brief window

**Activities:**
- Main harvest season
- Ines extremely busy
- Drying begins

### 8.3 Autumn

**Peak Harvests:**
- Root medicines (Devil's Claw, Valerian)
- Fruits and berries
