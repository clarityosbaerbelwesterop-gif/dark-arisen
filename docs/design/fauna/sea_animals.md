## 1. INTRODUCTION

The ocean in Dark Arisen is not water with ships on it. It is a three-dimensional living ecosystem with its own mysteries. This document defines 65 sea species across all regions' waters.

**Content Summary:**
- **Small Fish (Food/Bait):** 18 species
- **Medium Fish (Food/Trade):** 15 species
- **Large Fish (Game Fish):** 10 species
- **Whales:** 6 species (one legendary)
- **Dolphins/Porpoises:** 4 species
- **Sharks:** 5 species (one legendary)
- **Reef Creatures:** 4 species
- **Monster-Tier:** 3 unique creatures

**Regional Distribution:**
- **Rexan/Moran Waters (Tropical):** Reef life, tropical fish, sharks
- **Fjordlund Waters (Arctic):** Whales, cold-water fish, seals
- **Ashenmoor Coast (Volcanic):** Sparse but unique species
- **Pale Isle Waters (Temperate Imperial):** Regulated fisheries
- **Quiet Coast Waters (Temperate):** Classic fishing grounds
- **Region 06 Waters (Post-Game):** Impossible sea life
- **Deep Ocean:** Cross-regional monsters

## 2. FISHING SYSTEM OVERVIEW

### 2.1 Fishing Methods

**Hand Line (Basic):**
- Available from start
- Used while walking docks, ship deck
- Small to medium fish
- Patient, meditative

**Rod and Reel (Standard):**
- Chapter 2+
- Better than hand line
- Wider variety possible
- Active gameplay

**Spear Fishing (Tropical):**
- Rexan tradition
- Underwater mechanic (swim/dive)
- Specific fish only
- Skill-based

**Net Fishing (Commercial):**
- Ship-based
- Bulk catches
- Crew cooperation
- Economic focus

**Trawling (Ship):**
- Dragged nets
- Mass catch
- Ecological impact concerns
- Efficient but questionable

**Harpoon Fishing (Large Game):**
- Specific target (large fish, whales)
- Ship-mounted
- Crew coordination
- Story-weighted

**Trap Fishing:**
- Crab pots, fish traps
- Set and wait
- Passive income
- Port-based

### 2.2 Fishing Locations

**Shore Fishing:**
- From beaches, docks, piers
- Limited species
- Common and accessible
- Tutorial appropriate

**River Mouth:**
- Where rivers meet sea
- Unique species (salmon runs)
- Seasonal variation
- Rich ecosystems

**Coastal Waters:**
- Ship-based near shore
- Wide variety
- Good for medium fishing
- Common operation

**Open Ocean:**
- Deep water, far from shore
- Large species
- Weather-dependent
- Adventure fishing

**Deep Ocean / Abyss:**
- Very deep water
- Unique species
- Monster risks
- Legendary fishing

**Reefs:**
- Rexa/tropical specialty
- Colorful fish
- Dive or surface fishing
- Specific ecosystem

### 2.3 Weather and Fishing

**Calm Weather:**
- Best visibility
- Standard fishing
- Most species cooperative
- Predictable

**Light Wind:**
- Surface activity
- Good for some species
- Standard conditions

**Storm Approach:**
- Some fish more active
- Dangerous conditions
- Rare species possible
- Risk/reward

**During Storm:**
- Most fishing impossible
- Shelter in ports
- Crew manages ship
- No fishing window

**After Storm:**
- Coastal debris
- Displaced species
- Unique opportunities
- Temporary windows

### 2.4 Time of Day

**Dawn:**
- Feeding frenzy for many
- Best coastal fishing
- Beautiful atmosphere
- Prime window

**Morning:**
- Standard fishing
- Good conditions
- Wide variety

**Midday:**
- Reduced activity (some species)
- Heat affects surface
- Bottom fishing better

**Evening:**
- Second feeding peak
- Excellent fishing
- Sunset atmosphere

**Night:**
- Different species active
- Phosphorescent creatures
- Dangerous (poor visibility)
- Unique hauls possible

## 5. WHALES — THE LEVIATHANS

### 5.1 The Whaling Question

**Context:**
Whales exist in Dark Arisen as both creatures and cultural weights. Havfrue Modor (the Fjordlund boss) is the mythological mother of whales. Her presence shapes how Jake's actions affect the ocean.

**Peace Route Implications:**
- If Jake made peace with Havfrue, whaling is morally off-limits
- Crew reactions horrified if Jake hunts
- Reputation massive hit in Fjordlund
- Ghost Reindeer hunt also affected

**Combat Route Implications:**
- Whaling becomes available (if taboo)
- Reduced moral consequences
- Economic opportunity
- Different ending path

### 5.2 Whale Species

**Sperm Whale (Physeter macrocephalus):**
- **Size:** 16-20 meters, 35-45 tonnes
- **Habitat:** Deep open ocean
- **Behavior:** Deep divers, pod structures
- **Hunt Difficulty:** Extreme (whaling operation)
- **Materials If Hunted:**
  - Massive Blubber (oil production)
  - Ambergris (rare, valuable — perfume)
  - Bones (huge crafting)
  - Teeth (scrimshaw traditional)
  - Meat (feast-scale)
- **Cultural Significance:** Traditional whale hunt (if done)
- **Narrative Hooks:** **The Scarred Leviathan** (legendary)

**Humpback Whale (Megaptera novaeangliae):**
- **Size:** 12-16 meters, 25-30 tonnes
- **Habitat:** Migratory, coastal
- **Behavior:** Songs, breaching, social
- **Hunt Difficulty:** Hard but possible
- **Peace Alternative:** Watch and log migrations
- **Materials (If Hunted):**
  - Large Blubber
  - Bones
  - Meat
  - Valuable oil
- **Cultural Significance:** Majestic, some cultures sacred

**Blue Whale (Balaenoptera musculus):**
- **Size:** 25-30 meters, 100-180 tonnes (largest animal ever)
- **Habitat:** Deep ocean, migratory
- **Behavior:** Solitary or pairs, rarely seen
- **Hunt Difficulty:** Extreme (almost mythological)
- **Materials (If Hunted):**
  - Unprecedented haul
  - Legendary materials
  - Moral catastrophe
- **Cultural Significance:** Sacred to Fjordlund peoples

**Orca / Killer Whale (Orcinus orca):**
- **Size:** 6-10 meters, 3-7 tonnes
- **Habitat:** Coastal, pods
- **Behavior:** Intelligent predators, pod hunting
- **Hunt Difficulty:** Extreme (dangerous prey)
- **Materials (If Hunted):**
  - Premium meat (controversial)
  - Teeth (ornamental)
  - Bones
- **Cultural Significance:** Both feared and respected
- **Notes:** Sometimes attack whaling ships

**Minke Whale (Balaenoptera acutorostrata):**
- **Size:** 8-10 meters, 5-10 tonnes
- **Habitat:** Various
- **Behavior:** More abundant, less social
- **Hunt Difficulty:** Hard
- **Materials (If Hunted):**
  - Moderate blubber
  - Meat
  - Bones
- **Cultural Significance:** Common whale hunt (historically)

**Right Whale (Eubalaena glacialis):**
- **Size:** 14-18 meters, 40-70 tonnes
- **Habitat:** Coastal, temperate
- **Behavior:** Slow, surface-feeding, filter feeding
- **Hunt Difficulty:** Moderate (historically "right whale to hunt")
- **Materials (If Hunted):**
  - Baleen (massive)
  - Blubber (abundant)
  - Bones
- **Cultural Significance:** Commercial whaling target historically

### 5.3 The Scarred Leviathan — Legendary Sperm Whale

**The Scarred Leviathan (Physeter macrocephalus maximus):**
- **Size:** 24 meters, 60 tonnes (record-breaking)
- **Age:** Estimated 80-100 years (impossible long life)
- **Appearance:** Massive male sperm whale, covered in harpoon scars from past encounters, white patches where wounds healed, one eye cloudy-blind
- **Territory:** Deep ocean between Fjordlund and Pale Isle
- **Behavior:**
  - Survived 40+ whaling attempts
  - Has destroyed ships (3 documented)
  - Recognizes harpoons now
  - Active intelligence
  - Seems to seek out whaling vessels for revenge
- **The Hunt:**
  - Requires extreme preparation
  - Specific ship configuration
  - Multi-day pursuit
  - Ship can be sunk
  - Crew can die
  - The whale can escape (and does usually)
- **Peace Alternative:**
  - If Peace Route with Havfrue
  - The Leviathan appears to Jake
  - Non-hostile encounter
  - Spiritual moment
  - Blessing received (permanent)
- **Requirements:**
  - Post-Chapter 8
  - If hunting: extensive preparation
  - If peace: Havfrue Peace Route completed
- **Materials (If Killed):**
  - **Leviathan Bone:** Legendary weapon material
  - **Ancient Ambergris:** Unprecedented rarity
  - **Old Scar Harpoons:** Historical curiosities (found in body)
- **Blessing (If Peaceful):**
  - "The Ocean's Witness" — permanent buff
  - Whales never fear Jake's ship
  - Fjordlund reputation maximized
- **Narrative Weight:**
  - Moby-Dick territory
  - This is the ocean's memory
  - What the sea has suffered
  - Killing it: ultimate dominion
  - Sparing it: ultimate reconciliation
  - **Major character moment either way**

### 5.4 Whaling Mechanics (If Used)

**The Operation:**
- Requires specific harpoons (large)
- Ship-mounted launchers
- Multiple crew coordinating
- Spotter, harpooner, rope team
- Hours to days per hunt

**The Chase:**
- Track whale breathing patterns
- Approach carefully
- Strike at critical moment
- Hold on as whale tries to escape
- The "Nantucket sleigh ride"

**The Kill:**
- Exhaust whale
- Final lance strike
- Process at sea or tow to port
- Massive undertaking

**Processing:**
- Takes days for large whales
- Tryworks (oil boiling)
- Blubber strips
- Meat preservation
- Valuable oil barrels

## 9. MONSTER-TIER CREATURES

### 9.1 The Kraken

**The Kraken (Architeuthis maximus):**
- **Classification:** Legendary giant squid/octopus
- **Size:** 30-50 meter arm span, unknown mass
- **Habitat:** Deep ocean, specific trenches
- **Age:** Ancient (mythological)
- **Appearance:** Massive, dark purple-black, eyes larger than dinner plates, tentacles that dwarf ships
- **Behavior:**
  - Rises from deep ocean
  - Attacks ships directly
  - Can wrap ship and pull under
  - Not hostile unless disturbed
  - Ancient intelligence
- **The Encounter:**
  - Very rare random event
  - Only in deep ocean (specific zones)
  - Can sink ships
  - Ship of the line-level threat
- **Combat:**
  - Ship-based combat
  - Massive damage output
  - Must attack specific weak points (eyes)
  - Long, difficult battle
  - Crew casualties likely
  - Some crew can die permanently
- **Materials (If Defeated):**
  - **Kraken Ink:** Legendary alchemy ingredient
  - **Kraken Tentacle:** Massive food (feast-scale)
  - **Kraken Beak:** Legendary weapon
  - **Kraken Eye:** Unique artifact (possibly sentient?)
- **Narrative Weight:**
  - Spoken of in every port
  - Survivors are legendary themselves
  - Killing it is historic
  - Ship becomes known
- **Post-Combat:**
  - Crew survivors changed
  - Some develop PTSD-like responses
