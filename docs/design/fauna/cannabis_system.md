## 1. CANNABIS PHILOSOPHY — THE FIVE PRINCIPLES

### 1.1 Principle 1: Cannabis Is Transcendence

Where beer is community and tobacco is contemplation, cannabis is transcendence. The plant opens doors normally closed. It reveals what the conscious mind hides. In Dark Arisen, cannabis is not recreation — it is journey. To use it lightly is to disrespect it.

**Design intent:** Cannabis encounters are rare, weighted, meaningful. Not every player needs to engage with this system, but those who do receive deep experiences.

### 1.2 Principle 2: The Sacred Plant Tradition

Indigenous Rexan peoples treat cannabis as a sacred plant. It is grown in protected groves. Harvested with ceremony. Prepared with prayer. Consumed with intention. Mama Jacinta is its keeper, not its dealer. Approaching this system requires respect.

**Design intent:** Cultural authenticity respects shamanic traditions. Not "get high" mechanics — spiritual mechanics.

### 1.3 Principle 3: What You Bring, You Find

Cannabis reveals what is already inside the user. A peaceful soul finds peace. A troubled soul confronts trouble. A guilty soul faces guilt. Jake's psyche is full of trauma, rage, grief — cannabis will show him these faces.

**Design intent:** The Dream Fight reflects Jake's internal state. Players who built different paths see different dreams.

### 1.4 Principle 4: The Plant Heals Through Confrontation

Cannabis in Dark Arisen does not provide escape. It provides confrontation. Jake cannot run from what cannabis shows him. He must fight it, talk to it, accept it, or be destroyed by it. The healing is in the facing.

**Design intent:** Cannabis isn't relaxation mechanic. It's growth mechanic.

### 1.5 Principle 5: Some Doors Stay Open

Once you walk through cannabis's door, things change. Permanent changes — sometimes mechanical (buffs), sometimes psychological (dialogue options), sometimes spiritual (worldview shifts). The plant remembers you. You remember it.

**Design intent:** Cannabis use leaves marks. Permanent character development.

## 2. CANNABIS VARIETIES

Dark Arisen features 8 distinct cannabis varieties:

### 2.1 Common Hemp (Cannabis sativa industrialis)

**Origin:** Cultivated everywhere temperate
**Appearance:** Tall, thin leaves, low-resin
**Effects:** Minimal psychoactive (industrial use)
**Strength:** Negligible psychoactive
**Availability:** Common
**Uses:**
- **Rope and textile production** (primary use)
- **Sail material** (specific blends)
- **Paper production**
- **Building material** (hempcrete equivalent)
- **Seeds:** Nutritious food source
**Cultural:** Industrial commodity, not sacred
**Price:** 5-15 doubloons/kilo (raw fiber)

### 2.2 Rexa Wild Strain (Cannabis sativa silvestris)

**Origin:** Rexan jungles, growing wild
**Appearance:** Smaller plants, dense buds
**Effects:** Mild euphoria, relaxation
**Strength:** Light-medium
**Availability:** Found in jungle exploration
**Uses:**
- **Recreational smoking** (common)
- **Mild medicinal** (pain relief)
- **Cultural sharing** (indigenous communities)
**Cultural:** Common Rexan use, not sacred
**Price:** 20-40 doubloons/portion

### 2.3 Moran Mountain Cannabis (Cannabis indica moranensis)

**Origin:** Moran mountain villages
**Appearance:** Compact, dense, purple-tinged
**Effects:** Heavy relaxation, sedation
**Strength:** Strong (sedative)
**Availability:** Mountain villages only
**Uses:**
- **Sleep aid** (intense)
- **Pain management** (chronic)
- **Meditation** (deep)
**Cultural:** Mountain healers' tradition
**Price:** 40-70 doubloons/portion

### 2.4 Coastal Curative (Cannabis medicinalis):

**Origin:** Cultivated for medicine
**Appearance:** Standardized varieties, controlled
**Effects:** Targeted medical benefits
**Strength:** Variable (controlled)
**Availability:** Apothecaries, Ines crafts
**Uses:**
- **Pain relief** (post-surgery, chronic)
- **Appetite stimulant** (sick patients)
- **Anti-nausea** (sea sickness)
- **Anti-inflammatory** (joint conditions)
**Cultural:** Medical legitimacy
**Price:** 50-100 doubloons/dose (Ines crafted)

### 2.5 Imperial Restricted (Cannabis imperialis)

**Origin:** Imperial controlled growing
**Appearance:** Refined, controlled cultivation
**Effects:** Sophisticated, balanced
**Strength:** Medium
**Availability:** Imperial cities, regulated
**Uses:**
- **Aristocratic recreation**
- **Salon culture**
- **Status display**
**Cultural:** Class-restricted, regulated
**Price:** 100-200 doubloons/portion (high tax)

### 2.6 Fjordlund Northern Cannabis (Cannabis ruderalis arctica)

**Origin:** Fjordlund hardy strains
**Appearance:** Small, hardy plants
**Effects:** Mild, with cold-resistant properties
**Strength:** Light
**Availability:** Rare, Fjordlund cultivated
**Uses:**
- **Winter warming** (mild)
- **Cold tolerance** (slight)
- **Shamanic** (some traditions)
**Cultural:** Northern shamanic use
**Price:** 60-100 doubloons/portion

### 2.7 The Sacred Strain (Cannabis sacra)

**Origin:** Sacred grove (Mama Jacinta's care)
**Appearance:** Distinctive coloration, large flowering tops, almost crystalline trichomes
**Effects:** TRANSCENDENT — opens psychological vision
**Strength:** Reality-altering
**Availability:** **Sacred grove only, ceremonial use**
**Uses:**
- **The Dream Fight ritual**
- **Spiritual visioning**
- **Once-in-lifetime experiences**
**Cultural:** Most sacred plant in Rexan tradition
**Price:** Cannot be purchased — only earned through Mama Jacinta
**Narrative Weight:** This is the strain for the Dream Fight quest. Cultivated for centuries, kept secret from outsiders.

### 2.8 Region 06 Impossible Cannabis (Cannabis liminaris)

**Origin:** Region 06 only
**Appearance:** Each plant looks different (impossible)
**Effects:** Reality-bending, philosophical
**Strength:** Beyond categorization
**Availability:** Post-game discovery
**Uses:**
- **Post-game philosophical experiences**
- **Connection to Elin**
- **Final understanding moments**
**Cultural:** Post-game content
**Price:** N/A (story-locked)

## 4. PREPARATION AND CONSUMPTION

### 4.1 Drying

**Standard Process:**
- Cut at peak ripeness
- Hang in dark, ventilated space
- 1-2 weeks drying
- Quality preservation

**Sacred Process (Mama Jacinta):**
- Specific moon phase harvest
- Smoke-blessed drying
- 4-6 weeks slow process
- Prayer throughout

### 4.2 Curing

**Common Curing:**
- 2-4 weeks in jars
- Burping (releasing moisture)
- Standard quality

**Sacred Curing:**
- 3-6 months
- Buried clay pots in sacred earth
- Specific rituals
- Maximum potency

### 4.3 Consumption Methods

**Smoking (Most Common):**
- Rolled with tobacco (Rexan tradition)
- Pipe consumption
- Various tools
- Quick onset

**Edibles:**
- Cannabis-infused foods
- Slower onset
- Stronger and longer
- Risk of overconsumption

**Tinctures (Ines's Specialty):**
- Alcohol-extracted
- Medical applications
- Precise dosing
- Long shelf life

**Tea/Infusion:**
- Mild effects
- Traditional preparation
- Communal sharing
- Mama Jacinta's preference

### 4.4 The Sacred Ceremony

**Mama Jacinta's Method:**
- Specific preparation room
- Burning incense (palo santo)
- Drumming or chanting
- Tea, then sometimes smoke
- Hours-long ceremony

**Setting:**
- Dim, warm space
- Comfortable seating
- Specific imagery
- Protective presence

## 5. CONSUMPTION EFFECTS

### 5.1 Common Cannabis Effects

**Light Use (Rexa Wild, common strains):**
- +20% stamina regen (passive)
- -10% combat accuracy (debuff)
- Mild perception alterations
- 30-60 minute duration
- Munchies (food consumption increases)

**Medium Use (Imperial, Moran):**
- +30% stamina regen
- -20% combat accuracy
- Visual effects (mild)
- Time perception changes
- 1-2 hour duration
- Sleep induction possible

**Heavy Use (Strong strains, large dose):**
- +40% stamina regen
- -40% combat accuracy
- Significant visual/audio effects
- Possible "couch lock" (movement debuff)
- 2-4 hour duration
- Risk of paranoia

### 5.2 Medical Cannabis Effects

**Coastal Curative (Ines's preparations):**
- Specific buffs based on recipe
- Pain elimination (no debuffs)
- Targeted medical benefits
- Controlled experience

**Examples:**
- **Pain Tonic:** Eliminate pain debuffs from injuries
- **Sleep Aid:** Guaranteed restful sleep
- **Anti-Nausea:** Sea sickness immunity
- **Appetite:** Increased food consumption benefits

### 5.3 Strategic Considerations

**When to Use:**
- Long voyages (boredom relief)
- Recovery periods (healing)
- Social moments (sharing)
- Mental preparation

**When NOT to Use:**
- Before combat
- Critical decisions
- Stealth missions
- Combat training

### 5.4 Negative Consequences

**Overconsumption:**
- Severe debuffs
- Hours of impairment
- Crew may judge
- Reputation impact

**Frequent Use:**
- Tolerance develops
- Stronger doses needed
- Craft suffers
- Gradual character impact

**Addiction:**
- Specific quest path
- Recovery storyline
- Major character moment
- Choice consequences

## 6. THE SACRED STRAIN — DREAM FIGHT MECHANICS

### 6.1 The Sacred Effect

**Unlike Common Cannabis:**
- Not a buff or debuff system
- Triggers narrative experience
- One-time per ceremony
- Permanent consequences

**The Process:**
1. Mama Jacinta prepares sacred tea + smoke
2. Jake consumes ceremonially
3. Reality begins to shift
4. Jake enters the Dream Arena
5. Confronts manifestations
6. Returns transformed

### 6.2 The Dream Arena

**Setting:**
- Psychedelic version of Jake's childhood home
- Recognizable but dreamlike
- Colors heightened
- Time fluid
- Logic suspended

**Visual Style:**
- Saturated colors
- Soft edges
- Memory-textured
- Melancholy beauty

**Audio:**
- Distant familiar voices
- Heightened emotional resonance
- Specific musical themes
- Childhood sounds

### 6.3 The Combat-Conversation Hybrid

**Unique Combat System:**
- Standard combat available
- Dialogue options also available
- Some enemies must be talked to
- Some must be fought
- Some require both

**Each Dream Enemy:**
- Represents specific aspect of Jake's psyche
- Has dialogue revealing truth
- Provides combat challenge
- Defeat method varies

### 6.4 The Six Dream Encounters

**Encounter 1: The Childhood Self**
- Young Jake, before tragedy
- Innocent, hopeful
- Asks Jake "What happened to us?"
- Cannot be defeated by combat
- Must be **acknowledged** through dialogue
- Reveals: What Jake has lost of himself

**Encounter 2: The Coward**
- Manifestation of Jake's fear
- Tries to convince Jake to flee his quest
- Fights when challenged
- Must be **defeated** in combat
- Reveals: Jake's hidden fears

**Encounter 3: The Murderer**
- Manifestation of Jake's killing
- Wears blood of those Jake has killed
- Demands Jake acknowledge what he's becoming
- Can be **fought OR talked down**
- Different rewards based on choice
- Reveals: Cost of revenge path

**Encounter 4: The Lover Lost**
- Manifestation of love Jake never explored (Margarethe Fitzmueller? Or someone earlier?)
- Beautiful, sad, accusing
- Cannot be fought (player can't strike)
- Must be **accepted and released**
- Reveals: Connections Jake has refused

**Encounter 5: The Beast**
- Pure rage, animal form
- Manifestation of revenge itself
- Strongest combat encounter
- Must be **defeated AND tamed**
- Two-phase encounter
- Reveals: The thing that drives Jake

**Encounter 6: Ethan**
- Final dream opponent
- Appears as Ethan was BEFORE leaving
- Healthy, whole, brotherly
- **Cannot be fought** (Jake will refuse)
- Must be **conversed with deeply**
- Speaks truths about Jake's path
- The most important encounter
- Reveals: What Jake's quest is costing him spiritually

### 6.5 Dream-Ethan's Dialogue (Sample)

**Dream-Ethan:**
*"You came back for me. I've been waiting."*

**[Jake response options:]**
- "I thought you were dead."
- "I came back for revenge."
- "I came back too late."

**Dream-Ethan:**
*"Look at yourself, brother. Look at what you've become to find me."*
*"Do you remember when we were small? You wanted to be a captain. A good one. Like our father."*

**[Conversation continues based on player choices...]**

**Eventually:**
*"You can lay down the sword. You can build something. You can be the man I knew."*
*"Or you can finish this revenge and become something I won't recognize."*
*"It's still your choice. Even now. Even after everything."*

**[Jake must respond. Choice affects:]**
- Permanent passive buff direction
- Late-game dialogue options
- Specific ending paths
- Crew relationship dynamics

### 6.6 Awakening

**Return to Reality:**
- Hours have passed
- Mama Jacinta sitting nearby
- She knows what Jake saw (somehow)
- Brief conversation
- Permanent change

**Mama Jacinta's Words:**
*"What did the plant show you, Captain?"*
*"What you brought, you found. What you found, you carry now."*
*"This door is closed. But what you saw walks with you."*

## 7. PERMANENT CONSEQUENCES OF DREAM FIGHT

### 7.1 Mechanical Rewards

**Universal (Everyone Gets):**
- **"Dream-Clarity" Passive:** +10% critical hit rate, permanent
- **Specific dialogue options unlocked** with crew
- **Mama Jacinta becomes recurring NPC**
- **Cannabis no longer impairs Jake combat-wise** (built tolerance)

**Choice-Based (Varies):**

**If Jake "Acknowledged Childhood Self":**
- Specific compassion-based dialogue options
- Children NPCs respond differently
- Memory-based bonuses

**If Jake "Defeated the Coward":**
- Fear-based encounters easier
- Confidence in dangerous situations
- Stealth bonuses

**If Jake "Talked Down the Murderer":**
- Mercy options expanded
- Combat damage slightly lower
- Reputation easier to repair

**If Jake "Defeated the Murderer":**
- Combat damage higher
- Reputation harder to repair
- Crew unease increases

**If Jake "Accepted the Lover Lost":**
- Relationship building accelerated
- Margarethe Fitzmueller path enhanced
- Emotional dialogue depth

**If Jake "Tamed the Beast":**
- Rache more controlled
- Less collateral damage
- Honor-based bonuses

**If Jake "Defeated Beast Without Taming":**
- Rache more powerful
- Berserker tendencies
- Reputation cost

**Dream-Ethan Choice — The Big One:**

**If Jake "Promised to Lay Down the Sword":**
- Peace Route paths enhanced
- Specific ending becomes available
- Combat slightly less effective late-game
- But moral resolution deepens

**If Jake "Refused to Stop":**
- Combat enhanced significantly
- Specific dark ending becomes available

### 7.2 Story Consequences

**NPCs Notice:**
- Crew comments on Jake being different

**Mama Jacinta:**

**The Dreams Continue:**
- Jake occasionally has dreams reminding him
