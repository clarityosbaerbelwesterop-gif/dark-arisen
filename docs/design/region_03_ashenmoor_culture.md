# Region 03 — Ashenmoor Culture & The Anak Gunung

**Status:** Design Draft v1.0  
**Related:** region_03_ashenmoor_overview.md, settlements, volcanoes  
**Inspirations:** Balinese Hindu traditions, Javanese court culture, Studio Ghibli's respect for indigenous worldviews

---

## 1. Overview

The Anak Gunung — "Children of the Mountain" — are the indigenous civilization of Ashenmoor. They are not pirates, not colonists, not enemies. They are a people with thousands of years of history, their own language, their own gods, and their own understanding of what the three volcanoes mean. How Jake treats them determines whether Ashenmoor becomes a place he liberated or a place he exploited — and that choice echoes all the way to the final chapters of Dark Arisen.

This document defines the Anak Gunung as a faction, their beliefs, their political situation, and the three alliance paths Jake can take through Region 3.

---

## 2. Who Are the Anak Gunung?

### 2.1 Origin Myth
According to Anak Gunung legend, their people came from the First Burning. In the ancient past, the three volcanoes erupted together, and from the ash and fire the gods shaped the first humans. These humans were taught by the dragon — yes, the same dragon Jake will fight — how to live with fire instead of fleeing it. The Anak Gunung consider themselves the dragon's chosen people, even though no living Anak Gunung has ever seen it.

This origin story is the lore reason why the dragon knows the pre-human language: it taught that language to the first Anak Gunung, who forgot it over the millennia. Jake can re-learn it in the Primordial Library dungeon.

### 2.2 Social Structure
The Anak Gunung are organized by elevation. The higher you live, the more spiritual authority you have. This is why Kuil Atas sits at 1,800m — the monks there are considered the ultimate moral authority.

| Class | Elevation | Role |
|-------|-----------|------|
| Fishers | Sea level | Harvest the ocean, mediate with outsiders |
| Farmers | 400m | Grow rice, tend the terraces, keep traditions alive |
| Craftsmen | 800m | Forge, mine, build — the "working heart" |
| Monks | 1,800m | Guardians of knowledge and ritual |

Before the Crimson Armada arrived, this system worked peacefully for centuries. The classes supported each other through trade and tradition. Now it is broken — the Crimson Armada has specifically targeted Kota Api (the craftsmen) because they produce weapons, and by doing so has severed the vertical trust network that held the civilization together.

### 2.3 Religion & Spirituality
The Anak Gunung practice a form of ancestor worship combined with volcano deity veneration. They believe:
- The three volcanoes are a family of gods (Father, Mother, Child)
- The dead live inside Gunung Bapa (the dormant Father) until they are ready to be reborn
- Ancestors speak through dreams, incense smoke, and the calls of specific birds
- Every Anak Gunung carries a small carved stone from their birth village — losing it means losing one's soul
- The dragon is not worshipped as a god, but respected as a teacher who chose them

**Practical Gameplay Effect:** Certain Anak Gunung NPCs can only be helped if Jake participates in their rituals. Desecrating a shrine (attacking it, stealing offerings) can lock Jake out of entire questlines.

## 3. The Crimson Armada's Grip

The Crimson Armada came to Ashenmoor three years before Jake arrives. They didn't conquer it openly — they corrupted it from within. Here's how:

1. **They bribed Raden Suryo**, an Anak Gunung noble with ambition beyond his station, offering him power over Kota Api in exchange for loyalty
2. **They took the forges**, forcing master smiths to produce Crimson Armada weapons
3. **They conscripted miners**, demanding obsidian and sulfur as "taxes"
4. **They isolated Kuil Atas**, convincing the lower villages that the monks had abandoned them
5. **They left Tanjung Hitam and Desa Padi alone**, for now — these villages are next, unless something stops the Armada

This is Salazar's model applied to a whole culture: find the weak link, corrupt it, use it as a foothold. The parallel between Salazar's Rexa and the Crimson Armada's Ashenmoor is intentional — Draven Voss's empire uses the same playbook everywhere.

## 4. The Three Alliance Paths

This is the core choice architecture of Ashenmoor. Jake's actions throughout Chapters 5-7 determine which of three paths he ends up on. Each path has different quest availability, different endings for Ashenmoor, and different consequences in later chapters.

### 4.1 Path A: The Liberator (Lawful Good)
**How to trigger:** Help every Anak Gunung questline Jake encounters. Refuse bribes. Participate in rituals. Free the mines without looting. Protect civilians during the Kota Api conflict.

**Outcome:** Jake becomes a legendary figure to the Anak Gunung. He is given the honorary title "Anak Naga" (Child of the Dragon). The villages unite, rebuild their vertical trust network, and drive out the Crimson Armada remnants after Jake leaves.

**Benefits:**
- All Anak Gunung recruitable NPCs join Jake's crew (3 unique recruits)
- Free access to Master Smith Jaya's legendary weapons
- Story flag: Abbot Purnama blesses Jake with a unique ability usable in the final boss fight
- In Chapter 9, one Anak Gunung warrior appears as reinforcement during the Ethan confrontation

**Costs:**
- Longest path (most side quests required)
- Locks out some morally grey profit opportunities
- No alliance with local criminal networks

### 4.2 Path B: The Opportunist (Neutral)
**How to trigger:** Help some Anak Gunung, ignore others. Take bribes when offered. Play both sides between the Crimson Armada and the local factions. Complete main story but skip deep cultural content.

**Outcome:** Ashenmoor is "freed" in the sense that the worst Crimson Armada leaders are dead, but the region remains politically unstable. Some villagers thank Jake; others whisper behind his back. Raden Suryo is replaced, but by someone only marginally better.

**Benefits:**
- Fastest path through Region 3
- More Doubloon income through bribes and shady dealings
- Access to the Black Market that forms in the power vacuum
- One additional weapon recipe unavailable on other paths

**Costs:**
- No Anak Gunung crew members
- Master Smith Jaya refuses to forge for Jake
- No Abbot's Blessing
- Story flag: Jake feels the weight of roads not taken (reflective dialogue changes)

### 4.3 Path C: The Exploiter (Dark Path)
**How to trigger:** Take bribes from the Crimson Armada. Desecrate shrines. Kill civilians during combat. Sell out Anak Gunung resistance fighters. Complete the story through brutality.

**Outcome:** Jake leaves Ashenmoor a wealthier man, but the region is worse off than when he arrived. The Anak Gunung are broken as a unified people. Raden Suryo is replaced by someone more loyal to whatever faction Jake sided with. The dragon, if Jake defeated it, is remembered by the Anak Gunung as a second tragedy on top of everything else they lost.

**Benefits:**
- Highest Doubloon and Gold Bar gain in the region
- Unique dark-path weapons and armor
- Certain Crimson Armada NPCs become recruitable (morally grey crew)
- Access to the most powerful tinctures through Crimson Armada alchemists

**Costs:**
- No Anak Gunung alliance, no crew recruits from the region
- Kuil Atas closes its doors to Jake permanently
- Story flag: Ethan's ending options change — the dark path locks out the third "hidden" ending and makes the "kill" ending feel emptier
- Mira's loyalty drops significantly (risk of her leaving the crew)

## 5. The Anak Gunung Recruitable Crew Members (Path A Only)

If Jake completes Path A (The Liberator), three unique Anak Gunung characters join his crew:

### 5.1 Putu — The Young Warrior
- **From:** Tanjung Hitam (Pak Gede's son from the "Warrior's Path" quest)
- **Role:** Combat specialist, front-line fighter
- **Personality:** Eager, respectful, learning from Jake
- **Crew Benefit:** +10% crew morale in combat situations

### 5.2 Ketut — The Mountain Monk
- **From:** Kuil Atas (the Silent Novice)
- **Role:** Ship surgeon, spiritual counsel
- **Personality:** Quiet, observant, carries a dark personal past
- **Crew Benefit:** Faster crew healing, one daily prayer that boosts all stats 5% for 10 minutes

### 5.3 Wayan — The Master Smith's Apprentice
- **From:** Kota Api (Master Smith Jaya's daughter, rescued in "The Smith's Daughter" quest)
- **Role:** Ship weaponsmith, repairs, upgrades
- **Personality:** Fiery, proud, skilled beyond her years
- **Crew Benefit:** Weapon repair at sea (no need to visit a shop), 15% crafting material discount

## 6. Anak Gunung Language — Bahasa Gunung

For flavor and immersion, Anak Gunung NPCs sometimes speak in their own language. The player does not need to understand it, but subtle subtitles can be toggled on/off.

**Sample phrases:**
- *"Selamat datang"* — Welcome (spoken to Jake on arrival at villages)
- *"Anak Naga"* — Child of the Dragon (Path A honorific for Jake)
- *"Api ibu, api bapa, api anak"* — Fire of the Mother, fire of the Father, fire of the Child (ritual invocation)
- *"Jangan takut"* — Do not fear (common reassurance)
- *"Gunung hidup"* — The mountain lives

Bahasa Gunung is based on real Indonesian/Balinese phrases but should be treated as its own fictional language in-game. Important: we consult with cultural advisors before finalizing any dialogue to avoid misrepresentation.

## 7. Why Ashenmoor Culture Matters for the Whole Game

Ashenmoor is the first region where Jake's choices have long-range narrative consequences. Moran was survival. Rexa was revenge against a clearly evil target (Salazar). But Ashenmoor is morally ambiguous — the Crimson Armada here is not led by a monster like Salazar, and the Anak Gunung are not just victims waiting to be saved. They have their own agency, their own history, and their own judgment of Jake.

This is where Dark Arisen stops being just a revenge fantasy and starts asking the player: **What kind of man is Jake becoming?** The answer shapes Chapters 8, 9, and 10. The Ethan confrontation in Chapter 9 specifically references the Ashenmoor path: if Jake was The Liberator there, Ethan's dialogue acknowledges that Jake has become something better than their father ever was. If Jake was The Exploiter, Ethan coldly observes that Jake has become exactly what the Crimson Armada preaches is inevitable — that everyone becomes a monster, given time.

The Anak Gunung are not just a faction. They are the mirror the game holds up to the player.
