# Region 03 — Ashenmoor Settlements (Detailed)

**Status:** Design Draft v1.0  
**Related:** region_03_ashenmoor_overview.md  
**Inspirations:** Balinese villages, Javanese temple towns, Ghibli's Spirited Away bathhouse district (for Kota Api)

---

## 1. Overview

Ashenmoor has four major settlements at different elevations, connected by mountain paths, rope bridges, and carved stone stairways. Each settlement serves a distinct gameplay role and introduces Jake to a different face of the Anak Gunung civilization. Unlike Rexa's single sprawling city, Ashenmoor's population is distributed vertically — and that verticality is the defining design choice.

---

## 2. Tanjung Hitam — The Black Cape (Coastal)

**Elevation:** Sea level  
**Population:** ~300  
**Biome:** Black volcanic sand beach, turquoise tide pools, bamboo stilt houses, fishing longboats with carved dragon prows  
**Mood:** Peaceful, cautious, the first welcome after the open sea

### 2.1 Role in the Story
Tanjung Hitam is Jake's landing point in Chapter 5. After the naval battle approaching Ashenmoor, Jake anchors La Liberación off the cape and rows ashore with Mira. The villagers are initially suspicious but not hostile — they've seen too many pirates to trust easily, but they recognize Jake's crew isn't Crimson Armada.

### 2.2 Key NPCs
| NPC | Role | Notes |
|-----|------|-------|
| Pak Gede | Village Headman | First dialogue partner. Speaks broken English. Judges Jake by how he treats the villagers. |
| Ibu Wayan | Healer / Shaman | Performs the ritual that grants Jake the Ghost Amulet. Sees Jake's grief. |
| Kadek | Young fisherman | Comic relief, tells Jake rumors about the three volcanoes. |
| The Drowned Girl | Ghost NPC | Optional supernatural encounter — a child who drowned during a Crimson Armada raid. |

### 2.3 Locations
- **The Dragon Prow Dock** — where La Liberación anchors; first Rest Point in Ashenmoor
- **Pak Gede's Longhouse** — village center, meeting space
- **The Tide Shrine** — small temple on the rocks, site of the Ghost Amulet ritual
- **The Fish Market** — low-level merchant, sells food and basic supplies
- **Ibu Wayan's Hut** — healing items, tinctures with local ingredients

### 2.4 Side Content
- **"The Drowned Girl"** — optional ghost questline, lore-heavy
- **"The Stolen Boats"** — Crimson Armada raiders are stealing longboats at night; Jake sets an ambush
- **"The Warrior's Path"** — Pak Gede's son wants to prove himself; Jake can train him (unlocks him as a recruitable crew member later)

## 3. Desa Padi — The Rice Village (Mid-Slope)

**Elevation:** ~400m  
**Population:** ~500  
**Biome:** Terraced rice paddies climbing the mountainside, bamboo groves, small waterfalls, stone shrines covered in moss  
**Mood:** Serene, ancient, the heart of Anak Gunung agricultural life

### 3.1 Role in the Story
Jake reaches Desa Padi by climbing the mountain path from Tanjung Hitam. This is where he first sees the real scale of Ashenmoor's culture — entire families have farmed these terraces for generations. The Crimson Armada has begun demanding "taxes" from the village: a portion of each rice harvest, plus forced labor at the Kota Api mines. The villagers are quietly desperate.

### 3.2 Key NPCs
| NPC | Role | Notes |
|-----|------|-------|
| Mbah Seruni | Village Elder (Grandmother) | Ancient, wise, knows the old stories about the dragon. Central quest-giver. |
| Budi | Rice Farmer | Has lost his son to the Kota Api mines. Will join Jake's crew if his son is saved. |
| Si Kecil ("Little One") | Water Buffalo | Not a joke — an actual water buffalo that serves as a pack animal for a specific quest. |
| The Wandering Monk | Traveling Teacher | Gives Jake hints about Kuil Atas, the mountain monastery. |

### 3.3 Locations
- **The Great Terraces** — breathtaking vista point, reveals large chunk of minimap
- **The Village Square** — stone pavilion with carved guardian figures
- **Mbah Seruni's Longhouse** — questline starting point
- **The Rice Shrine** — small temple where offerings are left; unlocks a hidden puzzle
- **The Buffalo Pens** — where Si Kecil lives

### 3.4 Side Content
- **"The Missing Sons"** — main Desa Padi questline; rescue villagers forced into Kota Api
- **"The Dragon's Lullaby"** — Mbah Seruni teaches Jake an old song that calms the dragon briefly in the final dungeon
- **"The Water Buffalo's Path"** — escort Si Kecil through a dangerous mountain pass; lighthearted quest
- **"The Shrine Offerings"** — environmental puzzle involving placing items at five shrines around the terraces

## 4. Kota Api — Fire Town (Volcanic Hub)

**Elevation:** ~800m, on the slopes of Gunung Ibu  
**Population:** ~1,500 — largest settlement in Ashenmoor  
**Biome:** Constant ash haze, red glow from the volcano, smoking forges, obsidian and sulfur mines, lava channels diverted for industry  
**Mood:** Oppressive, industrial, beautiful in a terrifying way — like Spirited Away's bathhouse district meets Dante's Inferno

### 4.1 Role in the Story
Kota Api is the central hub and the main conflict zone of Ashenmoor. Originally a legitimate mining town where the Anak Gunung forged the finest weapons in the region, it has been taken over by the Crimson Armada. The local governor (a corrupted Anak Gunung noble named Raden Suryo) answers to a Crimson Armada lieutenant. The miners work in terrible conditions, the smiths are forced to produce weapons for the pirates, and anyone who complains disappears.

Jake's arrival here is the main story arc of Chapter 6. He must decide: liberate the town openly (hard, dangerous), infiltrate and undermine the Crimson Armada from within (stealthier), or exploit the chaos for his own ends (morally dark path that affects Ethan's ending later).

### 4.2 Districts Within Kota Api
| District | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| The Forges | Constant hammering, master smiths, the best weapons in the region forged here |
| The Mines | Obsidian and sulfur operations, forced labor, dangerous shafts going deep into the volcano |
| The Governor's Palace | Raden Suryo's residence, luxurious despite the surrounding ash, guarded |
| The Bath District | Volcanic hot springs repurposed into public and private baths, a surprisingly peaceful area |
| The Lava Docks | Where obsidian is loaded onto barges that travel lava channels — unique to Kota Api |
| The Red Lantern Quarter | Gambling dens, brothels, Crimson Armada hangouts |

### 4.3 Key NPCs
| NPC | Role | Notes |
|-----|------|-------|
| Raden Suryo | Corrupt Governor | Primary antagonist of Kota Api. Anak Gunung noble who sold out to the Crimson Armada. |
| Master Smith Jaya | Legendary Weaponsmith | Forced to forge for the pirates. Will make Jake legendary weapons if freed. |
| The Red Lieutenant | Crimson Armada Commander | Direct report to Draven's lieutenant who controls Ashenmoor. Story boss. |
| Old Miner Darma | Lore giver | Knows the secret way into the deep volcanic tunnels under Gunung Bapa (dragon lair). |
| The Bath Attendant | Optional companion | A former Anak Gunung warrior hiding as a bath attendant, can be recruited. |

### 4.4 Locations
- **The Forge Plaza** — central square, weaponsmith, Rest Point
- **The Governor's Palace** — infiltration mission, multiple approach routes
- **The Main Mine Shaft** — leads into the volcanic depths, eventually to the dragon
- **The Volcanic Baths** — unique healing location (full heal + buff for 30 min), local secret
- **The Red Lieutenant's Quarters** — story dungeon, mid-chapter boss fight
- **The Lava Barge Platform** — unique travel mechanic, fast travel within Kota Api

### 4.5 Side Content
Kota Api has the most side content of any single location in the game. Examples:
- **"The Smith's Daughter"** — rescue Master Smith Jaya's kidnapped daughter from the Red Lantern Quarter
- **"The Lava Channel Job"** — ride a lava barge across the town in a high-speed sequence
- **"The Mining Rebellion"** — organize the miners into a revolt
- **"The Bath House Mystery"** — a murder mystery with suspects in the private baths
- **"The Obsidian Shipment"** — hijack a Crimson Armada obsidian shipment
- **"The Governor's Ledger"** — steal financial records proving Raden Suryo's crimes

## 5. Kuil Atas — The Upper Temple (High Altitude Monastery)

**Elevation:** ~1,800m, just below the snow line  
**Population:** ~80 monks and novices  
**Biome:** Cold air, ash-fall "snow," cliff-edge temples carved from black volcanic rock, ancient stone guardians the size of buildings, prayer flags in the wind  
**Mood:** Transcendent, silent, sacred — the opposite of Kota Api

### 5.1 Role in the Story
Kuil Atas is the spiritual heart of the Anak Gunung. The monks here guard ancient knowledge about the three volcanoes, the dragon, and something older than all of them — an event they call "The First Burning" when the gods themselves walked Ashenmoor. For Jake, Kuil Atas serves two critical purposes: a sanctuary where he can reflect on the story so far, and a source of lore that recontextualizes the Crimson Armada and, eventually, Draven Voss himself.

### 5.2 Key NPCs
| NPC | Role | Notes |
|-----|------|-------|
| Abbot Purnama | Head Monk | Wise, patient, tests Jake through philosophical dialogues. Old Bones-style mentor figure. |
| Brother Agung | Combat Master | Teaches Jake advanced techniques: the Perfect Parry upgrade, a charged attack variant |
| Sister Rahayu | Historian | Gives Jake lore dumps about Ashenmoor's ancient history, reveals things about the dragon |
| The Silent Novice | Optional companion | A young monk with a dark past, can be recruited as a crew surgeon |
| The Stone Guardians | Environmental | Massive carved statues that occasionally "speak" through wind and acoustics — not enemies |

### 5.3 Locations
- **The Great Hall** — meditation space, Rest Point, beautiful vista of the three volcanoes below
- **The Training Grounds** — where Brother Agung teaches combat
- **The Archive** — ancient scrolls and texts, lore unlocks
- **The Prayer Garden** — environmental puzzle area with floating stones
- **The First Burning Shrine** — hidden entry to the optional Kuil Atas dungeon

### 5.4 Side Content
- **"The Abbot's Test"** — three philosophical trials that reward Jake with stat bonuses
- **"The Lost Scroll"** — retrieve an ancient text from a collapsed shrine
- **"The Silent Novice"** — recruitment questline for the monk companion
- **"The Combat Trials"** — training gauntlet with Brother Agung, rewards combat upgrades
- **"The Meditation Path"** — walk the prayer garden in silence, no combat, reveals hidden lore
