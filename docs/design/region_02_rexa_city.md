# Region 02 — Rexa City (Detailed)

**Status:** Design Draft v1.0  
**Related:** region_02_rexa_overview.md, GDD v1.2  
**Inspirations:** Saint Denis (RDR2), Havana (AC4), Yharnam vibes after dark

---

## 1. City Overview

Rexa is a port city carved out of the tropical coast — Spanish colonial architecture rotting under salt air, jungle reclaiming the edges, gas-lit streets, the constant smell of sugar and rum. By day it pretends to be civilized: merchants, churches, music. By night the masks come off — the slave trade runs in alleys, the bounty hunters drink with the men they'll kill tomorrow, and Salazar's people watch from rooftops.

**Population:** ~12,000 NPCs (mix of scripted and procedural)  
**Layout:** Six interconnected districts, no loading screens between them  
**Wanted System:** 5-star bounty system (city watch responds based on crimes)

## 2. The Six Districts

### 2.1 Harbor District ("El Puerto")
- **Mood:** Chaotic, loud, salty. Always busy.
- **Key Locations:** Main Docks, La Esperanza's berth (Jake's arrival point), Shipwright (where Jake later buys his ship), Tavern "La Sirena Negra"
- **NPCs:** Dock workers, sailors, the Bounty Hunter contact "Diego Cruz", Captain Rojas (Commander #3, patrolling)
- **Side Content:** Dock fights, dice games, naval supply trading

### 2.2 Market District ("El Mercado")
- **Mood:** Colorful, busy by day, dangerous by night
- **Key Locations:** Open-air market, Weapon Shop "Aceros del Sur", Apothecary "La Botica de Doña Lucia", Stables
- **NPCs:** Merchants, pickpockets, street performers, informants
- **Side Content:** Pickpocket missions, bounty board, horse purchase

### 2.3 Cathedral District ("La Catedral")
- **Mood:** Quiet, oppressive, holy. Painted gold over rot.
- **Key Locations:** La Catedral de los Huesos (Dungeon #2), Old Library, Confessional Booths
- **NPCs:** Corrupt clergy, La Viuda Negra (Commander #1, hides among nuns)
- **Side Content:** Hidden lore tablets, nun who is actually a Free Slaves' Underground contact

### 2.4 Noble Quarter ("Las Casas Altas")
- **Mood:** Wealthy, gated, hostile to outsiders
- **Key Locations:** Salazar's public estate (front, not the slaughterhouse), Governor's Mansion, Duelist's Square
- **NPCs:** Aristocrats, guards, Salazar's lieutenants
- **Side Content:** Duels for honor and gold, infiltration missions, fancy dress code (must change clothes to enter without aggro)

### 2.5 The Slums ("El Barrio Bajo")
- **Mood:** Desperate, dangerous, alive
- **Key Locations:** El Carnicero's Slaughterhouse (Dungeon #9), Black Market entrance, Underground Free Slaves safehouse
- **NPCs:** Beggars, escaped slaves, El Médico (Commander #5, runs a "clinic")
- **Side Content:** Help escaped slaves quest chain, expose Salazar's operation

### 2.6 The Cliffside ("El Acantilado")
- **Mood:** Quiet, dramatic, lookout point
- **Key Locations:** Old Spanish Lighthouse (climbable, reveals huge minimap area), Cliffside Tavern, Smuggler's Cove access
- **NPCs:** Old sailors with stories, treasure map sellers
- **Side Content:** First Treasure Map for the Spanish Galleon Hoard sold here

## 3. The Three City Commanders (Detailed)

### 3.1 La Viuda Negra — "The Black Widow"
- **Real Name:** Sister Catalina Vargas
- **Cover:** A nun in the Cathedral
- **Style:** Dual poisoned daggers, fast and lethal, uses incense smoke for cover
- **Backstory:** Was a noblewoman whose husband was killed in a Crimson Armada raid. Salazar offered her revenge in exchange for service. She kills with the same religious fervor she once prayed with.
- **Boss Arena:** Cathedral inner sanctum, pews and candles, smoke effects
- **Phase 1:** Dagger duel, poison DoT on hits
- **Phase 2:** Drops smoke bombs, becomes invisible, ambushes from behind
- **Reward:** Recipe: Antidote Tincture, Black Widow's Veil (light armor)

### 3.2 Captain Rojas — "The Iron Captain"
- **Real Name:** Antonio Rojas, former imperial navy
- **Cover:** Harbor patrol commander
- **Style:** Naval saber + flintlock pistol, leads 6 city guards
- **Backstory:** Sold out his unit to Salazar for gold years ago. Now runs the harbor as Salazar's eyes on incoming ships.
- **Boss Arena:** Harbor docks at night, multi-platform fight across moored ships
- **Phase 1:** Fights with his guards. Must thin them out first.
- **Phase 2:** Solo duel, alternates saber and pistol, uses cover
- **Reward:** Iron Captain's Saber (legendary one-handed), 2 Gold Bars

### 3.3 El Médico — "The Surgeon"
- **Real Name:** Dr. Esteban Vega
- **Cover:** Slum doctor who "helps" the poor
- **Style:** Surgical blades, throws acid vials, releases plague rats
- **Backstory:** A failed medical student turned sadist. Salazar lets him "experiment" on captives. The most disturbing of the five Commanders.
- **Boss Arena:** His underground "clinic" — operating tables, jars of organs, screaming patients in cages
- **Phase 1:** Throws acid and rats while retreating between rooms
- **Phase 2:** Confronted in the main surgical theater, uses adrenaline serum to enter berserk mode
- **Reward:** Recipe: Berserker Tincture, Surgeon's Scalpel (fastest dagger in the game), 1 Gold Bar

## 5. The Bounty Hunters Guild (Optional Faction)

Jake can join the Bounty Hunters Guild in the Harbor District. The contact is Diego Cruz, a one-armed veteran who lost his arm to the Crimson Armada. Joining gives Jake:

- Access to bounty contracts (procedural side missions for gold)
- Special Hunter weapons and gear
- Fellow hunters as temporary companions for tough fights
- A unique ending variation if all contracts are completed

**Trade-off:** Joining the Bounty Hunters makes the Free Slaves' Underground less trusting. Some quest options become locked. Choose your allies.

## 6. The Wanted System

Crimes commit in Rexa raise Jake's Wanted level:

| Stars | Crime Examples | Response |
|-------|----------------|----------|
| 1 | Pickpocket, minor theft | 2 guards investigate |
| 2 | Brawling, drawing weapon | 4 guards, slight chase |
| 3 | Murder of citizen | 8 guards, full chase |
| 4 | Murder of noble, robbery | Elite guards, snipers on rooftops |
| 5 | Killing a Commander openly | Entire district locked down, manhunt |

Bribes available at corrupt officials. Disguises lower stars. Hiding in safehouses (Free Slaves Underground or Bounty Hunters Guild) clears stars over time.

## 7. Day/Night Cycle in Rexa

- **Day:** Markets open, civilians on streets, nobles visible, less crime
- **Dusk:** Lamps lit, gambling starts, music in taverns
- **Night:** Slums become dangerous, slave trade visible in alleys, Salazar's men patrol rooftops, certain quests only available now
- **Dawn:** Almost empty streets, ideal for assassinations or escapes
