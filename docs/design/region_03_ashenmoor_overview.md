# Region 03 — Ashenmoor (Overview)

**Status:** Design Draft v1.0  
**Related:** GDD v1.2 §4.3, Story Chapters 5-7  
**Estimated Playtime:** 18-22 hours (LARGEST region in the game)  
**Inspirations:** Bali volcanic islands, Mount Bromo, Indonesian temple complexes, Sekiro's mountain regions

---

## 1. Overview

After Jake sails out of Rexa Harbor with his new ship and crew, the open sea takes him east toward the smoking peaks of Ashenmoor — a chain of volcanic islands rising violently out of the ocean, draped in tropical jungle, terraced rice paddies climbing impossible slopes, and ancient stone temples carved into living rock. This is where the Crimson Armada has its true heart, where the gods of the old world still walk in stories, and where Jake will find the first concrete trail leading to Ethan.

Ashenmoor is the largest region in Dark Arisen by playable area, content density, and verticality. It is also the most visually striking — a complete tonal break from the Caribbean-colonial feel of Moran and Rexa.

**Region Scale:** ~45 km² across multiple connected islands and one massive central volcanic island

---

## 2. Cultural Identity

Ashenmoor is inspired by the volcanic islands of Bali, Java, and the broader Indonesian archipelago. The people who live here are not pirates — they are an ancient seafaring civilization with their own language, religion, and traditions. They have lived on these islands for thousands of years, long before the colonial empires arrived. The Crimson Armada has carved out hidden bases here, but the locals are an independent culture that Jake must learn to respect (or exploit).

**Visual Markers:**
- Black volcanic sand beaches with turquoise water
- Terraced rice paddies climbing mountainsides
- Stone temples with fierce carved guardians
- Bamboo houses on stilts in jungle clearings
- Ornate longboats with carved dragon prows
- Bridges of woven bamboo and rope spanning chasms
- Ash-fall snow on the highest peaks

**Local Faction:** The Anak Gunung ("Children of the Mountain") — a peaceful but proud civilization. Initially neutral toward Jake. Can become allies or enemies based on Jake's choices.

## 3. Settlement Layout

Ashenmoor has no single capital city. Instead, the player encounters a network of settlements at different elevations connected by paths, bridges, and the central mining town.

### 3.1 Tanjung Hitam (Black Cape) — Coastal Fishing Village
- **Elevation:** Sea level
- **Population:** ~300
- **Role:** Jake's first landing point. Friendly but cautious villagers. Introduction to local culture.

### 3.2 Desa Padi (Rice Village) — Mid-Slope Farming Community
- **Elevation:** ~400m
- **Population:** ~500
- **Role:** Heart of the rice terraces. Quest hub for the Anak Gunung's daily life. Peaceful unless provoked.

### 3.3 Kota Api (Fire Town) — The Mining Hub at the Volcano's Base
- **Elevation:** ~800m, on the slopes of the active volcano
- **Population:** ~1500 — the largest settlement in Ashenmoor
- **Role:** Central hub. Sulfur and obsidian mines. Smiths who forge the best weapons in the region. The Crimson Armada has corrupted this town from within, exploiting the miners. Main story conflict zone.
- **Mood:** Constant ash haze, glowing red light from the volcano, smoke from forges, ringing of hammers

### 3.4 Kuil Atas (Upper Temple) — High-Altitude Monastery
- **Elevation:** ~1800m, just below the snow line
- **Population:** ~80 monks
- **Role:** Sacred site. Guardians of ancient knowledge. Old Bones-style mentor figure lives here. Combat training, lore dumps, and a hidden dungeon.

---

## 4. The Three Volcanoes

Ashenmoor's central island is dominated by three active volcanoes, each with its own mythology and gameplay role:

| Volcano | Local Name | Status | Role |
|---------|------------|--------|------|
| The Father | Gunung Bapa | Massive, dormant | Houses the dragon dungeon |
| The Mother | Gunung Ibu | Active, smoking | Caldera Titan boss arena |
| The Child | Gunung Anak | Erupting periodically | Dynamic environmental hazard, lava rivers |

## 5. Story Beats — Chapters 5-7

### Chapter 5: Blood Tides (Naval Approach)
1. Jake's ship approaches Ashenmoor from the open sea
2. First naval combat — encounters Crimson Armada patrol fleet
3. Lands at Tanjung Hitam, meets the local headman
4. Discovers the Crimson Armada has corrupted Kota Api
5. **Ghost Amulet acquired here** (story trigger — through a local shaman ritual)

### Chapter 6: The Crimson Fort (Kota Api Conflict)
1. Jake travels up to Desa Padi — sees the rice terraces, learns local culture
2. Reaches Kota Api — confronts the Crimson Armada's grip on the mining town
3. Multiple side quests with Anak Gunung who want their town back
4. Major story dungeon: **Fort Carrion** is hidden inside an extinct volcanic cone above Kota Api
5. Discovers Ethan was here recently — fresh trail

### Chapter 7: Ashes of the Old World (The Volcanic Depths)
1. Jake ventures into the deep volcanic tunnels under Gunung Bapa
2. Ancient ruins of a pre-colonial civilization
3. Encounters the **Ashen Wyrm (Dragon Boss)** in its lair
4. Discovers lore that recontextualizes the Crimson Armada — Draven knew about something here
5. Climbs Kuil Atas for answers from the monks
6. End of Chapter 7 — major story revelation, transition toward Chapter 8

## 8. Why This Region Matters

Ashenmoor is the moment Dark Arisen stops being "a pirate Souls-Like" and starts being "an unforgettable world." The cultural shift, the vertical traversal, the dragon at the bottom of a volcano, the temple monks at altitude — this is where the game becomes ambitious. It's also the longest region because the player needs to slow down here. Ashenmoor is where Jake stops just hunting and starts understanding the world he's been tearing through.
