**Status:** Design Draft v1.0  
**Phase:** Phase 7 — Colonial War, Castles & Dungeons (Colony Tier Document 3 — FINAL Colony Tier — Document 7 of 16)  
**Tier:** Colony Tier  
**Related:** colonial_war_overview.md, colonial_war/war_state_system.md, colonial_war/colony_system_core.md, colonial_war/imperial_colonies.md, colonial_war/krone_colonies.md, colonial_war/secret_bosses.md, npcs/cannabis_keeper_mama_jacinta.md, npcs/desert_sage_mbah_seruni.md, npcs/healer_mormor_astrid.md, npcs/black_market_fences.md, npcs/shippable_specialists.md, npcs/recurring_quest_givers.md, npcs/the_named_dead.md, physics/vegetation_physics.md, physics/water_physics.md, style_bible.md  
**Purpose:** Complete design document for the Liberation Alliance — its five regional networks, the Network Connection questlines, Liberation Strength growth in detail, the exclusive reward architecture (ancient maps, feared waters, secret sites), joint conquests, Alliance bastions, and the Liberation Event battles.  
**Inspirations:** Assassin's Creed Odyssey (third-faction ambitions), Haitian Revolution and maroon communities (fictionalized), real indigenous resistance networks, The Battle of Algiers (network cellular structure), Black Panther Party community-survival programs, Studio Ghibli (nature as ally)

## 1. ALLIANCE PHILOSOPHY — THE FIVE PRINCIPLES

### 1.1 Principle 1: The Alliance Does Not Exist Until Jake Builds It

**Cross-reference:** colonial_war_overview.md Section 2.3

The Empire and Albion run without Jake. **The Alliance does not.** At game start it is five separate survival networks that barely know each other exists: Mama Jacinta's cultural web in Rexa, Tito Diego's recovery underground, Mbah Seruni's mountain paths, Fjordlund's patient refusal, and the Quiet Coast's deed-judging settlers.

They survive. They do not resist — not organized, not yet. **Jake is the connective tissue, or nobody is.**

**Design intent:** The third power is the only faction the player creates rather than joins.

### 1.2 Principle 2: LS Cannot Be Bought

**Cross-reference:** war_state_system.md Section 3.3

Liberation Strength is the only meter in Phase 7 that no amount of doubloons touches. It is earned through freed people, returned artifacts, kept promises, delivered weapons, connected networks, and battles fought *beside* the Alliance rather than for it.

**Design intent:** A currency of trust, immune to the wealth that solves everything else.

### 1.3 Principle 3: Liberation Is Slower Than Conquest

Breaking a colony is a campaign. Liberating one is a relationship. The Alliance path is the longest in Phase 7 by design — Jarl Olaf's *"with time, with consistency, with showing up"* (npcs/recurring_quest_givers.md Section 4.1) is the entire faction's design philosophy stated by one NPC in Phase 5.

**Design intent:** Patience as a mechanic, matching the cultures it represents.

### 1.4 Principle 4: They Give What Cannot Be Sold

**Cross-reference:** colonial_war_overview.md Section 5.3

The Empire sells legitimacy. Albion sells contracts. **The Alliance gives access** — the ancient maps, the navigable dark waters, the jungle depths, the doors that exist only for people who were trusted enough to be shown them. No merchant stocks these. No treasury buys them.

**Design intent:** A reward economy orthogonal to money.

### 1.5 Principle 5: Jake Is Not the Liberator

The Alliance is not Jake's army and liberation is not his gift. He is a ship, a shield, a spark, and a set of guns — significant, and not the protagonist of this particular story. **Every Liberation Event is fought WITH them, and the design must never let Jake become the white captain who freed the natives.**

The sages lead. The networks decide. Jake shows up.

**Design intent:** The player is an ally, not a savior. Style Bible restraint applied to the entire faction.

## 2. STRUCTURE — FIVE NETWORKS, NO CENTER

### 2.1 The Cellular Reality

The Alliance has no capital, no fleet, no general, and no throne. It has five regional networks, each with its own leadership, culture, methods, and goals. They are compatible, not unified.

**This is a strength (empires cannot decapitate what has no head) and a weakness (nothing coordinates until Jake makes it).**

### 2.2 NETWORK ONE — The Cultural Web (Rexa)

**Anchor:** Mama Jacinta (npcs/cannabis_keeper_mama_jacinta.md)  
**Territory:** Rexan jungle, the Sacred Grove, el barrio que recuerda in Puerto de la Corona  
**Character:** cultural preservation as resistance; the old language, the ceremonies, the plants, the memory  
**Methods:** continuity, transmission, refusal to forget  
**Key figures:** Mama Jacinta · Tía Catalina (Casa de los Antepasados) · Mama Cecilia Bautista · Don Eduardo Mendoza · Tito Carlos · Tía Esperanza Morales · Doña Esmeralda Ortega

**What they need from Jake:** protection for what they preserve; the conquest archives from La Ciudadela; the barrio's safety when Corona shakes

**What they give:** the Dream Fight's deeper tiers, the jungle's hidden paths (physics/vegetation_physics.md), cultural authority that opens every indigenous door in Rexa

**Starting LS contribution:** 12 (the strongest starting network — two centuries of unbroken memory)

### 2.3 NETWORK TWO — The Recovery Underground (Cross-Regional)

**Anchor:** Tito Diego (npcs/black_market_fences.md Section 6.2)  
**Territory:** everywhere the empires took something — Imperial estates, Company vaults, Pale Isle collections  
**Character:** the sacred-item recovery network; *"We do not steal. We recover."*  
**Methods:** infiltration, smuggling, the fence networks, patient theft  
**Key figures:** Tito Diego · Doña Carmen Ruiz (ethical art dealer, npcs/luxury_merchants.md) · Mateusz Voltari (fence, sympathetic) · Madame Celestine · the Whisper Lady (transactional, not aligned)

**What they need from Jake:** a ship that can move recovered items; access to Imperial and Company vaults; the choice, every time, to return rather than sell

**What they give:** the fence network, infiltration expertise, and — critically — **the artifacts themselves, which are keys.** Cross-reference secret_bosses.md: several recovered items are literal keys to sealed places.

**Starting LS contribution:** 8

**Design note:** Tito Diego's question from Phase 5 — *"Which captain are you?"* — is this network's entire recruitment test. Every stolen sacred item Jake sells instead of returning is an answer.

### 2.4 NETWORK THREE — The Mountain Paths (Ashenmoor)

**Anchor:** Mbah Seruni (npcs/desert_sage_mbah_seruni.md)  
**Territory:** the volcanic highlands, the desert, everything above the ore line at Fuerte San Rafael  
**Character:** the oldest and most closed network; thirty years of stillness with an address  
**Methods:** the terrain itself. They do not fight the empires; they simply live where the empires cannot follow.  
**Key figures:** Mbah Seruni · Pak Setyo (legendary smith) · Bu Wira (legendary alchemist) · Ibu Ratna Setiawan · Pak Bambang · Yara Sun-Walker (cultural bridge, npcs/shippable_specialists.md)

**What they need from Jake:** Fuerte San Rafael gone; the 1846 patrol reports; and something about Ibarra that Mbah Seruni has not yet decided

**What they give:** the desert's safe paths (physics/sand_desert_physics.md), volcanic legendary crafting (Pak Setyo, Bu Wira — Tier 5), the ash-buried sites, and the mountain's silence

**Starting LS contribution:** 10

### 2.5 NETWORK FOUR — The Patient Coast (Fjordlund)

**Anchor:** Jarl Olaf Bjornson (npcs/recurring_quest_givers.md Section 4.1)  
**Territory:** the northern coast, the fjords, the waters between two forts  
**Character:** not a resistance — a refusal. They have outlasted worse and expect to outlast this.  
**Methods:** endurance, community cohesion, the sea, and time  
**Key figures:** Jarl Olaf · Mormor Astrid Halvorsdotter · Master Halvdan Halvorsson (Bjornshallen) · Helga Sigurdsdotter · Halvor Eriksson · Astrid Halvorsdotter (smith) · Helga Thorsdotter

**What they need from Jake:** **both** flags down (imperial_colonies.md Section 7.12, krone_colonies.md Section 7.12) — and the patience to do it properly

**What they give:** the northern waters (physics/water_physics.md, snow_ice_physics.md), the drowned mead halls and whale-bone barrows, the sagas that are also directions, and Fjordlund's own definition of loyalty — which, once given, does not move

**Starting LS contribution:** 9

**Design note:** Fjordlund's network is the slowest to build and the hardest to lose. Cross-reference regional_populations.md Section 5.4 — *"Once earned: deep loyalty, community-wide."*

### 2.6 NETWORK FIVE — The Deed-Judgers (Quiet Coast)

**Anchor:** Sheriff James Whitaker (npcs/recurring_quest_givers.md Section 6.1)  
**Territory:** the frontier settlements, the indebted councils, the free fringe  
**Character:** the only non-indigenous network — settlers who signed Albion's treaties and watched what they signed become something else  
**Methods:** community solidarity, barn-raising logic applied to war, and a cultural immunity to paper authority  
**Key figures:** Sheriff Whitaker · the Henderson family (forge, kitchen, bar — npcs/blacksmiths_and_armorers.md, general_merchants.md, tavern_keepers.md) · Doc Wallace · the settler family network

**What they need from Jake:** the Master Ledger and the contracts destroyed; the councils' debts erased

**What they give:** manpower, logistics, ordinary competence, and the Alliance's bridge to non-indigenous populations — proof this is not a race war but a freedom one

**Starting LS contribution:** 6 (the newest and least organized)

**Design note:** Cross-reference regional_populations.md Section 7.3 — *"deeds only."* The Quiet Coast is the only network that will judge Jake purely on actions, ignoring reputation entirely. Fastest to convince through deeds, immune to charm.

### 2.7 The Missing Sixth

**Region 06 has no network.** There is nobody left to organize. Its absence is deliberate and the Alliance does not speak of it. Cross-reference regional_populations.md Section 9 — the empty region is the one place liberation cannot reach.

## 3. LIBERATION STRENGTH — THE DETAILED SYSTEM

### 3.1 The Structure

**Cross-reference:** war_state_system.md Section 3.3

LS exists at two levels:

**Network Strength (per network, 0-100):** how organized, armed, and capable each of the five networks is  
**Regional LS (per region):** derived from which networks reach that region and how strong they are
Regional LS = (primary network strength × 0.6)
+ (adjacent/supporting networks × 0.2 each)
× Connection Multiplier

### 3.2 The Connection Multiplier — The Core Progression

**This is the Alliance's central mechanic.**

Networks connected to each other share strength, intelligence, and reinforcement:

| Networks Connected | Multiplier | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 0 (isolated) | ×1.00 | Five survival groups |
| 2 | ×1.15 | They can warn each other |
| 3 | ×1.25 | Coordinated action possible |
| 4 | ×1.40 | A regional power |
| **5 (all)** | **×1.60** | **The Free Archipelago is possible** |

**Design intent:** The Alliance's power comes not from arming individuals but from making them one thing. The multiplier IS the theme — division was the empires' method; connection is the answer.

### 3.3 Building Network Strength

| Action | Strength Gain | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Free forced/indentured laborers (per convoy) | +3 | Cross-ref colony_system_core.md Section 4.5 |
| Return a sacred item | +5 | Never sold — the choice matters |
| Deliver weapons to a cell | +5 | Requires trust tier 2+ |
| Complete a training quest chain | +8 | Multi-stage, per network |
| Expose an atrocity with evidence | +6 | The document artifacts |
| Defend a network cell from a raid | +7 | Reactive — retaliation creates these |
| Destroy a colony's enforcement records | +10 | Contracts, ledgers, archives |
| Win a Liberation Event | +25 | And the region turns |

### 3.4 Losing Strength

LS is not safe. Cross-reference retaliation_system.md:

- **Stage 3 counter-attacks** raid Alliance cells: −5 to −10 network strength
- **Stage 5 scorched earth** can devastate a network: −20
- **Betrayal:** if Jake takes an Empire/Albion contract directly against a network he has armed, that network's strength drops hard and its trust tier resets
- **Neglect:** a liberated region left undefended can be reconquered (war_state_system.md), costing the network its proudest gain

### 3.5 The Trust Tiers

Each network tracks a **Trust Tier** separate from strength — how much they will *let Jake in*:

**Tier 0 — Watched:** they know he exists. Nothing is offered.  
**Tier 1 — Tolerated:** basic contact, small requests, no secrets  
**Tier 2 — Trusted:** weapons deliveries accepted, cell locations shared, real quests  
**Tier 3 — Kin:** the ancient maps, the hidden paths, the network's leadership speaks freely  
**Tier 4 — Blood:** the secret sites, the deepest sage content, and the network will die beside him

**Each network gates trust differently:**
- **Cultural Web:** Mama Jacinta's ceremonies (the Dream Fight is a trust gate, not just a quest)
- **Recovery Underground:** the return-vs-sell choice, tracked cumulatively
- **Mountain Paths:** survival and respect — crossing the desert alone, honoring the shrines
- **Patient Coast:** time and repetition. Literally showing up across chapters.
- **Deed-Judgers:** actions witnessed by the community. Barn-raisings count.

## 4. THE NETWORK CONNECTION QUESTLINES

### 4.1 The Central Content

**Ten possible connections exist** (every pair among five networks). **Jake needs four to link all five.** Each connection is a multi-stage questline built on a real cultural obstacle — these people have reasons for not trusting each other.

### 4.2 CONNECTION: Cultural Web ↔ Recovery Underground

**Obstacle:** Mama Jacinta considers Tito Diego's methods dangerous — every recovery raid risks Imperial attention on the communities she protects. Diego considers her patience a slow surrender.

**The Quest:** a specific artifact — one of Mama Jacinta's ceremonial pieces, taken in 1651, now in a private collection in Puerto de la Corona's Noble District. Diego can get it. Jacinta will not authorize a raid that endangers the barrio.

**The Resolution:** Jake finds a third route (purchase through Doña Carmen, or leverage, or a trade with the collector) proving both methods can serve the same end.

**Reward:** ×1.15 multiplier begins; Diego's network gains cultural legitimacy; Jacinta's gains reach.

### 4.3 CONNECTION: Cultural Web ↔ Mountain Paths

**Obstacle:** none, and that is the point. Mama Jacinta and Mbah Seruni are cultural sisters who have never met — separated by four hundred miles of Imperial water and no ship that would carry them.

**The Quest:** **Jake is the ship.** The questline is a voyage: bringing Mbah Seruni down from her caves, across the sea, to the Sacred Grove — and the preparation, protection, and cultural protocol that requires.

**Cross-reference:** npcs/shippable_specialists.md — Yara Sun-Walker, Jacinta's cultural sister and possible Seruni inheritor, is the natural bridge and should be central to this quest.

**The Scene:** two old women who have each spent a lifetime being the last of something, meeting. The design should keep it short and give it no music.

**Reward:** the game's warmest connection; both networks gain; the deepest sage content in both threads opens.

### 4.4 CONNECTION: Mountain Paths ↔ Patient Coast

**Obstacle:** distance and total mutual unfamiliarity. Ashenmoor and Fjordlund are opposite ends of the world and opposite ends of climate, and neither has any concept of the other's life.

**The Quest:** trade, of all things. A volcanic-glass-for-northern-steel exchange (Pak Setyo ↔ Halvor Eriksson, or Astrid Halvorsdotter) that begins as commerce and becomes correspondence.

**Cross-reference:** npcs/blacksmiths_and_armorers.md — the two legendary/master smith traditions meeting produces **a crafting unlock available nowhere else** (volcanic-forged northern steel: full design in castle/equipment docs).

**Reward:** the multiplier, plus a unique crafting tier that is itself proof of what connection produces.

### 4.5 CONNECTION: Patient Coast ↔ Deed-Judgers

**Obstacle:** the Fjordlunders regard settlers — any settlers — with the reserve of people who have watched newcomers before. Whitaker's people are new to the archipelago and Fjordlund measures in centuries.

**The Quest:** the Northern Front's shared threat. Both empires press both peoples. A joint operation — a rescue, a supply run, a defense — where each side sees the other work.

**Reward:** the Alliance's proof-of-concept that this is not a race war. Cross-reference Section 2.6.

### 4.6 CONNECTION: Recovery Underground ↔ Deed-Judgers

**Obstacle:** Whitaker is a lawman. Diego is a thief, by any legal definition Whitaker has ever enforced. The obstacle is Whitaker's own self-concept.

**The Quest:** the Master Ledger, or the Blackwood Landing contracts. Diego's network can take them. Whitaker has to decide whether stealing a document that owns four thousand people is theft.

**The Resolution:** Whitaker, who was at the treaty signing, is the man best positioned to answer that. Cross-reference krone_colonies.md Section 4.2.

**Reward:** the multiplier, and the Quiet Coast's legal-moral cover for the Alliance's methods.

### 4.7 The Remaining Connections

Cultural Web ↔ Patient Coast, Cultural Web ↔ Deed-Judgers, Recovery ↔ Mountain, Mountain ↔ Deed-Judgers, Recovery ↔ Patient Coast: all possible, all with their own obstacles (Full Vision). **Only four are needed.** Which four Jake chooses shapes the Alliance's character — a Recovery-heavy Alliance behaves differently from a Coast-heavy one.

### 4.8 The Fifth Connection Event

When the fifth network links, a scene: **the first gathering.** Representatives from all five, in one place — probably the Sacred Grove, possibly Bjornshallen, possibly Casa de los Antepasados. No speeches. Food, introductions, awkwardness, and the slow realization in the room that there are more of them than anyone knew.

**Cross-reference:** style_bible.md Pillar 1 — five registers in one room, and the translation happening in real time is the scene's whole texture.

**Mechanical result:** ×1.60. The Free Archipelago becomes achievable.

## 5. THE EXCLUSIVE REWARDS

### 5.1 Design Law

**Cross-reference:** colonial_war_overview.md Section 5.1

None of this is purchasable, findable without trust, or obtainable on the Empire path. A Liberation player and an Empire player should have almost no overlap in what they possess.

### 5.2 THE ANCIENT MAPS (Trust Tier 3+)

**What they are:** pre-colonial charts, held by elders across all five networks, drawn in **cultural map-languages** the empires never learned to read.

**The map-languages:**
- **Rexan:** plant-and-water logic — routes described by which trees grow where, which streams run when
- **Ashenmoor:** star-and-ash — night navigation and volcanic landmarks, cross-reference physics/sun_shadow_systems.md, sand_desert_physics.md
- **Fjordlund:** saga-directions — the sagas ARE the charts; the stories encode the coast (cross-reference npcs/tavern_keepers.md Section 4.2, storytelling nights)
- **Recovery:** not maps but inventories — where taken things went
- **Quiet Coast:** the settlers' own rough charts of what the treaties actually covered

**Jake must learn to read them.** A short teaching sequence per language, gated by trust, taught by a named NPC. Cross-reference npcs/luxury_merchants.md — Madame Theodora Wegelius the cartographer would give her career for one of these, and her daughter Astrid Wegelius (npcs/recurring_quest_givers.md Section 8.1) is the one person outside the networks who might be *allowed* to see one.

**What they lead to:** treasure sites, dungeons (regional_dungeon_catalog.md), the feared waters' safe routes, and secret sites (Section 5.4).

### 5.3 THE FEARED WATERS AND JUNGLES (Trust Tier 3+)

**The design:** certain sea regions and jungle depths are lethal to outsiders — currents that kill, reefs that aren't charted, jungle that swallows. Cross-reference physics/water_physics.md, physics/vegetation_physics.md (hidden paths, canopy routes).

**Without the Alliance:** these are hard walls. Ships founder. Expeditions do not return. The map has edges.

**With the Alliance:** a guide, a route, a cultural key. **The map's edges open.**

**What's out there:**
- Uncharted islands with dungeon complexes
- The drowned mead halls (Fjordlund, physics/water_physics.md underwater)
- The ash-buried city (Ashenmoor, physics/sand_desert_physics.md Section 8.7)
- The deep-canopy temple network (Rexa, physics/vegetation_physics.md Section 11.4)
- Pre-colonial sites the empires never found because they never asked anyone

**Design intent:** the single most tangible Liberation reward — **the world literally gets bigger.**

### 5.4 THE SECRET SITES AND SECRET BOSSES (Trust Tier 4)

**Full design:** colonial_war/secret_bosses.md

**Gated at Blood tier**, and behind the ancient maps and the opened waters. These are the archipelago's oldest things — sealed places the networks have protected, avoided, or guarded for centuries.

**The gating chain:** trust → map → route → key (often a recovered artifact, Section 2.3) → the site.

**Design note:** this is the deepest content in Phase 7 and it is *entirely* unreachable on the Empire path. An Empire-path player will finish the game not knowing these places exist. That is intentional and correct.

### 5.5 LIBERATION DISCOUNTS AND THE GRATITUDE ECONOMY

Freed settlements and liberated regions trade at gratitude prices. Cross-reference the Geschäft Tier: liberated-region merchants offer:
- Substantial discounts (30-50%)
- Cultural goods unavailable elsewhere
- Free healing and repairs
- **Crafting access** — Pak Setyo, Bu Wira, Tía Esperanza, Astrid Halvorsdotter all deepen their offerings at high network trust

**The long-game economics:** the Liberate player is poor in doubloons and rich in *not needing them.*

### 5.6 THE SAGES' DEPTHS

**Cross-reference:** all four sage documents

The deepest dialogue and quest tiers of Mama Jacinta, Mbah Seruni, Mormor Astrid, and (differently) Don Alejandro unlock through Alliance trust rather than time. Specifically:
- Mama Jacinta's second ceremony (npcs/cannabis_keeper_mama_jacinta.md Section 7)
- Mbah Seruni's Bunga quest and the Ibarra decision (npcs/desert_sage_mbah_seruni.md Section 8, imperial_colonies.md Section 5.9)
- Mormor Astrid's Ghost Reindeer conversation at its fullest (npcs/healer_mormor_astrid.md Section 7)
- Don Alejandro — the exception: **his depths open on the Empire path AND the Liberation path, differently.** He is the one figure both routes can reach, and what he says is not the same.

### 5.7 THE ALLIANCE BASTIONS

**Cross-reference:** castle_ownership.md

Liberated fortresses become **shared strongholds** — not Jake's property but joint holdings. Different from an owned castle:
- Alliance garrison (free, loyal, culturally rooted)
- Cannot be sold or converted to a trade post
- Provides regional LS defense (resists reconquest)
- Hosts network gatherings and cross-network crafting
- **And Jake is a guest with a room, not a lord with a keep**

## 6. THE LIBERATION EVENT BATTLES

### 6.1 The Trigger

**Cross-reference:** war_state_system.md Section 4.6 Path A

When a colony Falls (or Albion withdraws) with Regional LS ≥ 50, the Liberation Event fires: a second battle, fought immediately, in which the Alliance takes and holds what fell.

### 6.2 The Design Rules

**Rule 1 — Jake is not the commander.** The network's leadership directs. Jake receives objectives, not authority.

**Rule 2 — The Alliance fights.** These are not escort missions with allied set-dressing. Alliance fighters are competent, numerous at high strength, and win or lose the battle themselves. Jake's contribution is decisive but not sufficient.

**Rule 3 — Each battle is regionally distinctive.** No two Liberation Events play the same way, because no two networks fight the same way.

**Rule 4 — Losses are named.** People Jake knows die in these battles, and they go into the_named_dead.md. Cross-reference: this is where several of Phase 5's semi-named NPCs may end.

### 6.3 The Five Battle Characters

**PUERTO DE LA CORONA — "The Quarter Rises"** (imperial_colonies.md Section 3.11)  
Urban warfare in a city the barrio knows better than the garrison. Jake's guns speak from the harbor while the quarter comes up through streets that have been mapped in memory for two hundred years. Objectives: the cathedral bell, the archive vaults, and keeping Herrera's response from reaching the barrio's heart.

**NUEVA ESPERANZA — "The Arithmetic Resolves"** (imperial_colonies.md Section 4.11)  
An uprising, and Jake is the shield. The instant the villages rise, Fuerte Esperanza's guns turn toward the fields — and Jake's entire job is making sure those guns are dealt with before they fire. Timing is the whole battle. **Mistime it and the reprisal is a massacre Jake caused.**

**FUERTE SAN RAFAEL — "Thirty Years of Stillness"** (imperial_colonies.md Section 5.12)  
The quietest battle in the game. The mountain closes. Alliance fighters come through paths no Imperial map records. Very little combat, enormous weight. **Cruz fights, well, and loses, and the game does not pretend that is a triumph.** Mbah Seruni comes down from the caves and sings names at the gate.

**BLACKWOOD LANDING — "Void for Impossibility"** (krone_colonies.md Section 6.11)  
A heist that becomes a bonfire. The strongroom, the four thousand originals, the yard. The battle is short; the fire is long. Ashcroft's bailiffs fight for wages and stop when the wages stop making sense.

**THE NORTHERN FRONT — "Both Flags Down"** (imperial_colonies.md 7.12, krone_colonies.md 7.12)  
Two campaigns, one liberation. Cold, patient, naval-heavy, and the only Liberation Event that requires breaking two powers. Fjordlund's fighting is efficient and unhurried. Bjornshallen fills afterward.

### 6.4 The Aftermath Scene

Every Liberation Event ends with the same structural beat, executed differently per region: **the network's leadership addressing their own people, with Jake in the crowd rather than at the front.**

Cross-reference Principle 5. The camera does not find Jake. He is present. That is all he was ever supposed to be.

## 7. RETALIATION AGAINST THE ALLIANCE

### 7.1 The Empires Understand the Threat

**Cross-reference:** retaliation_system.md

As LS rises, both empires shift their retaliation from Jake to **the networks** — because they correctly identify the Alliance as the existential threat that Jake alone is not.

**Stage 3:** cell raids (network strength loss)  
**Stage 4:** hostages taken from network membership specifically (the Attachment Score algorithm applies, and network members Jake has worked with rank high)  
**Stage 5:** scorched earth targeting Alliance territory, sacred sites, and liberated regions

### 7.2 The Sacred Site Threat

The empires learn, eventually, that destroying sacred sites is the fastest way to break a network's will. Cross-reference imperial_colonies.md Section 5.2 (Jaya died defending a shrine — the Empire has done this before and knows it works).

**Defending sacred sites becomes a recurring high-stakes mission type at Stage 4-5.** Failing costs network strength, trust tier, and something that does not come back.

### 7.3 The Reconquest Risk

A liberated region is not safe. Cross-reference war_state_system.md Section 4.7 — persistence cuts both ways. An empire at Stage 5 can retake a liberated region if the Alliance's regional strength has been ground down.

**Design intent:** the Liberation path does not end at victory. It ends at holding.

## 8. THE HONEST DIFFICULTIES

### 8.1 What the Alliance Cannot Do

- **No navy.** Until Jake gives them one (a Full Vision late-game thread: captured Imperial hulls crewed by freed sailors)
- **No siege artillery.** Alliance battles are infiltration, uprising, and terrain — not bombardment
- **No treasury.** They cannot fund, bribe, or buy
- **No unified command.** Even at ×1.60, five networks coordinating is slower than one empire deciding

### 8.2 The Internal Disagreements (Full Vision)

The Alliance is not harmonious. Real tensions:
- **Diego's methods vs Jacinta's caution** (Section 4.2)
- **Fjordlund's patience vs Rexa's urgency** — Jarl Olaf will wait a decade; el barrio has waited two centuries and is done waiting
- **The Quiet Coast's settlers among indigenous networks** — Whitaker's people are, by ancestry, part of the taking, and some Alliance members say so
- **What happens after** — nobody agrees on what a free archipelago should look like, and at ×1.60 that argument becomes real

**Design note:** these tensions should be *present and unresolved.* The Alliance is a coalition, not a chorus.

### 8.3 The Question Jake Cannot Answer

At full connection, someone will ask Jake what he wants out of this. He has no good answer. He came for revenge (style_bible.md Pillar 5) and built a liberation movement as a side effect, and everyone in the room knows it.

**Full Vision:** a scene where a network leader — probably Don Eduardo Mendoza or Tito Diego — says this plainly and does not soften it, and the Alliance follows Jake anyway, and that is worse than if they didn't.

## 11. STYLE BIBLE COMPLIANCE

- ✅ **Pillar 1 (Staggered Register):** Five networks, five registers, and the fifth-connection gathering is the game's most concentrated register scene — Rexan warmth, Ashenmoor patience, Fjordlund spareness, underground precision, frontier plainness, all in one room, translating.
- ✅ **Pillar 2 (Lore Through Objects):** The ancient maps in cultural map-languages. The recovered artifacts that are also keys. The returned sacred items. The burned contracts. The Alliance's entire reward economy is objects that mean something.
- ✅ **Pillar 3 (Layered Death):** Liberation Event losses are named and permanent. Cruz's defeat. The sacred sites that fall at Stage 5. Mbah Seruni singing names at San Rafael's gate. Style D throughout — liberation is not celebrated as a clean win.
- ✅ **Pillar 4 (Layered Humor):** The Fjordlund/Ashenmoor smiths' baffled correspondence. The traders profiteering off both forts. The awkwardness of the first gathering. Warmth, never mockery of the people fighting.
- ✅ **Pillar 5 (Theme — Revenge):** The Alliance is the theme's answer, and it is uncomfortable. Jake came for vengeance and accidentally built something that outlives it. And someone will say so to his face, and follow him anyway. **What do you do with what was done to you? Apparently: this, by accident, while trying to do something else.**

## 13. CONCLUSION

The Third Power. Five networks that do not know each other exist: Mama Jacinta's two centuries of unbroken memory, Tito Diego's patient recovery of everything that was taken, Mbah Seruni's mountain that the empires cannot climb, Jarl Olaf's thousand-year refusal, and Sheriff Whitaker's settlers who signed something and watched it become something else. None of them can win alone. All of them can wait.

Jake is not their liberator. He is a ship that can cross four hundred miles of Imperial water to bring one old woman to meet another old woman who has spent her life being the last of something. He is guns in a harbor when a plantation rises. He is the reason five things become one thing.

And when the archipelago is free — if it is — the maps he was shown, the waters he was allowed into, the doors that opened for him, and the people who fought beside him will all have been given rather than taken. Which, in a story about an empire that takes and a company that acquires, is the only argument the game makes for the other way of living.
