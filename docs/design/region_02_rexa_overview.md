# Region 02 — Rexa & The Mire (Overview)

**Status:** Design Draft v1.0  
**Related:** GDD v1.2, Story Chapters 3-4  
**Estimated Playtime:** 15-20 hours (largest content region in the game)

---

## 1. Overview

Rexa is the second region of Dark Arisen and the most content-dense area in the entire game. It combines a sprawling tropical pirate-colonial city — a mix of Havana and Saint Denis — with the dangerous swamplands surrounding it. This is where Jake transitions from castaway survivor to active player in the pirate world. By the end of Region 2, Jake will have purchased his own ship and recruited his first crew, fundamentally changing how the game is played from this point forward.

**Region Scale:** ~30 km² playable area. City + 4 swamp zones.

---

## 2. The Antagonist of Rexa — Don Mateo Salazar "El Carnicero" (The Butcher)

Don Mateo Salazar was once the wealthiest sugarcane plantation supplier in the colonies. He didn't grow sugar — he supplied the labor. For thirty years he ran one of the most brutal slave-trading operations in the southern seas. His personal cruelty became legendary even among other slavers: he killed children publicly when they disobeyed, used dismemberment as discipline, and kept a private collection of trophies from those who tried to escape.

When Draven Voss began attacking imperial slave convoys, most slavers fled or hid. Salazar did the opposite — he sought Draven out and offered his services. Not out of belief in Draven's cause, but because he saw an opportunity to keep doing what he loved under a new flag. Draven, in one of the moral compromises that defines his fall, accepted. Salazar's network, his contacts, his ability to move "cargo" — they were too valuable to refuse. Draven told himself he could control the man.

He cannot. Salazar now runs Rexa as his personal kingdom, hidden behind the legitimate pirate operations of the Crimson Armada. He is everything Draven once swore to destroy. He is without honor, without restraint, without humanity. Where Draven kills with reluctance and reason, Salazar kills for entertainment. Where Ethan kills for ideology, Salazar kills because he enjoys watching things break.

**Visual:** Tall, gaunt, mid-50s. Pale skin that doesn't tan despite the tropics. Always wears a white linen suit stained with old blood he refuses to wash out. Carries a curved Toledo blade. A trimmed black beard. Cold dark eyes. Gold rings on every finger, taken from his victims.

**Voice:** Soft, polite, almost gentle. Never raises his voice. Speaks Spanish-accented English. The horror is in the contrast between his elegant words and his actions.

**Boss Fight Theme:** Salazar fights with a fencing style — precise, ornamental, cruel. He toys with Jake. He stops mid-fight to comment on technique. He laughs softly when Jake takes damage.

## 3. The 5 Commanders (Mini-Bosses)

Before reaching Salazar, Jake must hunt down his five Commanders across Rexa and the Mire. Each is a separate quest with its own location, story, and combat encounter. They can be killed in any order.

| # | Commander | Title | Location | Specialty |
|---|-----------|-------|----------|-----------|
| 1 | La Viuda Negra | "The Black Widow" | Rexa Cathedral District | Poison + dual daggers |
| 2 | Brother Cleaver | "The Butcher's Priest" | Slave Plantation in the Mire | Heavy two-handed cleaver, fanatic |
| 3 | Captain Rojas | "The Iron Captain" | Rexa Harbor | Pistols + naval saber, leads guards |
| 4 | The Twin Hooks | "Los Garfios" | Smuggler's Hideout in the Mire | Twin brothers, dual fight |
| 5 | El Médico | "The Surgeon" | Rexa Black Market | Scalpels + chemical weapons, sadist |

After all 5 Commanders are dead, Salazar's location is revealed and the final Rexa story mission unlocks.

## 5. The Three Treasures — 20 Gold Bars

Rexa introduces the gold-bar economy that ultimately lets Jake buy his first real ship and crew. There are three major treasures hidden across the region, each requiring a treasure map and significant exploration.

| Treasure | Map Source | Location | Gold Bars |
|----------|------------|----------|-----------|
| The Spanish Galleon Hoard | Found in Smuggler's Hideout | Sunken Ship in the Mire Coast | 7 bars |
| Salazar's Personal Vault | Slaughterhouse dungeon | Hidden behind El Carnicero estate | 8 bars |
| The Old Fort Reserve | Earned in Old Spanish Fort | Buried inside the fort walls | 5 bars |

**Total: 20 Gold Bars** — exactly enough to purchase Jake's first proper ship at the Rexa Harbor (15 bars) and hire his initial crew of 5 (1 bar each). See `region_02_rexa_economy.md` (to be created) for the full ship and crew system.

## 6. Story Beats — Chapter 3-4

### Chapter 3: The Smuggler's Web (Rexa City Focus)
1. Jake arrives in Rexa Harbor on La Esperanza
2. Introduction to the city — first time seeing real civilization since the prologue
3. Meets the Bounty Hunter contact in a harbor tavern (optional faction recruitment)
4. First gold-bar treasure tutorial via a side quest
5. Tracks rumors of his brother Ethan to the Crimson Armada — leads to Salazar's network
6. First commander hunt: Captain Rojas at the harbor
7. Discovers the slave operation behind the Crimson Armada — moral revelation

### Chapter 4: Iron and Salt (Mire Focus, Ship Acquisition)
1. The hunt expands into the Mire — dangerous swamps full of Salazar's operations
2. Farmer side quest unlocks the Riesen-Alligator boss arena
3. Remaining commanders are hunted across the Mire
4. Second and third gold-bar treasures discovered
5. Final dungeon: The Plantation Master's Estate
6. **Boss: Don Mateo Salazar "El Carnicero"** in his slaughterhouse arena
7. After Salazar's death, Jake purchases La Liberación at Rexa Harbor and hires his first 5 crew members
8. End of Chapter 4 → Open Sea opens up, transition toward Region 3 (Ashenmoor)
