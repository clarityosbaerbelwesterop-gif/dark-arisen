## 1. CHARACTER OVERVIEW

### 1.1 Quick Reference

**Name:** Astrid Halvorsdotter (Mormor = Grandmother — what everyone calls her)

**Age:** 73 (Chapter 4 introduction) → 76+ (Chapter 10)

**Location:** Small coastal village in Fjordlund, traditional turf-roof home

**Profession:** Traditional healer (Völva tradition), Hearth Keeper, Wisdom Keeper

**Cultural Background:** Pure Fjordlund, multi-generational coastal family

**Register:** Middle, with shifts to High during traditional ceremony

**Voice Acting Style:** Warm, weathered, authoritative — think Liv Ullmann meets Stellan Skarsgård's grandmother

### 1.2 Story Role

**Primary Function:**
- Cultural anchor for Fjordlund region
- Mira's emotional connection to home
- Traditional healer (cultural knowledge)
- Hearth Keeper (cultural treasure)
- Source of Ghost Reindeer wisdom

**Story Significance:**
- Mira's Brother Bjorn knew her
- Connects Phase 4 Ghost Reindeer to story
- Cultural representative of Fjordlund
- Counter to Imperial influence in north

### 1.3 First Impression

**When Jake First Sees Her:**

Small house, turf roof. Smoke from chimney. Through frosted window — warm light. She opens door before Jake knocks. Smells of bread baking, herbs drying, wood smoke.

She looks at him. Not Mira's first. Jake's first.

*"You're the captain. Mira's captain. Come in. The fire is warm. You look cold."*

She knew his name. Mira hadn't sent word.

## 9. VOICE BIBLE

### 9.1 Speech Patterns

**General:**
- Patient pace
- Fjordlund accent
- Specific cultural cadence
- Norse-influenced phrases
- Laughs often
- Sings during work

### 9.2 Sample Dialogue

**Standard Greeting:**

*"You're the captain. Mira's captain. Come in. The fire is warm. You look cold."*

**Tea Offering:**

*"Sit. Drink. There is time for everything. The world will wait."*

**About Mira:**

*"That girl. She thinks no one knows her. But I knew her grandmother. I knew her mother. I know her."*

[Pause]

*"She carries her brother like a stone. You help her carry it. That is good."*

**About Bjorn:**

*"Bjorn was kind. Always kind. Too kind for the sea, perhaps. But the sea calls who it calls. We do not choose."*

[Pause]

*"He left here whole. Whatever came after... was after."*

**About the Hearth:**

*"This fire has burned since my grandmother's grandmother. Through three wars. Through plagues. Through every hard winter. It has not gone out."*

[Pause, looking at flames]

*"It will not go out while I live."*

**About Erik:**

*"My Erik. Lost twenty-five years now. Long enough that the missing is quiet. Not gone. Quiet."*

**About Cold:**

*"Cold is not enemy. Cold is teacher. Outsiders fight the cold. We learn from it. Different relationship entirely."*

**About the Ghost Reindeer:**

*"I saw it. Once. As a child. My grandmother held my hand and said: 'Watch, Astrid. Some things you only see once.'"*

[Long pause]

*"It looked at me. And I knew — the world is bigger than we know. Older than we know. And we are smaller than we think. That was a good thing to learn young."*

**Wisdom Moments:**

*"The dead don't leave us. They change form. They become memories. Stories. Songs. They become what we make of them."*

*"You have lost much, Captain. I see it. Mira sees it. Loss is not weakness. Loss is proof you loved."*

**Singing (Old Norse-influenced traditional songs):**

[Specific cultural music — to be composed authentically]

**Final Goodbye (Chapter 10):**

*"Sail well, Captain. Come back when you can. The fire will be warm. There will be tea."*

[Pause]

*"And if I am not here when you return — the fire will still be warm. Tradition continues. That is the promise."*
