## 1. MERCHANT NETWORK PHILOSOPHY

### 1.1 The Five Principles

**Principle 1: Merchants Make the World Real**

Players encounter general merchants more than any other NPC type. Their authenticity defines the everyday feeling of Dark Arisen's world. A great game has great merchants.

**Principle 2: Cultural Authenticity Through Goods**

What merchants sell reveals culture. Imperial markets carry refined goods. Indigenous markets have traditional items. Pirate haven markets sell stolen treasures. Goods are cultural narrative.

**Principle 3: Family Businesses Have Stories**

Most merchants are generational. Family histories. Inherited shops. Cultural continuity. Multi-generational depth makes simple shopping meaningful.

**Principle 4: Merchants Hear Everything**

People talk to merchants. Merchants hear gossip, rumors, news. They are information networks. Skilled players use this. Merchants make excellent quest gateways.

**Principle 5: Daily Necessities, Cultural Variety**

Food, cloth, tools, supplies — universal needs. Cultural execution varies enormously. The same need (eating) creates wildly different experiences across regions.
