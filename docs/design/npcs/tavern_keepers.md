## 1. TAVERN NETWORK PHILOSOPHY

### 1.1 The Five Principles

**Principle 1: Taverns Are Where Stories Happen**

Quest hooks. Romantic encounters. Crew bonding. Information exchange. Fights. Reconciliations. Music. Memory. Taverns are not merely shops selling drinks — they are the social infrastructure where Dark Arisen's stories take place.

**Principle 2: Each Tavern Has Personality**

A pirate haven dive bar feels nothing like an Imperial gentleman's club. An indigenous communal gathering feels nothing like a Fjordlund mead hall. The keeper's personality, the regulars, the music, the food — each combines into specific atmosphere.

**Principle 3: The Keeper Is the Heart**

Behind every great tavern is a keeper who shapes its character. Some are gruff. Some are charming. Some are mysterious. Some are family-like. Players remember keepers more than they remember locations.

**Principle 4: Music Carries Culture**

Cross-reference physics/sound_physics.md, mechanics/ship_navigation.md. Sea shanties in pirate havens. Hymns in Imperial Christian taverns. Drum traditions in indigenous gatherings. Long ballads in Fjordlund halls. Music is cultural revelation.

**Principle 5: Regular Customers Make Regularity**

Every great tavern has its regulars. NPCs who appear in the same seats. Musicians who play their corner. Drunks who stumble. Lovers who linger. Regulars create lived-in feeling.
