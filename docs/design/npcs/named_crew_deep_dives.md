## 1. INTRODUCTION

These five characters are not just crew members — they are the emotional infrastructure of Dark Arisen. Through them, Jake learns leadership, friendship, loyalty, and love. Through them, the game's themes echo. Through them, players invest emotionally in the journey.

Each character is documented with the depth typically reserved for protagonists in lesser games. Because in Dark Arisen, they ARE protagonists of their own stories.

# 2. MIRA — THE NAVIGATOR

## 2.1 Visual Description

**Physical:**
- Age: 32 (at story start)
- Height: 175 cm
- Build: Lean, strong, weathered
- Hair: Long dark braid, salt-streaked premature gray
- Eyes: Pale gray-blue (Fjordlund heritage)
- Skin: Tanned from sun, scarred (faint scars across knuckles, deeper across left forearm)
- Distinguishing: Compass-rose tattoo on left shoulder (covered usually), small scar through right eyebrow

**Clothing:**
- Practical sailor's gear
- Layers for weather variation
- Specific Fjordlund touches (carved bone buttons from her mother)
- Functional over fashionable
- Signature: Worn leather coat that was her brother's

**Voice:**
- Rich alto
- Slight Fjordlund accent (subtle)
- Direct delivery
- Quiet most of time, commanding when needed
- Laughs rarely but warmly when she does

## 2.2 Backstory

**Born:** Northern Fjordlund coastal village, 1844 (game starts 1876)

**Family:**
- **Father (Olaf, deceased):** Master sailor, taught her navigation
- **Mother (Helga, alive):** Healer in village, distant relationship
- **Brother (Bjorn, missing):** Older brother, navigator, vanished at sea five years before game starts
- **No partner, no children**

**Life Path:**

**Childhood (4-12):**
- Raised on her father's ship
- Learning navigation alongside Bjorn
- Mother wanted them educated formally — denied
- Idyllic until father's death (storm, age 12)

**Teenage Years (12-18):**
- Mother insisted she stay home, learn healing
- Bjorn took to sea full-time
- Mira chafed, ran away age 16
- Joined merchant fleet
- Proved herself, advanced quickly

**Young Adult (18-27):**
- Master sailor by 22
- Captained smaller vessels by 25
- Reputation as steady and skilled
- Specifically known for clean honest dealings
- Rejected pirate offers multiple times

**The Loss (27):**
- Bjorn went missing
- Sailed with Halvard's expedition (Fjordlund boss connection!)
- Ship returned, Bjorn didn't
- Story unclear — abandoned? Killed? Alive somewhere?
- Mira spent two years searching
- Eventually had to accept

**The Wandering (27-32):**
- Took various sailing positions
- Couldn't settle
- Drinking phase (recovered)
- Met Jake during one of his early journeys
- Saw something in his quiet pain that mirrored her own
- Joined as First Mate

## 2.3 Personality

**Core Traits:**
- Steady (her defining quality)
- Loyal (deeply, specifically)
- Reserved (hard to know, worth knowing)
- Practical (cuts through nonsense)
- Quietly emotional (deep currents)

**Personality Vector (Big Five):**
- Openness: 6/10 (open to new experiences but values tradition)
- Conscientiousness: 9/10 (reliable, organized)
- Extraversion: 4/10 (introverted, prefers small groups)
- Agreeableness: 7/10 (cooperative but firm)
- Neuroticism: 5/10 (controlled emotional life)

**Strengths:**
- Best navigator Jake will ever meet
- Calm in crisis
- Deeply loyal
- Sees through deception
- Strong in storm both literal and metaphorical

**Weaknesses:**
- Difficulty asking for help
- Tendency toward isolation
- Carries grief without sharing
- Can be too direct (sometimes hurts)
- Trusts slowly

**Fears:**
- Losing more family (Jake becomes family)
- Failing those who depend on her
- Discovering her brother's actual fate (and not being able to bear it)
- Storms that take captains

**Hopes:**
- Finding her brother (or his fate)
- Building a life with people who matter
- Returning to Fjordlund someday with peace
- Being known truly by someone

## 2.4 Mira's Personal Questline

**The Quest of the Lost Brother**

**Stage 1: The Mention (Chapter 4-5)**
- Mira mentions Bjorn briefly
- Refuses to elaborate
- Player can press (gentle approach earns trust)
- First glimpse of grief

**Stage 2: The Photograph (Chapter 5-6)**
- Player can find/discover photograph in Mira's quarters
- Or Mira shows after sufficient trust
- Bjorn's face revealed
- Specific details (compass tattoo matching Mira's)

**Stage 3: The First Lead (Chapter 6)**
- In Fjordlund, Mira asks Jake's help
- Old sailor remembers something
- Vague description: "Saw a man like that. Year ago. South."
- Direction set

**Stage 4: The Search Begins (Chapter 6-7)**
- Multiple ports investigated
- Specific NPCs questioned
- Trail goes cold
- Mira's hope and despair visible

**Stage 5: The Crucial Lead (Chapter 7)**
- A specific man in Pale Isle dock
- Has a story to tell — for a price
- "Saw a Fjordlund man on a Crimson Armada vessel."
- Brother possibly captured, possibly turned
- Hard truth

**Stage 6: The Investigation Deepens (Chapter 8)**
- Crimson Armada records sought
- Imperial intelligence
- Jake's connections used
- Multiple paths

**Stage 7: The Revelation (Chapter 9)**

**Three Possible Outcomes Based on Player Investigation:**

**Outcome A: Bjorn is Dead**
- Killed three years ago
- By Crimson Armada (after refusing to join)
- Body in unmarked grave
- Mira finally knows
- Memorial possible
- Closure (painful but real)

**Outcome B: Bjorn is Alive (Captive)**
- Held by Crimson Armada
- Forced labor situation
- Rescue possible
- Combat encounter
- Difficult choice (some who held him also have families)
- Reunion possible

**Outcome C: Bjorn Has Joined Them (Tragic)**
- Survived by joining Crimson Armada
- Now hunts ships (possibly Jake's)
- Confrontation between Mira and Bjorn
- Brother now monster
- Mira must choose: kill, capture, or try to redeem
- Most heart-wrenching outcome

### Stage 8: Aftermath (Chapter 9-10)

**Per Outcome:**

**A (Dead):**
- Mira mourns properly finally
- Returns to Jake fundamentally changed
- Stronger ally, deeper friendship
- New permanent buff: "Closure" (Mira combat performance +25%)

**B (Rescued):**
- Bjorn joins crew temporarily
- Healing journey for siblings
- Bjorn may sail his own ship eventually
- Different but equally meaningful

**C (Lost to Darkness):**
- Mira's grief redoubled
- May or may not survive emotionally
- Jake's support critical
- Major character development
- Possible Mira death if poorly handled

# 3. BIG TOM — THE BLACKSMITH

## 3.1 Visual Description

**Physical:**
- Age: 47 (at story start)
- Height: 188 cm
- Build: Massive, broad-shouldered, muscle on muscle
- Hair: Bald, often head-scarfed when forging
- Beard: Full grey-brown, well-kept
- Eyes: Brown, crinkled with laugh lines
- Skin: Ruddy, scarred forearms from years at forge
- Distinguishing: Missing left pinky (forge accident decades ago), elaborate chest tattoo (Imperial military origin)

**Clothing:**
- Heavy leather apron always when working
- Linen shirts, sleeves rolled
- Heavy boots
- Specific necklace (small wedding ring on chain)
- Always smells faintly of forge and tobacco

**Voice:**
- Deep baritone
- Loud (he's hard of hearing — old forge damage)
- Hearty laugh
- Distinct Pale Isle imperial accent (fading after years away)
- Speaks rhythmically when working

## 3.2 Backstory

**Born:** Pale Isle imperial city, 1829

**Family:**
- **Father (deceased):** Imperial smith, taught Tom basics
- **Mother (deceased):** Tom's connection to softer side
- **Brother (Mark, deceased 30 years ago):** Subject of major personal quest
- **Wife (Sarah, deceased 25 years ago):** Childhood sweetheart
- **No surviving children** (one died in infancy)

**Life Path:**

**Childhood (5-15):**
- Apprenticed to father young
- Brother Mark his constant companion
- Tom always larger, protective of younger Mark
- Father strict but fair
- Imperial city childhood

**Imperial Military (15-25):**
- Conscripted at 15
- Served as military smith
- Saw combat (didn't enjoy it)
- Met Sarah during leave
- Married at 20
- Brother Mark also conscripted

**The Tragedy (25):**
- Mark killed in pointless skirmish
- Sarah died in childbirth same year (baby also died)
- Tom's faith and military loyalty shattered
- Discharged dishonorably (struck a superior officer)
- Wandering years began

**The Wandering (25-40):**
- Traveled extensively
- Worked as itinerant smith
- Drank too much for years
- Slowly recovered
- Met Father Salvio at lowest point (Salvio saved him)
- Friendship cemented

**Pirate Life (40-47):**
- Joined pirate crew (under different captain originally)
- That captain died, crew dispersed
- Met Jake at port, recognized something
- Joined as ship's smith
- Found purpose again

## 3.3 Personality

**Core Traits:**
- Loud but gentle
- Loyal as bedrock
- Hides depth behind humor
- Protective of those he loves
- Skilled beyond seeming

**Personality Vector:**
- Openness: 7/10 (curious, accepting)
- Conscientiousness: 8/10 (master craftsman)
- Extraversion: 9/10 (life of party)
- Agreeableness: 8/10 (warm, friendly)
- Neuroticism: 6/10 (carries grief, manages it through humor)

**Strengths:**
- Master smith (legendary level)
- Combat veteran
- Crew morale anchor
- Father Salvio's brother in spirit
- Loyalty unshakeable

**Weaknesses:**
- Drinks more than ideal
- Avoids deep emotional topics (with humor)
- Hard of hearing (combat impact)
- Sometimes too trusting
- Old wounds slow him

**Fears:**
- Losing more family (crew is family)
- His own grief overwhelming him
- Dying alone
- Failing those he protects

**Hopes:**
- Justice for Mark eventually
- Crew thriving
- Finding peace before death
- Being remembered well

## 3.4 Big Tom's Personal Questline

**The Quest for Mark's Justice**

**Stage 1: The Old Photograph (Chapter 4)**
- Tom's quarters has small portrait
- Player notices
- Tom: "My brother. Long story."
- Initial reluctance

**Stage 2: The Drinking Story (Chapter 5)**
- After major battle, Tom drinks heavily
- Tells Jake about Mark
- The skirmish that killed him
- The officer who ordered it
- "Captain Reinhardt. Still alive. Still imperial."

**Stage 3: The Decision (Chapter 6)**
- Tom asks Jake's help
- "I need to know. After 30 years. I need to know."
- Quest unlocked
- Multiple paths possible

**Stage 4: Tracking Reinhardt (Chapter 6-7)**
- Now retired imperial colonel
- Lives in Pale Isle
- Wealthy, respected
- Old man now (70s)
- Multiple research methods

**Stage 5: The Decision Crystallizes (Chapter 7)**
- Tom struggles with decision
- Talks with Father Salvio extensively
- Player conversations matter
- Three paths emerging

**Stage 6: Confrontation (Chapter 8)**

**Path A: Confrontation and Forgiveness**
- Tom confronts Reinhardt
- Old man already broken by guilt
- Has been seeking redemption
- Tom forgives publicly
- Mutual healing
- Reinhardt dies peacefully later
- Tom achieves real peace

**Path B: Confrontation and Justice**
- Tom confronts Reinhardt
- Demands accountability
- Reinhardt admits, won't apologize
- Tom strikes him (proportionate, not killing)
- Public exposure of past actions
- Imperial fall from grace
- Tom satisfied (mostly)

**Path C: Vengeance**
- Tom kills Reinhardt
- Murder, however justified
- Imperial response
- Tom hunted now
- Possible exile from Pale Isle
- Heavy moral weight
- Pyrrhic victory

**Path D: Walking Away**
- Tom decides peace better than justice
- Doesn't confront
- Forgiveness internal
- Most spiritually mature path
- Father Salvio's preferred outcome

### Stage 7: Aftermath (Chapter 8-9)

**Per Path:**

**Path A & B:**
- Tom transformed
- Lighter spirit
- Personal closure
- New permanent buff: "Released" (Tom combat +25%)

**Path C:**
- Tom haunted but determined
- Imperial pursuit
- New permanent debuff later (PTSD-adjacent)
- Different but real arc

**Path D:**
- Tom at peace
- Wisdom growth
- Father Salvio approves
- Different buff: "Wisdom" (whole crew morale +10%)

# 4. INES — THE ALCHEMIST

## 4.1 Visual Description

**Physical:**
- Age: 38 (at story start)
- Height: 168 cm
- Build: Wiry, careful movements
- Hair: Dark brown, often pinned up practically, beginning to gray at temples
- Eyes: Dark brown, intense
- Skin: Olive (Rexan/Imperial mixed heritage)
- Distinguishing: Acid scar on right hand (alchemy accident long ago), small dot tattoo on inner wrist (commemorating someone)

**Clothing:**
- Practical alchemist's gear
- Leather apron with multiple pockets
- Layered for protection
- Always carrying small flasks
- Specific brooch (her mother's, treasured)

**Voice:**
- Cool soprano
- Educated diction
- Slight Rexan accent emerging when emotional
- Precise, careful
- Rarely raises voice (when she does, EVERYONE listens)

## 4.2 Backstory

**Born:** Rexa coastal village, 1838

**Family:**
- **Father (Imperial, deceased):** Imperial trader, mother of mixed heritage
- **Mother (Carmen, deceased):** Rexan healer, taught Ines alchemy
- **Brother (Carlos, missing/uncertain):** Older, soldier
- **Daughter (Esperanza, missing — central to questline):** Born when Ines was 22, lost when Esperanza was 8

**Life Path:**

**Childhood (5-15):**
- Mother's apprentice in healing
- Spent days in jungles learning plants
- Mother's philosophy: medicine is sacred
- Father gone often (trade)
- Idyllic if simple

**Teen Years (15-22):**
- Mother died (illness mother could have cured but waited too long)
- Father blamed Ines somehow (grief mostly)
- Ines fled, became apprentice to Imperial alchemist
- Education advanced
- Met Esperanza's father (relationship brief)

**Young Mother (22-30):**
- Esperanza born (1860)
- Father not in picture
- Ines built small practice
- Small comfortable life
- Esperanza intelligent, beautiful, beloved

**The Loss (30):**
- Crimson Armada raid on village
- Esperanza taken (age 8)
- Ines wounded trying to defend
- Recovered slowly
- Searched for years (alone)
- Eventually had to take rest

**Joining Ben Crew (35):**
- Joined another pirate crew (looking for leads on daughter)
- That captain was cruel
- Ines secretly sabotaged, made the men sick
- Crew fell apart in mutiny
- Ines escaped, took knowledge with her

**Joining Jake (38):**
- Met Jake during port stop
- Jake's reputation reached her
- Specifically: Jake helped abducted persons in past
- Joined as Alchemist, secretly hoping he could help find Esperanza

## 4.3 Personality

**Core Traits:**
- Brilliant
- Controlled
- Watching always
- Caring deeply but selectively
- Carrying immense grief invisibly

**Personality Vector:**
- Openness: 9/10 (constantly learning)
- Conscientiousness: 9/10 (precise, reliable)
- Extraversion: 4/10 (selective, professional)
- Agreeableness: 6/10 (warm with chosen, distant otherwise)
- Neuroticism: 7/10 (anxiety carefully managed)

**Strengths:**

- Loyal once committed

**Weaknesses:**

**Fears:**

**Hopes:**
