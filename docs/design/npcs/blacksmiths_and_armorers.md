## 1. SMITH NETWORK PHILOSOPHY

### 1.1 The Five Principles

**Principle 1: Smithing Is Cultural**

Each region's smithing tradition reflects its culture. Fjordlund's cold steel reveals northern survival. Imperial forging reveals systematic refinement. Rexan smithing reveals indigenous artistry. Ashenmoor reveals volcanic mastery.

**Principle 2: The Forge Is Sacred**

In every culture, the forge has spiritual weight. Not just workshop — sacred space. Smiths inherit traditions. Apprentices earn knowledge. Forges burn for generations.

**Principle 3: Quality Has Hierarchy**

Village smith for village needs. Master smith for serious work. Legendary smith for legendary weapons. Players climb this hierarchy through reputation and resources.

**Principle 4: Big Tom Is Your Smith**

The crew has Big Tom. Other smiths are alternatives, complements, occasional rivals. Big Tom's relationship is foundation — others are extensions.

**Principle 5: Smiths Have Stories**

Every master smith carries history. Trauma forged into work. Joy hammered into beauty. Their lives shape their craft. Players discover this through patient relationship.

### 1.2 The Five Smith Tiers

**Tier 1 — Village Smiths:**

Multiple, interchangeable
- Basic repair, tools
- No specialty
- Low cost
- Common throughout regions

**Tier 2 — Town Smiths:**

Named NPCs, regional
- Quality work
- Some specialty
- Moderate cost

**Tier 3 — City Master Smiths:**

Distinct individuals
- Specialty work
- Reputation matters

**Tier 4 — Regional Masters:**

Cultural treasures
- Master-grade work
- Cultural significance
- High cost / hard to access
- Iconic characters

**Tier 5 — Legendary Smiths:**

Few, mythical
- Legendary weapons possible
- Cultural pinnacle
- Maximum cost / extreme requirements
