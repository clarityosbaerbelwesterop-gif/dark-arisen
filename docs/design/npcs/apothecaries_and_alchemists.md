## 1. APOTHECARY NETWORK PHILOSOPHY

### 1.1 The Five Principles

**Principle 1: Medicine Is Cultural**

Imperial science systematizes. Indigenous traditions integrate. Fjordlund herbalism preserves. Ashenmoor alchemy transforms. Each culture's medical tradition reveals its worldview.

**Principle 2: Healing Has Ethics**

Some apothecaries heal. Some poison. Some both. Cultural traditions have ethical frameworks. Imperial guilds have rules. Underground networks have codes. Ethics matter.

**Principle 3: Quality Has Hierarchy**

Village healer for village ailments. Master apothecary for serious wounds. Legendary healer for impossible cases. Players climb this hierarchy through reputation and need.

**Principle 4: Ines Is Your Crew Alchemist**

Cross-reference npcs/named_crew_deep_dives.md Section 4. The crew has Ines. Other apothecaries are alternatives, complements, occasional rivals. Ines's relationship is foundation.

**Principle 5: Plants Tell Stories**

Cross-reference fauna/plants_and_herbs.md. Every herb has cultural meaning. Where it grows. Who harvests it. How it's prepared. Apothecary work is cultural work.

### 1.2 The Five Apothecary Tiers

**Tier 1 — Village Healers:**

Multiple, basic
- Common ailments
- No specialty
- Low cost
- Common throughout

**Tier 2 — Town Apothecaries:**

**Tier 5 — Legendary Healers:**
