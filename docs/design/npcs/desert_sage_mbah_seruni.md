## 1. CHARACTER OVERVIEW

### 1.1 Quick Reference

**Name:** Mbah Seruni (Mbah = honored elder/grandmother, Seruni = chrysanthemum, traditional name)

**Age:** 81 (Chapter 4 introduction) → 84+ (Chapter 10) — possibly older

**Location:** Hidden cave-dwelling near active volcano in Ashenmoor

**Profession:** Volcanic Shaman, Last Keeper of Pre-Imperial Ashenmoor Traditions

**Cultural Background:** Indigenous Ashenmoor (specific volcanic mountain people)

**Register:** Middle, with shifts to High during volcanic ceremony

**Voice Acting Style:** Deep, weathered, hypnotic — think Anjelica Huston meets traditional Indonesian wayang storytellers

### 1.2 Story Role

**Primary Function:**
- Volcanic wisdom keeper
- Ashen Wyrm survivor (witness to history)
- Cultural representative for Ashenmoor
- Counter-balance to other regional sages
- Critical to Wyrm boss encounter context

**Story Significance:**
- Saw the Ashen Wyrm as a child (60+ years ago)
- Knows the true story of the Wyrm
- Her reaction to Jake's Wyrm choice = game-defining
- Last of her tradition

### 1.3 First Impression

**When Jake First Sees Her:**

A figure sitting cross-legged at a cave entrance. Volcanic light flickering behind. Sand drifts past. She doesn't move when Jake approaches. The heat alone would kill most people. She sits comfortably.

When she finally speaks, her voice carries despite no apparent effort:

*"You walked far, Captain. The mountain knew you were coming. So did I. Sit. The stones are warm. The conversation will be longer than you think."*

She didn't need to be told who he was.

## 9. VOICE BIBLE

### 9.1 Speech Patterns

**General:**
- Patient pace, almost meditative
- Indonesian/Filipino-influenced cadence
- Specific cultural phrases
- Pauses for thought
- Sings during ceremony
- Whispers carry

### 9.2 Sample Dialogue

**Standard Greeting:**

*"You walked far, Captain. The mountain knew you were coming. So did I. Sit. The stones are warm. The conversation will be longer than you think."*

**About the Volcano:**

*"This mountain has been here longer than memory. It will be here when memory ends. We are temporary. The mountain teaches this if we listen."*

[Long pause]

*"Most people do not listen."*

**About the Ashen Wyrm:**

*"I saw it once. As a child. My grandmother brought me here, to this place, and we waited three days. On the third day, it came."*

[Very long pause]

*"It looked at me, Captain. And in its eyes, I saw what no child should see — and what every child should see. The world is older than us. Wiser than us. We share it briefly. Then we leave."*

[Pause]

*"That is what the Wyrm taught me."*

**About Imperial Loss:**

*"They came with their fire and their books. They said our gods were demons. Our medicines were witchcraft. Our songs were noise."*

[Pause]

*"My son died trying to protect them. He was twenty-two. Imperial soldiers cut him down for refusing to leave his shrine."*

[Long pause]

*"His name was Jaya. It means 'victory.' I gave him that name. Imperial soldiers took it."*

**About Bunga:**

*"My daughter lives in your Imperial city now. She wears their clothes. Speaks their language. She has not spoken to me in fifteen years."*

[Pause]

*"I hope she is happy. I hope she misses me. Both can be true."*

**During Ceremony:**

*"The ash marks the face. The face marks the soul. The soul marks the path. The path marks the choice."*

*"What you choose here, mountain witnesses. Mountain remembers. Mountain decides."*

**About Cold (Compared to Other Sages):**

*"Mormor Astrid in the north — she fights the cold by accepting it. Mama Jacinta in the jungle — she dances with it. Don Alejandro on his veranda — he cultivates it like garden."*

[Pause]

*"I sit with fire. We share heat. We share silence. We share knowing."*

**Wisdom Moments:**

*"The volcano does not erupt because it is angry. It erupts because it is full. Like grief. Like joy. Like memory."*

*"You cannot kill what has always been. You can only delay your own ending."*

*"Some things should be left to sleep. Some things should be allowed to wake. The wisdom is knowing which is which. I do not always know."*

**Specific Cultural Phrases:**

*"Hati-hati..."* (be careful — said with affection)

*"Anak ku..."* (my child — for those she cares for)

*"Gunung mengingat..."* (the mountain remembers)

[Indonesian/Filipino-influenced phrases — culturally authentic, subtitled]

**About the Wyrm Choice:**

*"You will face the Wyrm soon, Captain. I know this. You will choose. I know this too."*

[Long pause]

*"What you choose will tell me who you are. What you become will tell you who you are."*

[Pause]

*"Choose carefully. The mountain is listening."*

**Final Goodbye (Chapter 10):**

*"Sail well, Captain. The mountain has spoken to me about you. It is satisfied — or it is troubled. I will not tell you which."*

[Pause]

*"Some things you must learn for yourself. The mountain is patient. So am I."*

[Smile, rare]

*"Go. The sea waits. So does whatever you became here."*
