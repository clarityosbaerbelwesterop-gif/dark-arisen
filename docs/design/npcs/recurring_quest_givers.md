## 1. RECURRING QUEST GIVER PHILOSOPHY

### 1.1 The Five Principles

**Principle 1: NPCs Have Lives**

Cross-reference npcs/npc_recurrence_system.md. Recurring quest givers don't exist only when Jake interacts with them. They live their lives. Their families grow. Their businesses succeed or fail. Their bodies age. When Jake returns, things have changed.

**Principle 2: Quests Are Relationships**

A recurring quest giver's third quest hits differently than their first. Trust has built. History exists. Players don't just complete tasks — they invest in ongoing relationships.

**Principle 3: Cultural Distribution**

Each region has recurring quest givers reflecting cultural diversity. Imperial bureaucrats. Indigenous community leaders. Pirate captains. Fjordlund elders. Ashenmoor traditionalists. Cultural representation matters.

**Principle 4: Quest Variety**

Some recurring quests are simple (deliver this). Some are complex (multi-stage investigation). Some are personal (help my daughter). Some are political (investigate Imperial corruption). Variety keeps engagement.

**Principle 5: Reputation Threading**

How Jake handles one quest affects how other NPCs respond. Word travels. Trust builds or breaks. The web of recurring relationships creates emergent narrative.
