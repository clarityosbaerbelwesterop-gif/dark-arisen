# Region 02 — The Mire (Swamplands)

**Status:** Design Draft v1.0  
**Related:** region_02_rexa_overview.md, region_02_rexa_city.md  
**Inspirations:** RDR2 Bayou Nwa, Far Cry 3 swamps, Princess Mononoke spirit forest

---

## 1. Overview

The Mire is the swampland that surrounds Rexa to the north and west. Where Rexa is civilization rotting from within, the Mire is nature reclaiming what man stole. Spanish plantations lie half-swallowed by water and vines. Slave operations hide in the deep bog. Voodoo practitioners worship things older than the colonies. And in the deepest, blackest waters, the Riesen-Alligator waits — a creature locals call "El Padre del Pantano" (The Father of the Swamp).

**Region Scale:** ~12 km² of swampland in 4 zones  
**Traversal:** Horse (shallows), boat (deep water), foot (paths), swimming (dangerous)

## 2. The Four Mire Zones

### 2.1 The Drowned Plantation Zone
- **Biome:** Half-flooded sugarcane fields, ruined plantation house, slave quarters
- **Mood:** Haunting, melancholy, the past refusing to die
- **Key Locations:** The Drowned Plantation (Dungeon #1), Hanged Man's Tree, Slave Cemetery
- **Enemies:** Salazar's overseers, plantation ghouls, swamp dogs
- **Story Role:** First Mire dungeon. Reveals Salazar's slave operation.

### 2.2 The Deep Bog
- **Biome:** Black water, twisted cypress, knee-deep mud, hanging moss
- **Mood:** Oppressive, primal, dangerous in every direction
- **Key Locations:** The Crocodile Pits (Dungeon #8), Voodoo Hut (Dungeon #6, hidden), Witch's Stones
- **Enemies:** Mud Lurkers, Bog Spiders, Voodoo Cultists, normal alligators
- **Story Role:** Path to the legendary alligator, voodoo lore

### 2.3 The Smuggler's Reach
- **Biome:** River delta, hidden coves, false-bottom boats, mangrove tunnels
- **Mood:** Tense, sneaky, eyes on you
- **Key Locations:** The Smuggler's Hideout (Dungeon #3), The Old Spanish Fort (Dungeon #7), Hidden Docks
- **Enemies:** Smugglers, Twin Hooks (Commander #4), fort guards
- **Story Role:** Two more Commanders, two Gold Bars, weapon recipes

### 2.4 The Plantation Master's Domain
- **Biome:** Salazar's hidden estate, manicured gardens hiding mass graves, private dungeons
- **Mood:** Unsettling — beautiful surface, horror underneath
- **Key Locations:** The Plantation Master's Estate (Dungeon #10), Private Slaughterhouse, Hidden Vault
- **Enemies:** Salazar's elite guards, hunting dogs, Brother Cleaver (Commander #2)
- **Story Role:** Final Mire dungeon, Treasure Map #3, path to Salazar boss arena

## 3. The Two Mire Commanders (Detailed)

### 3.1 Brother Cleaver — "The Butcher's Priest"
- **Real Name:** Padre Joaquín Soler
- **Cover:** Plantation chaplain who "blessed" the slaves before they were sold
- **Style:** Massive two-handed cleaver, wears blood-stained priest robes, fanatic believer
- **Backstory:** Genuinely believes his work is God's will. The most disturbing kind of evil — sincere. Quotes scripture during the fight.
- **Boss Arena:** Drowned Plantation chapel, half-flooded, hanging chains
- **Phase 1:** Slow, devastating cleaver swings. Telegraphed but lethal.
- **Phase 2:** Drinks "holy wine" (blood), becomes faster and immune to stagger for 30 seconds
- **Reward:** The Cleaver's Cleaver (legendary two-handed), Recipe: Holy Water (anti-undead bombs)

### 3.2 The Twin Hooks — "Los Garfios"
- **Real Names:** Mateo and Mateo Reyes (twins, same name, longstanding family joke)
- **Cover:** Smuggler captains running goods for Salazar
- **Style:** Each wields a hook-sword, extremely synchronized, attack from opposite sides
- **Backstory:** Born conjoined, separated as children, the surgery was botched and they share constant phantom pain. They blame the world.
- **Boss Arena:** Smuggler's Hideout main hall, multiple levels with rope bridges
- **Phase 1:** Both attack together. Killing one enrages the other.
- **Phase 2 (one twin dead):** Surviving twin enters berserk grief mode, faster and reckless
- **Reward:** Twin Hooks (dual-wield daggers), 1 Gold Bar

## 4. The Farmer Side Quest (Unlocks Riesen-Alligator)

### Quest: "El Padre del Pantano"
**Quest Giver:** Old Tomás, a farmer at the edge of the Mire  
**Trigger:** Found at his farm during open exploration in Chapter 4

**Step 1 — The Missing Cattle:**  
Tomás's cattle have been disappearing. He blames "El Padre" — the legendary alligator. He shows Jake claw marks on a fence post: too big for a normal gator. Jake is asked to investigate.

**Step 2 — The Tracking:**  
Jake must follow blood trails through three locations in the Deep Bog. At the third location, he finds half a horse — Tomás's prize draft horse, killed by something massive.

**Step 3 — The Bait Trap:**  
Tomás suggests using his last surviving cow as bait at the old fishing dock at midnight. Jake must protect the cow while waiting. Three normal alligators attack first. Then nothing. False alarm? No — at dawn, the cow is gone, snatched silently. The Riesen-Alligator is real, and it's smart.

**Step 4 — Tomás's Pferd (The Horse):**  
Tomás reveals he has one last asset — a wild stallion he caught years ago but never broke. He gives it to Jake as payment for hunting El Padre. **This is where Jake gets his first horse in the Mire region**, fully introducing the horse system mechanically and emotionally. The horse is named "Sombra" (Shadow).

**Step 5 — The Hunt:**  
With Sombra, Jake can now traverse the deep Mire fast enough to reach El Padre's lair. The arena unlocks: a massive flooded clearing with a sunken ship in the center, surrounded by black water.

**Step 6 — Boss Fight: Jaw of the Mire (Riesen-Alligator)**  
See section 5 below.

**Step 7 — The Reward:**  
Tomás keeps Sombra with Jake (the horse stays bonded), pays Jake 200 Doubloons, and tells him a secret: the Voodoo Hut deep in the bog has something Jake will need against Salazar. This unlocks the hidden Voodoo Hut dungeon (Dungeon #6).

## 5. Boss: Jaw of the Mire (Mythical Riesen-Alligator)

- **Size:** 12 meters long, the size of a small whale
- **Appearance:** Ancient, scarred, one milky eye, moss growing on his back, barnacles on his hide. Older than the colonies.
- **Arena:** Flooded clearing, sunken ship in center provides high ground, water on all sides
- **Phase 1 (100-60% HP):**  
  - Lurks underwater, surfaces with massive bite attacks
  - Tail sweep when Jake is on the sunken ship
  - Drag-grab if Jake stays in the water too long
- **Phase 2 (60-30% HP):**  
  - Becomes enraged, breaches the water like a crocodile-whale hybrid
  - Can climb onto the sunken ship briefly, forcing Jake off
  - Death roll grab — instant kill if not parried
- **Phase 3 (30-0% HP):**  
  - Takes the fight onto land, slower but more aggressive
  - Roars summon 2 normal alligators as adds
  - Final desperate lunge attack
- **Reward:** Primeval Scale (crafts Swamp Amulet), Riesen-Alligator Tooth (legendary dagger), 100 Doubloons

## 6. The Legendary Alligator (Hidden Mythical)

Beyond Jaw of the Mire, in the deepest part of the Crocodile Pits, lives an even older creature. White-scaled, blind in both eyes, navigating by vibration alone. The locals don't even have a name for him — they just call him "El Antiguo" (The Ancient One).

**Unlock Requirement:** Complete the Voodoo Hut dungeon and obtain the Spirit Amulet  
**Arena:** Underground cave system with bioluminescent water  
**Difficulty:** Highest in Region 2 — comparable to mid-game story bosses  
**Reward:** Recipe for the legendary armor "Scales of the Ancient One" (best light armor in early game)
