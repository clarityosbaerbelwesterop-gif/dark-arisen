# Region 01 — Moran (Demo Region)

**Status:** Design Draft v1.0  
**Related:** GDD v1.2 §4.2, Story Chapters 1-2  
**Estimated Playtime:** 6-8 hours first playthrough

---

## 1. Overview

Moran is the starting region of Dark Arisen and the entirety of the free demo. It introduces every core system: combat, exploration, survival, crafting, treasure hunting, and the first taste of pirate intrigue. The region transitions from idyllic Maldives-like coastline through dense tropical jungle to a hidden paradise lake, ending with Jake boarding a Spanish galleon that takes him to Region 2 (Rexa).

**Region Scale:** Approximately 100 km of explorable coastline, full island ~25 km² playable area.

---

## 2. Zone Breakdown

### Zone A: The Shattered Coast (Tutorial Zone)
- **Biome:** White sand beaches, turquoise shallows, coral reefs, shipwreck debris
- **Size:** ~15 km of coastline
- **Purpose:** Tutorial for movement, basic combat, survival
- **Key NPCs:** Mira (first ally), washed-up castaways
- **Enemies:** Coconut Crabs, Reef Sharks, Scavenger Pirates (groups of 2-3)
- **Dungeon:** None (open exploration)

### Zone B: The Mangrove Shallows
- **Biome:** Dense mangrove forest, knee-deep water, bioluminescent pools
- **Size:** ~5 km²
- **Key NPCs:** Koa (hidden trading post)
- **Enemies:** Mud Lurkers, Mangrove Spiders, Lost Sailors
- **Dungeon 1:** **The Sunken Chapel** (mini-dungeon, optional)

### Zone C: The Deep Jungle
- **Biome:** Triple-canopy rainforest, ancient ruins, river systems
- **Size:** ~8 km²
- **Key NPCs:** Old Bones (mentor)
- **Enemies:** Jungle Stalkers, Vine Constrictors, Temple Guardians, Poison Dart Tribes
- **Dungeon 2:** **The Overgrown Temple** (main story dungeon)

### Zone D: The Paradise Lake (NEW)
- **Biome:** Hidden inland lake, waterfalls, lush meadows, ancient ruins on islets
- **Size:** ~4 km²
- **Mood:** Eerily peaceful — too peaceful. Sacred ground.
- **Dungeon 3:** **The Drowned Library** (underwater ruins beneath the lake)
- **Hidden Boss:** The Lake Guardian (optional mythical boss)

### Zone E: The Pirate Encampment
- **Biome:** Cliffside fortified camp on the western coast
- **Purpose:** First treasure map discovery, introduces gold/treasure mechanic
- **Dungeon 4:** **The Smuggler's Caves** (caves below the camp)
- **Mini-Boss:** Captain "Three-Finger" Vance

### Zone F: The Galleon Cove
- **Biome:** Eastern bay where the Spanish galleon "La Esperanza" is anchored
- **Purpose:** End of Moran — Jake boards the galleon, transitions to Rexa
- **Dungeon 5:** **The Galleon's Hold** (final Moran dungeon — Jake fights through to earn passage)
- **Boss:** The Jungle Warden (final Moran boss — fought BEFORE reaching the galleon, blocks the path)

## 4. Survival & Crafting Systems (NEW)

### 4.1 Material Gathering
| Material | Source | Use |
|----------|--------|-----|
| Plant Fiber | Bushes, vines | Bandages, rope, bowstrings |
| Hardwood | Trees (chop with machete) | Weapon handles, arrows, repairs |
| Coconut | Palm trees | Food, water, crafting |
| Iron Scrap | Shipwrecks, enemies | Weapon upgrades |
| Tobacco Leaf | Wild plants | Cigars (Rache fuel, locked until Ch.9) |
| Mangrove Sap | Mangrove trees | Tinctures |
| Boar Tusk | Wild boars | Tinctures |
| Reef Coral | Tide pools | Decorative + crafting |

### 4.2 Hunting & Food
- **Wild Boar:** Forest interior. Aggressive. Drops meat, tusks, hide.
- **Tropical Birds:** Canopy. Drops feathers, small meat.
- **Reef Fish:** Shallows. Spear-fishable. Drops fish meat.
- **Iguana:** Rocks and beach. Easy kill. Drops scaled hide.

Cooking at campfires: raw meat → cooked meat (heals 50 HP over 30 sec, prevents food poisoning).

### 4.3 Weapon Crafting (Demo Tier)
At Koa's Trading Post:
- **Repair Broken Cutlass:** 2x Iron Scrap + 1x Hardwood
- **Craft Reinforced Cutlass:** 5x Iron Scrap + 2x Hardwood + 100 Doubloons
- **Craft Hunting Bow:** 3x Hardwood + 5x Plant Fiber
- **Craft Arrows (x10):** 1x Hardwood + 2x Plant Fiber

## 5. The Treasure System (Tutorial)

Moran introduces the treasure mechanic that scales across the entire game:

1. **Discovery:** In Zone E (Pirate Encampment), Jake finds his first Treasure Map after defeating Captain Three-Finger Vance
2. **Map Reading:** Map shows landmarks (a twin palm, a rock shaped like a turtle, a waterfall)
3. **Hunting:** Player must navigate to the location WITHOUT a marker (BotW-style organic discovery)
4. **Reward:** First treasure chest contains 500 Doubloons (introduces gold currency mechanic)
5. **Repeat:** 3 more treasure maps hidden across Moran for completionists

**Why this matters:** This is the player's first taste of the treasure-hunting loop that becomes central in later regions when crew members hand Jake maps (see GDD §20.4).

## 6. Story Beats

### Chapter 1: Castaway (Zones A-B)
1. Jake washes ashore at Driftwood Beach
2. Tutorial: movement, picking up items, basic interaction
3. First combat: a Coconut Crab (forced encounter)
4. Meets Mira, who tends his wounds at her cove
5. Mira teaches him about the island, points him toward Koa
6. First Scavenger Pirate encounter (real combat tutorial)
7. Reaches Koa's Trading Post → unlocks crafting and weapon repair

### Chapter 2: Into the Green (Zones C-F)
1. Old Bones finds Jake in the jungle, offers training
2. Optional: Sunken Chapel mini-dungeon
3. The Overgrown Temple — main story dungeon
4. Discovery of the Paradise Lake (optional Drowned Library)
5. Discovery of the Pirate Encampment, defeat Captain Vance, get first Treasure Map
6. Reach the Galleon Cove — the path is blocked by the Jungle Warden
7. **Boss: The Jungle Warden** (3-phase fight, see boss spec)
8. Board "La Esperanza" — Jake meets the Spanish captain who agrees to take him to Rexa
9. **End of Demo / Transition to Region 2**
