# THE LIGHT ELVES

**Status:** Design Draft v1.0
**Phase:** Phase 8 — Highmoore (Movement Three — Document 22 of ~30)
**Related:** highmoore_overview.md, the_wizard.md, wizard_questline.md, light_elf_boss.md, house_system.md, highmoore_region.md, highmoore_settlements.md, physics/sun_shadow_systems.md, physics/wind_systems.md, physics/weather_physics.md, bosses/ashen_wyrm.md, npcs/regional_populations.md, npcs/children_and_families.md, colonial_war/dungeon_design_philosophy.md, style_bible.md
**Purpose:** What the Light Elves are, what they do, the inconsistent descriptions, Highmoore's practices about them, why the practices are failing, the takings, and the design law that none of it is ever resolved.
**Inspirations:** Celtic and Norse álfar traditions (the genuinely unsettling versions, before Victorian prettification), Picnic at Hanging Rock (a disappearance that is not explained), Annihilation, The Willows by Algernon Blackwood, changeling folklore, real folk practices observed by people who deny believing in them

## 1. THE DESIGN LAW

### 1.1 Never Explained

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_overview.md Section 4, bosses/ashen_wyrm.md, crystal_guardian.md Section 8.

**The third refusal in Dark Arisen**, after the Ashen Wyrm and the Crystal Guardian.

No document in the game says what they are, where they come from, what they want, or whether *they* is even the right word. Cross-reference Section 3.3 — **the plural is a Highmoore convention and may be wrong.**

### 1.2 Not Fantasy Elves

**Design law:** no pointed ears, no bows, no forest kingdom, no language, no culture, no nobility, and **no dialogue, ever.**

The name is Highmoore's. Cross-reference Section 3.1 — it is what four hundred years of people have called a thing they cannot describe, and cross-reference the_wizard.md Section 5.4: *"there is nothing in any of us that the shape fits into."*

### 1.3 The Register

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_overview.md Section 4.4.

Nobody in Highmoore says *"magic."* They say *"the light,"* *"after the light,"* *"my grandmother said,"* and *"we don't go up there."*

## 2. WHAT THEY DO

### 2.1 They Take

**Cross-reference:** Section 6, wizard_questline.md Section 4.1.

People go and do not come back. Occasionally people go and come back.

**They do not kill.** Nobody in four hundred years of Highmoore record has found a body. Cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md Section 5.2 — the abbey has the register and it is very clear about this.

### 2.2 The Rate

**Cross-reference:** Section 5, wizard_questline.md Section 4.1.

Historically: two or three a year across the whole basin. Cross-reference wizard_questline.md Section 3.1 — Lady Anwen Mere's *"my grandmother lost two or three a year to it."*

**Currently: roughly nine.** And cross-reference Section 5.1 — **nobody had counted.**

### 2.3 Who

**Cross-reference:** npcs/children_and_families.md.

Adults, overwhelmingly. Herders, travellers, and people alone on the High Moor.

**Design law:** cross-reference npcs/children_and_families.md — **no child in Highmoore is ever taken, in any record, in any account, in any variant, and the protection rules hold absolutely.** The abbey register is explicit and a player can read it.

## 3. THE DESCRIPTIONS

### 3.1 The Name

*Light elves.* Four hundred years old, Highmoore's own, and cross-reference Section 1.2 — **it is a name for a shape that will not fit anywhere else.**

### 3.2 What Everyone Agrees On

**The light.** Cross-reference physics/sun_shadow_systems.md, highmoore_region.md Section 3.2 — Highmoore's signature visual is a shaft breaking through cloud and crossing open moorland, and **every account in four hundred years describes it identically:**

- A shaft, in daylight, on open ground
- **It stops moving.** Cross-reference physics/wind_systems.md — cloud shadows cross the moor at wind speed and this one does not.
- It is warm. Everyone says warm.

### 3.3 What Nobody Agrees On

**The figure.**

Cross-reference the_wizard.md Section 5.4, wizard_questline.md Section 4.1 — **four witnesses in four villages give four descriptions that share nothing at all.**

*"Tall as the church door."*
*"Small. Like a child that isn't."*
*"There wasn't a shape. There was a — I don't have it."*
*"Same as my father. Exactly the same as my father, and he'd been ten years dead."*

**Design law:** cross-reference Section 1.1 — the game never reconciles these and never indicates which is right, and **the possibility that all four are correct is left open and never stated.**

### 3.4 The Sound

**Cross-reference:** physics/sound_physics.md.

Nothing. Every account, four hundred years, agrees: **in the shaft there is no wind noise.** Cross-reference physics/wind_systems.md — Highmoore is a wind region and the wind is permanent and audible everywhere.

**Design note:** cross-reference light_elf_boss.md Section 3.4 — this is a mechanic.

## 4. THE PRACTICES

**Cross-reference:** house_system.md Principle 1.3 — *the practices are older than the grievances.*

### 4.1 The Rules Everyone Observes

- **Do not stand in a stopped shaft.** The oldest and most absolute.
- **Do not go onto the High Moor alone between the harvest and the wet.** Cross-reference house_system.md Section 4.5 — the same window as the burning season, which is not a coincidence anybody has ever examined.
- **Charms over the door.** Iron, and a specific knot, and cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md Section 2 — **every fortified farm in the Folds has one and none of them are new.**
- **Do not answer.** Cross-reference Section 6.4.

### 4.2 The Denial

**Cross-reference:** wizard_questline.md Section 3.2.

**Nobody in Highmoore says they believe in it.**

Every lord, every yeoman, and every villager will describe the practices as *what my grandmother did*, *the old nonsense*, and *habit* — and will observe every one of them absolutely, without exception, for their entire lives.

**HIGHMOORE AMBIENT:**
*"...I don't hold with it. Course I don't. And I'll not be up on the tops after the harvest and neither will you, so let's not have the conversation."*

*"...the knot's my mother's. I'd not take it down. Not because of anything. Because it's hers."*

### 4.3 The Design Statement

**Cross-reference:** house_system.md Section 8, style_bible.md Pillar 2.

Highmoore has: a season, a settlement procedure, a hostage convention, and **a set of rules about light, observed universally by people who deny believing in them.**

**Cross-reference house_system.md Principle 1.1** — nobody remembers why the Houses fight either.

## 5. WHY IT IS GETTING WORSE

### 5.1 Nobody Counted

**Cross-reference:** wizard_questline.md Section 4.1, highmoore_settlements.md Section 5.2.

Every House keeps its own losses. Cross-reference house_system.md — there is no central authority, and the only place the numbers sit together is **an abbey nobody visits for that purpose.**

Ellis added them up nine years ago. Cross-reference the_wizard.md Section 4.1 — *"I have spent nine years telling lords about this and being given wine and shown the door."*

### 5.2 The Practices Are Failing

**Cross-reference:** wizard_questline.md Section 4.3.

The four rules used to work. Cross-reference Section 4.1 — people who observed them were not taken.

**Three of the nine this year were observing all four.**

### 5.3 Ellis's Argument

**ELLIS:** *"Everyone tells me people have got careless. They have not. I have sat with the families. Three of them did everything their grandmothers did and were taken anyway."*

**ELLIS:** *"Which means it is not the people who changed."*

### 5.4 What Changed

**Never stated.** Cross-reference Section 1.1, light_elf_boss.md Section 9 — **the boss does not answer this and the campaign's resolution does not answer this.**

**What the campaign achieves:** cross-reference light_elf_boss.md Section 9.2 — the rate returns to two or three a year.

**Not zero. It has never been zero.**

## 6. THE ONES WHO COME BACK

**Cross-reference:** wizard_questline.md Section 4.2.

### 6.1 The Rate

Roughly one in six. Four days, or eleven days, or once ninety.

### 6.2 What Is Wrong With Them

**Design law:** cross-reference npcs/children_and_families.md, style_bible.md Pillar 3 — **nothing visible, nothing dramatic, and nothing the game depicts.**

They are polite, employed, married, functional, and **they cannot describe where they were.** Not will not. Cannot.

**THE MAN (Stage Two):** *"I've had eleven years to make a sentence out of it and I have not got one."*

### 6.3 What They Say Instead

All of them, across four centuries of abbey record, say some version of one thing:

*"It was not unkind."*

**Cross-reference wizard_questline.md Section 4.2** — *"that is not the same as saying it was kind and I want you to hear the difference."*

### 6.4 The Fourth Rule

**Cross-reference:** Section 4.1 — *do not answer.*

Every returned person, when pressed, eventually says the same thing about the taking itself:

*"It asked me something."*

**And not one of them, in four hundred years, has ever been able to say what.**

## 7. THE PLACES

**Cross-reference:** wizard_questline.md Section 4.4, colonial_war/dungeon_design_philosophy.md.

### 7.1 The Three Sites

**THE STANDING STONES (High Moor).** Cross-reference the_lesser_houses.md Section 4.5 — House Brae keeps Highmoore's oldest records and cannot read most of them. **The stones are older than the records.**

**THE BARROW.** Cross-reference colonial_war/regional_dungeon_catalog.md for the Fjordlund equivalent — Highmoore has its own barrow set, and one of them is not like the others.

**THE BARE GROUND.** Cross-reference physics/vegetation_physics.md — an irregular patch of High Moor, roughly two hundred metres across, where nothing has grown in living memory and the soil is ordinary.

### 7.2 What They Have in Common

**Cross-reference:** physics/sun_shadow_systems.md.

**All three are places where a shaft would stop.** Cross-reference Section 3.2 — and a player who has learned to read Highmoore's light can find the third site without being told.

## 9. MVP CORE vs FULL VISION

### 9.1 MVP CORE

- The takings, the rate, and the fact that no body has ever been found
- The universal agreement about the light and total disagreement about the figure
- The four practices, universally observed and universally denied
- The three-of-nine were observing all four
- *"It was not unkind."*
- One site: the bare ground

### 9.2 FULL VISION

- Four witness descriptions that share nothing
- The abbey register, readable, four centuries deep
- The man who came back and his eleven years without a sentence
- *"It asked me something"* and four hundred years of nobody knowing what
- All three sites with the practices-as-puzzle-language

## 10. STYLE BIBLE COMPLIANCE

- ✅ **Pillar 1 (Staggered Register):** an entire region describing the same thing in three registers and agreeing only about the light. And *"I don't hold with it, course I don't, and I'll not be up on the tops after harvest."*
- ✅ **Pillar 2 (Lore Through Objects):** iron charms over every farm door in the Folds, none of them new, one of them somebody's mother's. Standing stones older than the records of a House that keeps records it cannot read.
- ✅ **Pillar 3 (Layered Death):** nobody dies. In four hundred years no body has ever been found, one in six comes back, and all of them say the same six words.
- ✅ **Pillar 4 (Layered Humor):** *"the knot's my mother's. I'd not take it down. Not because of anything. Because it's hers."* Highmoore's total lack of embarrassment about believing something it insists it does not believe.
- ✅ **Pillar 5 (Theme — Revenge):** there is nobody to take revenge on and nothing to avenge. **It is the only threat in Dark Arisen with no antagonist, no motive, and no body**, and it sits in the middle of the phase where Jake kills three hundred people over a woman who asked him not to.

---

## 11. CONCLUSION

The Light Elves. Four hundred years of a name for a shape that will not fit anywhere else. Two or three a year across the basin, no body ever found, one in six coming back after four days or eleven or once ninety — and every one of them, for four centuries, saying *it was not unkind*, and wanting it understood that this is not the same as saying it was kind.

Every account agrees about the light: a shaft, in daylight, on open ground, that stops moving when cloud shadows do not, and is warm, and inside which there is no wind noise in a country where the wind never stops. And no two accounts have ever agreed about the figure — tall as the church door, small like a child that isn't, no shape at all, and *exactly the same as my father, and he'd been ten years dead.*

Four rules, observed absolutely by every person in the basin, all of whom will tell you it is their grandmother's nonsense and none of whom will be on the tops after harvest. And this year, nine instead of three, and three of the nine were doing everything their grandmothers did.

*It asked me something.* Four hundred years, and not one of them has ever been able to say what.
