## 2. THE FOUR DECKS

**Cross-reference:** the established four-deck design.

### 2.1 WEATHER DECK

Wheel, capstan, boats, guns, the forecastle.

**Cross-reference crew_system.md Section 4.1** — *"nobody on the forecastle at dusk"* is a morale read, and cross-reference Section 6.2: **it is where hands go to be off duty in sight of everyone.**

### 2.2 UPPER DECK

The great cabin aft (Section 3). The galley. Officers' berths — **the specialists' eight.** Cross-reference crew_system.md Section 2.2.

Cross-reference physics/sound_physics.md — **the galley is the loudest interior on the ship and the great cabin is directly aft of it**, which is deliberate and which Jake never complains about.

### 2.3 MID DECK

**Where the hands live.** Hammocks, sea chests, the mess tables that come down for meals and go up between.

Cross-reference crew_system.md Section 6.2 — **an empty hammock nobody has claimed.**

Cross-reference npcs/named_crew_deep_dives.md — **Big Tom's forge is here**, aft, near the mainmast partners, and cross-reference physics/sound_physics.md: **you can hear it from every deck.**

### 2.4 HOLD

Cargo, water, powder, and **Ines's cockpit** — cross-reference crew_system.md Section 3: the surgeon works below the waterline because that is where the shot does not reach.

Cross-reference colonial_war/colony_system_core.md Section 3 — **and this is where transported people are berthed**, and cross-reference Section 7.2: **it changes.**

## 6. UPGRADES

**Cross-reference:** mechanics/ship_management.md, economy_core.md.

### 6.1 They Change the Room

**Design law:** cross-reference progression_overview.md Section 1.1 — **every ship upgrade is visible as a physical change**, not a stat line.

| Upgrade | What the player sees |
|---|---|
| Heavier guns | different carriages on the weather deck, and the deck sits lower |
| Copper sheathing | visible at the waterline in harbour, and **the ship is quieter** |
| Better pumps | the hold is drier and the pump noise changes |
| Extended berthing | **more hammocks**, cross-ref crew_system.md §2.3 |
| Surgeon's cockpit | Ines gets a proper space and says so |
| **Chart table** | cross-ref travel_system.md §4 — the great cabin gains a working chart surface |

### 6.2 What Cannot Be Upgraded

**The ship is not replaceable.** Cross-reference Section 1.2 — **there is no second ship, no fleet management, and no ship-selection screen.**

Cross-reference colonial_war/castle_sieges.md Section 3.2 — Jake can command allied vessels in an engagement. **He does not own them.**

## 9. STYLE BIBLE COMPLIANCE

- ✅ **Pillar 1:** four decks with four social registers — the great cabin, the officers' berths, the mid deck at night, and the hold full of people who did not choose to be there.
- ✅ **Pillar 2:** a shelf of black candles and itemised notices that becomes evidence. An empty hammock. Copper at the waterline that makes the ship quieter.
- ✅ **Pillar 3:** the hold at night in a calm is the quietest habitable place in the game and it is used once, for a poisoning.
- ✅ **Pillar 4:** the galley is the loudest room on the ship and the captain's cabin is directly behind it and he never mentions it.
- ✅ **Pillar 5:** the ship has to be genuinely good, because in Chapter 6 the game starts offering Jake somewhere else, and the whole ownership arc depends on that being a real choice.

## 10. CONCLUSION

La Liberación. Four decks, walkable keel to maintop, at sea and at anchor, with no loading screen and no menu — a galley that is the loudest room aboard with the captain's cabin directly behind it, a forge amidships you can hear from every deck, mess tables that come down twice a day and go up again, and a forecastle that fills at dusk.

A ship that rolls, so that every interior rolls and Jake walks differently below in a seaway. A hold beneath the waterline where the surgeon works because that is where shot does not reach, which at night in a calm is the quietest habitable space in the game and is used for exactly one thing.

Upgrades that are rooms rather than numbers — more hammocks, a drier hold, copper that makes her quieter, a chart table. No second ship, no fleet screen, no replacement.

And a hold that fills with people who did not choose to be there, whom Jake can go down and listen to.
