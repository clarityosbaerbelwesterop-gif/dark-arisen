# BATTLE CATALOG — THE NAMED ENGAGEMENTS

**Status:** Design Draft v1.0
**Phase:** Phase 9 — Progression, Economy & Armies (FINAL — Document 6 of 6)
**Related:** battle_system.md, army_system.md, economy_core.md, colonial_war/castle_sieges.md, colonial_war/indigenous_liberation_alliance.md, colonial_war/imperial_colonies.md, colonial_war/krone_colonies.md, colonial_war/retaliation_system.md, house_system.md, highmoore_settlements.md, highmoore_reconstruction.md, princess_quest_belos.md, npcs/the_named_dead.md, style_bible.md
**Purpose:** Every large engagement in Dark Arisen — the twenty-one named battles across both continents, their type, scale, terrain, stakes, the one that breaks all the rules, and the design law that none of them are mandatory.
**Inspirations:** Total War campaign battles as discrete authored encounters, Mount & Blade's Battle of Pendraic, the Anglo-Scottish border battles, Agincourt's terrain, the Haitian Revolution's plantation risings, and Kurosawa's insistence that a battle is a place before it is an event

## 1. THE CATALOG'S LAWS

### 1.1 None Are Mandatory

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_overview.md Pillar 3, colonial_war/war_state_system.md.

**Twenty-one named battles. Zero required to finish Dark Arisen.**

Cross-reference colonial_war/war_state_system.md Section 5 — the colonial war runs without Jake, and cross-reference Section 6: **regions resolve through drift, rival conquest, and Profitability collapse whether or not he ever draws a sword on land.**

### 1.2 Each Is a Place First

**Cross-reference:** battle_system.md Section 1.3, colonial_war/castle_catalog.md Principle 1.5.

**No generic battlefields.** Every entry below has terrain that decides it, and cross-reference Section 3.1: **the ford is the ford.**

### 1.3 Scale Discipline

**Cross-reference:** battle_system.md Section 1.3.

**Three battles reach 800+. All three are optional. Fourteen sit between 150 and 500.**

## 2. THE COLONIAL WAR — SIEGES AND ASSAULTS

**Cross-reference:** colonial_war/castle_sieges.md, imperial_colonies.md, krone_colonies.md.

### 2.1 THE FALL OF FUERTE ESPERANZA

**Type:** Rising · **Scale:** 120 garrison vs. an uprising of ~2,000 unarmed · **Terrain:** the inner yard and the firing steps

**Cross-reference:** imperial_colonies.md Section 4.11, colonial_war/castle_sieges.md Section 5.2.

**Not a battle. A timing puzzle.** Cross-reference colonial_war/castle_sieges.md Section 5.2 — the guns face the fields, and the instant the villages rise, Jake's entire job is silencing them before they fire.

**The objective is not the garrison.** Cross-reference colonial_war/colonial_war_bosses.md Section 13.2 — **it is Overseer-Captain Domingo Salas and the gun positions.**

**Failure:** cross-reference imperial_colonies.md Section 4.11 — *"mistime it and the reprisal is a massacre Jake caused."*

---

### 2.2 THE QUARTER RISES — PUERTO DE LA CORONA

**Type:** Rising · **Scale:** ~600 garrison vs. Alliance + barrio · **Terrain:** urban, vertical, and the undercity

**Cross-reference:** imperial_colonies.md Section 3.11, castle_catalog.md Section 3.3.

**The largest battle in Dark Arisen.** Cross-reference colonial_war/indigenous_liberation_alliance.md Section 6.3 — three-dimensional: streets the barrio has mapped in memory for two hundred years, the undercity beneath, and the bell tower above.

**Objectives:** the cathedral bell · the archive vaults · **keeping Herrera's response from reaching the barrio's heart.** Cross-reference colonial_war/colonial_war_bosses.md Section 4.

**Requires:** LS 50+ and cross-reference colonial_war/indigenous_liberation_alliance.md Section 3.2 — realistically the ×1.40 multiplier or better.

---

### 2.3 THE QUIET CLOSURE — FUERTE SAN RAFAEL

**Type:** Assault · **Scale:** 80 garrison vs. ~200 · **Terrain:** shade galleries, cistern complex

**Cross-reference:** imperial_colonies.md Section 5.12, castle_catalog.md Section 5.

**The smallest and heaviest.** Cross-reference colonial_war/indigenous_liberation_alliance.md Section 6.3 — *"very little combat, enormous weight."* The mountain closes; Alliance fighters arrive by paths no Imperial map records.

**Cross-reference physics/lava_heat_physics.md** — heat is a combatant and the cisterns are the only cool ground.

---

### 2.4 THE PURCHASE — CASTILLO DORADO

**Type:** Assault, or **none** · **Scale:** 200 garrison who have never fought · **Terrain:** reception wing, ballroom, gardens

**Cross-reference:** imperial_colonies.md Section 6.11, castle_sieges.md Section 5.2.

**The battle that can be bought.** Cross-reference colonial_war/castle_sieges.md Section 5.2 — bribe the gate rotation and there is no engagement at all.

**If fought:** cross-reference colonial_war/colonial_war_bosses.md Section 7.2 — Captain Aldana is the actual combat and de Silva runs between rooms making offers.

---

### 2.5 THE STARVING — PUESTO DEL NORTE

**Type:** Defence *(Jake attacking a fort that will not surrender)* · **Scale:** 90, eleven years tired · **Terrain:** wall walk, unfinished bastion, ice

**Cross-reference:** imperial_colonies.md Section 7.11, castle_catalog.md Section 7.

**Cross-reference physics/snow_ice_physics.md** — Vega does not slip and Jake does.

**Design note:** cross-reference colonial_war/colonial_war_bosses.md Section 8.4 — **Vega cannot be spared**, and cross-reference imperial_colonies.md Section 7.11: *"Style D from the first shot."*

---

### 2.6 THE COUNTING HALLS — NEW COVENTRY

**Type:** Assault · **Scale:** 150 marines + Regulators · **Terrain:** four storeys, vertically

**Cross-reference:** krone_colonies.md Section 4.10, castle_catalog.md Section 8.3.

**The walls fold and the rooms do not.** Cross-reference colonial_war/assassin_network.md Section 3 — **Company Regulators are the hardest garrison troops in Phase 7.**

**Cross-reference castle_catalog.md Section 8.3** — the clerks do not flee, and some keep working under the desks.

---

### 2.7 THE BONFIRE — BLACKWOOD LANDING

**Type:** Rising · **Scale:** 100 marines + bailiffs vs. 4,000 indentured · **Terrain:** the Hall, the walled yard

**Cross-reference:** krone_colonies.md Section 6.11, castle_catalog.md Section 10.6.

**Cross-reference castle_catalog.md Section 10.6** — bailiffs leave individually as the fighting progresses, and **the arena empties around Ashcroft in real time.**

**The objective is the strongroom.** Cross-reference krone_colonies.md Section 6.8 — **four thousand original contracts, and destroying them ends the colony rather than taking it.**

---

### 2.8 THE APPROACHES — PORT STERLING

**Type:** Naval · **Scale:** Sterling's squadron · **Terrain:** the harbour approaches

**Cross-reference:** krone_colonies.md Section 5.10, colonial_war_bosses.md Section 10.

**The hardest engagement in Dark Arisen** — and cross-reference colonial_war/colonial_war_bosses.md Section 10.4: **it should feel like a failure of imagination even when it is a victory**, because the alliance path exists.

---

### 2.9 THE STRAIT — FORT RESOLUTE

**Type:** Assault, or **withdrawal** · **Scale:** 110, rotating, unadapted · **Terrain:** modern batteries, ice

**Cross-reference:** krone_colonies.md Section 7.9, castle_catalog.md Section 11.7.

**Cross-reference krone_colonies.md Section 7.9** — the elegant route is not a battle at all: **take Thorne's dispatches to New Coventry and the shareholders withdraw the fort from beneath him.**

---

### 2.10 THE CONVOY ACTIONS

**Type:** Naval, repeatable · **Scale:** 1-6 ships

**Cross-reference:** colonial_war/colony_system_core.md Section 4.4.

Supply · materiel · **treasure fleets** · **people convoys.** Cross-reference colonial_war/colony_system_core.md Section 4.5 — **the raid-to-liberation pipeline lives here**, and it is the most emotionally charged repeatable content in the game.

---

### 2.11 THE COUNTER-ATTACKS

**Type:** Defence, generated · **Scale:** 200-600 attacking

**Cross-reference:** colonial_war/retaliation_system.md Section 3.4, castle_sieges.md Section 7.

**Not authored — produced by the retaliation system.** Cross-reference colonial_war/castle_sieges.md Section 7.5 — and the naval relief option means **Jake can fight the siege from outside it while his garrison holds without him.**

**Cross-reference colonial_war/retaliation_system.md Section 3.4** — at Stage 3+ with multiple holdings, **two arrive at once and one is lost.**

## 3. HIGHMOORE

**Cross-reference:** house_system.md, highmoore_settlements.md.

### 3.1 THE FORD — DUNFORD

**Type:** Field action, recurring · **Scale:** 150-400 a side · **Terrain:** a river crossing

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_settlements.md Section 7, house_belos.md Section 3.2.

**Everyone's father fought at the ford.** Cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md Section 6.3 — the largest graveyard in the basin serves a village of forty households.

**Design law:** cross-reference house_system.md Section 4.5 — **it recurs.** A player in Highmoore across three burning seasons can fight at Dunford three times, on different sides, and cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md Section 7.2: *"there's a boy old enough now and he asks about the ford."*

---

### 3.2 THE ASHBY FIELDS

**Type:** Field action, recurring · **Scale:** 100-250 · **Terrain:** open farmland

**Cross-reference:** the_lesser_houses.md Section 3.1, highmoore_settlements.md Section 3.2.

**Taken eleven times in forty years.** Cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md Section 7.3 — **the villagers have a forty-minute drill and know their part.**

---

### 3.3 THE ARION YARD

**Type:** Assault · **Scale:** **ten men** · **Terrain:** a cobbled outer yard in rain

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_arion_falls.md Section 3.

**The smallest named engagement in the game and among the heaviest.** Cross-reference princess_quest_arion_falls.md Section 3.4 — **Ser Rowland Vance offers to fetch his own lord out, and there is no dialogue option to accept.**

---

### 3.4 BELOS

**Type:** **none of the four** · **Scale:** 300 · **Terrain:** the Outer Court

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_belos.md, battle_system.md Section 1.1.

**Design law:** cross-reference battle_system.md Section 1.1 — **this sequence does not use the battle system and is explicitly not its model.**

No segments. No Cohesion. No orders. One man, nine minutes, and cross-reference crystal_katana.md Section 8.2: **the escalation is designed to feel wrong while it is being played.**

**Cross-reference princess_quest_belos.md Section 8** — there are three exits and none are signposted.

---

### 3.5 THE RECONSTRUCTION ACTIONS

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_reconstruction.md Section 7.

**Full Vision:** with two Great Houses gone, Cadwall moves and Ridd sells itself to everyone. Cross-reference highmoore_reconstruction.md Section 7.1 — **the first taking Lord Gwilym has made in forty years**, and Jake can be on either side of it or neither.

## 8. MVP CORE vs FULL VISION

### 8.1 MVP CORE

- **Four battles:** Fuerte Esperanza (the timing puzzle), Blackwood Landing (the bonfire), the Ford (recurring field action), the Arion Yard (ten men)
- One generated counter-attack
- The convoy actions as repeatable naval content
- All four battle types represented once

### 8.2 FULL VISION

- All twenty-one named engagements
- The Quarter Rises at full urban scale with the undercity
- Port Sterling's naval engagement and its avoidance
- The Ford across three burning seasons on different sides
- The reconstruction realignment actions

## 9. STYLE BIBLE COMPLIANCE

- ✅ **Pillar 1 (Staggered Register):** twenty-one engagements across five military cultures, and a Highmoore village explaining its evacuation drill with professional pride.
- ✅ **Pillar 2 (Lore Through Objects):** a ford with a graveyard too large for it. Fields taken eleven times. Guns that face the wrong way. A strongroom better built than a magazine.
- ✅ **Pillar 3 (Layered Death):** the smallest named engagement is ten men in a rainy yard and it is among the heaviest, and the largest is a city that has waited two hundred years.
- ✅ **Pillar 4 (Layered Humor):** clerks who keep working under the desks. A governor running between rooms improving his offer. Bailiffs leaving one at a time and being shouted at by name.
- ✅ **Pillar 5 (Theme — Revenge):** twenty-one battles and none required, a war that resolves without him, a basin that keeps fighting at a ford nobody can explain — and one sequence in a Stonecountry courtyard that does not use this system at all, on purpose.

## 11. CONCLUSION

The Named Engagements. Twenty-one of them and not one required — a colonial war that resolves by drift and foreclosure and predation whether or not Jake ever draws a sword on land, and a basin that goes on fighting at a river crossing nobody can explain the reason for.

A plantation where the guns face the fields and the whole battle is reaching them before they fire. A city where the quarter comes up through streets it has had two hundred years to memorise. A mining post where the mountain simply closes. A counting house where the clerks keep working under the desks. A great hall where the bailiffs leave one at a time and are shouted at by name as they go.

A ford where everyone's father fought, that recurs, that a player can fight at three times on three different sides across three burning seasons — with a graveyard beside it too large for the forty households it serves.

And a cobbled yard in the rain with ten men in it, which is the smallest named engagement in Dark Arisen and one of the heaviest, because the last of them offers to go and fetch his own lord out and there is no way to say yes.
