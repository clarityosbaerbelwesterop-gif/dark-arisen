# ELOWEN ARION — THE PRINCESS

**Status:** Design Draft v1.0
**Phase:** Phase 8 — Highmoore (Movement Two — Document 12 of ~30)
**Related:** highmoore_overview.md, house_arion.md, house_belos.md, house_system.md, highmoore_settlements.md, princess_quest_overview.md, princess_quest_the_ball.md, princess_quest_the_lake.md, princess_quest_the_truth.md, npcs/the_named_dead.md, npcs/children_and_families.md, style_bible.md
**Purpose:** The complete character document for Elowen Arion — who she is independent of Jake, her biography, her voice, the foreshadowing a player can catch, her real letter, her decision and its reasoning, and the design rules that keep her a person rather than a motivation.
**Inspirations:** Red Dead Redemption 2 (Mary Linton — a relationship with its own weather), Hamlet's Ophelia handled correctly, The Leopard (Concetta), Wolf Hall (women as assets, written from inside), Éowyn, Toni Morrison's refusal to explain her characters to the reader

---

## 1. THE DESIGN LAW

### 1.1 The Problem This Document Exists to Solve

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_overview.md Section 6.2.

The default version of this questline is: a beautiful woman appears, is charming, sleeps with the protagonist, dies, and her interiority is delivered posthumously by a villain's exposition to justify a rampage.

**That version is not being built.**

### 1.2 The Four Rules

**RULE 1 — SHE EXISTS BEFORE JAKE AND WITHOUT HIM.**
Cross-reference Section 4. Her life, her boredom, her competence, her relationships, and her decision all predate the ball. Jake is not the cause of anything that happens. He is a variable introduced late into a situation that was already resolving.

**RULE 2 — SHE TELLS HER OWN TRUTH.**
Cross-reference Section 9, princess_quest_the_truth.md. **The real letter is in her hand and her words.** The father confirms; he does not reveal.

**RULE 3 — THE PLAYER CAN SEE IT COMING.**
Cross-reference Section 8. Five separate observable signals across two sequences. A player who is paying attention will feel the shape of it before the lake, and cross-reference Section 8.5: **the game never confirms their suspicion until it is too late to act on.**

**RULE 4 — NOTHING IS DEPICTED.**
Cross-reference highmoore_overview.md Section 6.3, house_belos.md Section 6.2, npcs/children_and_families.md. No flashback. No detail. No scene. **Her letter is the only account of anything that happened to her**, it is short, and it is factual.

## 2. WHO SHE IS

### 2.1 Quick Reference

**Name:** Elowen Arion
**Age:** 19
**House:** Arion. Cross-reference house_arion.md.
**Register:** High by training. Cross-reference Section 5 — **and something underneath it that is not.**
**Cross-reference:** the user's own framing — *"wunderschön wie Helena und absolut friedlich."*

### 2.2 The Peacefulness

The trait everyone notices first, and it is real, and it is not serenity.

**Cross-reference Section 7.2.** Elowen is peaceful the way someone is peaceful after a decision. She has been at Arion, in her father's house, in a hall full of people who like her, being pleasant and accomplished and warm — **since she made up her mind.** It has been eleven weeks.

**Design note:** the player meets her four weeks before the lake. Everything charming about her at the ball is genuine and none of it is an act, and cross-reference Section 8.1 — **the calm is the tell, and it reads as loveliness.**

### 2.3 What She Is Good At

- **Horses.** Cross-reference mounted_travel.md Section 8.1, house_arion.md Section 5 — she rides an Arion grey and rides it beautifully, and Jake does not.
- **Languages.** Three, and cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md Section 5.2 — she was taught at the abbey and Brother Padarn thought she should have stayed.
- **Reading people.** Cross-reference Section 8.2 — **she knows Jake is lying inside four sentences and lets him.**
- **Being liked.** Cross-reference house_arion.md Section 4 — the household adores her, the hostage-guests follow her around, and Master Odo pretends to be exasperated by her and is not.

### 2.4 What She Wants

Nothing available.

**Cross-reference house_system.md Section 4.2, house_arion.md Section 6.4.** She has been valued out loud since she was eleven. Cross-reference house_arion.md Section 6.2 — **the Long Gallery is a wall of women who married out with the House and the year on the plaque**, and there is a space where hers will go.

She has never once been asked what she would do instead, including by herself, and cross-reference Section 9: **the real letter contains the only answer she ever gave**, and it is four words about rabbits.

## 3. THE BIOGRAPHY

### 3.1 Childhood

Arion. Loved, expensively educated, and cross-reference house_arion.md Section 3.2 — her father is a good host, funny at table, excellent with horses, and she adored him.

**Cross-reference Section 6.** She had rabbits.

### 3.2 Age Fifteen — The Truce

**Cross-reference:** house_system.md Section 4.1, house_belos.md Section 6.

Arion and Belos agreed a truce. Standard terms. Each House sent a child. Cross-reference the_lesser_houses.md Section 5 — the practice is ordinary, benign, and the player has met six other examples of it.

**She went to Belos for eighteen months.**

**Cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md Section 5.4** — the abbey ledger records it in a fair hand, with a date, and nothing else.

### 3.3 What Happened There

**Cross-reference:** house_belos.md Section 6.2 (the absolute restraint rule), Section 9 below.

**The only account in the game is her letter.** No other character describes it. It is not depicted, referenced in detail, or dramatised. Cross-reference Rule 4.

### 3.4 Coming Home

She came home at sixteen. The truce was considered a success and held another six months.

**Nobody spoke about it.** Cross-reference house_belos.md Section 6.3 — nothing was done at Belos either. Cross-reference house_system.md Section 4.4: **there was a settlement procedure available and neither House used it.**

### 3.5 Ages Sixteen to Nineteen

**Cross-reference:** Section 9, princess_quest_the_truth.md.

The letter covers this in two sentences. **The document does not expand on it**, and cross-reference Rule 4 and house_arion.md Section 3.2 — Lord Cassian Arion is not written as a monster, is never played as one, and everything the player sees of him before the truth is true.

Both of those things are the case.

### 3.6 Eleven Weeks Ago

She learned she was pregnant.

### 3.7 Eight Weeks Ago

**She decided.** Cross-reference Section 7.

### 4.2 Her Relationships

**BROTHER PADARN** — cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md Section 5.3. Taught her Latin. Thought she should have stayed at the abbey. **He was not joking and she knew he was not joking and she did not go**, and cross-reference Section 9.

**HER FATHER** — cross-reference Sections 3.5, 8.4, and house_arion.md Section 3.

## 5. VOICE

### 5.1 The Register

**Cross-reference:** style_bible.md Pillar 1, house_arion.md Section 11.

High by training — she can do the whole aristocratic performance flawlessly, and does, at the ball, for about ninety seconds.

**And then she stops**, and what is underneath is plain, dry, direct, and slightly unnerving. Cross-reference Section 8.1 — **the switch is the first signal.**

### 5.2 Sample — The Ball, Formal

*"You are very welcome at Arion. My father sets a good table and a better cellar and he will tell you about the horses. Please look interested. It matters to him more than he lets on."*

### 5.3 Sample — The Ball, After the Switch

*"You're not an ambassador."*

*(a beat)*

*"No — don't. I'm not going to tell anyone. I'd just rather we both knew where we are."*

### 5.4 Sample — Outside, on the Terrace

*"My father is the most reasonable man in this basin. Ask anyone. They'll all say it and they'll all mean it."*

*"I had rabbits. Six of them. I fed them every morning for four years."*

*(and she does not say what happened, and cross-reference Section 6, and if the player asks she changes the subject, gently, and it is the only time all night she does that)*

### 5.5 Sample — The Lake

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_the_lake.md.

*"You ride like a man who has spent his life on something that moves under him for other reasons."*

*"That's not an insult. It's nearly a compliment. Stop looking like that."*

*"I'm glad it was you. I want to say that now, in case I forget, because I forget things when I'm happy."*

### 5.6 Sample — The Register Underneath

*"Do you know what the worst of it is? Everyone is kind. Every single person in that house is kind to me every day."*

## 7. THE DECISION

### 7.1 It Is Hers

**Cross-reference:** Rule 1, highmoore_overview.md Section 6.2.

Elowen decided to die eight weeks before Jake arrived at Arion. **He did not cause it, could not have prevented it, and was not part of the plan.**

### 7.2 The Reasoning

Cross-reference Section 9 — her letter gives it, and it is not a despair note. It is a piece of reasoning, and it is coherent, and that is what makes it unbearable.

**The three elements:**

1. She cannot continue and will not say more about that
2. If she dies quietly at Arion, her father buries it, and cross-reference Section 9 — **nothing changes and she has died for nothing**
3. If she dies on Belos land, it is a killing, and Highmoore has procedures for killings

### 7.3 Why the Lake

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_region.md Section 2.3, house_belos.md Section 7.3, princess_quest_the_lake.md.

The Highmoore Lake is beautiful and it is **just** across the Belos boundary. Cross-reference house_system.md Section 3.3 — the boundary stone is right there, with three sigils on it.

She chose it because it is both.

### 7.4 Why Jake

**Cross-reference:** Section 9, and this is the questline's emotional centre.

**She did not choose him to be there.** Cross-reference Section 7.5 — that was her father.

She chose him **to see once more.** Cross-reference Section 9 — the letter says why, and the reason is that he was the only person who did not know what she was worth.

### 7.5 What Her Father Did

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_the_truth.md, house_arion.md Section 3.

Cassian Arion found out. Not everything — cross-reference Section 8.4 — but enough.

**And he improved it.**

He dictated the false letter (Section 9.1). He arranged the timing. He made certain that when it happened, **Jake was standing there** — because a foreign visitor who believes the child is his is simultaneously an explanation for the pregnancy, a witness to a Belos killing, and cross-reference princess_quest_arion_falls.md: **a weapon.**

**Design law:** Elowen does not know this. Cross-reference Section 9 — her letter is written by someone who thinks she is dying alone.

## 8. THE FORESHADOWING

**Cross-reference:** Rule 3. Five signals, all observable, none confirmed.

### 8.1 The Calm

Cross-reference Section 2.2. She is the only person in Highmoore who is not tired, worried, or braced. In a region defined by exhaustion (cross-reference highmoore_region.md Section 5.3), **her peace is anomalous** and reads as loveliness.

### 8.2 She Reads Jake Instantly

Cross-reference Section 5.3. She knows he is lying in four sentences and does not care, at all, about anything he might actually be. **Someone with a future is more curious than that.**

### 8.3 The Terrace

Cross-reference Section 5.4, Section 6.2. She changes the subject exactly once all night, and it is the rabbits.

### 8.4 Her Mother's Line

**Cross-reference:** house_arion.md Section 4.2, princess_quest_the_ball.md.

Lady Maerwyn, at the ball, slightly drunk, to Jake, apropos of nothing:

*"You're very kind to her. People are. It's the one thing I can say for this house — everyone is very kind to her."*

*(a pause that is a half-second too long)*

*"Have some more of the white. It's the only good thing we make."*

**A first-time player hears a tipsy hostess. A second-time player hears the worst sentence in the game.**

### 8.5 The Ride to the Lake

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_the_lake.md, mounted_travel.md Section 8.1.

Nine minutes, capped gait, and she talks the whole way, and cross-reference Section 5.5: three of the things she says are goodbyes.

*"I'm glad it was you. I want to say that now, in case I forget."*

**Design law — Rule 3's second half:** the player may be certain by minute six. **There is nothing they can do.** No dialogue option, no intervention, no check. Cross-reference princess_quest_the_lake.md — the ride cannot be sped up, diverted, or ended early.

## 9. THE REAL LETTER

**Cross-reference:** Rule 2, princess_quest_the_truth.md, princess_quest_arion_falls.md Section 4.

### 9.1 The Two Letters

**THE FALSE LETTER** — delivered by the Wizard after the Light Elf campaign. Cross-reference wizard_questline.md. In her handwriting. **Dictated by her father.** It says the child is Jake's and asks him to come.

**THE REAL LETTER** — written the same week, hidden, and cross-reference Section 9.2: given to Alys.

### 9.2 Where It Is Found

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_arion_falls.md Section 4.

Jake finds it in her chambers, **during the attack on Arion**, on his way to the courtyard to kill her father.

Cross-reference house_arion.md Section 4.2 — **Alys is not at Arion when Jake comes back.** She left it where Elowen told her to and she went.

**Design note:** he reads it mid-slaughter, with ten men dead behind him, and then he goes down to the yard. Everything that happens in the courtyard happens after he already knows.

### 9.3 The Letter

---

*Jake —*

*You told me your parents worked in government. I knew before you finished the sentence. I let you because I wanted to hear you talk about something.*

*The child is not yours. I am sorry about the other letter. It was mine to write and it was not mine to say.*

*It is my father's. I will not write more about that. You will understand it or you will not and either way there is nothing in the world you can do with it.*

*Before that, at fifteen, there was Belos. Four of them. I told my father when I came home and he asked me who else knew.*

*That was the whole of it. That was what he asked.*

*I have chosen the lake because it is beautiful and because it is eleven yards on their side of the stone. If I die at home he will bury it in the churchyard and in the ledger and nothing will have happened at all. If I die on Belos land it is a killing, and this basin has procedures for killings, and somebody will have to write something down.*

*I did not ask you to come. I wanted to see you once. You are the only person I have met in nineteen years who did not know what I was worth, and for one evening I was a girl at a party talking to a liar, and I have thought about it every day since.*

*Do not do anything. Please. You will want to and it will not help and it will not be for me.*

*You asked me at the ball what I do here. I never answered.*

*I fed the rabbits. He had them killed when I was twelve, to teach me that I should not want things. It worked, mostly.*

*That is the true answer. In nineteen years it is the only thing that was mine.*

*— E.*

---

### 9.4 The Design Notes on the Letter

**Cross-reference:** Rule 4, style_bible.md Pillar 3.

- **Two hundred and eighty words.** Cross-reference house_belos.md Section 6.2 — it is the only account of anything in the game and it is short.
- **Nothing is described.** The Belos line is nine words. The father is one sentence and a refusal.
- ***"He asked me who else knew."*** — the most damaging sentence in Dark Arisen, and it is about a question, not an act.
- ***"Do not do anything. Please."*** — cross-reference princess_quest_belos.md: **she asked him not to**, in writing, before he did it, and he had already read it.
- **The rabbits.** Cross-reference Section 6 — three touches across ten hours, and this is the third.
- **She signs it E.** She signed the false letter *Elowen*, in full, because her father was watching her write it.

## 10. AFTER

### 10.1 She Is Not a Ghost

**Cross-reference:** npcs/the_named_dead.md, style_bible.md Pillar 3.

No visions. No dream sequences. No voice in Jake's head. Cross-reference npcs/the_named_dead.md Section 2.3 — **the game offers no closure mechanic and no posthumous appearance.**

### 10.2 Where She Is Buried

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_settlements.md Section 6, princess_quest_the_return.md.

The Arion churchyard. Cross-reference her letter: *"he will bury it in the churchyard and in the ledger."*

**He does.** She is buried before Jake gets back, correctly, with the family, and the stone is good, and cross-reference Section 9.3 — that is exactly what she said would happen and it is why she went to the lake.

### 10.3 What Changes Because of Her

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_the_truth.md, highmoore_reconstruction.md.

She was right that a killing on Belos land requires a written record. **The record exists.** Cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md Section 5.4 — the abbey has it, Brother Padarn wrote it, and it says a daughter of Arion was killed by an arrow on Belos ground.

**Cross-reference highmoore_reconstruction.md:** it is the only thing she wanted, it is one line in a ledger, and it outlasts both Houses.

### 10.4 The Portrait Space

**Cross-reference:** house_arion.md Sections 6.2 and 8.3.

The gap in the Long Gallery is still there. Cross-reference highmoore_reconstruction.md — **it can be filled, the game does not suggest it, and nothing rewards it.**

## 13. STYLE BIBLE COMPLIANCE

- ✅ **Pillar 1 (Staggered Register):** she performs High flawlessly for ninety seconds and then stops, and what is underneath is plain and dry and direct — and the switch is both her characterisation and the first clue.
- ✅ **Pillar 2 (Lore Through Objects):** six empty hutches. A gap in a gallery of plaques. Two letters in the same hand, one signed *Elowen* and one signed *E.* An abbey ledger entry recording eighteen months at Belos in a fair hand with nothing else.
- ✅ **Pillar 3 (Layered Death):** nothing is depicted. The Belos line is nine words. The father is one sentence and a refusal. The worst thing in the letter is a question he asked her. She is buried correctly, before Jake gets back, exactly as she predicted, and she never appears again in any form.
- ✅ **Pillar 4 (Layered Humor):** she is genuinely funny at the ball. Cross-reference Section 5.5 — she is funny at the lake. **The player must like her, not pity her**, and the humour is how.
- ✅ **Pillar 5 (Theme — Revenge):** she wrote *do not do anything, please, it will not help and it will not be for me* — and Jake read it, in her room, with ten men dead behind him, and then went down to the courtyard anyway. **Everything he does afterward, he does having been asked not to, by her, in writing.**

## 15. CONCLUSION

Elowen Arion. Nineteen, of a house of white and gold, who rides better than anyone Jake has met and speaks three languages and is patient with the hostage children in a way that is completely unforced. Who is the only person in a basin defined by exhaustion who is not tired at all, and whose peace reads as loveliness and is actually a decision eight weeks old.

Who does the whole aristocratic performance flawlessly for ninety seconds at a ball and then stops and says *you're not an ambassador* and does not care what he actually is. Who mentions six rabbits on a terrace and changes the subject once all evening.

Who wrote two letters in one week — one signed in full because her father was watching, and one signed with a single letter and left with a maid who is gone by the time it is read. Two hundred and eighty words, nothing described, and the worst sentence in it is a question somebody asked her when she was sixteen.

And who wrote, near the end of it: *do not do anything, please, it will not help and it will not be for me.*

He reads that in her room with ten men dead behind him, and then he goes down to the yard.
