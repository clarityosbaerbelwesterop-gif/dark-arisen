# THE LESSER HOUSES — THE REST OF HIGHMOORE

**Status:** Design Draft v1.0
**Phase:** Phase 8 — Highmoore (Movement Two — Document 10 of ~30)
**Related:** highmoore_overview.md, house_system.md, house_arion.md, house_belos.md, highmoore_settlements.md, highmoore_smiths.md, highmoore_reconstruction.md, mounted_travel.md, colonial_war/castle_catalog.md, npcs/regional_populations.md, npcs/children_and_families.md, style_bible.md
**Purpose:** The remaining Houses — two Great, six Middle, eight Minor — each with castle, colours, lord, specialty, feud position, and the business a player can involve themselves in. Plus the benign hostage-guests the questline's restraint rule requires.
**Inspirations:** Game of Thrones (the pleasure of a large legible house roster), Mount & Blade (faction texture at scale), Kingdom Come: Deliverance (minor nobility as real people), Icelandic sagas, the Border Reiver surnames

## 1. THE CATALOG'S FUNCTION

### 1.1 Why These Exist

**Cross-reference:** house_system.md Section 8.3.

Arion and Belos carry the questline. **The other sixteen Houses carry Highmoore.**

They are the reason the region reads as a living political system rather than a two-house stage set, and cross-reference house_system.md Principle 1.5 — they are the evidence that Lord Arion did nothing Highmoore's practices do not permit, because the player has watched fifteen other lords use the same practices without incident.

### 1.2 The Standard

Cross-reference colonial_war/castle_catalog.md Principle 1.5 — **no generic castles**, and the standard holds across all sixteen. Each entry gives: colours, sigil, castle silhouette, lord, specialty, feud position, and available business.

### 1.3 The Restraint Requirement

**Cross-reference:** house_system.md Section 4.1 (Design Law).

**Six benign hostage-guests appear in this catalog**, established warmly, before the player learns anything about Elowen Arion. Cross-reference Sections 3.2, 4.1, 4.4, 5.2, 5.6 — one of them is very funny.

## 2. THE OTHER TWO GREAT HOUSES

### 2.1 HOUSE CADWALL — Blue and Black

**Sigil:** a tower, black on blue
**Castle:** **Cadwall Keep** — the only genuinely defensive castle in Highmoore. Cross-reference colonial_war/castle_catalog.md Section 2.1: a proper concentric fortress on a spur in the High Moor, and it has never been taken.
**Lord:** **Lord Gwilym Cadwall**, 63. Cautious, patient, and the only lord in Highmoore who has never lost a holding permanently.
**Specialty:** stone and masons. Cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md — Cadwall built half the castles in the basin, including Arion's washed white walls.
**Feud position:** neutral by policy. Cadwall does not raid; Cadwall answers raids and then stops.
**Holdings:** 19

**Voice:**
*"I have outlived four Belos lords and three Arions and I have done it by declining, politely, to be interesting."*

**Business:** masonry contracts, garrison work, and cross-reference highmoore_reconstruction.md — **Cadwall's masons are the reconstruction's primary resource**, and Gwilym will negotiate hard.

**Hostage-guests:** Cadwall currently hosts **two** and sends **one**. Cross-reference Section 3.2.

---

### 2.2 HOUSE MERE — Russet and White

**Sigil:** three fish, white on russet
**Castle:** **Merehall** — built on piles at the edge of the Waterlands, half timber, and cross-reference physics/water_physics.md: the causeway is passable four hours in six.
**Lord:** **Lady Anwen Mere**, 44. Widowed, rules in her own right, and cross-reference house_system.md Section 4.2 — she has refused eleven marriage offers and enjoys refusing them.
**Specialty:** water. Cross-reference house_system.md Section 5 — **Mere alone can move an army through the Waterlands**, which is why nobody has ever besieged them.
**Feud position:** allied to Arion by two marriages, and cross-reference Section 8.2: **after the questline this becomes complicated.**
**Holdings:** 17

**Voice:**
*"Everyone in this basin thinks they are the clever one. I have a bog. The bog is cleverer than all of them and it does not require feeding."*

**Business:** guiding through the Waterlands, eel and fish trade, and cross-reference princess_quest_the_lake.md — **the Highmoore Lake is at the edge of her country and she knows every path to it.**

## 3. THE MIDDLE HOUSES

### 3.1 HOUSE ASHBY — Green and Yellow

**Sigil:** a sheaf
**Castle:** **Ashby Grange** — barely a castle. A fortified manor with a curtain wall added in a hurry.
**Lord:** **Ser Edmund Ashby**, 39
**Specialty:** wool and grain. The best fields in the Folds.
**Feud position:** **the Ashby fields have changed hands eleven times in forty years.** Cross-reference house_system.md Section 1.2, highmoore_region.md Section 5.3 — the region's ambient dialogue is *about* Ashby.
**Holdings:** 11 (currently 9)

**Voice:**
*"We're not a House. We're a table everyone else eats off. My grandfather said that. He said it a lot."*

**Business:** everything. Ashby is always losing something and always paying someone to get it back.

---

### 3.2 HOUSE VANE — Red and Grey

**Sigil:** a broken wheel
**Castle:** **Vanemoor** — a tower house on the High Moor, isolated, and the wind never stops.
**Lord:** **Lord Tobias Vane**, 55, and cross-reference house_arion.md Section 4.2 — **Ser Rowland Vance is his cousin**, and after the questline this matters.
**Specialty:** cavalry. Small, expensive, and the only mounted shock force in the basin.
**Feud position:** sworn to Belos for two generations, and the oath is fraying.
**Holdings:** 13

**HOSTAGE-GUEST — BRAN, 12** *(benign example)*
Cadwall's second son, at Vanemoor eight months. He is homesick, he has adopted a moor pony that hates him, and he has decided Lord Vane is the greatest man alive because Lord Vane once let him hold a lance.

*"He let me hold it. The actual one. I nearly dropped it but he said that happens to everyone."*

---

### 3.3 HOUSE THORN — Black and White

**Sigil:** a bare tree
**Castle:** **Thornrest** — built into a limestone scar in the Stonecountry, half cave.
**Lord:** **Lady Isolde Thorn**, 51. Cross-reference highmoore_smiths.md.
**Specialty:** iron and forge. **The best smiths in Highmoore**, and cross-reference highmoore_smiths.md Section 3 — this is the House the archipelago's smiths would want to meet.
**Feud position:** neutral and unbothered. Everyone needs iron.
**Holdings:** 12

**Voice:**
*"I sell to Arion, I sell to Belos, I sell to whoever comes with silver. They can settle it between themselves. I'll be here."*

**Business:** the finest crafting in Phase 8. Cross-reference highmoore_smiths.md.

---

### 3.4 HOUSE FEN — Brown and Blue

**Sigil:** a heron
**Castle:** **Fenwick** — Waterlands, sworn to Mere, and cross-reference physics/water_physics.md: it floods twice a year on purpose.
**Lord:** **Ser Owain Fen**, 33. Young, energetic, and slightly out of his depth.
**Specialty:** fowling and the bog paths
**Feud position:** Mere's man, entirely
**Holdings:** 8

---

### 3.5 HOUSE STRAND — Yellow and Black

**Sigil:** two crossed staves
**Castle:** **Strandholt** — the Burnt March, and cross-reference highmoore_region.md Section 2.3: it has been burnt three times and rebuilt three times and Lord Strand will tell anyone about it.
**Lord:** **Lord Merrick Strand**, 60
**Specialty:** endurance. Cross-reference house_system.md Section 4.5 — Strand survives the burning season better than anyone because Strand has nothing left worth burning.
**Feud position:** hates everyone equally and has outlasted four of them
**Holdings:** 9

**Voice:**
*"Third time. Third. And I'll tell you what — the third rebuild's the best of them. Learned something each go."*

---

### 3.6 HOUSE CARRICK — Purple and Silver

**Sigil:** a hound
**Castle:** **Carrick Hall** — the Folds, and it is genuinely lovely and everyone forgets it exists.
**Lord:** **Lady Rhian Carrick**, 47
**Specialty:** hounds and hunting, second only to Belos and bitter about it
**Feud position:** sworn to Arion, loosely
**Holdings:** 10

## 4. THE MINOR HOUSES — THE FOLDS AND THE HIGH MOOR

### 4.1 HOUSE PENNARD — Green and White

**Castle:** a tower and a wall. **Lord:** Ser Alun Pennard, 41. **Specialty:** the road between Arion and Cadwall, sworn and kept. **Holdings:** 4

**HOSTAGE-GUEST — MEG, 16** *(benign example — cross-reference house_system.md Section 8.2)*
Two years at Cadwall, now home, and cross-reference the ambient line established in house_system.md: *"came back with better manners than her brothers."* **She is the region's clearest evidence that hostage-guesting works**, and she says so, at length, to anyone.

---

### 4.2 HOUSE OKE — Brown and Gold

**Castle:** Okeby, a fortified farm with pretensions. **Lord:** Ser Hal Oke, 29. **Specialty:** nothing, and he is very aware of it. **Holdings:** 3

**Voice:** *"We're a House. It says so on the stone. My father had the stone cut."*

---

### 4.3 HOUSE DRURY — Blue and Yellow

**Castle:** Drurystead. **Lady:** Lady Cerys Drury, 38. **Specialty:** cheese, and it is the best in the basin, and she is furious that this is what her House is known for. **Holdings:** 5

---

### 4.4 HOUSE LYNN — Grey and Red

**Castle:** Lynnwater. **Lord:** Ser Padrig Lynn, 52. **Specialty:** the mills. **Holdings:** 4

**HOSTAGE-GUEST — TAM, 14** *(benign example — the funny one)*
A Strand boy, three months in, who has decided that being a hostage is the greatest career available to a fourteen-year-old and is trying to arrange a transfer to a bigger House.

*"See, at Strand I'm the fourth son. Here I'm a *guest*. I sit up the table. I've written to my father suggesting Cadwall next and he hasn't written back which I take as encouragement."*

**Cross-reference house_system.md Section 8.3** — Tam is the single most important comedic character in Phase 8 and his function is structural.

---

### 4.5 HOUSE BRAE — Black and Green

**Castle:** Braehead, High Moor, isolated. **Lord:** Lord Ewan Brae, 66. **Specialty:** the standing stones and the old boundaries; Brae keeps Highmoore's oldest records and cannot read most of them. **Holdings:** 6

---

### 4.6 HOUSE HALFORD — White and Blue

**Castle:** Halford Cross. **Lady:** Lady Nesta Halford, 35. **Specialty:** the abbey and the region's only school. Cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md Section 5. **Holdings:** 5

---

### 4.7 HOUSE RIDD — Red and Black

**Castle:** Riddmoor. **Lord:** Ser Gareth Ridd, 44. **Specialty:** mercenary work; Ridd's levy fights for whoever pays and everyone employs them and nobody trusts them. **Holdings:** 3

---

### 4.8 HOUSE WYNNE — Gold and Green

**Castle:** Wynnefold. **Lord:** Lord Idris Wynne, 58. **Specialty:** horses, second to Arion. Cross-reference mounted_travel.md Section 4.4 — **Wynne breeds the crossbred working horses that most of Highmoore actually rides.** **Holdings:** 7

## 5. THE HOSTAGE-GUEST ROSTER

**Cross-reference:** house_system.md Section 4.1, Design Law.

**Six benign examples, established before the questline turns:**

1. **Bran, 12** — Cadwall's son at Vanemoor (Section 3.2)
2. **Tam, 14** — Strand's son at Lynn, campaigning for a promotion (Section 4.4)
3. **Meg, 16** — Pennard's daughter, returned from Cadwall (Section 4.1)
4. **The Cadwall boy, 14** — at Arion. Cross-reference house_arion.md Section 4.2
5. **The Mere girl, 11** — at Arion. Cross-reference house_arion.md Section 4.2

**Design law:** the player must have met at least four of these, warmly, before princess_quest_the_truth.md. Cross-reference house_system.md Section 8.3 and npcs/children_and_families.md — the protection rules apply to all of them without exception.

## 6. THE FEUD MAP

### 6.1 Current Alignment

**Arion bloc:** Arion, Mere (by marriage), Carrick, Pennard, Halford, Drury
**Belos bloc:** Belos, Vane (fraying), Ridd (paid), Oke
**Neutral:** Cadwall, Thorn, Brae, Wynne
**Nobody's:** Ashby (the table), Strand (nothing left), Fen (Mere's), Lynn

### 6.2 The Active Feuds

- **Belos vs Ashby** — the fields. Perpetual. Cross-reference Section 3.1.
- **Vane vs Carrick** — a road, and a marriage that went wrong in 1859
- **Ridd vs everyone** — payment disputes
- **Arion vs Belos** — cross-reference house_system.md Principle 1.1: nobody remembers why, and there has been a truce, and it held

### 6.3 After the Questline

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_reconstruction.md, house_system.md Section 7.3.

Belos is gone. Arion is headless. **The feud map has no procedure for this.**

- **Cadwall** moves, cautiously, for the first time in forty years
- **Mere** is Arion's ally and Arion has no lord, and cross-reference Section 2.2 — Lady Anwen has a decision to make
- **Vane's** oath to Belos has nothing to attach to. Cross-reference Section 3.2 and house_arion.md Section 4.2 — **Ser Rowland Vance died in the Arion yard and Lord Vane is his cousin**
- **Ashby** briefly, absurdly, holds its own fields
- **Ridd** offers its services to everyone at once

## 10. STYLE BIBLE COMPLIANCE

- ✅ **Pillar 1 (Staggered Register):** sixteen lords, sixteen voices, and Lady Anwen's bog, Ser Hal's stone, and Lady Cerys's cheese are three completely different kinds of aristocratic register.
- ✅ **Pillar 2 (Lore Through Objects):** Strand's third rebuild. Oke's cut stone that says House. Brae's records nobody can read. Ashby's boundary stones, recut eleven times.
- ✅ **Pillar 3 (Layered Death):** *"we're a table everyone else eats off,"* said by a man whose grandfather said it a lot.
- ✅ **Pillar 4 (Layered Humor):** **Tam.** Cross-reference Section 4.4 — a fourteen-year-old campaigning for a hostage transfer to a better House is the funniest thing in Phase 8, and his function is structural: he is why the player believes hostage-guesting is fine.
- ✅ **Pillar 5 (Theme — Revenge):** sixteen Houses conducting the same six-generation feud correctly, with settlements and seasons and children swapped between halls — and after Jake, two of the eighteen are gone and the system has no procedure and Cadwall moves for the first time in forty years.

## 12. CONCLUSION

The Rest of Highmoore. Cadwall on its spur, never taken, whose lord has outlived four Belos lords by declining politely to be interesting. Mere on piles at the edge of a bog that is cleverer than everyone. Thorn in a limestone scar selling iron to both sides and intending to still be there. Strand on its third rebuild and pleased with the improvements. Ashby, which is not a House but a table everyone else eats off, and whose grandfather said that a lot.

And Tam, fourteen, three months into a hostage-guesting at House Lynn, who has worked out that being a hostage is the best job available to a fourth son and has written to his father proposing Cadwall next and taken the lack of reply as encouragement.

He is the reason the player thinks the system is fine. He is supposed to be.
