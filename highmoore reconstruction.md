## 1. THE DESIGN LAW

### 1.1 It Does Not Balance Anything

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_overview.md Section 8.3, style_bible.md Pillar 5.

Jake can spend forty hours rebuilding Highmoore, and **the game never once says that this makes up for anything.**

- No redemption meter
- No NPC who says he has made it right
- No scene in which anyone forgives him
- Cross-reference npcs/the_named_dead.md Section 2.3 — **no closure mechanic exists**

**Design law:** every reconstruction reward is material, economic, or aesthetic. **None of them are moral.**

### 1.2 It Is Optional

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_overview.md Pillar 3.

A player can leave Highmoore after the return and never come back.

### 1.3 It Is Genuinely Good Content

**Cross-reference:** colonial_war/castle_ownership.md — the same engine, the same satisfactions, and Highmoore's version has a landmass and a broken political system to work with rather than a single fortress.

**The tension is the point.** Cross-reference Section 1.1 — building something excellent in a country you helped ruin, with nobody thanking you, is the phase's last argument.

## 2. THE BROKEN MAP

**Cross-reference:** house_system.md Section 7.3, the_lesser_houses.md Section 6.3.

### 2.1 What Jake Left

**HOUSE BELOS — destroyed.** Cross-reference princess_quest_belos.md. A Great House gone, the castle collapsed, roughly three hundred dead, and cross-reference house_belos.md Section 9.4: the undercroft.

**HOUSE ARION — decapitated.** Cross-reference princess_quest_arion_falls.md Section 8.1. The castle stands, the household is intact, and there is no lord and no heir.

**Cross-reference house_system.md Section 1.2** — *"nobody is trying to win."* **Highmoore has no procedure for a Great House ceasing to exist.**

### 2.2 The Realignment

**Cross-reference:** the_lesser_houses.md Section 6.3.

- **CADWALL** moves, cautiously, for the first time in forty years. Cross-reference the_lesser_houses.md Section 2.1 — Lord Gwilym has outlived four Belos lords by declining to be interesting, and now there are no Belos lords at all.
- **MERE** is Arion's ally and Arion has no lord. Cross-reference the_lesser_houses.md Section 2.2 — Lady Anwen has a decision.
- **VANE'S** oath to Belos has nothing to attach to, and cross-reference princess_quest_arion_falls.md Section 3.4: **Ser Rowland Vance died in the Arion yard and Lord Vane is his cousin.**
- **ASHBY** holds its own fields, briefly and absurdly. Cross-reference the_lesser_houses.md Section 3.1.
- **RIDD** offers its services to everyone at once.
- **THORN** sells iron to whoever is left. Cross-reference the_lesser_houses.md Section 3.3 — *"I'll be here."* She was right.

### 2.3 The Vacuum

**Cross-reference:** colonial_war/war_state_system.md Section 4.6.

**The same logic as the Fork.** Something fills the space. Cross-reference Section 3 — Jake can be one of the things that fills it, and cross-reference Section 7: **so can Cadwall, and Cadwall is not gentle.**

## 3. WHAT JAKE CAN REBUILD

**Cross-reference:** colonial_war/castle_ownership.md, npcs/building_evolution.md.

### 3.1 The Engine

The seven-state building system, applied at regional scale. Cross-reference npcs/building_evolution.md — the same development stages, the same visible progress between visits, and cross-reference colonial_war/castle_ownership.md Section 3.3: **Jake returns to changes he did not watch happen.**

### 3.2 CASTLES

**BELOS** — cross-reference house_belos.md Section 2.3: millstone grit shatters and the western range is rubble.

**It can be cleared.** Cross-reference Section 6.3 — and cross-reference princess_quest_belos.md Section 6: **clearing the western range means clearing the undercroft**, and the design's instruction is unchanged from that document: **the game does not show what is down there.**

**ARION** — cross-reference house_arion.md Section 8.2. Undamaged, and cross-reference Section 5: it does not need rebuilding. It needs a lord, and Jake is not one.

**THE LESSER HOUSES** — cross-reference the_lesser_houses.md Section 3.5: **Strand is on its third rebuild and Lord Merrick will happily discuss the improvements at length.**

### 3.3 FORGES

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_smiths.md.

Thorn's iron, the Stonecountry works, and cross-reference house_belos.md Section 2.2 — **the Belos bowyer's shop, which made the finest weapons in Phase 8 and is now under a collapsed wall.**

### 3.4 ROADS

**Cross-reference:** house_system.md Section 4.3, highmoore_settlements.md Section 8.3.

Road-oath is a House's most visible political statement, and cross-reference Section 2.1: **two Houses can no longer make it.**

**Jake can swear roads.** Cross-reference Section 7.2 — and doing so is the single most consequential thing available in the reconstruction, because it is the first time in six generations that a road has been sworn by somebody who is not a House.

### 3.5 THE FORD

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_settlements.md Section 7.

*"My father fought at the ford, and his father, and I've a boy old enough now and he asks about the ford."*

**A bridge is buildable.** Cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md Section 6.3 — the ford's graveyard is the largest in the basin and serves a village of forty households.

**Design note:** it is expensive, it takes chapters, Cadwall's masons are required (cross-reference the_lesser_houses.md Section 2.1), and **nobody asks Jake to do it.**

### 3.6 MILLS, VILLAGES, AND THE BURNT MARCH

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_settlements.md Section 9.3, highmoore_region.md Section 2.3.

Village-level rebuilding, and cross-reference highmoore_region.md Section 2.3 — **the Burnt March has not been farmed in living memory**, and it can be.

## 4. THE UNIFIED ECONOMY

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_overview.md Section 8.2, house_system.md Section 2.3.

### 4.1 The Problem

Highmoore's forges are isolated, its roads are unsafe, its trade is throttled, and cross-reference house_system.md Section 1.2 — **it has never been unified because there has never been anything larger than a House.**

### 4.2 What Connection Produces

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_smiths.md, colonial_war/indigenous_liberation_alliance.md Section 4.4 for the Phase 7 precedent.

- **Thorn's iron + Cadwall's stone + the Stonecountry quarries** — building capacity at a scale Highmoore has never had
- **Belos bowyery + Thorn iron** — cross-reference highmoore_smiths.md Section 5: **the finest ranged weapons in Dark Arisen, and the tradition is currently under a wall**
- **Wynne's crossbreds + Arion's greys** — cross-reference mounted_travel.md Section 4.4
- **Wool, grain, and the ford** — the basin's actual economy, currently strangled by roads

### 4.3 The Archipelago Connection

**Cross-reference:** crystal_caves.md Section 9.1, colonial_war/colony_system_core.md.

**The Crystal Caves are a permanent two-way route.**

**Full Vision:** Highmoore goods can reach the archipelago and archipelago goods can reach Highmoore, and cross-reference colonial_war/colony_system_core.md Section 4 — **Jake can run a supply line through a mountain.**

**Design note:** cross-reference highmoore_smiths.md and npcs/blacksmiths_and_armorers.md — **Halvor Eriksson and Lady Isolde Thorn have never heard of each other**, and connecting them is a Full Vision thread with a genuine crafting payoff.

### 4.4 What It Is Not

**Cross-reference:** Section 1.1, colonial_war/castle_ownership.md Section 10 (the Governor Problem).

**Jake does not become Highmoore's lord.** Cross-reference colonial_war/castle_ownership.md Section 10.2 — the same accumulation problem applies, in a place where he killed three hundred people, and **the word changes in mouths here faster than it did in the archipelago.**

## 5. ARION

### 5.1 The Household Continues

**Cross-reference:** house_arion.md Section 8.2.

Two hundred people, intact, in a castle with no lord. Master Odo runs it because Master Odo has always run it.

### 5.2 MAERWYN

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_the_truth.md Sections 5.2 and 8.3, princess_quest_arion_falls.md Section 7.4.

Alive. In the castle. **Having known for two years.**

**Design law:** cross-reference princess_quest_the_truth.md Section 8.3 — **the game does not resolve what she is owed or what she owes.**

She does not accuse Jake. She does not thank him. She receives him if he comes, because Arion receives people, and cross-reference house_system.md Section 6.2: **guest-right is real and she keeps it.**

**MAERWYN:** *"You'll want the small hall. It's warmer. Odo will see to it."*

*(and that is the entire interaction, every time, for the rest of the game)*

### 5.3 If He Told Her Husband She Was Happy

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_arion_falls.md Section 6.6, princess_quest_the_return.md Section 6.4.

**Full Vision:** the household in the doorways heard it, and it reached her, and **it is the only thing anyone has ever told her about her daughter's last day.**

**She says so. Once.**

**MAERWYN:** *"They told me what you said to him. About the lake."*

*(a pause)*

**MAERWYN:** *"Thank you."*

*(and cross-reference Section 1.1 — **it is not absolution and the game does not treat it as any**)*

### 5.4 The Gallery Space

**Cross-reference:** house_arion.md Sections 6.2 and 8.3, the_princess.md Section 10.4.

Six generations of portraits, every second one a daughter who married out with the House and the year on the plaque. **And a gap.**

**It can be filled.** Cross-reference the_princess.md Section 10.4 — **the game does not suggest it, does not prompt it, and nothing rewards it.**

### 5.5 The Hutches

**Cross-reference:** house_arion.md Section 7.4, the_princess.md Section 6.

Six of them. Well-built. Maintained. Empty.

**Full Vision:** they can be filled too. **Nothing rewards this either and no NPC ever comments.**

### 5.6 THE HALL

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_music.md Section 5, princess_quest_the_return.md Section 7.3.

The Great Hall can be restored to use. Cross-reference house_arion.md Section 4.3 — two hundred people, musicians in the gallery, gold light.

**And the musicians play her theme, because it is a dance tune, and they have always played it, and they do not know.**

**Design law:** cross-reference highmoore_music.md Section 5 — **no reaction shot, no camera move, no dialogue.** It is in the background of a restored hall full of people having a good evening.

## 6. THE FOUR PATHS

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_the_return.md Section 6.

### 6.1 THE STANDARD PATH

Belos destroyed, letter read, stair refused. Everything in this document is available.

### 6.2 IF HE TURNED WEST AT ARION

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_arion_falls.md Section 8.4.

**Belos stands.** Aldric Belos is alive. Cross-reference house_belos.md Section 3.5 — and he learns, eventually, why an Arion daughter died on his ground, and cross-reference Section 6.5.

**The reconstruction is entirely different:** Highmoore keeps two Great Houses, Arion is decapitated but Belos is not gone, and cross-reference Section 3 — **there is far less to rebuild and far more to negotiate.**

**Design note:** this path's content is diplomatic rather than constructive, and it is the harder version.

### 6.3 IF HE WENT TO THE STAIR

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_belos.md Section 9.5, princess_quest_the_return.md Section 6.3.

**No reconstruction content is available.**

Cross-reference highmoore_overview.md Pillar 5 — the region remains open, the Crystal Caves remain traversable, and **every rebuilding option in this document is absent from the interaction menus, with no explanation given anywhere.**

Cross-reference the_wizard.md Section 7.4 — Ellis has not spoken since.

### 6.4 IF HE MISSED THE LETTER

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_arion_falls.md Sections 4.5 and 8.5.

**The letter is still on the pillow.**

Cross-reference house_arion.md Section 2.3 — her chambers are in the castle Jake did not burn, in a household that is intact, and cross-reference Section 5.1: **Master Odo has not had the room cleared, because nobody told him to.**

**Design law:** a player can walk back into that room at any point in the reconstruction. Months later. Years of in-game time later.

**And the letter is where Alys left it.**

Cross-reference the_princess.md Section 9 — **reading it then is the worst moment Dark Arisen contains, and nothing warns anyone.**

### 6.5 ALDRIC, IF HE LIVED

**Cross-reference:** Section 6.2, house_belos.md Section 3.5.

**Full Vision:** on the turned-west path, Aldric Belos eventually learns everything — the truce, the four men, and what his not-looking cost.

**He does not send for Jake and Jake does not send for him.** Cross-reference house_belos.md Section 3.5 — *"I've had four years and I still don't know."*

**And then it is fourteen years and he still does not.**

## 7. THE POLITICAL CONSEQUENCE

### 7.1 Cadwall

**Cross-reference:** Section 2.2, the_lesser_houses.md Section 2.1.

**Lord Gwilym moves.** Slowly, legally, and with masons.

**Design note:** cross-reference the_lesser_houses.md Section 2.1 — he has never lost a holding permanently in forty years, and he has never taken one either, and **the reconstruction is the first time he does.**

He is not a villain. He is a careful man taking what is available, and cross-reference house_system.md Section 8: **that is what Highmoore's system does, correctly administered.**

### 7.2 The Sworn Roads

**Cross-reference:** Section 3.4.

**A road sworn by somebody who is not a House has never happened.**

Cross-reference house_system.md Section 4.3, colonial_war/castle_ownership.md Section 10 — and cross-reference Section 4.4: **this is where the Governor Problem starts in Highmoore**, and the ambient dialogue registers it before Jake does.

### 7.3 Ashby

**Cross-reference:** the_lesser_houses.md Section 3.1.

*"We're not a House. We're a table everyone else eats off."*

**Full Vision:** with Belos gone and Cadwall slow, **Ashby holds its own fields for four consecutive seasons for the first time in forty years**, and Ser Edmund does not know what to do with the surplus, and cross-reference style_bible.md Pillar 4: **it is the only genuinely happy outcome in Phase 8 and it is small and it is about grain.**

- Maerwyn's one interaction
- The gallery gap and the hutches, fillable and unrewarded

## 10. STYLE BIBLE COMPLIANCE

- ✅ **Pillar 1 (Staggered Register):** Maerwyn's five words about a warm hall, repeated forever. Strand's cheerful third rebuild. Ashby not knowing what to do with a surplus.
- ✅ **Pillar 2 (Lore Through Objects):** a gap in a gallery that can be filled and is not rewarded. Six hutches that can be filled and are not remarked on. A letter still on a pillow because nobody told Odo to clear the room.
- ✅ **Pillar 3 (Layered Death):** clearing the western range means clearing the undercroft, and the design's instruction is unchanged: **the game does not show what is down there.**
- ✅ **Pillar 4 (Layered Humor):** Lord Merrick Strand discussing his improvements at length. Ashby's grain problem, which is the only happy ending in Phase 8 and is about wheat.
- ✅ **Pillar 5 (Theme — Revenge):** forty hours of building something genuinely excellent in a basin he broke, with no meter, no forgiveness, nobody thanking him, and musicians in a restored hall playing her dance tune in the background because they have always played it and do not know.

## 11. CONCLUSION

What Comes After. A basin with one Great House under rubble and another with no lord, a feud map with no procedure for it, a careful man in a blue and black castle taking a holding for the first time in forty years because there is finally one available, and a table everyone used to eat off holding its own fields for four seasons running and not knowing what to do with the grain.

Roads that can be sworn by somebody who is not a House, which has never happened. A bridge at a ford where everyone's father fought. Forges connected to forges that have never heard of each other, through a mountain nobody in this country knows has another side.

And a woman in a castle who has known for two years, who will not accuse him and will not thank him, and who says *you'll want the small hall, it's warmer, Odo will see to it* — every time, for the rest of the game.

A gap in a gallery that can be filled. Six hutches that can be filled. A room nobody cleared because nobody told Odo to. A restored hall with two hundred people in it having a good evening, and musicians in the gallery playing a dance tune they have always played.

**Phase 8 Document 25 of ~30.**

**It does not balance anything. Build it anyway.** 🔨
