## 2. REXA & MORAN — THE GREEN DEPTHS

### 2.1 THE CENOTE OF THE FIRST MOTHER *(Tier B)*

**Discovery:** environmental — a sinkhole in the deep jungle, invisible until the player is at its lip. Cross-reference physics/vegetation_physics.md Section 5.3.
**Puzzle Language:** Rexan (water routing)
**Hazard:** water / drowning

**The Image:** the descent ends in a flooded chamber where sunlight from the sinkhole above hits the water at exactly one hour, and the whole room turns green-gold. Cross-reference physics/sun_shadow_systems.md and physics/water_physics.md — the light shaft is a timing mechanic and it is also the reason people come here.

**The Boss:** **The Keeper Below** — a vast, ancient caiman that has lived in the cenote for longer than the Empire has existed. Cross-reference fauna/sea_animals.md and land_animals.md. It does not guard anything. It simply lives here and the chamber is its.

**The Reward:** offerings, centuries of them. Cross-reference indigenous_liberation_alliance.md Section 2.2 — **Mama Jacinta's network knows this place and did not tell Jake about it until Trust Tier 3.** Everything in it belongs to somebody.

---

### 2.2 LA GARGANTA VERDE — "The Green Throat" *(Tier B)*

**Discovery:** environmental — a cave mouth behind a waterfall
**Puzzle Language:** Rexan (root circuits)
**Hazard:** vegetation / rot

**The Image:** a colonial survey party's camp, forty years old, exactly where they left it — and the jungle has grown *through* it. A tent with a tree in it. Cross-reference physics/vegetation_physics.md Section 2.

**The Boss:** **The Survey** — what happened to them. Cross-reference dungeon_design_philosophy.md Section 6.2 (Remnants) and style_bible.md Pillar 3: the fight is short and the design does not explain it.

**The Reward:** the survey maps — cross-reference dungeon_design_philosophy.md Section 3.1 (documentary discovery): they mark two other sites, and one of them is wrong in an informative way.

---

### 2.3 THE ROOT TEMPLE OF IX'CHEL *(Tier B)*

**Discovery:** cartographic — Rexan map-language required
**Puzzle Language:** Rexan (flowering sequence)
**Hazard:** vegetation / vertical falls

**The Image:** a pyramid entirely swallowed by a single strangler fig, so that the temple and the tree are structurally one object. Cross-reference physics/vegetation_physics.md Section 4 — the vine traversal is *inside* the tree that is inside the temple.

**The Boss:** **The Canopy Jaguar** — cross-reference fauna/land_animals.md: not the Patriarch, but from the same line, and it fights vertically.

**The Reward:** a ceremonial piece Doña Carmen Ruiz would refuse to sell. Cross-reference npcs/luxury_merchants.md Section 3.3.

---

### 2.4 THE DROWNED PLANTATION *(Tier B)*

**Discovery:** environmental / documentary — a failed 1780s Imperial venture, flooded when a river changed course
**Puzzle Language:** Colonial (clockwork — the sluice mechanisms still work)
**Hazard:** water / structural

**The Image:** the great house's ballroom, waist-deep, with the chandelier still hanging and fish moving through it.

**The Boss:** **The Overseer's Dogs** — descendants, gone feral across ninety years, denned in the stables. Cross-reference fauna/land_animals.md.

**The Reward:** the plantation's ledgers — **and cross-reference imperial_colonies.md Section 4.6: these are older than Nueva Esperanza's, and they name families whose descendants are still in the cane.**

---

### 2.5 THE HOLLOW CITY *(Tier C — Major Complex)*

**Discovery:** relational — cross-reference indigenous_liberation_alliance.md Section 3.5: Trust Tier 3 with the Cultural Web. Mama Jacinta tells Jake it exists. She does not offer to come.
**Puzzle Language:** Rexan (all three types, escalating)
**Hazard:** vegetation, water, and darkness in sequence

**The Image:** eleven square kilometres of pre-colonial city under the canopy, and the canopy is so complete that from above there is nothing there at all. **The Empire has never found it. It is thirty miles from Puerto de la Corona.**

**Structure:** four sub-areas, two bosses, interlocking shortcuts, and a full Tier C sense of place.

**The Bosses:** **The Watcher at the Gate** (a guardian construct, still working) and **The Thing in the Reservoir** (fauna, and the design should not name what it is).

**The Reward:** the city's own records — cross-reference npcs/the_named_dead.md Section 5.2: the indigenous relationship to their dead, and this is where a great many of them are. **And an ancient map that opens two Tier E sites.**

## 3. FJORDLUND — THE COLD DEPTHS

### 3.1 THE WHALE-BONE BARROW *(Tier B)*

**Discovery:** environmental — a mound on a headland that is not a natural mound
**Puzzle Language:** Fjordlund (saga sequence)
**Hazard:** cold / darkness

**The Image:** the burial chamber's roof is the ribcage of a whale, and it has been standing for six hundred years.

**The Boss:** **The Barrow-Keeper** — cross-reference dungeon_design_philosophy.md Section 6.2 (Remnants). It was a person. It was buried here to keep something, and it has.

**The Reward:** grave-goods, and cross-reference npcs/healer_mormor_astrid.md Section 9 — **Mormor Astrid knows whose barrow it is, and taking from it is a conversation.**

---

### 3.2 THE DROWNED MEAD HALL *(Tier B)*

**Discovery:** environmental — visible at low tide only. Cross-reference physics/water_physics.md.
**Puzzle Language:** Fjordlund (tide windows)
**Hazard:** water / tidal timer

**The Image:** the long hall, floors flooded, and the mead benches still in their rows — and twice a day the whole room drains and you can walk it dry for forty minutes.

**The Boss:** **The Last Guest** — and the fight has a tide timer. Cross-reference dungeon_design_philosophy.md Section 6.3: the dungeon's hazard, weaponised.

**The Reward:** the hall's drinking horn, and cross-reference npcs/tavern_keepers.md Section 4.1 — **Master Halvdan will put it on the wall at Bjornshallen and tell the story properly, which he has been telling wrong for thirty years.**

---

### 3.3 THE ICE CAVES OF SVART FJORD *(Tier B)*

**Discovery:** environmental
**Puzzle Language:** Fjordlund (freeze/melt routing)
**Hazard:** cold / ice traversal. Cross-reference physics/snow_ice_physics.md.

**The Image:** a chamber where the ice is clear enough to see forty metres down through it, and there is a ship down there.

**The Boss:** **The White Below** — cross-reference fauna/sea_animals.md, and cross-reference npcs/healer_mormor_astrid.md Section 7: it is not the Ghost Reindeer, and the design should let players briefly fear that it is.

**The Reward:** cold-forged material, cross-reference npcs/blacksmiths_and_armorers.md Section 4 — Halvor Eriksson and Astrid Halvorsdotter both want it and will not compete for it.

---

### 3.4 THE STONE FIELD'S UNDER-CHAMBER *(Tier B)*

**Discovery:** relational — Trust Tier 3, Patient Coast. Cross-reference indigenous_liberation_alliance.md Section 2.5.
**Puzzle Language:** Fjordlund (rune genealogies)
**Hazard:** darkness / cold

**The Image:** beneath the standing stones for the sea-taken — cross-reference npcs/the_named_dead.md Section 5.3 — there is a chamber, and the names carved on the stones above continue down the walls. **Thousands of them. Going back.**

**The Boss:** none. **This dungeon has no boss.** Cross-reference dungeon_design_philosophy.md Principle 1.5 by deliberate exception — it is the one Tier B site in the game with nothing at the bottom but the names.

**The Reward:** cross-reference npcs/named_crew_deep_dives.md Section 2 — **if Bjorn is dead, his name is down here already, carved by someone before Mira knew.** The design does not put a marker on it.

---

### 3.5 THE HALL UNDER THE GLACIER *(Tier C)*

**Discovery:** cartographic — Fjordlund saga-map, and cross-reference dungeon_design_philosophy.md Section 5.4: the saga *is* the map, told at Bjornshallen.
**Puzzle Language:** Fjordlund (all types)
**Hazard:** ice, cold, structural

**The Image:** a jarl's hall, complete, preserved in the glacier for eight hundred years, with everything and everyone still in it.

**The Bosses:** **The Hearth-Guard** and **The Jarl** — and the second is Style D in a way the design should handle very carefully.

**The Reward:** the archipelago's oldest continuous cultural artifact, and **Jarl Olaf's people would like it back**. Cross-reference indigenous_liberation_alliance.md.

## 4. ASHENMOOR — THE BURNING DEPTHS

### 4.1 THE OBSIDIAN VAULT *(Tier B)*

**Discovery:** environmental — a fissure in a lava field
**Puzzle Language:** Ashenmoor (heat channelling)
**Hazard:** heat. Cross-reference physics/lava_heat_physics.md.

**The Image:** a chamber lined entirely in polished volcanic glass, and the player's torch reflects in every surface — **there are a hundred Jakes in the room and one of them moves wrong.**

**The Boss:** **The Reflection** — and the design should keep the explanation to itself.

**The Reward:** legendary obsidian, cross-reference npcs/blacksmiths_and_armorers.md Section 5 — Pak Setyo needs this and has needed it for years.

---

### 4.2 THE ASH-BURIED STREET *(Tier B)*

**Discovery:** environmental / documentary — a 1741 eruption town
**Puzzle Language:** Ashenmoor (ash-fall reading)
**Hazard:** collapse / gas. Cross-reference physics/sand_desert_physics.md, lava_heat_physics.md.

**The Image:** the ash preserved everyone exactly where they were standing. A market. Mid-transaction. Cross-reference style_bible.md Pillar 3 — **positioned, never posed**, and the design's restraint here is the whole point.

**The Boss:** **The Thing That Dug Down** — fauna, and it has been feeding here for a hundred and thirty-five years.

**The Reward:** the town's name, which nobody living remembers, and **Mbah Seruni does.** Cross-reference npcs/desert_sage_mbah_seruni.md.

---

### 4.3 THE SINGING CAVERNS *(Tier B)*

**Discovery:** environmental — audible before visible. Cross-reference physics/sound_physics.md and sand_desert_physics.md Section 7.6 (singing sand).
**Puzzle Language:** Ashenmoor (acoustic-star hybrid)
**Hazard:** heat / disorientation

**The Image:** wind through the caverns produces a sustained chord, and it changes as the player moves. **The puzzle is the music, and the music is the map.**

**The Boss:** **The Silence** — an encounter defined by the chord stopping.

**The Reward:** cross-reference indigenous_liberation_alliance.md Section 5.2 — an Ashenmoor star-map, and one of the few that survived.

---

### 4.4 THE VENT-SHRINE *(Tier B)*

**Discovery:** relational — Trust Tier 3, Mountain Paths
**Puzzle Language:** Ashenmoor (shadow-hour)
**Hazard:** heat / gas

**The Image:** a shrine built directly over a volcanic vent, and the mountain breathes through it — the whole structure exhales, on a cycle, warm.

**The Boss:** none by default. **If Jake supported Fuerte San Rafael, the shrine has been cleared and there is an Imperial detachment here.** Cross-reference imperial_colonies.md Section 5 and Cruz's list. The dungeon changes based on the war.

**The Reward:** cross-reference npcs/desert_sage_mbah_seruni.md — this may be one of the shrines on Cruz's list. It may be the one.

---

### 4.5 THE MOUNTAIN'S OWN *(Tier C)*

**Discovery:** relational — Trust Tier 4, Mountain Paths. Cross-reference indigenous_liberation_alliance.md Section 3.5.
**Puzzle Language:** Ashenmoor (all types)
**Hazard:** heat, gas, and lava flow. Cross-reference physics/lava_heat_physics.md throughout.

**The Image:** a lava tube system that goes down further than seems possible, and at the deepest accessible point the rock is warm to the touch and moving very slightly. **The mountain is alive down here in a way that is not a metaphor.**

**The Bosses:** **The Ash-Walker** and **The Deep Heat**.

**The Reward:** cross-reference bosses/ashen_wyrm.md — **material evidence of the Wyrm's passage, ancient, and Mbah Seruni saw this creature as a child in 1805.** The Hollow City has records; the Mountain has the thing itself.

## 5. THE SEA — THE SUNKEN DEPTHS

### 5.1 THE REEF OF NAILS *(Tier B)*

**Discovery:** environmental — a ship graveyard on a reef. Cross-reference physics/water_physics.md, ship_physics.md Section 7.
**Puzzle Language:** Colonial (a wreck's own mechanisms)
**Hazard:** water / breath

**The Image:** forty ships across two centuries, stacked, and the newest is Imperial and eleven years old.

**The Boss:** **The Reef Itself** — an environmental boss encounter. Cross-reference fauna/sea_animals.md.

**The Reward:** salvage, and one ship's log that names a survivor who should not have survived.

---

### 5.2 THE GHOST HULL *(Tier B)*

**Discovery:** environmental — a derelict, upright, adrift, crewless
**Puzzle Language:** Colonial (acoustics — the hull carries sound)
**Hazard:** structural / darkness. Cross-reference physics/sound_physics.md Section 9 (underwater acoustics) and destruction_physics.md.

**The Image:** the ship is intact, provisioned, and the galley fire is *warm*.

**The Boss:** cross-reference dungeon_design_philosophy.md Section 8.2 — **this is one of the two or three survivor encounters**, and it is not a boss fight until it is.

**The Reward:** cross-reference bosses/crimson_armada.md — the hull is Crimson, and the log is a lead.

---

### 5.3 THE SUNKEN CHAPEL *(Tier B)*

**Discovery:** cartographic
**Puzzle Language:** Colonial (cathedral acoustics, underwater). Cross-reference physics/sound_physics.md Sections 4 and 9.
**Hazard:** water / breath timer

**The Image:** a colonial chapel, fully submerged, and the bell still rings when the current moves it. **Cross-reference physics/sound_physics.md Section 9.2 — sound travels faster and further underwater, and sailors above have been hearing this bell for a century.**

**The Boss:** **The Congregation**.

**The Reward:** cross-reference npcs/named_crew_deep_dives.md Section 5 — **Father Salvio has opinions about this place**, and they are not the ones the player expects.

---

### 5.4 THE DEEP HARBOUR *(Tier C)*

**Discovery:** relational + cartographic — requires the feared waters opened. Cross-reference indigenous_liberation_alliance.md Section 5.3.
**Puzzle Language:** hybrid (Rexan water logic and Colonial mechanism)
**Hazard:** water, pressure, darkness

**The Image:** an entire pre-colonial harbour, submerged when the sea rose, with the quays and the warehouses and the moorings intact — **and ships still tied up at them.**

**The Bosses:** **The Harbourmaster** and **What Came In With The Tide**.

**The Reward:** the archipelago's oldest navigational knowledge, and cross-reference indigenous_liberation_alliance.md Section 5.3: **this opens two more feared-water regions on its own.**

## 6. COLONIAL — THE EMPIRES' OWN DARK

### 6.1 THE VALDEÑA MINE *(Tier B)*

**Discovery:** environmental / documentary — abandoned Imperial silver works
**Puzzle Language:** Colonial (clockwork drainage)
**Hazard:** structural / flooding / gas

**The Image:** the deepest gallery is full of tools, dropped where they were held, and the shift bell is still hanging.

**The Boss:** **The Shift** — cross-reference dungeon_design_philosophy.md Section 6.2 (The Colonial Dead).

**The Reward:** the mine's labour rolls — **cross-reference imperial_colonies.md Section 4.6 and Section 2.4 above: the third set of ledgers, and together they trace families across a century.**

---

### 6.2 THE SIEGE TUNNELS OF LA CIUDADELA *(Tier B)*

**Discovery:** relational — Trust Tier 3, Cultural Web. **Cross-reference castle_catalog.md Section 3.3: this is the undercity, and it is also a dungeon.**
**Puzzle Language:** hybrid (Colonial engineering laid over pre-colonial foundations)
**Hazard:** flooding / structural

**The Image:** Imperial 1651 engineering visibly built on top of something much older, and in one chamber the join is exposed: two architectures, two centuries apart, load-bearing together.

**The Boss:** **The Foundation** — a guardian that predates the fort above it and does not know the fort is there.

**The Reward:** the undercity route into La Ciudadela. Cross-reference castle_sieges.md Section 4.2 — **the best tactical asset in Phase 7 is at the bottom of a dungeon.**

---

### 6.3 THE PLAGUE VAULT *(Tier B)*

**Discovery:** documentary — a sealed Imperial site, referenced in the Conquest Archives
**Puzzle Language:** Colonial (locks, and the Empire locks things)
**Hazard:** structural / darkness / contamination

**The Image:** the Empire sealed this vault in 1782 with people still inside it, and the seal held.

**The Boss:** **The Sealed** — Style D, and cross-reference style_bible.md Pillar 3 at maximum restraint.

**The Reward:** the sealing order, signed, with a name on it. Cross-reference imperial_colonies.md Section 3.6 — **it belongs in the Conquest Archives and the Archives do not mention it.**

---

### 6.4 THE COMPANY WORKS *(Tier C)*

**Discovery:** environmental / documentary — an HAC industrial site, abandoned when it stopped paying. Cross-reference krone_colonies.md Section 3.
**Puzzle Language:** Colonial (industrial clockwork at scale)
**Hazard:** structural / machinery

**The Image:** the machines still run. Nobody has been here in nine years and the waterwheel is still turning and the looms are still moving, weaving nothing, into a room that is full of it.

**The Bosses:** **The Works** (environmental) and **The Last Shift Supervisor** (survivor variant — cross-reference dungeon_design_philosophy.md Section 8.2).

**The Reward:** the works' books — cross-reference krone_colonies.md Section 3.4: **the Company withdrew and left the indentured contracts enforceable. These are those people. Some of them are still nearby.**

Cross-reference npcs/regional_populations.md Section 9, dungeon_design_philosophy.md Section 5.6.

### 7.2 THE FIRST HOUSE *(Tier B)*

**Discovery:** environmental — it is the first building in the settlement and the door is open
**Puzzle Language:** **Fjordlund, but wrong.** The tide table is for a tide that does not happen here.
**Hazard:** none. **The dungeon has no hazard and this is the most frightening thing about it.**

**The Image:** a meal, set for six, half-eaten, and not spoiled.

**The Boss:** none.

**The Reward:** nothing. **Cross-reference dungeon_design_philosophy.md Principle 1.5 — the second deliberate exception, and the design should let players complete it and find that they have received nothing at all.**

## 8. CROSS-DUNGEON SYSTEMS

### 8.1 The Ledger Trilogy

Three sets of labour records in three dungeons and one castle:
- The Drowned Plantation's ledgers (1780s — Section 2.4)
- The Valdeña Mine's rolls (Section 6.1)
- Fuerte Esperanza's labor ledgers (cross-reference castle_catalog.md Section 4.6)
- The Company Works' books (Section 6.4)

**Together they trace families across a hundred years**, and cross-reference imperial_colonies.md Section 4.6: freed families use these to find each other. **This is the single most valuable non-combat reward chain in Phase 7 and it is assembled from four unrelated places.**

### 8.2 The Map Chain

Cross-reference dungeon_design_philosophy.md Section 3.1. Dungeons contain maps to dungeons:
- The Green Throat's survey maps → two sites
- The Hollow City's ancient map → two Tier E sites
- The Singing Caverns' star-map → Ashenmoor Tier E
- The Deep Harbour's charts → two feared-water regions

**Design intent:** the exploration layer self-propagates. Finding one dungeon is finding three.

### 8.3 The War-Reactive Dungeons

Some sites change with the war state:
- **The Vent-Shrine** (Section 4.4) — cleared or intact depending on San Rafael
- **The Company Works** (Section 6.4) — the survivor's situation depends on whether Albion withdrew or was broken
- **The Stone Field Under-Chamber** (Section 3.4) — cross-reference npcs/named_crew_deep_dives.md: Bjorn's name is there or it is not

### 8.4 The Return Question

Cross-reference dungeon_design_philosophy.md Section 7.3, indigenous_liberation_alliance.md Section 2.3.

**Approximately forty artifacts across this catalog belong to living communities.** Sell, keep, or return — and cross-reference npcs/black_market_fences.md Section 6.2: Tito Diego's network exists to handle exactly this, and every returned piece is +5 network strength and something that cannot be bought back.

## 9. MVP CORE vs FULL VISION

### 9.1 MVP CORE

- **Three Tier B dungeons:** the Cenote of the First Mother (Rexan), the Drowned Mead Hall (Fjordlund), the Obsidian Vault (Ashenmoor)
- One Tier C: the Hollow City
- The map chain functioning between them
- The return question active on all artifacts
- Three Tier 4 bosses across three hazard categories

### 9.2 FULL VISION

- All 20 Tier B, 5 Tier C, 4 Tier D, 6 Tier E sites
- The complete ledger trilogy across four locations
- All war-reactive variants
- Region 06's unreliable grammar including The First House's deliberate nothing
- The survivor encounters (Ghost Hull, Company Works)
- The Stone Field's Bjorn variant

## 10. STYLE BIBLE COMPLIANCE

- ✅ **Pillar 1 (Staggered Register):** near-total silence, broken by the sages who teach the puzzle languages, Master Halvdan's corrected saga, and two or three survivors whose register is its own thing entirely.
- ✅ **Pillar 2 (Lore Through Objects):** the ledger trilogy. The drinking horn. The sealing order with a name on it. The town's name that only Mbah Seruni remembers. Forty artifacts that belong to somebody.
- ✅ **Pillar 3 (Layered Death):** the ash-preserved market, positioned and never posed. The Sealed. The Jarl. Bjorn's name carved before Mira knew. And no child remains anywhere in this catalog.
- ✅ **Pillar 4 (Layered Humor):** essentially absent, by design. Cross-reference dungeon_design_philosophy.md Section 11 — the humour resumes on the ship afterwards, in the light, and the contrast is what makes both work.
- ✅ **Pillar 5 (Theme — Revenge):** The First House gives the player nothing at all after a full dungeon, and the Stone Field's under-chamber may have Bjorn's name in it, carved by a stranger, before anyone told his sister. The depths are where Phase 7's war stops mattering and something older is still going on.

## 12. CONCLUSION

The Archipelago's Depths. A cenote that turns green-gold for one hour a day, with something ancient in the water that is not guarding anything. A jungle survey camp with a tree growing through the tent. Eleven square kilometres of city under a canopy thirty miles from the Empire's capital, which the Empire has never found. A mead hall that drains twice a day. A chamber of names beneath the stones for the sea-taken, going down and back. A room lined in volcanic glass where a hundred reflections move and one of them is wrong. A market preserved in ash, mid-transaction, since 1741. A chapel under the water whose bell sailors have been hearing for a century. A vault the Empire sealed in 1782 with people inside, and the seal held.

And in Region 06, a house with the door open, a meal set for six and half-eaten and not spoiled, no hazard, no boss, and no reward of any kind.

**Phase 7 Subsystem 15 of 16 — complete.**

**Thirty-five doors. Forty things that belong to somebody. One that gives you nothing.** 🗿🌊
