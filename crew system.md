**Status:** Draft v1.0

## 8. IMPLEMENTATION

```cpp
struct Crew {
    NamedMember named[5];       // permanent unless dead
    Specialist  berths[8];      // optional
    uint8_t     hands;          // 40–90
    uint8_t     semiNamed;      // ~12 of the hands
    uint8_t     morale;         // never displayed §4.1
    uint8_t     share;          // pay, per chapter
};
```

**Prohibited:** party-select screens · approval notifications · affinity meters · **any UI that lists the crew as combat units.**

## 9. STYLE BIBLE COMPLIANCE

- ✅ **Pillar 1:** five named people in five registers, plus twelve semi-named hands with one line each.
- ✅ **Pillar 2:** a shelf of black candles and itemised death notices. An empty hammock nobody has claimed.
- ✅ **Pillar 3:** losses are permanent, teachers are not replaced, and the mechanical role comes back while the person does not.
- ✅ **Pillar 4:** Big Tom saying he does not fancy going into a mountain after his captain.
- ✅ **Pillar 5:** the one crew member with no mechanical function whatsoever is the only one who asks a second question.

## 10. CONCLUSION

The People on the Ship. Five named, up to eight specialists, and forty to ninety hands of whom about a dozen have a face and a name Jake uses and one line they always say. Nobody standing in a designated spot waiting to be talked to — a working ship on a schedule, where morale is read by whether anyone is singing on the mid-deck and whether there is an empty hammock nobody has claimed.

No party select. Who comes ashore is asked, and two of them will say no to certain places, and none of them come to Highmoore, because the loneliness is why the ball works.

All five are teachers and all five can be taken by the hostage algorithm, and a lost one is a lost node forever with no replacement, and the role gets refilled badly by a hand stepping up so that the function returns and the person does not.

And the only crew member with no mechanical effect on anything — no repair rate, no handling bonus, no combat contribution — is the one who asks the second question when Jake comes back through the mountain.
