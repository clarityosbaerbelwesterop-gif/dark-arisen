## 1. THE ECOLOGY

**Cross-reference:** fauna/regional_ecosystems.md for the archipelago's framework, physics/vegetation_physics.md.

### 1.1 The Character

Cross-reference highmoore_region.md Section 3.1 — heather, gorse, bracken, bog, and grass, under a sky that is overcast more often than not.

**Highmoore's ecology is sparse, hardy, and legible.** Cross-reference fauna/regional_ecosystems.md — after the archipelago's jungle density, the moor's emptiness is the point: **a player can see an animal at eight hundred metres.**

### 1.2 The Five Zones

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_region.md Section 2.3.

- **HIGH MOOR** — grouse, hare, raptors, and very little else
- **THE FOLDS** — farmland edge; deer, fox, rabbit, and everything that lives near people
- **THE WATERLANDS** — cross-reference physics/water_physics.md: wildfowl, otter, eel, heron
- **THE STONECOUNTRY** — goats, raptors, and cave-dwellers
- **THE BURNT MARCH** — cross-reference highmoore_region.md Section 2.3: **scrub reclaiming it, and the best hunting in the basin because nobody goes there**

## 2. THE HUNT AS INSTITUTION

**Cross-reference:** house_system.md Section 4, fauna/hunting_system.md.

### 2.1 It Is Political

**Cross-reference:** house_system.md Section 5.

The right to hunt a holding is a House right, and cross-reference house_system.md Section 3.3 — **hunting across a boundary is a raid**, formally, with a settlement procedure attached.

**Design note:** a player who hunts casually across the moor without checking whose ground they are on **generates a genuine political incident**, and it is handled by lawyers rather than soldiers, and it is very funny for about ten minutes.

### 2.2 The Great Hunt

**Cross-reference:** house_system.md Section 4.5, house_arion.md Section 4.3.

At the end of the burning season, before the wet. Cross-reference princess_quest_the_ball.md Section 2.4 — **the same window as the levy coming home**, and the Houses hunt together under truce.

**Cross-reference the_lesser_houses.md Section 3.6** — House Carrick is second only to Belos at it and bitter about it.

### 2.3 Jake Can Be Invited

Cross-reference house_system.md Section 6.3. **Full Vision:** a Great Hunt is available as free-roam content, with four Houses present, and cross-reference style_bible.md Pillar 4 — **it is one of the funniest sequences in Phase 8** and it is in the same window as the ball.

## 4. THE GAME

### 4.1 Grouse

**Cross-reference:** fauna/birds.md, physics/sound_physics.md.

The moor's signature bird. Cross-reference physics/sound_physics.md — **the flush is the loudest sudden sound on the High Moor** and it is used deliberately: a player crossing open ground in silence gets startled by grouse roughly once an hour and it never stops working.

### 4.2 Hare

Cross-reference fauna/land_animals.md. Fast, and cross-reference physics/movement_physics.md — **uncatchable on foot and barely catchable mounted.**

### 4.3 Red Deer

The Folds and the Burnt March. Cross-reference fauna/hunting_system.md — Highmoore's largest game and the Great Hunt's actual quarry.

### 4.4 Wildfowl

Cross-reference the_lesser_houses.md Section 3.4 — **House Fen's whole specialty**, and Ser Owain will explain fowling at extraordinary length.

## 5. THE PREDATORS

### 5.1 Fox

Everywhere, and cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md — an ordinary agricultural nuisance that the yeomanry discuss constantly.

### 5.2 The Fell Wolf

**Cross-reference:** fauna/land_animals.md.

Rare, high country, and cross-reference highmoore_region.md Section 2.3 — **the Stonecountry and the high fells.** Not a boss, not legendary, and genuinely dangerous to a man alone on foot.

**Design note:** cross-reference mounted_travel.md Section 5.3 — **horses bolt from them at low bond**, and that is where a player usually first meets one.

### 5.3 Raptors

Cross-reference fauna/birds.md — hen harriers, merlins, and eagles on the high fells. Cross-reference house_system.md Section 3.2: **House Brae's sigil is not a raptor and House Cadwall's is a tower, and nobody in Highmoore uses birds on banners**, which is unusual and never explained.

## 6. THE TWO THAT ARE NOT FAUNA

### 6.1 The Horse

**Cross-reference:** mounted_travel.md.

Not in this document. Cross-reference mounted_travel.md Principle 1.1 — **it is a relationship, not a species**, and it has its own.

### 6.2 The Rabbits

**Cross-reference:** the_princess.md Section 6, house_arion.md Section 7.4.

**Six hutches in the Arion gardens. Well-built. Maintained. Empty.**

**Design law:** rabbits exist in Highmoore's ecology as ordinary fauna, in the Folds, in numbers, huntable and unremarkable — **and the game never once draws a line between them and the hutches.**

Cross-reference style_bible.md Pillar 2 — **the player draws it or does not.**

## 7. THE LIGHT

**Cross-reference:** light_elves.md Sections 2 and 7.

### 7.1 The Absence

Cross-reference light_elves.md Section 7.1 — the bare ground, two hundred metres across, where nothing has grown in living memory.

**Nothing lives there either.** Cross-reference fauna/regional_ecosystems.md — no grouse, no hare, no fox, no birds overhead.

**Design law:** cross-reference light_elves.md Section 1.1 — **the game never remarks on this**, and a player who has spent twenty hours learning to read a moor notices the silence before they notice the ground.

### 7.2 The Animals and the Shaft

**Full Vision:** cross-reference light_elves.md Section 3.2 — **animals leave a stopped shaft.** Grouse flush, hare runs, and cross-reference mounted_travel.md Section 5.3: **the horse will not enter it at any bond level.**

Cross-reference light_elf_boss.md Section 3.3 — which is why the fight is on foot, and no document ever says so.

## 11. CONCLUSION

What Lives on the Moor. Grouse that flush loud enough to stop a man's heart on open ground, hares nothing catches, deer in the Burnt March because nobody goes there, and fell wolves in the high country that horses bolt from before their riders see them.

Kennels that are the best-kept building in a castle where everything else is functional. Terriers that are appalling and beloved. A House that is second-best at hounds and has been bitter about it for three generations.

Hunting rights that are House rights, so that following a deer across a boundary is formally a raid with a settlement procedure attached, resolved by lawyers, at length, and it is very funny for about ten minutes.

Rabbits in the Folds, in numbers, ordinary, huntable, unremarkable — and six empty hutches in a garden eight kilometres away that the game never once connects to them.

And two hundred metres of ground where nothing grows and nothing lives and no bird crosses overhead, which a player notices as silence before they notice as ground.
