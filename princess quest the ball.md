# THE BALL — MOVEMENTS ONE TO THREE

**Status:** Design Draft v1.0
**Phase:** Phase 8 — Highmoore (Movement Three — Document 14 of ~30)
**Related:** princess_quest_overview.md, the_princess.md, house_arion.md, house_system.md, highmoore_region.md, mounted_travel.md, princess_quest_the_lake.md, princess_quest_the_truth.md, npcs/children_and_families.md, physics/sound_physics.md, physics/sun_shadow_systems.md, physics/cloth_hair_physics.md, style_bible.md
**Purpose:** The full sequence design for the ride, the arrival, the hall, the meeting, the dance, the terrace, the night, and the morning — including all major dialogue, the lie, the rabbits, Maerwyn's line, and Lord Arion's ejection.
**Inspirations:** Red Dead Redemption 2 (Arthur and Mary's outings; the theatre sequence), Pride and Prejudice (the Netherfield ball as social geometry), The Leopard (Visconti's ball, forty-five unhurried minutes), Brideshead Revisited, Roman Holiday (a fixed number of hours, and both people know)

## 1. THE SEQUENCE'S BRIEF

### 1.1 What It Has to Achieve

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_overview.md Section 1.2.

By the end of this sequence the player must want it to work out. Not find her interesting. **Want it.**

### 1.2 The Length

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_overview.md Section 2.

Forty minutes of riding, fifty to seventy minutes in the hall, ten in the morning. **The longest uninterrupted non-combat stretch in Dark Arisen**, and cross-reference princess_quest_overview.md Section 5.1: no timers, no failure states, no urgency of any kind.

### 1.3 The Discipline

**No quest marker at any point.** Cross-reference princess_quest_overview.md Section 5.3 — **the player should not be certain they are in a questline until the letter arrives four hours later.**

## 2. MOVEMENT ONE — THE CASTLE ON THE HORIZON

### 2.1 The Trigger

There is none. Cross-reference highmoore_region.md Section 6.3 — Arion is the most beautiful thing on the skyline from the moment Jake steps out of the Crystal Caves, and the player rides toward it because of that, and nothing has prompted them.

### 2.2 The Ride

**Cross-reference:** mounted_travel.md, highmoore_region.md Section 2.4.

Forty minutes. Sworn road, safe, and cross-reference house_system.md Section 4.3 — Arion keeps the best roads in the Folds and the player will notice before they know why.

**What is on it:**
- Fortified farms, watched from. Cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md Section 2.3
- Two road wardens in white and gold who are polite
- A drover with a hundred sheep, who takes eleven minutes to move, and **the game does not let Jake go around him**
- Cross-reference physics/sun_shadow_systems.md — the light is late afternoon and the castle takes it well

### 2.3 The Growth

**Design law:** the castle is in frame for the entire forty minutes and it grows continuously. Cross-reference house_arion.md Section 2.1 — pale limestone washed white, copper roofs gone warm ochre, five towers, and the bell tower.

**And around minute thirty-two, the player hears the bell.** Cross-reference highmoore_region.md Section 1.3 — the same bell they heard from the mountain shelf, and cross-reference physics/sound_physics.md Section 2.2: this time it arrives less than a second late.

### 2.4 The Approach

The last kilometre is people. Cross-reference house_system.md Section 4.5, house_arion.md Section 4.3 — **the burning season has ended and the levy is coming home.**

Carts. Families walking out to meet men on the road. Someone's mother crying. A man with a boy on his shoulders. Cross-reference npcs/children_and_families.md — the warmth standard applies in full.

**Design note:** Jake rides into Arion in the middle of six hundred people having the best day of their year.

## 3. THE GATES

### 3.1 Guest-Right

**Cross-reference:** house_system.md Section 6.2.

Jake is stopped, asked *"whose man are you?"*, gives an answer (Section 5.1 — **the lie can be told here or deferred to the hall**), and is admitted.

**Nobody searches him. Nobody takes the sword.** Cross-reference crystal_katana.md Section 6.2 — **if he is carrying the Crystal Katana visibly, the gate guard's reaction is the first time in Highmoore anybody reacts to it properly**, and he is admitted anyway, and that is what guest-right means.

### 3.2 The Outer Yard

Cross-reference house_arion.md Section 2.3.

Chaos in the best sense. Horses being taken. Barrels. A man being embraced by three children. Master Odo shouting at somebody about the trestles.

### 3.3 The Choice

The player can:
- Go to the hall
- Explore the castle first — cross-reference house_arion.md Section 7.1: the stables, the gallery, **the gardens and the six empty hutches**
- Leave

**Cross-reference the_princess.md Section 4.1** — a player who explores may encounter Elowen before the ball, in the stables at dusk, and cross-reference Section 4.4: that changes the meeting.

## 4. MOVEMENT TWO — THE HALL

### 4.1 The Entrance

**Cross-reference:** house_arion.md Sections 2.4 and 4.3, physics/sound_physics.md, physics/sun_shadow_systems.md.

Two hundred people. Marble underfoot. Candles by the hundred. Musicians in the gallery. Warriors home from a season with their families. Children under the tables. Gold light and a warm short reverb built for music.

**Design law:** after two hours in the Crystal Caves and a day on open moor, this room must be overwhelming. Cross-reference physics/fire_physics.md Section 3.5 — the light budget for this scene is the largest interior in the game.

### 4.2 Jake in It

He is filthy. He has been in a mine and on a moor. Cross-reference physics/cloth_hair_physics.md — **the game does not clean him up**, and everyone in the room is dressed, and he is not.

**Nobody minds.** Cross-reference house_system.md Section 6.2 — guest-right is real and Arion is proud of keeping it.

### 4.3 Lord Cassian Arion

**Cross-reference:** house_arion.md Section 3.

He finds Jake within four minutes because that is what a good host does.

**CASSIAN:** *"You'll be the one from the road. Sit, sit — no, further up, you're a guest and my daughter will have my head if I've put you below the salt."*

**CASSIAN:** *"Ambassador. Ambassador. From — forgive me — from where, exactly?"*

*(and cross-reference house_arion.md Section 3.3: a beat, and then he lets it go, and **the letting-go is a courtesy and not suspicion**)*

**CASSIAN:** *"Well. You'll be hungry whatever you are."*

**Design law:** cross-reference house_arion.md Section 3.2 — **no foreshadowing that reads as villainy.** The player must like him. The player is supposed to like him.

### 4.4 The Voice From Behind

**Cross-reference:** the_princess.md Section 5.

**ELOWEN:** *"I've never seen you before."*

*(the user's own line, and it is the first thing she says, and the design keeps it exactly)*

She has been watching him for a while. Cross-reference the_princess.md Section 2.3 — she reads people, and a stranger who is filthy and armed and sitting above the salt is the most interesting thing in the room.

**If the player met her in the stables at dusk:** she says instead —

**ELOWEN:** *"You clean up worse than I expected."*

## 5. THE CONVERSATION

### 5.1 THE LIE

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_overview.md Section 4, house_system.md Section 6.1.

**ELOWEN:** *"And what brings a — what are you?"*

**Player options:**
- **[LIE]** *"An ambassador. My parents are in government."*
- **[TRUTH]** *"A ship's captain. From the other side of the mountain."*
- **[NOTHING]** *"Passing through."*

**If TRUTH:** she laughs. Genuinely, and warmly, and it is not cruel.
*"There isn't another side of the mountain. Try again, and this time make it duller — you'll be believed."*

**And then Jake lies anyway**, in the follow-up, because the truth was not available as a thing that could be believed. Cross-reference princess_quest_overview.md Section 4.1 — **the lie is a reflex, not a con.**

**If LIE or NOTHING:** she nods, and lets him finish, and then —

### 5.2 THE SWITCH

**Cross-reference:** the_princess.md Section 5.

**ELOWEN:** *"You're not an ambassador."*

*(a beat)*

**ELOWEN:** *"No — don't. I'm not going to tell anyone. I'd just rather we both knew where we are."*

**Design note:** the register drops here and does not come back up for the rest of the night. Cross-reference the_princess.md Section 5.1 — she performs High flawlessly for about ninety seconds and then stops, and everything after this is plain and dry and direct.

### 5.3 The Music

**ELOWEN:** *"They'll play four more before the tables come out. Do you dance?"*

**Player options:**
- **[YES]** — he does not
- **[NO]** — *"Good. Neither does anyone here. They just do it loudly."*
- **[DECLINE]** — cross-reference princess_quest_overview.md Section 3.2: **this ends the questline.** She is not offended. She says something kind and goes back to her evening and the player never sees her again.

## 6. THE DANCE

### 6.1 The Design

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_overview.md Section 5.2.

**It is not a minigame.** No rhythm prompts, no button sequence, no score.

It is a long single shot with the camera doing very little, and the player has control of nothing except when to talk. Cross-reference physics/cloth_hair_physics.md — **this is where that system does its best work in the game.**

### 6.2 Jake Cannot Dance

Cross-reference mounted_travel.md Section 12 (Pillar 4), the_princess.md Section 5.5 — **Jake is bad at everything that happens on land**, and she is kind about it, and that is the joke that runs from here to the lake.

**ELOWEN:** *"You're counting. Don't count. Nobody's watching and the ones who are can't do it either."*

### 6.3 What They Talk About

Cross-reference princess_quest_overview.md Section 5.2 — long branches, no hurry.

**Available topics (player-selected, six to ten exchanges):**
- The hall, the House, her father
- Highmoore, the war, the ford
- The sea — **and she has never seen it**, and cross-reference Section 7.4: this comes back
- What he does — the lie, extended, and cross-reference Section 5.2: she lets him build it
- What she does — cross-reference the_princess.md Section 9.3: *"you asked me at the ball what I do here. I never answered."*

**Design law:** she genuinely does not answer that one. She deflects, lightly, and the player may or may not notice.

### 6.4 The Room

Cross-reference physics/sound_physics.md — musicians, two hundred voices, cutlery, children. **The dialogue is mixed slightly too quiet against it**, and both of them lean in, and that is the whole staging.

## 7. THE TERRACE

### 7.1 Her Suggestion

**ELOWEN:** *"It's too loud and I'm too warm. Come outside. If my father asks, we went to look at the horses."*

**Player options:** accept, or decline (cross-reference Section 5.3 — declining ends it here).

### 7.2 Outside

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_region.md Section 3.2, physics/wind_systems.md, physics/sun_shadow_systems.md.

The terrace above the gardens. Wind. The moor in darkness going out further than the eye can follow. The hall's noise behind them, muffled, and cross-reference physics/sound_physics.md Section 2.4: **it gets quieter every time somebody opens and closes the door**, and the door opens four times during the scene.

### 7.3 What She Talks About

**Cross-reference:** the_princess.md Sections 4, 5.4.

Her father — *"the most reasonable man in this basin. Ask anyone. They'll all say it and they'll all mean it."*

Her grandmother's chair — *"my grandmother's chair is in the solar. My great-grandmother's chair is in the solar. I will have a chair."*

The abbey — cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md Section 5.3: Brother Padarn taught her Latin and thought she should have stayed and **was not joking, and she knew he was not joking, and she did not go.**

### 7.4 THE RABBITS

**Cross-reference:** the_princess.md Section 6, house_arion.md Section 7.4.

**ELOWEN:** *"I had rabbits. Six of them. I fed them every morning for four years."*

*(and she stops)*

**Player options:**
- **[ASK]** — *"What happened to them?"*
- **[SAY NOTHING]**

**If ASK:** cross-reference the_princess.md Section 8.3 — **she changes the subject.** Gently, immediately, and it is the only time all night she does that.

*"You've never told me a true thing about yourself. Go on. One. It doesn't have to be a good one."*

**Design law:** the deflection is the clue and it is the only one on the terrace. Cross-reference the_princess.md Section 6.4 — three touches across ten hours: the empty hutches, this, and one line in the letter.

### 7.5 She Asks About Him

**Cross-reference:** Section 7.4.

*"One true thing."*

**Player options:**
- **A true small thing** — *"I can't dance."* / *"I've never been on a horse until this week."*
- **A true large thing** — **the option to say Ethan's name exists here**, and cross-reference characters/ethan_harlow.md: it is the only place in Highmoore it can be said, and she does not know who that is, and she listens, and she does not ask a second question, and she does not offer comfort. She just listens.
- **Another lie**

**If Ethan is named:** cross-reference princess_quest_the_lake.md Section 6.3 — **it changes her last conversation.**

### 7.6 The Ask

**Cross-reference:** the user's original outline.

**ELOWEN:** *"I'm going to say something and you're going to be a gentleman about it either way."*

*(a beat)*

*"Would you come upstairs?"*

**Player options:** accept, or decline.

**If declined:** she is entirely fine. *"Right. Good. That's — yes. Fine."* And cross-reference princess_quest_overview.md Section 3.2 — **the questline continues.** The morning happens, the letter still comes, the lake still happens.

**Design note:** declining is not a rejection of the questline and the game must not punish it. The false letter's arithmetic is her father's fabrication regardless. Cross-reference the_princess.md Section 9.1.

## 8. THE NIGHT AND THE MORNING

### 8.1 The Night

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_overview.md Section 6.3, style_bible.md Pillar 3 (the restraint standard).

**Cut.** Cross-reference the game's established discipline — nothing depicted, and the design does not linger.

### 8.2 The Morning

**Cross-reference:** the user's original outline — the bath, and the feast.

Warm and unhurried. A bath, and cross-reference house_arion.md Section 2 — Jake, who has been in a mine and on a moor and in a hall, is finally clean, in the best room he has ever been in, and the sun is coming through.

Breakfast in the small hall. Cross-reference house_arion.md Section 4.2 — **Master Odo is extremely disapproving and does it silently and it is very funny.**

### 8.3 MAERWYN'S LINE

**Cross-reference:** the_princess.md Section 8.4, house_arion.md Section 4.2.

Lady Maerwyn, at breakfast, slightly drunk at nine in the morning, to Jake, apropos of nothing:

**MAERWYN:** *"You're very kind to her. People are. It's the one thing I can say for this house — everyone is very kind to her."*

*(a pause that is a half-second too long)*

**MAERWYN:** *"Have some more of the white. It's the only good thing we make."*

**Design law:** the scene moves on immediately. No reaction shot. No lingering. Cross-reference the_princess.md Section 8.4 — **first-time players hear a tipsy hostess.**

### 8.4 THE EJECTION

**Cross-reference:** house_arion.md Section 3.5, house_system.md Section 6.2.

Cassian walks Jake to the gate himself. **He is not angry.** He is brisk, apologetic, and entirely reasonable, and he has had a horse saddled.

**CASSIAN:** *"You'll understand. She is not — Captain, whatever you are — she is not something that can be spent. Not on a night. Not on a man passing through."*

**CASSIAN:** *"Take the grey. No, take it, it's not a bribe. I'd rather you were far away quickly and I'd rather you didn't die on my road doing it."*

**CASSIAN:** *"Don't come back. I mean that in the kindest way it can be meant, and I do mean it kindly."*

**Design law:** cross-reference house_arion.md Section 3.5 — **guest-right held. He could have had Jake killed and Highmoore would have understood it.** Instead: breakfast, a horse, and a walk to the gate.

Cross-reference princess_quest_the_truth.md — **that first line is the most horrifying thing anyone says in Dark Arisen, and nobody knows it yet, including him, because he means the first half sincerely.**

### 8.5 The Last Look

**Cross-reference:** the_princess.md Section 4.1.

She is not at the gate. Cross-reference house_arion.md Section 2.3 — **she is in the gallery window**, and the player may or may not look up.

If they do: she raises a hand. That is all.

## 9. WHAT THE PLAYER TAKES AWAY

### 9.1 Mechanically

- The grey. Cross-reference mounted_travel.md Section 4.4 — **the best horse Jake will own**, given to him by a man he will kill.
- Arion's gates are now closed to him. Cross-reference house_arion.md Section 7.3.

### 9.2 Emotionally

Cross-reference princess_quest_overview.md Section 1.2. **The player should be slightly annoyed at Cassian and looking forward to coming back.**

That is the correct emotional state at the end of this sequence, and everything after it is built on the assumption that it worked.

## 12. STYLE BIBLE COMPLIANCE

- ✅ **Pillar 1 (Staggered Register):** Cassian's warm cultured High, Odo's aggrieved Middle, the hall's Low roar — and Elowen doing a flawless ninety seconds of High before dropping it permanently.
- ✅ **Pillar 2 (Lore Through Objects):** the six empty hutches in the garden, findable an hour before she mentions rabbits. The gallery of married-out daughters. The grey.
- ✅ **Pillar 3 (Layered Death):** none in this sequence, anywhere, at all. **That is its function.**
- ✅ **Pillar 4 (Layered Humor):** the dancing. Odo. The drover. *"They just do it loudly."* **This is the funniest sequence in Phase 8 and possibly in the game, and it is load-bearing.**
- ✅ **Pillar 5 (Theme — Revenge):** absent entirely — the only extended sequence in Dark Arisen where Jake is not carrying it. **He spends the evening as a fictional man who has no dead brother, and it is the happiest he has ever been, and it is a lie he told at a door.**

## 14. CONCLUSION

The Ball. Forty minutes of road toward a white and gold castle that grows the whole way, into a bell you last heard from a mountain, into a thousand people having the best day of their year because the fighting season ended and everyone came home. A hall with two hundred in it, marble underfoot, musicians in the gallery, and a filthy man from a mine sitting above the salt because the host is a good host.

A voice from behind saying *I've never seen you before.* Ninety seconds of flawless performance and then *you're not an ambassador — no, don't, I'm not going to tell anyone, I'd just rather we both knew where we are.* A dance that is not a minigame, that he is bad at, that she is kind about. A terrace, wind, the moor going out further than the eye follows, and six rabbits she fed every morning for four years and will not say what happened to.

*One true thing. It doesn't have to be a good one.*

And in the morning: a bath, a breakfast, a mother who says everyone in this house is very kind to her and pauses half a second too long, and a reasonable man who walks a stranger to the gate, gives him the best horse he will ever own so he does not die on the road, and asks him kindly not to come back.

The player should end this slightly annoyed at the father and looking forward to returning.
