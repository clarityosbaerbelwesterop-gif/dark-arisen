# HOUSE ARION — WHITE AND GOLD

**Status:** Design Draft v1.0
**Phase:** Phase 8 — Highmoore (Movement Two — Document 8 of ~30)
**Related:** highmoore_overview.md, house_system.md, the_princess.md, house_belos.md, princess_quest_the_ball.md, princess_quest_arion_falls.md, princess_quest_the_truth.md, highmoore_region.md, mounted_travel.md, colonial_war/castle_catalog.md, npcs/regional_populations.md, style_bible.md
**Purpose:** House Arion — the castle, Lord Cassian Arion, the household, the horse tradition, the wealth, the marriage-brokerage, the daily life the player experiences, and the complete design of a place that must be beautiful before it is anything else.
**Inspirations:** Game of Thrones (Highgarden's beauty concealing calculation), Brideshead Revisited (a great house as a system that consumes its children), The Leopard (aristocracy that is genuinely cultured and genuinely monstrous), Downton Abbey (household hierarchy as texture), Versailles, real English great houses

## 1. THE DESIGN BRIEF

### 1.1 The Requirement

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_region.md Section 4.2, house_system.md Section 8.3.

**House Arion must be the most beautiful thing in Dark Arisen.**

Not the most impressive — the archipelago has volcanoes, cathedrals, and a crystal cavern the size of a cathedral. **The most beautiful.** Warm, gold, lit, musical, and full of people enjoying themselves.

The player must want to be here. They must be glad they rode eight kilometres toward it. Cross-reference princess_quest_the_ball.md — everything the questline does later depends on the player having genuinely liked this place.

### 1.2 The Trap

Cross-reference house_system.md Principle 1.5. Arion is not a beautiful lie. **It is a beautiful place that is also where this happened**, and both are true simultaneously, and the design never resolves the contradiction because there is no resolution.

## 2. THE CASTLE

### 2.1 The Approach

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_region.md Section 1.2, mounted_travel.md.

Arion sits on rising ground in the Folds. **It is visible from the Crystal Caves shelf**, eight kilometres out, as a white and gold point on the skyline — and cross-reference highmoore_region.md Section 6.3: it is the most beautiful thing in frame and the player rides toward it because of that and nothing prompted them.

**The approach is a forty-minute ride** and the castle grows the entire way. Cross-reference mounted_travel.md Section 6.4 — the road is good, sworn, and safe.

### 2.2 The Silhouette

**Cross-reference:** colonial_war/castle_catalog.md Principle 1.5.

Not a fortress. **A great house that has walls.**

Pale limestone from the Stonecountry quarries, and it has been washed white. Gold on the roofs — not gilding, but copper gone to a warm ochre and maintained. Five towers, none of them defensive, and the tallest is a bell tower.

**Design note:** cross-reference house_belos.md Section 2 for the contrast. Arion looks like money. Belos looks like a fist.

### 2.3 The Districts

- **The Outer Yard** — stables (cross-reference Section 5), workshops, and the household's working life
- **The Great Hall** — cross-reference Section 4.3. Marble floor, gold leaf, a musicians' gallery, and it is where the ball happens.
- **The Long Gallery** — portraits, six generations, and cross-reference Section 6.2
- **The Solar and the private apartments** — cross-reference the_princess.md Section 5: **her chambers are here**, and cross-reference princess_quest_arion_falls.md Section 4.
- **The Bell Tower** — cross-reference highmoore_region.md Section 1.3: **the bell Jake heard from the shelf, eight kilometres away, was this one.**
- **The Gardens** — cross-reference Section 7.4, and cross-reference the_princess.md Section 6: **the rabbit hutches are here, and they are empty.**

### 2.4 The Light

**Cross-reference:** physics/sun_shadow_systems.md, physics/fire_physics.md Section 3.5.

Arion is the best-lit interior in Dark Arisen. Candles by the hundred, and cross-reference physics/sound_physics.md — the Great Hall has a warm, short reverb built for music.

**Design law:** after two hours in the Crystal Caves and a day on open moor, the Great Hall at night should be overwhelming. Cross-reference princess_quest_the_ball.md Section 2.

## 3. LORD CASSIAN ARION

### 3.1 Quick Reference

**Name:** Lord Cassian Arion
**Age:** 51
**Register:** Anglo-noble High — cultured, warm, genuinely charming
**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_arion_falls.md for the full encounter

### 3.2 The Design Rule

**Cross-reference:** house_system.md Principle 1.5, style_bible.md Pillar 3.

**Cassian Arion is not written as a monster and is never played as one.**

He is a good host, an excellent judge of horses, funny at table, well-read, generous to his household, and respected across Highmoore as the region's steadiest hand. His people like him. His roads are the safest in the Folds. He has settled four feuds without a death.

**Everything the player sees of him before the truth is true.** Cross-reference princess_quest_the_truth.md Section 3 — the reveal does not retroactively make him a different man. It makes him the same man, with something else also being the case.

**Design law:** no foreshadowing that reads as villainy. No lingering shots, no ominous scoring, no line that a player could point at afterward and say *there it was.* Cross-reference Section 3.4 for what is permitted instead.

### 3.3 Voice

*"Sit, sit — no, further up, you're a guest and my daughter will have my head if I've put you below the salt."*

*"Ambassador. Ambassador. From — forgive me — from where, exactly?"* (a beat, and then he lets it go, and the letting-go is a courtesy and not suspicion)

*"I have three good roads, two good quarries, and the best horses in the basin. I have no enemies I cannot name and no debts I cannot pay. A man in my position ought to be content. Ask me sometime whether I am."*

### 3.4 What Is Permitted

**Cross-reference:** Section 3.2. The design may plant things that are **only** legible in retrospect and read as nothing at all on a first pass:

- He speaks about Elowen in the third person while she is present. Once. In a sentence about her marriage value. **Nobody in the room finds this remarkable**, and cross-reference house_system.md Section 4.2: nobody should.
- The empty rabbit hutches are visible in the garden and cross-reference the_princess.md Section 6 — she mentions them at the ball, sadly, in passing, and does not say what happened.
- He does not go into the Long Gallery. It is never mentioned that he does not.
- **The morning he throws Jake out, he is not angry.** Cross-reference princess_quest_the_ball.md Section 8: he is brisk, apologetic, and entirely reasonable, and he gives Jake a horse.

### 3.5 The Ejection

Cross-reference princess_quest_the_ball.md Section 8, house_system.md Section 6.2.

Guest-right held. He could have had Jake killed and Highmoore would have understood it. **Instead he gives him breakfast, gives him a horse, walks him to the gate, and asks him not to come back.**

*"You'll understand. She is not — Captain, whatever you are — she is not something that can be spent. Not on a night. Not on a man passing through."*

*"Take the grey. No, take it, it's not a bribe, I'd rather you were far away quickly and I'd rather you didn't die on my road doing it."*

**Cross-reference princess_quest_the_truth.md:** that line is the most horrifying thing anyone says in Dark Arisen, and nobody knows it yet, including him — because he means the first half sincerely.

## 4. THE HOUSEHOLD

### 4.1 Scale

Cross-reference house_system.md Section 2.2 — Arion is a Great House: twenty-three holdings, sixty household knights, and a levy of six hundred.

### 4.2 The Named Household

**LADY MAERWYN ARION** — Cassian's wife, 48. Cross-reference the_princess.md Section 4. She is warm, distracted, and drinks more than she used to. **She knows. She has known for two years.** Cross-reference princess_quest_the_truth.md — this is never stated and never confirmed, and there is exactly one line at the ball that a second playthrough hears completely differently.

**SER ROWLAND VANCE** — captain of the household guard, 44. Straightforward, decent, and cross-reference princess_quest_arion_falls.md Section 3: **he is one of the ten in the courtyard**, and he fights well, and he says something before he does.

**MASTER ODO** — the steward, 60s. Runs everything. Comedy. Cross-reference style_bible.md Pillar 4.

**ALYS** — Elowen's maid, 22. Cross-reference the_princess.md Section 8 and princess_quest_the_truth.md Section 4 — **she is the one who kept the real letter**, and she is not at Arion when Jake comes back.

**THE HOSTAGE-GUESTS** — cross-reference house_system.md Section 4.1: Arion currently hosts two, a Cadwall boy of fourteen and a Mere girl of eleven. **They are having a lovely time.** Cross-reference house_system.md Design Law — this is one of the benign examples, and it must be established warmly and at length before the questline turns.

### 4.3 The Great Hall at Night

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_the_ball.md.

Arion feasts. Cross-reference house_system.md Section 4.5 — it is the end of the burning season, the levy has come home, and there is a reason for it.

**What the player walks into:** two hundred people, musicians in the gallery, marble underfoot, gold light, warriors home from a season with their families, children underfoot, and cross-reference npcs/children_and_families.md — the protection rules and the warmth standards both apply in full.

## 5. THE HORSES

**Cross-reference:** mounted_travel.md Section 4.4, house_system.md Section 5.

Arion breeds the Highmoore greys: tall, fast, beautiful, and delicate. They are the region's finest and its most impractical, and cross-reference house_belos.md Section 5 for the contrast — Belos blacks are heavier, braver, and uglier, and in a fight they are better.

**Design note:** cross-reference mounted_travel.md Section 8.1 — **Elowen rides an Arion grey, and she rides it beautifully, and Jake does not.**

The stables are the best-run part of the castle and Cassian is in them daily. Cross-reference Section 3.2 — his competence with horses is real and it is one of the reasons the player likes him.

## 6. THE WEALTH AND HOW IT WORKS

### 6.1 Sources

- **Land** — the best holdings in the Folds
- **Horses** — sold across Highmoore
- **Wool** — cross-reference house_system.md Section 5
- **And marriage.** Cross-reference Section 6.3.

### 6.2 The Long Gallery

Six generations of portraits, and cross-reference style_bible.md Pillar 2 — **the player can read Arion's entire strategy off the wall.**

Every second portrait is a woman who married out. The plaques give the House she went to and the year, and cross-reference house_system.md Section 4.2: the gallery is a ledger of assets deployed, hung in gold frames, and the family are proud of it.

**Elowen's portrait is not there.** She is nineteen and unmarried and there is a space.

### 6.3 The Brokerage

**Cross-reference:** house_system.md Section 4.2.

Arion's real business is that everyone marries into it. Cassian has brokered eleven marriages in fifteen years, four of them settling feuds without a death, and **this is genuinely admirable and genuinely how he is regarded.**

### 6.4 Elowen's Value

Cross-reference the_princess.md Section 2.

She is the most valuable unspent asset in Highmoore and everyone knows it including her, and it has been discussed in her presence since she was eleven, and cross-reference house_system.md Section 4.2 — **nobody considers this cruel.**

## 7. WHAT THE PLAYER DOES HERE

### 7.1 Before the Ball

Cross-reference highmoore_overview.md Pillar 3 — Arion is enterable, explorable, and has content without the questline ever triggering.

- Stables and horse trading
- The hall, the gallery, the gardens
- Household business — cross-reference house_system.md Section 6.3
- The hostage-guests, who will talk to anyone
- Master Odo, who will complain to anyone

### 7.2 The Ball

Cross-reference princess_quest_the_ball.md. **It is not a scheduled event the player is invited to.** It is happening when they arrive, if they arrive in the right window, and cross-reference house_system.md Section 4.5 — the window is the end of the burning season and it is signposted by the world, not by a marker.

### 7.3 After the Ejection

Cross-reference princess_quest_the_ball.md Section 8 — Jake is asked not to come back, and the gates are closed to him, and **he can still ride up to them and be turned away**, and cross-reference npcs/regional_populations.md: the guards recognise him and are apologetic about it.

### 7.4 The Gardens

Cross-reference Section 2.3, the_princess.md Section 6.

**The rabbit hutches.** Six of them, well-built, maintained, and empty. A player who explores the gardens before the ball finds them and cross-reference style_bible.md Pillar 2 — **the game says nothing**, and then she mentions rabbits at the ball, and then much later the real letter says what it says.

## 8. AFTER

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_arion_falls.md, highmoore_reconstruction.md.

### 8.1 The Fall

Ten household knights in the outer yard. Cross-reference princess_quest_arion_falls.md — **they are the last of the sixty**, because the rest are on their holdings, and cross-reference Section 3.5: Cassian did not set a trap. He was at home.

### 8.2 What Remains

Cross-reference physics/destruction_physics.md, npcs/building_evolution.md.

Arion is **not burned.** Cross-reference princess_quest_belos.md for the contrast — Jake burns Belos. He does not burn Arion. He kills eleven men in the yard and leaves.

The castle stands. The household is intact. Lady Maerwyn is alive. The hostage-guests are alive and are sent home the following week.

**And cross-reference highmoore_reconstruction.md:** Arion continues, headless, and what happens to it is one of the reconstruction's longest threads.

### 8.3 The Long Gallery Afterward

The space where Elowen's portrait would have gone is still there.

**Full Vision:** in the reconstruction content, it can be filled. Cross-reference highmoore_reconstruction.md — and the design's instruction is that **the player chooses whether to, and the game does not suggest it, and nothing rewards it.**

## 13. CONCLUSION

White and Gold. Pale Stonecountry limestone washed white, copper roofs gone warm ochre, five towers and none of them defensive, and a bell you can hear from the mountain eight kilometres away. The best-lit room in Dark Arisen, with two hundred people in it and musicians in the gallery and the levy home from the season and children under the tables.

A lord who is a good host, funny at table, excellent with horses, respected across the basin, who has settled four feuds without a death and keeps the safest roads in the Folds — and who says *she is not something that can be spent* and means it, and gives a stranger a grey so he will not die riding away.

A gallery of six generations where every second portrait is a daughter who married out, with the House and the year on the plaque, hung in gold frames, and the family are proud of it. And a gap in the row where the nineteen-year-old's would go.

And in the garden, six rabbit hutches. Well-built. Maintained. Empty.

**The most beautiful thing in the game. That is the design requirement.** 👑🕯️
