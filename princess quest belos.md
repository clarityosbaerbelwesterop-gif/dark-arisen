## 1. THE BRIEF

### 1.1 What This Sequence Must Achieve

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_overview.md Section 6.4, princess_quest_overview.md Section 8.3.

**It must feel wrong while it is being played.** Not afterward. Not when a wizard says something. **During.**

Cross-reference crystal_katana.md Section 1.1 — the failure mode is a power fantasy with a lecture attached, and every mechanic in this document exists to prevent it.

### 1.2 The First Death Was a Boy Who Followed the Rules

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_the_lake.md Section 9.

The rampage did not start here. It started at a lake with a nineteen-year-old who shot a trespasser on his own House's land at range in bad light, exactly as his oath required, and who came down to see what he hit.

**The game never says this and the player either noticed or did not.**

### 1.3 What She Wrote

**Cross-reference:** the_princess.md Section 9.3, princess_quest_arion_falls.md Section 4.4.

*"Do not do anything. Please. You will want to and it will not help and it will not be for me."*

**He read it forty minutes ago.**

## 5. THE NINE MINUTES

**Cross-reference:** crystal_katana.md Section 8.2 for the escalation design.

### 5.1 MINUTES 1-3 — COMPETENCE

**The design permits the player to feel powerful, and it is a trap.**

Cross-reference crystal_katana.md Sections 4.1-4.3 — fastest attack chain in the game, ignores armour, and cross-reference house_belos.md Section 4.3: **Belos doctrine is range, and Jake is inside it in eleven seconds.**

The first arrow volley. Cross-reference physics/combat_physics.md, physics/wind_systems.md — in rain, at close range, into a moving target among their own men.

**Cross-reference physics/sound_physics.md:** the Katana rings on every impact. Cross-reference crystal_katana.md Section 3.2 — **the reverb in a walled court begins to build immediately.**

### 5.2 MINUTES 3-6 — DETERIORATION

**Cross-reference:** crystal_katana.md Sections 5.2-5.3.

Jake's stamina collapses. His posture breaks. He takes arrows — cross-reference physics/combat_physics.md: **he is hit repeatedly and does not stop**, and the wounded animation set engages and does not disengage.

Breathing changes. Stance collapses. Off-hand hangs.

**And the sword does not change.** Cross-reference crystal_katana.md Section 5.2 — flat stamina cost, identical swing at three percent as at full.

### 5.3 THE HELD SHOT

**Cross-reference:** crystal_katana.md Section 8.3.

**Somewhere between minute six and minute seven, the game takes control for two seconds and holds on Jake's hands.**

They are shaking. The sword is not.

**No music. No dialogue. Two seconds. Control returns.**

### 5.4 MINUTES 6-8 — THE SURRENDER

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_overview.md Section 6.4, house_belos.md Section 4.4.

**Around the hundred and fiftieth, they begin to stop.**

Cross-reference physics/combat_physics.md — spears go down. Men back away. Some run. Some kneel. Cross-reference house_belos.md Section 4.2 — **an entire population that learned archery as recreation is discovering what it is actually for.**

**The game does not stop the player.**

**Design law — the absolute rule:**
- Every surrendered man is a valid target and the input connects
- **There is no non-lethal option**
- **There is no prompt to stop**
- Cross-reference crystal_katana.md Section 8.2 — **the Katana kills a man holding nothing exactly as efficiently as it killed the first one**

**And the player can also simply stand still.** Cross-reference Section 8 — **that is available at every moment of these nine minutes and nothing indicates it.**

### 5.5 THE FACES

**Cross-reference:** highmoore_overview.md Section 6.4, npcs/children_and_families.md.

**Some of them are very young.** Seventeen. Cross-reference house_belos.md Section 4.2 — they learned in the butts.

**The protection rules hold absolutely: there are no children in the Outer Court, at any point, in any variant.** Cross-reference npcs/children_and_families.md.

**Design law:** the design does not hide the seventeen-year-olds and does not point at them. Cross-reference physics/combat_physics.md — the faces are just there, in the normal way faces are there.

### 5.6 MINUTES 8-9 — THE FIRES

**Cross-reference:** physics/fire_physics.md, physics/destruction_physics.md, house_belos.md Section 2.3.

**Jake does not set the fires.** Cross-reference physics/fire_physics.md Section 2.1 — a brazier goes over in the fighting, and cross-reference physics/wind_systems.md: there is wind, and the Outer Court has stores along the western range.

**Cross-reference house_belos.md Section 2.3** — millstone grit shatters. It does not crumble. The western range comes down in about forty seconds and takes the gatehouse with it and **cross-reference physics/destruction_physics.md: the collapse is structural, unstoppable, and Jake did not plan it.**

### 5.7 THE END OF IT

Cross-reference physics/fire_physics.md, physics/weather_physics.md.

**Jake standing in a burning courtyard in the rain**, which is not enough rain, among roughly three hundred people, most of whom are not moving.

Cross-reference physics/sound_physics.md — **and the reverb from three hundred sword impacts has been building in a walled court for nine minutes and it is now almost continuous and it does not stop when he does.**

## 7. ALDRIC

**Cross-reference:** house_belos.md Sections 3.3 and 3.5.

### 7.1 Where He Is

Cross-reference house_belos.md Section 3.2 — he trains with his levy and he does not command from a tower.

**He is in the Outer Court from minute two.** He is fifty-eight. He fights.

### 7.2 The Ninety Seconds

Cross-reference house_belos.md Section 3.5. He goes down around minute seven and he is not dead and Jake stands over him.

**ALDRIC:** *"...you're not Arion's. You've his horse and you're not his."*

**ALDRIC:** *"Say it, then. I'd like to know what this was."*

**Player option — and it is the only time in the entire sequence Jake speaks:**
- **[SAY HER NAME]**

**ALDRIC:** *"...ah."*

*(and cross-reference princess_quest_arion_falls.md Section 6.4 — **the second man in one evening to say that, in the same register, and both of them are her fathers in the way that matters**)*

**ALDRIC:** *"Four years ago."*

**ALDRIC:** *"I knew some of it. Not all. I didn't look for the rest."*

**ALDRIC:** *"There was a truce holding and four hundred of mine breathing and I'd put two boys in the ground at that ford already."*

**ALDRIC:** *"So no. I didn't hang them. You'll want me to say it was the wrong sum."*

*(a long pause — cross-reference house_belos.md Section 3.5)*

**ALDRIC:** *"I've had four years and I still don't know."*

### 7.3 The Kill

**Gameplay. One input.** Cross-reference princess_quest_arion_falls.md Section 7.2 — the same four restraints: no slow motion, no finisher, no music, no camera flourish.

### 7.4 The Four Men

**Cross-reference:** house_belos.md Section 6.4, princess_quest_the_truth.md Section 8.5.

**Two of them are in this courtyard.** They are not identified, not marked, not named, and not given a scene.

**They die somewhere among three hundred and the player never knows which.**

**Design law:** the game never offers the option to find and kill only them. Cross-reference house_belos.md Section 6.4 — **that absence is the entire moral structure of the sequence.**

### 9.2 The Line

*(and Jake is still holding the sword, and the player still has control, and **the Wizard is a valid target**, and cross-reference the_wizard.md Section 9: attacking him does not work and he does not defend himself)*

## 13. STYLE BIBLE COMPLIANCE

- ✅ **Pillar 1 (Staggered Register):** Jake speaks once in nine minutes and it is a name. Aldric talks like a yeoman while dying like a lord. The Wizard uses a register that belongs to nobody in Highmoore and asks a question instead of making a speech.
- ✅ **Pillar 2 (Lore Through Objects):** a blocked stair in rubble on the western side, visible from where the player is standing, unmarked. A horse standing blown in a gateway. A sword that rings the same note three hundred times.
- ✅ **Pillar 3 (Layered Death):** Style B for the first hundred and Style D for the rest, with no change in the weapon, the music, or the camera. No finisher. No slow motion. Three hundred people and one held shot on a pair of shaking hands.
- ✅ **Pillar 4 (Layered Humor):** **none, anywhere, at all** — and cross-reference princess_quest_the_ball.md Section 12: the ball is the funniest sequence in Dark Arisen precisely so that this one can be the emptiest.
- ✅ **Pillar 5 (Theme — Revenge):** he read *do not do anything, please, it will not help and it will not be for me* forty minutes ago, in her bedroom, in her handwriting. He could have turned west in the yard. He could have turned at the gate. He could have stopped swinging at any second. Nothing in the game suggested any of it. And a man he had liked, who had explained his arithmetic and admitted he still does not know if it was right, is at his feet — and a hundred and forty people under the west range have heard all nine minutes.

## 15. CONCLUSION

Belos. Thirty miles east on a horse with the stamina switched off, past villages where people are shooting at butts in the evening for fun. A castle that sees a single rider at two kilometres and has twenty minutes, and uses them correctly: a hundred archers up, two hundred spears in the court, and the household down the undercroft stair, because that is the drill and they are good at the drill.

Three minutes of feeling powerful. Three minutes of a man falling apart while the thing in his hand does not. Two seconds where the game takes the camera and holds on his hands. Two minutes where they stop fighting back and there is no non-lethal option and no prompt and the sword kills a man holding nothing exactly as efficiently as it killed the first one. Forty seconds where a brazier goes over and the wrong stone shatters instead of crumbling and the west range comes down.

A man of fifty-eight on his back who asks what this was, and hears a name, and says *ah*, and explains an arithmetic about a truce and four hundred people breathing and two sons already in the ground at a ford, and finishes with *I've had four years and I still don't know.*

And then a man standing where he was not standing a moment before, who does not moralise and does not forgive, and asks whether it was worth it and means it as a question and will wait — and then points out that a hundred and forty people are under the west range, have been since before the gate, can hear everything, and have heard all nine minutes.

*So: do you still have the courage?*
