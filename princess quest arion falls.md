## 1. THE SEQUENCE'S BRIEF

### 1.1 What It Is

Twenty minutes. A ride, eleven men, one room, and one conversation.

### 1.2 What It Is Not

**It is not the rampage.** Cross-reference princess_quest_belos.md — that is next and it is nine minutes and it is a different thing entirely.

**This one is small.** Cross-reference Section 4 — its centre is a man alone in a girl's bedroom reading two hundred and eighty words.

### 1.3 The Structural Law

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_overview.md Section 8.1, princess_quest_the_truth.md Rule Two.

**The letter is read before the courtyard.** Everything Cassian says, he says to a man who already knows, and everything Jake does afterward, he does having been asked in writing not to.

## 2. THE RIDE

### 2.1 The Trigger

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_the_lake.md Section 10.3.

The player mounts the horse. **That is the only trigger and there is no confirmation prompt.**

### 2.2 The Mechanics

**Cross-reference:** mounted_travel.md Section 8.2.

**The stamina system suspends.** The horse does not tire. The gallop does not end. Cross-reference crystal_katana.md Section 1.2 — **it is the same design idea as the sword, and the player is now riding one and carrying the other.**

**Four minutes. One unbroken gallop across the Folds. Still raining.**

### 2.3 What Is On the Road

Cross-reference highmoore_settlements.md, princess_quest_the_ball.md Section 2.2.

**The same road as the first ride.** The fortified farms. The wardens in white and gold, who recognise him and do not stop him. Cross-reference physics/weather_physics.md — in the rain, at a flat gallop, at the wrong hour.

**Design law:** no music. Cross-reference physics/sound_physics.md — hooves, rain, and breathing, for four minutes.

### 2.4 The Drover

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_the_ball.md Section 2.2.

The man with a hundred sheep who took eleven minutes to get out of the way on the first ride **is on the road again.**

**This time the game lets Jake go around him.**

## 3. THE GATE AND THE TEN

### 3.1 The Gate

Open. Cross-reference house_arion.md Section 8.1 — **Cassian did not set a trap.** He is at home, it is evening, and there is no reason for the gate to be shut.

### 3.2 The Ten

**Cross-reference:** house_arion.md Section 8.1, Section 4.1.

Ten household knights in the outer yard. **They are the last of the sixty** — the rest are on their holdings, because the burning season ended and men went home. Cross-reference house_system.md Section 4.5.

**They come out because a man rode through the gate at a gallop in the rain.**

### 3.3 The Fight

**Cross-reference:** physics/combat_physics.md, mechanics/combat_feel.md, crystal_katana.md.

**Gameplay. Full control. No scripting.**

They are good — cross-reference house_arion.md Section 4 — household knights of a Great House, in mail, in a yard they know.

**With the Crystal Katana:** cross-reference crystal_katana.md Section 4.3. It takes about ninety seconds.
**Without it:** cross-reference crystal_guardian.md Section 8.4, crystal_katana.md Section 9 — **it is genuinely hard and Jake is badly hurt by the end of it.**

### 3.4 SER ROWLAND VANCE

**Cross-reference:** house_arion.md Section 4.2, the_lesser_houses.md Section 3.2.

Captain of the household guard, forty-four, straightforward and decent, and **cousin to Lord Vane of Vanemoor.**

He is the last of the ten and he says something before he fights:

**ROWLAND:** *"Captain. You've a horse of ours under you and a sword I've never seen and you've come through that gate at a gallop."*

**ROWLAND:** *"Whatever's happened — tell me. I'll go and get him myself. I'll bring him out here to you."*

*(a beat)*

**ROWLAND:** *"No? Right."*

**Design law:** cross-reference style_bible.md Pillar 3 — **there is no dialogue option to accept.** The offer is real, and it is not available, and the sequence does not explain why.

### 3.5 The Yard After

Cross-reference physics/destruction_physics.md, physics/weather_physics.md — eleven bodies including Rowland's, in rain, on the cobbles of the yard where Jake watched a man be embraced by three children.

## 4. HER CHAMBERS

### 4.1 The Detour

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_the_truth.md Rule Two, the_princess.md Section 9.2.

The way from the outer yard to the inner courtyard passes the private apartments. Cross-reference house_arion.md Section 2.3.

**There is no prompt. No marker. No objective.** The door is open and it was not open before.

**Design law:** the player may walk past. Cross-reference Section 4.5 — **and if they do, the sequence continues without the letter**, and cross-reference Section 8.5: the consequences are severe and permanent.

### 4.2 The Room

Cross-reference the_princess.md Section 4.

Small. Neat. Cross-reference physics/sun_shadow_systems.md — grey evening light through one window.

**What is in it:**
- A bed, made
- Books. Cross-reference the_princess.md Section 2.3 — three languages, and one of them is Brother Padarn's Latin
- Her riding clothes, hung
- **Nothing packed. Nothing prepared. Nothing to indicate that anyone was going anywhere.** Cross-reference the_princess.md Section 2.2 — she made her decision eight weeks ago and then lived here, ordinarily, being pleasant.
- **The letter.** Cross-reference Section 4.3.

**And cross-reference house_arion.md Section 4.2:** Alys's things are gone from the adjoining room. Cross-reference princess_quest_the_truth.md Section 5.3 — **she was told where to leave it and told to go, and she went.**

### 4.3 The Letter

**Cross-reference:** the_princess.md Section 9.3 for the full text.

Where Elowen told Alys to leave it. **On the pillow.**

### 4.4 The Reading

**Design:**

- **Full player control.** He picks it up when the player picks it up.
- **No cutscene.** No camera move. Cross-reference physics/sound_physics.md — **rain on the window and nothing else.**
- **The letter is displayed in full and the player reads it at their own pace.** There is no skip and there is no timer.
- **No music.**
- **No reaction.** Jake does not speak, does not break anything, and does not make a sound. Cross-reference style_bible.md Pillar 3.

**And when the player closes it, they are standing in a bedroom with eleven dead men outside and a letter in their hand that says *do not do anything, please.***

### 4.5 If the Player Walks Past

**Cross-reference:** Section 8.5, princess_quest_belos.md Section 2.

The door was open. They did not go in.

**Cassian's scene plays differently** — cross-reference Section 6.5 — and **the letter is never found**, and cross-reference princess_quest_the_return.md: Jake goes back through the mountain not knowing, and cross-reference highmoore_reconstruction.md — **it is findable later, months later, and reading it then is one of the worst moments the game contains.**

## 5. THE INNER COURTYARD

### 5.1 The Approach

Cross-reference house_arion.md Section 2.3. Through the hall — **the same hall**, cross-reference princess_quest_the_ball.md Section 4.1, with the trestles down and the candles out and two hundred fewer people in it.

### 5.2 The Household

**Cross-reference:** npcs/children_and_families.md, house_arion.md Section 4.2.

**They are in the building and Jake does not touch them.**

- Master Odo, in a doorway, saying nothing
- Servants against walls
- **Lady Maerwyn on the gallery stair**, cross-reference princess_quest_the_truth.md Section 5.2 — she does not move, she does not speak, and she watches the whole thing
- **The two hostage-guests.** Cross-reference house_arion.md Section 4.2 — the Cadwall boy of fourteen and the Mere girl of eleven, **who are held back by a woman with her hand over the girl's eyes**, and cross-reference npcs/children_and_families.md: the protection rules hold absolutely and neither child is ever in danger for one frame.

**Design law:** the game does not permit Jake to harm anyone in this building. Not as a prompt refused — **as an absence.** There is no attack input that connects with a non-combatant in Arion.

### 5.3 The Courtyard

Small. Cobbled. Rain. Cross-reference house_arion.md Section 2.3 — the gardens are through the arch, and cross-reference house_arion.md Section 7.4: **the six empty hutches are visible from where Cassian is standing.**

## 6. CASSIAN

### 6.1 How He Is Found

**Standing. Alone. Unarmed.**

Cross-reference house_arion.md Section 3.2 — he has heard ninety seconds of fighting and eleven men die, and he has come out into his own courtyard in the rain and he is standing there.

### 6.2 The Redirect

**Cross-reference:** the user's original outline — *"pass auf Jake der Feind lauert in den eigenen Reihen."*

He tries it. It is the plan working, and it is fluent, and it is what he has prepared:

**CASSIAN:** *"Captain. Stop. Stop — listen to me, and then do whatever you came to do."*

**CASSIAN:** *"It was Belos. The arrow was Belos, on Belos ground, and I will show you the boundary myself if you want it."*

**CASSIAN:** *"And there is more and you will not want to hear it. Four years ago she was a guest in that castle for eighteen months and four of their men— "*

**CASSIAN:** *"The enemy is not in this yard. The enemy is thirty miles east and there are two hundred of them and I will give you every man I have left—"*

### 6.3 THE LETTER

**Cross-reference:** Section 4.3.

**Jake holds it up.**

No dialogue. No line. **The player presses one button and Jake raises his hand with two hundred and eighty words in it.**

### 6.4 The Stop

**Cross-reference:** house_arion.md Section 3.2 — the reveal does not make him a different man.

He stops mid-sentence. He looks at it. He knows exactly what it is, because he knows his daughter's hand and he knows he did not dictate that one.

*(a long time)*

**CASSIAN:** *"...ah."*

**CASSIAN:** *"She wrote two."*

**CASSIAN:** *"Of course she did. Of course she did, she was— "*

*(he does not finish it)*

### 6.5 The Question

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_the_truth.md Section 8.2, style_bible.md Pillar 3.

He does not defend himself. He does not explain, plead, threaten, or offer terms. Cross-reference house_arion.md Section 3.2 — **he is not written as a monster and is not played as one, here least of all.**

He asks about her.

**CASSIAN:** *"Was she— "*

*(and he has to start again)*

**CASSIAN:** *"At the lake. Was she happy? Before it. Was she— was there a while where she was— "*

**CASSIAN:** *"I wasn't there. I wasn't— I have not seen her since the morning she rode out and I would like to know if there was a while."*

### 6.6 The Answer

**Player options — and this is one of the two most important choices in Phase 8:**

- **[TELL HIM]** — *"She swam. We danced in the rain."*
- **[SAY NOTHING]**
- **[LIE]** — *"No."*

**Design law:** cross-reference style_bible.md Pillar 5. **All three are available and the game does not favour one.** Cross-reference princess_quest_the_return.md — the choice is remembered.

### 6.7 If Walked Past (No Letter)

**Cross-reference:** Section 4.5.

The redirect works. Cassian is fluent, and grieving, and telling the truth about Belos, and **Jake kills him anyway** — cross-reference Section 7 — but the scene is entirely different: a man protesting his innocence about a thing he is innocent of, while being guilty of the thing nobody has told Jake about.

**And Section 6.5's question is never asked**, because Cassian never realises he has been found out.

## 8. AFTER

### 8.1 Arion Is Not Burned

**Cross-reference:** house_arion.md Section 8.2.

Jake kills eleven men in the outer yard and one man in the courtyard **and leaves.** The castle stands. The household is intact. Cross-reference house_arion.md Section 8.2 — the hostage-guests are sent home the following week.

### 8.2 The Walk Out

Cross-reference Section 5.2 — back through the hall, past Odo, past the servants, past the stair.

**Nobody stops him. Nobody says anything. Cross-reference physics/sound_physics.md — the hall has the same reverb it had during the ball and there is nothing in it to reverberate.**

### 8.3 The Horse

**Cross-reference:** mounted_travel.md Section 8.2, princess_quest_the_ball.md Section 9.1.

It is standing in the outer yard where he left it, among the eleven, **and it is the horse Cassian gave him.**

### 8.4 The Choice

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_belos.md Section 2, princess_quest_overview.md Section 10.2.

**The questline can end here.**

Jake can ride west, back toward the mountain. Cross-reference princess_quest_the_return.md — there is a version of Phase 8 where nobody goes to Belos, and cross-reference highmoore_reconstruction.md: **Highmoore's balance survives, one Great House is decapitated instead of two destroyed, and roughly three hundred people are alive.**

**The game does not suggest this. There is no prompt, no dialogue, and no marker pointing west.**

### 8.5 If the Letter Was Missed

**Cross-reference:** Section 4.5, princess_quest_the_return.md.

Jake rides to Belos believing Cassian's redirect, kills three hundred people over a version of events that is true in its particulars and false in its shape, and goes home not knowing.

**And the letter is still on the pillow.** Cross-reference highmoore_reconstruction.md — **months later, in the reconstruction content, a player can walk back into that room.**

## 11. STYLE BIBLE COMPLIANCE

- ✅ **Pillar 1 (Staggered Register):** Rowland's plain soldier's Middle offering to fetch his own lord. Cassian's cultured High collapsing into *"...ah"* and then into a sentence he cannot finish. And Jake, who says nothing at all for twenty minutes and holds up a piece of paper instead.
- ✅ **Pillar 2 (Lore Through Objects):** a letter on a pillow in a room where nothing is packed. Six empty hutches visible over the shoulder of the man who emptied them. A horse in a yard full of bodies, given as a kindness.
- ✅ **Pillar 3 (Layered Death):** eleven men in ninety seconds and one man in one motion, with no slow motion, no finisher, no music, and a camera that holds four seconds too long on a woman who does not move.
- ✅ **Pillar 4 (Layered Humor):** one joke in the entire sequence — the drover, and the game letting Jake go around him this time — and it is not funny, and it is placed there precisely so the player registers what has changed.
- ✅ **Pillar 5 (Theme — Revenge):** he reads *do not do anything, please, it will not help and it will not be for me* in her bedroom, alone, with rain on the window, and then he walks downstairs. **And afterward he can turn west, and nothing in the game suggests it.**

## 13. CONCLUSION

Arion Falls. Four minutes of flat gallop in the rain on a horse that does not tire, past the wardens who recognise him, past the drover the game finally lets him go around. An open gate, because nobody set a trap and there was no reason to shut it. Ten household knights who are the last of sixty because the fighting season ended and everyone went home.

And a captain of the guard who offers, genuinely, to go and fetch his own lord out into the yard — and there is no option to say yes.

Then a door that is open and was not before, and a small neat room with grey light and books in three languages and riding clothes hung up and nothing packed at all, and a maid's room next door already emptied, and two hundred and eighty words on a pillow. No cutscene. No music. Rain on the window. The player reads it at their own pace and there is no skip.

And downstairs, in the rain, a man who has heard eleven of his people die comes out unarmed into his own courtyard and delivers a fluent, prepared, entirely true redirection toward House Belos — until a hand comes up with a letter in it, and he stops, and says *ah*, and *she wrote two*, and then asks whether there was a while at the lake where his daughter was happy, because he was not there.
