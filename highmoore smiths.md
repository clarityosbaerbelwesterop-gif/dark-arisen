## 1. THE THREE TRADITIONS

**Cross-reference:** house_system.md Section 5, npcs/blacksmiths_and_armorers.md for the archipelago's framework.

Highmoore has three crafts that matter and they belong to three Houses:

- **IRON** — House Thorn, Stonecountry. Cross-reference the_lesser_houses.md Section 3.3.
- **BOW** — House Belos. Cross-reference house_belos.md Section 2.2.
- **STONE** — House Cadwall. Cross-reference the_lesser_houses.md Section 2.1.

**Design law:** cross-reference npcs/blacksmiths_and_armorers.md Section 1.1 — the archipelago's principle holds. **Smithing is cultural**, and Highmoore's three traditions are recognisably a different civilisation's answer to the same problems.

## 2. HOUSE THORN — IRON

### 2.1 Thornrest

**Cross-reference:** the_lesser_houses.md Section 3.3.

Built into a limestone scar, half cave. Cross-reference physics/sound_physics.md — **the forge halls are inside the rock and the sound of them carries across three kilometres of Stonecountry**, and it is the first thing a rider notices.

### 2.2 LADY ISOLDE THORN

**Age:** 51 | **Register:** flat Middle, entirely unbothered

**Cross-reference:** the_lesser_houses.md Section 3.3 — *"I sell to Arion, I sell to Belos, I sell to whoever comes with silver. They can settle it between themselves. I'll be here."*

**She was right.** Cross-reference highmoore_reconstruction.md Section 2.2.

**ISOLDE:** *"You'll want to know what side I'm on. None. I've been none for thirty years and I've buried nobody at that ford."*

**ISOLDE:** *"You've a sword there I can't do anything with. No — I've looked. Give it here again."*

*(cross-reference Section 6)*

### 2.3 The Iron

**Cross-reference:** mechanics/inventory_crafting.md, physics/combat_physics.md.

Highmoore's iron is good and its steel is better than anything in the archipelago outside the legendary tiers. Cross-reference npcs/blacksmiths_and_armorers.md — **Thorn work sits between the archipelago's Master and Cultural tiers.**

**What she makes:** longswords, arming swords, mail, plate, and the region's tools.

### 2.4 The Craft Tier

**THORN WORK** — cross-reference mechanics/inventory_crafting.md. Excellent, durable, and cross-reference physics/combat_physics.md: **the best guard-breaking weapons available to a player who skipped the Crystal Guardian.**

**Cross-reference crystal_guardian.md Section 8.4** — a player without the Katana equips Thorn steel for the whole of Phase 8, and cross-reference crystal_katana.md Section 9: **it is the version of the questline about futility, and this is what he carries into it.**

## 3. HOUSE BELOS — BOW

### 3.1 The Bowyer's

**Cross-reference:** house_belos.md Section 2.2, Section 7.1.

**The finest weapons in Phase 8** and cross-reference house_belos.md Section 4.2: **the whole population shoots**, so the bowyer's is not a specialist shop. It is the busiest building in the castle.

### 3.2 MASTER BOWYER GRUFFYDD

**Age:** 66 | **Register:** yeoman Middle, technical

He has made bows for three Belos lords and cross-reference house_belos.md Section 3.2: he made the ones Aldric's two sons carried to the ford.

**GRUFFYDD:** *"Yew, and I'll want two winters of it before I touch it. Everyone wants it quick. Everyone gets it in two years or gets someone else's."*

**GRUFFYDD:** *"You draw wrong. No — don't apologise, you draw like a man who learned on something else. I can fix it or I can sell you something that suits the wrong draw. Which?"*

### 3.3 The Weapons

**Cross-reference:** physics/combat_physics.md, physics/wind_systems.md.

**Highmoore warbows.** Heavy draw, enormous range, and cross-reference physics/wind_systems.md — **Highmoore is a wind region and Belos bowyery is built around it.**

**Design note:** cross-reference princess_quest_the_lake.md Section 8.2 — a two-hundred-metre shot in rain and wind, and it is one of these.

### 3.4 What Is Lost

**Cross-reference:** princess_quest_belos.md Section 5.6, highmoore_reconstruction.md Section 3.3.

**The bowyer's is under the western range.**

Cross-reference highmoore_reconstruction.md — it can be cleared and the tradition can be restarted, **and Gruffydd is not there to restart it.**

## 6. THE CRYSTAL KATANA PROBLEM

**Cross-reference:** crystal_katana.md Section 5.1, npcs/blacksmiths_and_armorers.md.

### 6.1 Nobody Can Work On It

Cross-reference crystal_katana.md Section 5.1 — Big Tom could not find anywhere to hold it.

**Neither can Isolde Thorn**, who is the best smith in Highmoore and who has now looked at it four times.

**ISOLDE:** *"Fourth time. Same answer. There's no tang, there's no edge, there's no bevel, the material just stops."*

**ISOLDE:** *"I've been at this thirty years and I can tell you what every piece of steel in this basin wants. That thing doesn't want anything."*

*(a pause)*

**ISOLDE:** *"Take it off my bench."*

### 6.2 Nobody Recognises the Form

**Cross-reference:** crystal_katana.md Section 3.1.

No smith in Highmoore or the archipelago has seen a katana. Cross-reference highmoore_endgame_state.md Section 3.4 — **it is the only object that crosses the mountain and nobody on either side can name it.**

## 9. STYLE BIBLE COMPLIANCE

- ✅ **Pillar 1 (Staggered Register):** Isolde's flat unbothered Middle, Gruffydd's technical yeoman, and Gwilym's aristocratic High that says yes immediately and wants it noted.
- ✅ **Pillar 2 (Lore Through Objects):** yew seasoned two winters. Arion's white walls, built by Cadwall. A bench a sword gets taken off four times.
- ✅ **Pillar 3 (Layered Death):** Gruffydd made the bows Aldric's two sons carried to the ford, and he is under the western range.
- ✅ **Pillar 4 (Layered Humor):** *"Take it off my bench."* Gwilym saying yes immediately and demanding it be noted. Gruffydd offering to sell a man something that suits the wrong draw.
- ✅ **Pillar 5 (Theme — Revenge):** the finest craft tradition in Phase 8 is under a wall Jake brought down, and it can be dug out, and the man who knew it is not coming back.

## 10. CONCLUSION

Iron, Bow, and Stone. A forge cut into a limestone scar whose sound carries three kilometres, run by a woman who has been on nobody's side for thirty years and has buried nobody at that ford and was right about all of it. A bowyer's in a castle where the whole population shoots, run by a man of sixty-six who wants two winters of yew before he touches it and who made the bows two dead sons carried.

Masons who built half the basin including a set of white walls eight kilometres from a crystal mine, whose lord will build a bridge at a ford where four generations drowned, and wants it noted that he said yes immediately.

And a sword that gets taken off a bench four times by the best smith in the country, who can tell you what every piece of steel in the basin wants, and who says that thing doesn't want anything.
